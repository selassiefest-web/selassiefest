✦ WHAT IS THE REASONING CIRCLE?

The Reasoning Circle is the intellectual and spiritual heart of SelassieFest — a gathering place inspired by the Rastafarian tradition of “reasoning”: open, respectful dialogue under the shade of a tree or inside a humble hall. Here, we do not simply talk; we listen, challenge, learn, and uplift one another.

Rooted in the legacy of His Imperial Majesty Haile Selassie I and the global Pan‑African movement, the Reasoning Circle offers a safe container for exploring Ethiopian history, Rastafarian livity, social justice, and personal transformation. Whether you are a lifelong elder or a first‑time seeker, your voice belongs here.

Throughout the day, you can join community‑led dialogues, receive direct wisdom from respected Rastafarian elders, or roll up your sleeves for hands‑on workshops that teach practical skills rooted in heritage. Come with an open heart, leave with a deeper understanding of self and community.

✦ THREE WAYS TO REASON

Community Dialogues

Open, moderated conversations on topics like Rastafarian theology, Ethiopian history, Pan‑African liberation, reparations, and climate justice. Every voice matters — no hierarchy, only mutual respect.

Elders Circle

Intimate sessions led by Rastafarian elders and Ethiopian scholars. Oral traditions, ancestral wisdom, and guidance for navigating modern life while keeping roots strong.

Hands‑On Workshops

Learn by doing: Ital cooking, Nyabinghi drumming, Amharic language basics, herbal medicine, and natural hair/body care. Practical skills that honour livity.
