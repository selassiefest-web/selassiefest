A guide for families: how a free DJ class at Rainbow Beach works

Hours, ages, cost and what a child actually does during a Tuesday at the Rainbow DJ Lab

By CLAUDETTE WHITFIELD-NASH (pseudonym)

For South Side families looking for something free and constructive after school, the Rainbow DJ Lab at Rainbow Beach Park offers a weekly class with a clear shape.

When: every Tuesday from 4 to 6 p.m., all year. The Lab opened Jan. 12, 2027. Where: the Rainbow Beach Park fieldhouse in South Shore. Who: students in grades 6 through 12. Cost: free.

A child can start on any Tuesday, so there is no waiting for a new session. Each student gets a dedicated DJ controller at one of 12 stations, and two resident instructors teach every class.

Progress is tracked in a passport, not a report card. There are 14 Missions and 42 skills, and an instructor stamps each one when the student shows it. Students move at their own pace, and a missed week costs nothing.

A guest DJ visits each week. Organizers book women DJs so students can see more of the careers in music.

The program is a project of Ras Tafari Inc., a Chicago nonprofit. Families can read the schedule, age groups, safety information and FAQ, and sign up, using the links at the top of this page.
