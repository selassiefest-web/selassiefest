Youth

Free DJ lab at Rainbow Beach gives South Side youth a controller and a mentor

Students in grades 6 to 12 learn mixing, performance and music business every Tuesday afternoon, at no cost

By LORETTA MBEKI-GRANT (pseudonym)

Young people on the South Side now have a free place to learn a skill that powers concerts, radio, festivals and nightlife.

The Rainbow DJ Lab at Rainbow Beach Park meets every Tuesday from 4 to 6 p.m. Open to students in grades 6 through 12, it is a program of Ras Tafari Inc., a Chicago nonprofit. Each student works on a dedicated DJ controller, and there are 12 stations in the room.

Two resident instructors lead each class. A guest DJ visits every week, including women who can speak to what a career in music looks like.

Students earn stamps in a passport for each of 42 skills. A child can join any Tuesday, move at their own pace and finish when all skills are stamped.

The year closes with the Rainbow Showcase, where families and neighbors see the students play. Parents can find the schedule, locations, safety information and enrollment on the Lab’s pages.
