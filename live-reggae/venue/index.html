You set the price

Every room is different, so you decide what works for yours. One price covers everyone: the full band, the featured singer, the DJ and the crew.

Option A
You name the fee

A flat fee per night, at a number you are comfortable with. Simple and predictable, whatever the crowd.

Option B
Or share the night

You choose the structure, for example:

  • a split of the door
  • a guarantee, or the door share if that's higher
  • a guarantee plus a share of the bar

How it starts

  1. Tell us your night and which option you'd like, using the form below.
  2. We'll call you to set the details. Already set up for live music? We plug right in. If not, we bring our own sound and lighting.
  3. We book one trial night. If it works for you, it goes on the weekly rotation.
  4. Every confirmed night is listed on our public calendar at selassiefest.com/live-reggae, so your regulars can find it.
