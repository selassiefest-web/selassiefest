Bob Marley: Live Forever. His final concert, played live in your club.

On September 23, 1980, Bob Marley played the Stanley Theatre in Pittsburgh. It was the last concert he ever gave. Jay RebL, backed by Ifficial Reggae Movement, plays that whole night again, all 20 songs in the order Bob played them. Our first choice for the date is his birthday, Saturday, February 6, 2027.

Why it fills a room

A real night, not a greatest-hits setTwo days before Pittsburgh he collapsed in Central Park and was told to stop touring. He played anyway. This is the set he played that night, released by his estate in 2011 as Live Forever.
His birthday weekendFebruary 6 is the night reggae fans already look for a Marley show. Your room becomes the place to be for it.
A crowd theme to promoteBob's denim on denim for everyone, and the I Threes look (prints and head wraps) for the ladies. Guests dress up, post photos and bring friends.
Jay RebL as BobA Kingston, Jamaica singer and guitarist, and a featured vocalist with Ifficial Reggae Movement. See Jay RebL
