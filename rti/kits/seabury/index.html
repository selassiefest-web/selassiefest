1

Email Seabury first (today)

Why first: two required questions don't fit a new organization. Seabury wants a profit and loss statement for the most recently completed fiscal year; ours ends Dec. 31, 2026. And it funds organizations headquartered in the City of Chicago, while our IRS and Illinois records still show South Holland. Their form says to email them when either applies.

Email (copy and send from stephen@selassiefest.com)

A profit and loss statement (statement of activities): 2026 income and expenses to date, or whatever Seabury says in its reply. PDF, 2 MB max. Owner: Paul.
