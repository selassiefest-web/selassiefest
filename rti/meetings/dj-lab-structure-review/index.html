Ras Tafari Inc. · board meeting

DJ Lab Structure Review

The board reviews the Rainbow DJ Lab as it is now settled, and signs off on it. The Lab runs year-round on Tuesdays, children work through 14 Missions at their own pace, and a child graduates when all 42 Passport skills are stamped. Each item below links to the pages the board is approving.

Date and time: to be setLength: about 90 minutesPresenter: Stephen HenryStatus as of: Oct. 20, 2026
How the sign-off works. Each director has a personal signing link for the packet Rainbow DJ Lab program structure, revised policies, pay and 2027 budget. Albert Harris, as Secretary, signs first; the moment he signs, every other director receives a link. Links are personal. Nothing is approved until all five directors have signed, and no class starts until every item on the launch checklist is complete.

1. Why one structure

5 min

The Lab’s plan is now one structure. The pages and policies were updated to match, and on October 17 the board decided that any policy change takes effect only when the board approves it. This meeting is that approval.

2. The program structure

20 min
  • Every Tuesday, 4:00 to 6:00 pm, Rainbow Beach Park fieldhouse. Opening Tuesday planned for January 12, 2027; 39 class Tuesdays in 2027; no class in a federal-holiday week or during CPS spring break.
  • Twelve stations, one deck per child. Children join any Tuesday and never lose progress for a missed class.
  • A 90-minute core of nine blocks, then 30 minutes with a guest woman DJ.
  • Two resident instructors, one man and one woman, each coaching six students.
  • Rainbow Showcases for families in spring, at midyear and at year end.

4. Graduation rule

10 min

A child graduates when all 42 Passport skills are stamped by a mentor and a final set is ready to play, then plays the next Rainbow Showcase. A coach records each criterion as met or not met, the program coordinator confirms it, and no one can waive a criterion. Performing is not a criterion. This completes launch checklist item 18. Page: graduation guarantee.

20 min
