Ras Tafari Inc. · for funders and partners

Free music in Chicago's parks, built to last

Ras Tafari Inc. is a Chicago 501(c)(3) that brings free DJ classes, youth DJ nights, concerts and festivals to neighborhood parks, starting at Rainbow Beach Park in South Shore. This page has what a reviewer needs: who we are, what each program costs, where it stands and who stands with us.

Accountability

Governance

  • Board: Stephen Henry, President; Paul Kelly, MA, MHS, LPC, Vice President and Treasurer; Albert Harris, Secretary. Officers are unpaid today; any paid role needs approval by directors with no stake in it.
  • Bylaws plus conflict-of-interest, gift acceptance and records policies adopted in June 2026; board actions signed and filed.
  • Two-signature rule on larger payments.

Youth safety

  • Every adult who works with young people clears the Chicago Park District's background check.
  • Every adult is an Illinois mandated reporter.
  • Mentoring is led by Paul Kelly, MA, MHS, LPC, a licensed professional counselor and founder of Self-Improvement Philosophy, a mentoring curriculum for ages 15 to 20.
  • Written youth protection, emergency and privacy policies: read them.

Transparency

  • Program budgets published before launch.
  • A public season report: attendance, girls' participation, performers and spending.
  • Documents on request: IRS determination letter, Illinois good standing, bylaws, budgets.

How to fund this work

  • Program grants: fund one program or one season; we report on exactly that.
  • Sponsorships: a Rainbow Wednesday, a concert or a class cohort.
  • Gear and in-kind: DJ controllers, headphones, laptops, printing, food for family nights.
  • Gifts of any size: donate online; contributions are tax-deductible.
