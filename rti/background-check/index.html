Ras Tafari Inc. · Chicago Park District background check

Your Park District background check, in about 10 minutes

Every Ras Tafari Inc. member and associate completes the Chicago Park District's background check. It starts with the Park District's volunteer application. Fill in this page and it types your answers into the Park District's own form for you, then emails it to their volunteer office.

  1. Confirm your email with the 6-digit code we send you. This page is for Ras Tafari Inc. members only.
  2. Fill in the form. Starred fields are required. It takes about 10 minutes.
  3. Sign with your finger or mouse, then press Review application and download a copy to check it.
  4. Press Send. The Park District emails you the background check instructions.
Goes to: volunteers@chicagoparkdistrict.com, with a copy to you
Ras Tafari Inc. sees: only your name, email, phone and role, so we know you've applied. Your date of birth, ID number and emergency contacts go only to the Park District.
Your progress saves as you go. Stop any time and pick up on any phone or computer: sign in with your email again and your answers come back. They're stored encrypted and used only to fill in your application. After you send it, your SSN or license digits are erased from the saved copy.
The Park District runs the background check under its own rules. Questions? Email stephen@selassiefest.com.
