Ras Tafari Inc. Form 8283, Donee Acknowledgment

When a donor gives us non-cash property worth more than $500 (equipment, goods), they need our signature in Part V, Donee Acknowledgment, of IRS Form 8283 (Rev. 12-2025). This page fills in our side in your browser. Nothing is sent or stored on a server.

Optional: the donor's Form 8283

If the donor sent you their filled-in 8283, choose it here and we add Part V to it. Leave empty to use a blank form (the donor then fills in the rest).

Part V. About the gift

We affirm we are a qualified organization under section 170(c) and that if we sell, exchange or otherwise dispose of the property within 3 years we will file Form 8282. Signing does not agree with the donor's claimed value.
