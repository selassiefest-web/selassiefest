Governance and agreements

  1. Separate finances. Each entity keeps its own bank accounts, accounting and records.
  2. Conflict-of-interest management. A Ras Tafari director or officer with an interest in an outside organization discloses it and does not take part in the approval.
  3. Fair-market transactions. Ras Tafari pays outside businesses only for legitimate services that further its charitable mission, at reasonable, documented prices. Private inurement and excess-benefit rules apply to anything involving insiders.

Collaborative Advisory Council (proposed)

One central Ras Tafari board governs. Each collaborator names a point of contact and may sit on a voluntary Collaborative Advisory Council that coordinates projects. Council members are not directors and hold no nonprofit governance authority. This protects Ras Tafari's 501(c)(3) independence when it contracts with NP2 or Prestige Sound.
