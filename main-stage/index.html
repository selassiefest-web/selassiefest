Your Host

FIELD MARSHALL

Born and raised in Chicago, Field Marshall is one of Chicago's most respected and enduring reggae selectors, known for his deep knowledge of classic reggae, lovers rock, roots, and dancehall music. For decades, he has been a driving force within the city's reggae community, earning recognition for his ability to blend timeless classics with crowd-favorite anthems that keep dance floors energized and audiences engaged.

A fixture at many of Chicago's premier reggae events, Field Marshall has built a reputation for authenticity, musical excellence, and an unwavering commitment to preserving reggae culture. His carefully curated selections reflect a profound appreciation for the genre's rich history while celebrating its continuing influence across generations.

Whether performing at intimate gatherings, cultural celebrations, or major festivals, Field Marshall delivers an unforgettable musical journey that connects people through the spirit of reggae. His dedication to the music and the community has made him a beloved ambassador of reggae culture throughout Chicago and beyond.

"Field Marshall brings his signature blend of roots reggae, lovers rock, and dancehall energy to SelassieFest, continuing a tradition of excellence that has made him a favorite among reggae lovers throughout the Midwest."
