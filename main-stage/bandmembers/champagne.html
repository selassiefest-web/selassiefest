✦ Biography Roots · Rhythm · Redemption

Ricardo Phillips, known on stage as Champagne, is a professional drummer whose rhythmic journey began in the heart of Jamaica. From the age of 13, Ricardo was immersed in the pulsing energy of church music, laying the foundation for a career that would span continents and genres.

His path took him from gospel bands in high school to the vibrant reggae resort circuit of Jamaica in his early 20s. For nearly a decade, he performed full-time at hotel resorts, honing his craft alongside some of the island's finest musicians.

Relocated to the United States — Over the last 10 years, Ricardo has become a cornerstone of the Midwest reggae scene, bringing his signature groove to stages from Chicago to Cincinnati, Minnesota to Indiana.

Today, Champagne continues to back some of the most respected names in international reggae, while remaining deeply connected to his gospel roots — a testament to his versatility and soul.
