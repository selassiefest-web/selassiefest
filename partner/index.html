What is Full Spectrum?

A free, year-round music and culture program at Rainbow Beach Park, 3111 E. 77th St., so South Shore families don't have to travel downtown for it.

  • aDJ classes every Tuesday for grades 6 to 12, open to all ages, with a woman guest DJ teaching every week
  • bRainbow Wednesdays, free summer music nights on the beach
  • cLive stage shows with DJs and local and major recording artists
  • dFestivals, including Caribbean and African heritage days and SelassieFest
  • eFall & holiday parties, from the Kids Kostume Party to Noon Year's Eve
