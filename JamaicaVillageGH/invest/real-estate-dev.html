✦ THE OPPORTUNITY

Ghana's real estate sector is booming, driven by a growing middle class, urbanisation, and a strong diaspora investment culture. Jamaica Village offers a unique combination of affordable land, scenic beauty, and proximity to Accra.

Residential Development

High‑demand housing — from affordable starter homes to premium eco‑villillas. Target diaspora returnees, local professionals, and families seeking a peaceful, green community.

  • 200+ housing units potential
  • Gated community with green spaces
  • Pre‑approved building permits

Commercial & Retail

A central commercial hub serving the village and surrounding region: shops, offices, co‑working spaces, and light industrial units. Capitalise on the growing local economy.

  • 5–8 acres for commercial zone
  • Market-rate rental income
  • High footfall from tourism + locals

Community Infrastructure

Schools, health clinics, community centres, and recreational spaces. These anchor the community, increase property values, and align with government development goals.

  • Public-private partnership model
  • Tax incentives for social infrastructure
  • Long‑term community goodwill & value
📋 PHASED DEVELOPMENT PLAN

A strategic, low‑risk approach that allows for capital recycling and market adaptation across three phases.

Phase 1: Enabling & Showcase

Land clearing, road access, utilities (water, solar, internet), and a model show home/commercial unit. Establishes the brand and pre‑sells units.

  • 12–18 months
  • $500k – $1.2M investment
  • Builds market confidence

Phase 2: Core Development

Full construction of residential units (100+ homes), commercial hub, and community amenities. Revenue from pre‑sales and rentals begins.

  • 24–36 months
  • $3M – $6M investment
  • Occupancy & rental yield

Phase 3: Expansion & Premium

Additional units (premium villas), expansion of commercial space, and vertical integration (e.g., hotel or retirement village). Maximum land value capture.

  • 36–60 months
  • $2M – $5M investment
  • High‑margin exit or hold

Government & Policy Alignment

Tax Incentives

5‑year tax holiday on qualifying real estate developments in rural/peri‑urban zones.

Diaspora Property Rights

Ghana allows non‑citizens to own land via leasehold (up to 50 years) with clear title registration.

Infrastructure Grants

Public‑private partnerships for roads, water, and electricity receive government co‑funding.

Green Building Certification

Ghana's EDGE certification program offers incentives for sustainable, energy‑efficient designs.
