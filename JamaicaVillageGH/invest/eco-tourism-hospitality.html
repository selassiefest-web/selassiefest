✦ THE OPPORTUNITY

West Africa's tourism sector is projected to grow 6.5% annually through 2030. Jamaica Village is uniquely positioned to capture the high-end, culturally conscious traveller.

Prime Location

Nestled in the lush Eastern Region, just 90 minutes from Accra's international airport. Easy accessibility meets complete seclusion — the perfect balance for a premium retreat.

  • 1.5 hrs from Kotoka International Airport
  • Rolling hills with panoramic valley views
  • Adjacent to the Rita Marley studio & heritage site

Market Demand

The global wellness tourism market is worth over $800 billion. Travellers are seeking authentic, community-integrated experiences — not just hotels, but cultural immersion.

  • 45% annual increase in "roots tourism" to Ghana
  • Growing diaspora & Pan-African travel movement
  • Untapped luxury eco-lodge segment in the region

Unique Assets

No other site in Ghana offers this combination of natural beauty, reggae heritage, and community readiness. Your investment builds on a story that's already being told.

  • Pre‑approved zoning for eco‑hospitality
  • Existing water, solar, and road infrastructure
  • Active community land‑trust partnership
🏗️ DEVELOPMENT OPTIONS

Choose a concept — or combine them. Our feasibility studies cover each model with detailed cost breakdowns and revenue forecasts.

Boutique Eco-Lodges

Low‑impact, high‑design lodges built with local materials. 8–12 luxury units with private verandas, outdoor showers, and panoramic hill views.

  • $1.2M – $2.5M estimated build
  • 70–85% projected annual occupancy
  • Average nightly rate: $280–$450

Wellness & Retreat Centre

A dedicated space for yoga, meditation, sound healing, and herbal wellness programs — leveraging the village's existing healing grove and knowledge keepers.

  • $800k – $1.8M estimated build
  • Group retreat packages (5–20 pax)
  • Partnership with global wellness brands

Farm-to-Table Restaurant & Cultural Pavilion

An open‑air restaurant featuring Ital and Ghanaian cuisine, plus a pavilion for storytelling, drumming, and cultural demonstrations. A central gathering space for guests and community.

  • $350k – $600k estimated build
  • Daily dining & event revenue streams
  • Showcases local produce and culinary heritage

Community Impact & Sustainability

This is not extractive tourism. Every lodge, every retreat, every meal supports local livelihoods, preserves cultural heritage, and protects the environment.

Local Employment

120+ direct and indirect jobs created — from guides and chefs to artisans and maintenance staff.

Regenerative Design

Rainwater harvesting, solar energy, composting, and native landscaping. Zero waste to landfill target.

Cultural Preservation

Revenue funds the Rita Marley archive, youth drumming and dance programs, and the preservation of oral history.

Community Equity

All projects include a community profit‑share agreement, ensuring the village benefits directly from tourism growth.
