✦ THE OPPORTUNITY

Ghana offers one of the most favourable agricultural climates in West Africa. Combined with strong export incentives and a growing domestic market, Jamaica Village is perfectly positioned for agri-investment.

Industrial Hemp & Medicinal Cannabis

The global hemp market is projected to reach $18.6 billion by 2030. Ghana's climate allows for 2–3 harvests per year, with lower production costs than Europe or North America.

  • Fibre, seed, and oil applications
  • Medicinal cannabis aligned with Higher Learning Roots
  • Export-ready to EU & US markets

Premium Organic Cocoa

Ghana is the world's second-largest cocoa producer. Our focus is on fine-flavour, organic, and fair-trade cocoa — commanding premium prices in the specialty chocolate market.

  • Heirloom varieties with unique terroir
  • Direct-trade relationships with European chocolatiers
  • On-site processing for value addition

Synergy with SelassieFest

This vertical is deeply integrated with the festival's Higher Learning Roots pavilion — creating a living classroom for cannabis education, terpene discovery, and sustainable agriculture.

  • Terpene Discovery Bar on-site
  • Farm-to-dispensary educational series
  • Smart Buds BioLab for research & cultivation
🌾 CROP BREAKDOWN

Diversify your risk with two complementary cash crops. Each has distinct markets, cycles, and revenue profiles.

Hemp (Industrial & Medicinal)

Grown on 50+ acres. Dual-purpose: fibre and seed for industrial applications, plus high-CBD flower for medicinal extraction.

  • Estimated yield: 2,500–3,500 kg fibre/acre
  • CBD flower: 800–1,200 kg/acre (dry)
  • Revenue per acre: $12k – $28k (depending on market)

Organic Cocoa

Grown on 30+ acres under shade trees. Focus on fine-flavour beans with deep terroir expression — targeting the premium craft chocolate segment.

  • Estimated yield: 600–900 kg beans/acre
  • Premium price: +40–60% over commodity
  • Revenue per acre: $4k – $8k annually (mature trees)

On-Site Processing Hub

Value addition is key. Plans include a small-batch CBD extraction facility and a bean-to-bar chocolate micro-factory — capturing higher margins.

  • CBD oil & distillate production
  • Craft chocolate bars & cocoa butter
  • Packaging & branding for export
