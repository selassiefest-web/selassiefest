    8C I · Manual printed page 103–104

    10 · Earthworm — internal dissection

    Dissected specimen · internal view

    Label

    • Mouth
    • Pharynx
    • Esophagus
    • Crop
    • Gizzard
    • Intestine
    • Aortic arches (5 pairs), or hearts
    • Nephridia
    • Intersegmental septa
    • Anus
    • Seminal vesicles
    • Seminal receptacles
    • Brain (two ganglia)
    • Ventral nerve cord

    The manual lists “aortic arches (5).” These occur as five pairs in Lumbricus. Nephridia are not present in every segment.
