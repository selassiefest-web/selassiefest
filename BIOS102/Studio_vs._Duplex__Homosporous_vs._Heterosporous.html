Studio vs. Duplex

Homosporous = a studio apartment: one space, everything happens in it. Heterosporous = a duplex: two units, two jobs. Tap to flip between floor plans.

  • 1 spore type grows into 1 gametophyte with both sexes.
  • Gametophyte is bigger and usually free-living.
  • Examples: most ferns, horsetails, many club mosses.
  • Micro = male. Small spore, male gametophyte, sperm.
  • Mega = female. Big spore, female gametophyte, eggs. Say it: "Mega eggs."
  • Gametophytes are tiny and stay inside the spore wall, fed by the parent.
  • Examples: Selaginella, Isoetes, water ferns, all seed plants. This is the stepping stone to seeds.

Quiz yourself (tap to reveal)

How many spore types in a duplex?

Two: microspores and megaspores.

Which plant group does the studio describe?

Homosporous plants such as most ferns and horsetails.

Which one led to seeds?

Heterospory. Keeping the female gametophyte inside the parent is the first step toward an ovule.

Exam trap: is "heterosporous" the same as "dioecious"?

No. Heterosporous is about spores. Dioecious is about separate male and female individual plants (or flowers) in seed plants.
