The two threads

Most of what a sponge does hangs on two paths. Learn the paths first and the cell types fall into place.

Follow the water
Porestiny holes in the body wall (Porifera = pore-bearer). Porocytes are listed as a cell type.
Incurrent canalswater enters
Radial canalslined by collar cells (choanocytes)
Spongocoelcentral cavity
Osculumwater exits at the open end

Manual p. 81: “Water enters the incurrent and radial canals and then passes to the central cavity called the spongocoel; water exits through the sponge’s open end called the osculum.” The manual never says the pores feed the incurrent canals; that link is the obvious inference.

Follow the food
Food in the waterparticles ride the current in
Collar cellsfilter and take it in Quiz 5 Q7: feeding
Amoebocytesdigest and distribute Teaching (Review Q6 asks, manual doesn’t answer)
Every cellno gut, so cells pass food along
Waste outleaves with the outgoing water Teaching

Manual gives only “filter-feeding heterotrophs” with “no mouths or digestive tract.” Everything between the water and the cell is something you are expected to work out. The food thread and the water thread are the same current.
