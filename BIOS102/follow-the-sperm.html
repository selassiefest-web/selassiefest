A sponge cannot swim to a mate. First fix the error hiding in the textbook paragraph, then trace one sperm cell from its birth in one sponge to the egg it fertilizes in another.

Find and fix

One word in this paragraph is wrong

Tap the word you think is incorrect. Then type the right one.

Mesohyl is the jelly-like layer between a sponge's outer cells and its collar cells. Amoebocytes crawl through it, and eggs develop in it.

Mesophyll is the middle tissue of a plant leaf, where photosynthesis happens. Sponges are animals, so the word cannot apply to them.
