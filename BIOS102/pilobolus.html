Collecting Pilobolus Data

Exercise 3 data sheet, lab manual pp. 39–40. Enter your group's numbers in Table 1. Table 2 pools every group's entries into the class data and averages them automatically.

  1. You have 30 minutes to make your observations and fill in your results in Table 1.
  2. You have data for two variables (constant light and no light).
  3. Measure the extent of mycelium growth with the provided grid: count how many squares show mycelium growth.
  4. Count the stalks not yet topped by a black sporangium: these are developing sporangia (may have an orange color tip).
  5. Count the stalks that are topped by a black sporangium: these are developed sporangia.
  6. Under other observations, write down any other information you might consider important.
