🔊 Pronunciation Guide

Recurring root — worth learning now -phyte / -phyta = plant (Gk. phyton). Exercise 4 is full of it: Bryophyta, Hepatophyta, gametophyte, sporophyte. The front half tells you what kind of plant or which generation:
gametophyte
  • gameto- gamete (egg or sperm)
  • -phyte plant — the haploid (n) plant that makes gametes
sporophyte
  • sporo- spore
  • -phyte plant — the diploid (2n) plant that makes spores
