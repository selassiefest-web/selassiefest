🔊 Pronunciation Guide

Recurring roots — worth learning now -cyte = cell (Gk. kytos) and -zoa = animals (Gk. zōion). The front half tells you what kind of cell or animal, so the sponge and cnidarian cell types sort themselves out.
choanocyte
  • choano- funnel, collar
  • -cyte cell — the sponge collar cell that moves water and traps food
cnidocyte
  • cnido- nettle, sting
  • -cyte cell — the cnidarian stinging cell
hydrozoa
  • hydro- water
  • -zoa animals — also Scyphozoa, Anthozoa, Parazoa, Eumetazoa
