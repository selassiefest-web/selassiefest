Morpheme Breakdown

Exercise 2 (The Protists) & Exercise 3 (Fungi) — only the terms that actually break down into meaningful prefixes, roots, and suffixes. Names like Peziza or Agaricus aren't here because they don't decompose into recognizable word parts.

These are simplified, classroom-level breakdowns meant to help you remember and guess unfamiliar terms — not strict linguistic etymology.

🔊 Pronunciation Guide — hear these same terms spoken aloud.

Recurring root — worth learning now chloro- = green. This one won't stop showing up: Kingdom Plantae (Exercises 4–6, coming up) is built almost entirely around photosynthesis, so expect it lab after lab. You've already met it below in Chlorophytes — here it is again, spelled out:
chlorophyll
  • chloro- green
  • -phyll leaf — the light-absorbing pigment itself
chloroplast
  • chloro- green
  • -plast a formed/molded body — the organelle that holds the chlorophyll
