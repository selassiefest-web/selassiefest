title: Our promises to you
description: What every grown-up at the Rainbow DJ Lab promises every kid.
---

## We promise

1. **You'll never be alone with one grown-up out of sight.** There are always at least two grown-ups. Other people can always see you.
2. **You choose your level.** Mild, Medium or Spicy. You pick.
3. **You never have to perform.** Spotlight is only for kids who raise their hand. Shows are only if you **and** your grown-up say yes. You can change your mind any time, even on stage.
4. **Your headphones won't be too loud.** The volume is capped. Earplugs are free.
5. **Mistakes are OK.** A crash is "Data!" Nobody gets in trouble for it.
6. **Nobody will laugh at you.** And nobody will make fun of you, hit you or shame you.
7. **You can always get water and go to the bathroom.**
8. **You'll only go home with a grown-up on your list.** We check their ID first.
9. **We'll never drive you anywhere.** Your family brings you and picks you up.
10. **We'll never ask you to keep a secret** about touching or private messages.
11. **We'll listen and believe you** when you tell us something is wrong.
12. **We'll call your grown-up** if you get hurt or feel sick.

## Grades? Nope

There are no grades and no tests. You rate yourself, and you earn Passport stamps when you show a coach a new skill.

## If a grown-up breaks a promise

Tell another grown-up you trust. You can tell your grown-up at home. See [Who to talk to](/dj-lab/kids/who-to-talk-to.html) and [If you feel unsafe](/dj-lab/kids/if-you-feel-unsafe.html).
