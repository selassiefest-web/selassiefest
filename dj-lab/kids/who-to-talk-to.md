## You can talk to

- **Any coach at the Lab.** There are always at least two grown-ups in class.
- **Your grown-up at home.** Tell them about your day, the good parts and the hard parts.
- **Stephen Henry.** He leads the Lab. Your grown-up can call or email him.

## What you can talk about

Anything! For example:

- "I don't understand the mission."
- "I don't want to play in Spotlight today."
- "My headphones are too loud."
- "I need a break."
- "Someone was mean to me."
- "Something made me feel weird or scared."

## Stuck on a DJ skill?

First, ask your **Co-pilot** one question. Still stuck? Ask a coach.

## Need a calm-down break?

Tell a coach. You can sit in a calm spot. A grown-up will be where they can see you, and other people can see them too.

## Something feels unsafe?

Read [If you feel unsafe](/dj-lab/kids/if-you-feel-unsafe.html). It's never your fault. You can tell any grown-up you trust. In an emergency, call **911**.
