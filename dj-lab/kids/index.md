## Welcome!

The Rainbow DJ Lab is a free class where kids learn to DJ. You'll learn how to mix songs, find the beat, and make a crowd dance.

The grown-ups are still getting the Lab ready. There are no class dates yet. Your grown-up at home will tell you when it starts.

## What you'll do

- Use a real DJ controller. It has lights that help you match the beat.
- Work in a team of 4 kids.
- Take turns with fun jobs: **DJ**, **Co-pilot**, **Hype** and **Logger**.
- Earn stamps in your **Passport**.
- Dance in a **Move Break**.
- Play for the class in **Spotlight**, but only if you want to!

## One big thing to remember

You are the boss of your own "yes" and "no" about playing for people. You never have to perform. Ever.
