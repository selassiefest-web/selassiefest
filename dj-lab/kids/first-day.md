---
title: Your first day
description: What happens on your first day at the Rainbow DJ Lab, step by step.
---

## Before you come

- Your grown-up signs you in. You don't come in alone.
- Eat something and bring a water bottle.
- If you feel sick or have a fever, stay home and rest. You can come next time.

## When you get there

Your grown-up signs you in with a coach. Then you get your **Passport**. It's a card where you collect stamps for new skills.

## What happens in class

A class is 90 minutes. That's an hour and a half. Here's the plan:

| Part | What you do |
|---|---|
| **Drop In** | Music is playing. Find the beat! Clap on 1, stomp on 4. |
| **Mission Card** | Find out today's mission. Guess what will happen. |
| **Watch Me Think** | A coach shows you how a DJ thinks. |
| **Deck Time 1** | Your team takes turns on the controller. |
| **Move Break** | Get up and dance! |
| **Deck Time 2** | Try again. You pick **Mild**, **Medium** or **Spicy**. |
| **Wordplay Lab** | Silly word games with song names. |
| **Spotlight** | Kids who **want** to play for 60 seconds on the big speaker. You only play if you raise your hand. |
| **Check Out** | Rate yourself. Collect your stamps. |

## Your team and your jobs

You're on a team of 4 kids. Your team shares one controller. Every few minutes, you switch jobs:

- **DJ:** Hands on the decks. You try the mission.
- **Co-pilot:** Headphones on. Count the beat out loud. Ask the DJ, "Did it work?"
- **Hype:** You are the crowd! Practice saying one line on the beat.
- **Logger:** Write down what happened in the Mix Log.

Everybody gets a turn at every job.

## At the end

You give yourself a traffic light:

- **Green:** I got it!
- **Yellow:** Getting there.
- **Red:** Not yet.

Red is OK! It just tells you what to practice. Then you finish this sentence: "Next time I'll…"

There are no grades and no tests.

## Going home

You only leave with a grown-up on your family's pickup list. A coach checks their ID. You don't walk home alone unless your grown-up said yes in writing first.

## Feeling nervous?

That's normal! Lots of kids feel that way on day one. Tell a coach. They'll help you.
