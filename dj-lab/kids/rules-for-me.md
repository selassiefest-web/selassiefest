## Our five rules

### 1. Be kind

Cheer for every DJ. When someone plays, give them a **glow**. That's one thing they did well.

### 2. Take turns

Your team shares one controller. Everyone gets a turn at every job: DJ, Co-pilot, Hype and Logger.

### 3. Take care of the gear

Gentle hands on the controller. No food or drinks near the decks. Help put the gear away at the end.

### 4. Protect your ears

Keep your headphones at the safe volume. Stay out of the spot right in front of the big speakers. Want earplugs? Just ask. They're free.

### 5. Stay where a grown-up can see you

Don't go off on your own. Need the bathroom? Take a buddy, and tell a coach. (See [Bathroom and buddy](/dj-lab/kids/bathroom-and-buddy.html).)

## Two more things

- **Clean words only.** We use clean versions of songs.
- **Mistakes are OK.** When a mix crashes, say **"Data!"** and try again. (See [What "Data!" means](/dj-lab/kids/data-mistakes.html).)

## What if someone forgets a rule?

Everybody forgets sometimes. Here's what happens:

1. A coach gives you a reminder.
2. If you need it, the coach helps you switch to something else.
3. If you need a break, you can sit in a calm spot, where a grown-up can see you.
4. Sometimes a coach calls your grown-up at home.

Nobody will hit you, yell mean things at you, or make fun of you. And you can always get water and go to the bathroom.
