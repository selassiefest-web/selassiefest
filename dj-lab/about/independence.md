## What we mean by independence

At the Lab, independence means **children learn to make their own decisions at the decks** and to judge their own progress honestly. It does **not** mean children are left unsupervised. A child can be in charge of the music and still be surrounded by screened adults.

## What your child decides

### At the decks
- **Which song comes next.** Kids build their own crate of clean songs and choose what to play.
- **When to bring the next song in.** By session 4 they're waiting for beat 1 of a new phrase and making the call themselves.
- **How to fix a mistake.** When a mix crashes, the station shouts "Data!" and the DJ names one reason, then decides what to try next.
- **What goes in their set.** In session 13 each child fills in their own Set Map (Opener, Build, Theme Clash, Peak, Exit) and writes their own Plan B.

### About their challenge
- **Their level.** In Deck Time 2, each child picks Mild, Medium or Spicy, and can switch any time without asking.
- **Their prediction.** At the start of each session, kids guess whether the Mission will be easy, medium or hard for them, and say why.

### About their own progress
- **Their self-rating.** At Check Out, kids tap a traffic light: I got it / Getting there / Not yet.
- **Their next step.** Each child writes a "Next time I'll…" line.
- **Whether to perform.** Spotlight is volunteers only. Coaches never call on a child who didn't sign up.

### In their group
- **Taking a job seriously.** Every child rotates through DJ, Co-pilot, Hype and Logger. The Co-pilot asks the DJ "Did it work?" Kids learn to coach each other.

## How adults support it

- **The coach thinks out loud** in "Watch Me Think," so kids hear how an experienced DJ makes decisions before they make their own.
- **Coaches ask before they touch** a child's controls.
- **Coaches compare** each child's self-rating with what they saw. If the two differ, they talk about it. That conversation is part of the lesson.

## What children are never left to do alone

These are policies Ras Tafari Inc. commits to. Formal board adoption is a launch requirement.

- **Never alone with one adult out of sight.** At least two screened adults are with children at all times. Any one-on-one moment must be observable and interruptible.
- **Never leave on their own.** Children are signed in by a parent or guardian and released only to an adult on the authorized-pickup list, after a photo ID check. Children aged 8–11 never walk home alone unless a parent or guardian gives written permission in advance.
- **Never go to the restroom alone.** Children go in pairs, and an adult waits at the restroom entrance. Little DJs go with their own grown-up.
- **Never handle their own medication unsupervised.** Medication is stored locked, except rescue medicine, which stays within reach of the trained adult. See [Medication storage](/dj-lab/safety/medication-storage.html).
- **Never face bad weather alone.** Staff decide when to move indoors. In lightning, the group stays inside until 30 minutes after the last thunder.
- **Never in the water.** Children do not swim during the program.
- **Never handle a conflict alone.** Staff step in. See [Peer harm protocol](/dj-lab/safety/peer-harm-protocol.html).
- **Never set their own volume past the cap.** Headphone volume is capped on every laptop.
