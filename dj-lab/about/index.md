## What Rainbow DJ Lab is

Rainbow DJ Lab is a free DJ class for children, teens and adults. It is the DJ-class part of **Full Spectrum at Rainbow Beach**, a proposed year-round music and culture program at Rainbow Beach Park on Chicago's South Side. The live class app calls the course "Rainbow DJ Academy."

Kids work in small groups of four at one DJ controller, taking turns at four jobs. A coach shows each new skill while thinking out loud, then kids try it, fix it, and try again. There are no grades and no tests. Children rate themselves and earn Passport stamps for skills they've shown.

## Who runs it

The Lab is run by **Ras Tafari Inc.**, an Illinois not-for-profit corporation and 501(c)(3) public charity at 7700 S. Stony Island Ave., Chicago, IL 60649.

It is a member of several South Side park advisory councils, including the Rainbow Beach Park Advisory Council. No coaches or other staff have been hired yet. See [Instructors](/dj-lab/about/instructors.html) for how they will be chosen.

## Where it will meet

Rainbow Beach Park, 3111 E. 77th St., Chicago, IL 60649 (Chicago Park District, South Region, 7th Ward).

- **Fall through spring:** indoors at the park fieldhouse, when space is available.
- **Summer:** outdoors on the park lawn, as the park approves.
- **Never in the water.** Children do not swim during the program.

See [Locations](/dj-lab/enroll/locations.html) for more.

## Where things stand today

**Status: Proposed. Not running yet.**

- The Chicago Park District has not approved or permitted the program.
- The Rainbow Beach PAC vote is pending.
- No class dates are set.
- No staff are hired.

No class runs until every item on the [launch checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html) is met.
