## Stephen Henry, program lead and family contact

Stephen Henry is President of **Ras Tafari Inc.**, the Chicago not-for-profit that runs Rainbow DJ Lab. He leads the Lab and is the person to call with any question, before enrollment or after.

Ras Tafari Inc. produces SelassieFest, Chicago's roots reggae and cultural festival, and is a member of several South Side park advisory councils, including the Rainbow Beach Park Advisory Council.

**How to reach Stephen:**

- Phone: 414-909-3279
- Email: stephen@selassiefest.com
- Mail: Ras Tafari Inc., 7700 S. Stony Island Ave., Chicago, IL 60649

As program lead, Stephen is responsible for making sure every launch requirement is met before any class runs, and for making sure the policies on this site are followed. If he works directly with children, he goes through the same Chicago Park District background check and Lab training as every other adult (see [staff screening](/dj-lab/safety/staff-screening.html)), and his completion dates will be posted here the same way. Stephen is not paid for his work on the Lab; no Ras Tafari Inc. officer, director or staff member is.

## Coach profiles: coming once coaches are hired and cleared

**No coaches have been hired yet.** A coach's profile goes up only after they've cleared the Chicago Park District background check and finished every Lab training step. Coaches are DJ trainers who are not members of Ras Tafari Inc.; they train only. Each profile will list:

| Item | What you'll see |
|---|---|
| Name and photo | Who they are, so your child can recognize them |
| Role | Lead coach, mentor, or Empress Decks coach |
| DJ background | What they play and how long they've played |
| Chicago Park District background check | Date cleared (run by the Park District under its rules) |
| References | Date checked |
| Youth-protection training | Date completed |
| DCFS mandated reporter training | Date completed |
| Pediatric first aid/CPR/AED (if they hold it) | Issuing body and expiration date |
| Next background re-check due | On the Park District's schedule |

We'll post dates, not the private results or documents themselves.
