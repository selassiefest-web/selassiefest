Spring Fast Track

The six two-hour workshops that compress the Rainbow DJ Lab course into a spring fast track.

What the fast track is

The spring fast track covers the main skills of the 14-session course in 6 workshops of 2 hours each. It's designed for spring, ahead of the proposed summer season, and ends with a dress rehearsal outdoors.

Like the full course, it's free, all equipment is provided, and there are no grades or tests. No dates are set yet, and the program is not running yet. See the launch checklist.

If your child is new to DJing and you want the gentler pace, the full 14-session course gives more time on each skill.

The six workshops

# Title Covers Mission Passport skills
W1 Beatmatching and Blending Red + Orange (sessions 1–4) I can count the phrase and start my second song on the one. Load, play and fade two songs · Count 8 bars while mixing · Start song B on the one
W2 Building a Set Yellow + Green (sessions 5–8) I can match two songs' speed and blend them without mud. Match tempo with the lights · Swap the bass on a phrase · Build a crate by color
W3 Live Looping and Editing Blue (sessions 9–10) I can grab any moment and make songs talk to each other. Set hot cues · Loop one word · Flip a phrase
W4 Playing with Live Instruments Indigo, part (session 12) I can leave space for a live musician and say one line on the mic. Leave space for a musician · Mic basics · Deliver one MC line
W5 The Theme Clash Indigo, part (session 11) I can build one Theme Clash with an MC line. Run the fit check · Play one Theme Clash · Add an MC line
W6 Dress Rehearsal on the Beach Violet (sessions 13–14) I play a planned 10-minute set outdoors on the real system. Follow my Set Map · Use my Plan B · Play 10 minutes outdoors

W6 is a listen-back day: each child records 2 minutes and reviews it with a mentor.

"On the Beach" means outdoors at the park, on the lawn. Children do not swim and the program never takes place in the water. Outdoor sessions follow our lakefront weather rules and sunscreen and hydration practices.

The shape of a 2-hour workshop

A workshop uses the same pattern as a 90-minute session, with an extra movement break and an extra round of deck time.

Block Minutes
Drop In 0–7
Mission Card 7–12
Watch Me Think 12–20
Deck Time 1 20–35
Move Break 35–40
Deck Time 2 40–55
Wordplay Lab 55–70
Move Break 2 70–75
Deck Time 3 75–90
Spotlight 90–110
Check Out 110–120

Stations, rotating jobs, Mild/Medium/Spicy levels, "Data!", volunteer-only Spotlight, and Passport stamps all work the same way as in the full course.

Things to know as a parent

  • Two hours is a long block for some 8- to 11-year-olds. The two movement breaks are built in for exactly that reason. On hot days, there are water breaks every block.
  • Because each workshop covers two or more sessions' worth of material, Mild is always available, and switching levels is always allowed.
  • Graduates of the fast track follow the same pathway after graduation.
