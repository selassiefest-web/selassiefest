## Status: roster is empty

**No one is listed yet, because no one is hired yet.** The Rainbow DJ Lab is proposed. This page explains the requirement and what will be posted. The roster stays empty until each adult has passed screening and their certification has been checked.

Having pediatric first aid/CPR/AED certification for at least one adult per class is item 6 on the [launch checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html). No class runs until every item is checked.

These are policies Ras Tafari Inc. commits to; formal board adoption is a launch requirement.

## The requirement

- **At least one adult in every class** holds current **pediatric first aid/CPR/AED** certification from the **American Red Cross** or the **American Heart Association**.
- "Pediatric" matters: the course must cover children and infants, not only adults.
- If the certified adult is absent, that class doesn't run unless another certified adult is present.
- That adult is also the "trained adult" who carries rescue medicine for children with plans. See [Epinephrine Protocol](/dj-lab/safety/epinephrine-protocol.html).
- Every adult on the roster has also completed the Lab's screening. See [Staff Screening](/dj-lab/safety/staff-screening.html) and [Mandated Reporter Training](/dj-lab/safety/mandated-reporter-training.html).

## How a certification is checked

1. The adult gives the Lab a copy of their certificate (or digital certificate).
2. Stephen Henry checks it against the issuing body's online certificate lookup, where available, and confirms the course type and expiry date.
3. A copy is kept on file.
4. The roster is updated. Sixty days before a certification expires, the adult is reminded to renew. **An expired certification doesn't count.**
