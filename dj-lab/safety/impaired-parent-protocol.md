## Status

This is a policy Ras Tafari Inc. commits to; formal board adoption is a launch requirement.

## The rule

**We do not release a child to an adult who appears impaired.** Staff call another authorized adult. If the child is in danger, staff call 911 and make a DCFS Hotline report as required.

This applies to every adult, including a parent. Our job at that moment is to make sure your child gets home safely.

## What "appears impaired" means

Coaches are not testing anyone. They act on what they can see, hear and smell, for example:

- slurred speech, stumbling, unsteady on their feet;
- strong smell of alcohol or cannabis;
- confused, very drowsy, or unable to follow a simple conversation;
- visibly intending to drive while showing these signs;
- behavior that is aggressive or out of control.

A medical condition can look similar. Coaches stay respectful and do not accuse. The question is simply: "Can this adult take this child home safely right now?"

## Step by step

| Step | Who | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Second adult | Keeps the child (and other children) inside with the group, out of the conversation. |
| 2 | Lead coach | Speaks with the adult **privately and calmly**: "We want to make sure everyone gets home safely. We're going to call someone else on the pickup list." |
| 3 | Lead coach | Offers alternatives: another adult on the authorized-pickup list, or the adult may have someone else come for them as well. |
| 4 | Lead coach | Calls other adults on the authorized-pickup list and emergency contacts until one is reached and on the way. |
| 5 | Lead coach | Releases the child only to that authorized adult, with photo ID checked and logged in the [sign-out tool](/dj-lab/portal/signout.html). |
| 6 | Lead coach | If the impaired adult insists, takes the child, or tries to drive away with the child: **does not physically struggle**. Calls **911** at once with the vehicle description, plate if seen, and direction of travel. |
| 7 | Lead coach | If a child is in danger, calls **911**; and makes a **DCFS Hotline** report, **1-800-25-ABUSE (1-800-252-2873)**, as required of a mandated reporter (see [Mandated reporter training](/dj-lab/safety/mandated-reporter-training.html)). |
| 8 | Lead coach | Tells Stephen Henry (Lab lead) the same day and writes an incident report. |

While waiting, two screened adults stay with the child the whole time. Staff never drive a child home.

## Afterward

1. The enrolling parent or guardian receives a call the same day (if they were not the adult involved) and a written report within 24 hours. See [Incident reporting](/dj-lab/safety/incident-reporting.html).
2. The Lab lead follows up privately with the family to plan safe pickups going forward.
3. Your child stays welcome in the Lab. A family having a hard day is not a reason to exclude a child.

## What you'll be told

- If a pickup was refused: the reason, the steps taken, and who your child went home with.
- Whether 911 or DCFS was called. If DCFS or police tell us not to share certain details, we follow their direction.
