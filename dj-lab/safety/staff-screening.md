## Status

No staff or volunteers have been hired yet, and no one has been screened. This is a policy Ras Tafari Inc. commits to; formal board adoption is a launch requirement. "Background checks complete for every adult" is item 4 on the [launch checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html). No class runs until it is checked.

## Who is screened

Every adult who works with children at any Lab activity, paid or volunteer: coaches, mentors, the women DJs who teach Empress Decks, guest DJs who are with children, and Ras Tafari Inc. officers who attend classes, including the President.

Parents who stay with their own Little DJ (ages 5–7) are not Lab adults and are not screened, but they are never counted in the ratio, are never left in charge of other children, and follow the [Youth protection policy](/dj-lab/safety/youth-protection-policy.html) conduct rules.

## The screening steps, in order

| Step | What happens | Who does it | Before |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Application | Written application with work and volunteer history with children, and a signed disclosure of any criminal convictions, pending charges, registry status, and any past removal from youth work | Applicant | Interview |
| 2. Interview | Questions about boundaries, supervision and how they would handle scenarios on this site | Lab lead plus one other adult | References |
| 3. References | At least two references, at least one of whom has seen the person work with young people. Checked by phone, with notes kept. | Lab lead | Background check |
| 4. Sex offender registry checks | Name search of the **Illinois Sex Offender Registry** (Illinois State Police) and the **national registry at NSOPW.gov** | Lab lead | First class |
| 5. Fingerprint background check | **Fingerprint-based** criminal background check through an Illinois State Police–approved (licensed live scan) fingerprint vendor | Applicant goes to vendor; results to Ras Tafari Inc. | First class |
| 6. Any additional checks required by the Chicago Park District | Whatever the park's permit or agreement requires | As required | First class |
| 7. Youth-protection training | Training on abuse prevention, grooming, boundaries, and our [Youth protection policy](/dj-lab/safety/youth-protection-policy.html) | Applicant | First class |
| 8. Mandated reporter training | Illinois DCFS mandated reporter training; certificate on file. See [Mandated reporter training](/dj-lab/safety/mandated-reporter-training.html). | Applicant | First class |
| 9. Sign the policies | Signs the Youth protection policy, [Substance-free policy](/dj-lab/safety/substance-free-policy.html) and a promise to report any new arrest or charge within 72 hours | Applicant | First class |
| 10. Final sign-off | Lab lead confirms every step is complete and recorded; a second Ras Tafari Inc. officer double-checks the file | Lab lead + one officer | First class |

**No step can be skipped, and no one works with children "while results are pending."** A person who has not finished every step does not attend class, even as an observer helping out.

## How results are judged

We use a two-part approach.

**Automatic disqualification** (no exceptions, no appeal):

- Listed on any sex offender registry (state or national).
- Any conviction for a sex offense.
- Any conviction for an offense involving a child (abuse, neglect, endangerment, exploitation, child pornography).
- Any conviction for a violent felony (for example, murder, kidnapping, armed robbery, aggravated battery).
- A finding by any agency that the person abused or neglected a child, where we learn of it.
- Being removed from any youth program for misconduct with a child.
- Lying on the application or hiding a conviction or charge.
- Refusing any screening step.

**Individual review** (two Ras Tafari Inc. officers decide together, in writing):

- Other convictions, such as drug, alcohol-related driving, or property offenses. The reviewers consider how serious, how recent, and how related the offense is to working with children. Where the law limits how a record may be used in a decision, we follow the law.
- Pending charges. The person does not work with children until the charge is resolved and reviewed.

When in doubt, the answer is no.

## Renewal

| Item | Renewed |
|---|---|
| Fingerprint background check | Every 2 years |
| Illinois and national registry checks | Every 2 years, **and** a fresh registry search before each season |
| Youth-protection training | Every 2 years |
| Mandated reporter training | Every 2 years (see [Mandated reporter training](/dj-lab/safety/mandated-reporter-training.html)) |
| Pediatric first aid/CPR/AED (where held) | Before the certificate's expiration date |
| Duty to report a new arrest or charge | Continuous: within 72 hours |

An adult whose renewal lapses stops working with children on the lapse date until it is renewed.

## How records are kept

- Background check results are confidential, kept in a locked or access-controlled file, seen only by the Lab lead and the reviewing officer, and used only for the screening decision.
- Results are never posted, emailed to families, or stored in the class app.

## What parents can ask to see

You may ask, for each adult who works with your child:

| You can see | You cannot see |
|---|---|
| The date the fingerprint background check was completed | The background check report itself |
| The date of the most recent registry checks | Any details of a person's record |
| The date youth-protection training was completed | Interview notes or reference comments |
| The date mandated reporter training was completed | Home address, birth date or other personal data |
| Whether pediatric first aid/CPR/AED is current, and its expiration date | |

To ask, call or email Stephen Henry: 414-909-3279, stephen@selassiefest.com. We will answer within 3 business days.

## What you'll be told

- Before your child's first class: confirmation that every adult on the team has completed every screening step.
- If a new adult joins mid-season: confirmation that they completed every step before their first class.
- If an adult is removed for a screening reason: that the adult is no longer with the Lab (not the reason, where privacy law or an investigation prevents it).
