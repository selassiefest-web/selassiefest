title: Cool-Down Space
description: The Rainbow DJ Lab's calm corner, which is always in view of an adult, never isolated, never locked, with time limits and a clear way back.

## Status

These are policies Ras Tafari Inc. commits to; formal board adoption is a launch requirement. The Lab is **proposed**. Where the space goes in the fieldhouse and on the lawn will be set after the site walk with park staff.

## What it is

A calm corner in the same room (or the same lawn area) as the class. It might have a chair or cushion, a fidget, a water bottle spot and a small "how I feel" card. It is **out of the speaker zone,** so it's quieter ([Hearing Safety](/dj-lab/safety/hearing-safety.html)).

It is a place to **reset, not a punishment.** Any child can ask to use it, any time, without being in trouble.

## The rules

1. **Always in view of an adult.** An adult can see the child the whole time, and the child can see the adults.
2. **Never isolation.** It is never another room, a hallway, a closet, a bathroom or outside the program area.
3. **Never locked or blocked.** Nothing closes the child in, and no one stands in the way.
4. **Never alone with one adult out of sight.** If an adult sits with the child, it's in the open, where the other adult can see ("observable and interruptible").
5. **Water and the restroom are still allowed.**
6. **The child doesn't lose their place.** Their station spot and stamps are kept.

## How it works

| Step | Who | Action | Timing |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Child or adult | Child asks for a break, or an adult offers or directs one (step 3 of the [Discipline Ladder](/dj-lab/safety/discipline-ladder.html)) | Any time |
| 2 | Adult | Says calmly where to go and that they can come back when ready. Notes the time | At start |
| 3 | Adult | Quick check-in from nearby: "Ready, or want a couple more minutes?" | About 3 minutes |
| 4 | Child | Returns when ready | Usually within 5 minutes |
| 5 | Adult | If not ready, sits nearby (in view) and talks it through, or offers a different job when they return | By 10 minutes |
| 6 | Lead adult | If the child still can't return, calls the parent | After about 15 minutes, or sooner if the child is very upset |

These are guides. A child who wants to come back after one minute can come back. A Little DJ's own grown-up stays with them.

## Coming back

- The child rejoins at their station in the next job rotation, with no lecture and no announcement.
- If it was a step-3 break, the adult has a 30-second private chat: what happened, and one thing to try.
- The next block is a fresh start.

## What you'll be told

- At pickup: if your child used the cool-down space because an adult directed them to, and why.
- If they asked for it themselves: we'll mention it if it seems useful, without treating it as trouble.
- A phone call: if your child couldn't return after about 15 minutes, or was very distressed.
