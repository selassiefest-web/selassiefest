# Security and Privacy Reporting

If you find a security weakness or a privacy problem in the Rainbow DJ Lab site, live class app or portal, please tell us.

## How to report

Email **stephen@selassiefest.com** with:

- what you found and where (page, URL or file);
- how to reproduce it;
- what you think the impact is.

Please do **not** report security issues in public GitHub issues.

## Do not include children's data

Do not send us any child's or family's personal information, even if you were able to see it. Describe the kind of data you could reach (for example, "guardian phone numbers") instead of copying it. Do not download, keep or share any data beyond what is needed to show the problem exists.

## What to expect

- We will acknowledge your report **within 2 business days.**
- We will tell you what we are doing about it and when it is fixed.
- If children's or families' personal information was exposed, we will notify affected families without unreasonable delay, as the Illinois Personal Information Protection Act (815 ILCS 530) requires.

## Safe harbor

We will not pursue legal action or report you to law enforcement for good-faith security research that:

- stays within what is needed to find and demonstrate the issue;
- does not access, change or delete other people's data beyond the minimum needed;
- does not disrupt the service or a live class;
- gives us a reasonable time to fix the issue before you disclose it publicly.

## Not a security issue?

For a child-safety concern, see [Report a concern](https://selassiefest.com/dj-lab/safety/report-a-concern.html). If a child is in immediate danger, call **911**. To report suspected abuse or neglect, call the **DCFS Hotline at 1-800-25-ABUSE (1-800-252-2873)**.
