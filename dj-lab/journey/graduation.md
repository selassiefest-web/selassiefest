## Session 14 · Violet · Rainbow Showcase

**Mission:** "I play my set for a real crowd."

The last session of the full course is the Rainbow Showcase. **Families attend.** Every child gets a slot to play their set, and every performer gets glow cards.

*The Lab is proposed and not running yet. No showcase date is set.*

## Getting there: sessions 9 to 13

| Session | Color | Title | In one line |
|---|---|---|---|
| 9 | Blue | Hot Cues and Loops | Switch to Serato DJ Lite. Mark moments on pads and loop a single word. |
| 10 | Blue | Word Flip | Make songs talk with call and response. Build a sentence from lyrics. *(Listen-back day.)* |
| 11 | Indigo | Theme Clash Lab | Drop a cartoon or game theme into a hit: Tease, Swap or Stack. |
| 12 | Indigo | Mic, MC and Live Lab | Say one MC line on the beat, read the crowd, and play with a young live musician. |
| 13 | Violet | Build Your Set | Fill in a Set Map (Opener, Build, Theme Clash, Peak, Exit) and a Plan B card. *(Listen-back day.)* |

In session 13, your child plans what they'll do if a song won't load, the laptop freezes, or they blank. The coach models it: *"If my laptop freezes, Plan B: I talk on the mic and load my backup song."*

## What happens at the showcase

- **Crowd warm-up:** the whole room, families included, learns one dance step together.
- **Every child gets a slot to play.** Your child chooses:
  - **Mild:** 5 minutes with a partner.
  - **Medium:** their own 10-minute set.
  - **Spicy:** their set with a Theme Clash and an MC line.
- **Glow cards:** each child writes one glow for every performer.
- **Passport:** the last 3 stamps, toward the "full spectrum" of 42.

Before your child starts, the coach's words are: *"Deep breath. Find the 1. You've already done this."*

## If your child doesn't want to perform

That's okay. Playing with a partner is always an option, and no child is ever forced on stage. Talk to the coach before the showcase, and see [Performing: opt out](/dj-lab/performing/opt-out.html). Performance photos and recordings follow the consents you gave. See [Performance consent](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/performance-consent.html) and [Photos and recordings](/dj-lab/privacy/photos-recordings.html).

## Safety at the showcase

The showcase is a busier day, with more adults in the room. The same rules apply:

- You sign your child in. They're released only to an adult on the authorized-pickup list, after a photo ID check.
- Restroom buddy system, with an adult at the entrance.
- Children stay out of the zone in front of the speakers, and the main speaker is kept at a level checked with a sound meter. Earplugs are free.
- Clean lyrics only.
- No alcohol, tobacco, vaping or cannabis, for anyone.

## Your Passport passes

| Passport | What it means |
|---|---|
| 42 stamps | The full spectrum: 3 skills × 14 sessions |
| 7 color badges | Six stamps complete each color |

There are no grades. Stamps show skills a mentor saw your child do.

## What to ask at dinner

**This week's home question:** *"You played for a crowd. What was the best moment?"*

Also try:
- "What glow did you get?"
- "What glow did you give?"
- "Did you need your Plan B?"
