## Session 1 · Red · Meet the Machine

**Mission:** "I can play a song and bring in a second one."

Week 1 is about getting comfortable. Your child touches a real DJ controller, plays two songs, fades from one to the other, and leaves with a DJ name.

*The Lab is proposed and not running yet. "Week 1" assumes one session a week. See the [schedule](/dj-lab/enroll/schedule.html).*

## Arrival (for you)

1. **Sign your child in** with a coach. You'll do this every week.
2. **Check that your pickup list is right.** Your child goes home only with an adult on it, after a photo ID check.
3. **Stay and watch if you'd like.** See the [Open door policy](/dj-lab/families/open-door-policy.html).

## What your child does, block by block

| Block | Minutes | In session 1 |
|---|---|---|
| Drop In | 0–7 | Music from the red crate is already playing. Kids clap on beat 1, stomp on beat 4, and get their Passport. |
| Mission Card | 7–12 | Kids hear the Mission and guess: easy, medium or hard? |
| Watch Me Think | 12–20 | A controller tour with color stickers: red is volume and faders, blue is tempo, green is EQ, yellow is pads. Load, play, cue. **Everyone sets their headphone volume caps together.** |
| Deck Time 1 | 20–35 | Everyone tries Mild: load two songs and fade from one to the other. Jobs rotate every 3:45. |
| Move Break | 35–40 | Freeze dance: freeze every time Coach stops the music. |
| Deck Time 2 | 40–55 | Kids pick a level. **Mild:** fade between two songs. **Medium:** use the crossfader instead of the channel faders. **Spicy:** bring song 2 in exactly on the chorus of song 1. |
| Wordplay Lab | 55–70 | DJ Name Builder: your name + a sound + a color. Try three and pick a favorite. |
| Spotlight | 70–82 | Volunteers play 60 seconds on the big speaker. Classmates give a glow and a grow. |
| Check Out | 82–90 | Traffic-light self-rating, a "Next time I'll…" line, Passport stamps, gear away. |

During Watch Me Think, the coach says things like: *"I'm watching song 1 run out. I want song 2 ready before that, so I press cue now."*

## Passport skills this week

- Load and play two songs
- Fade between them
- Find a song in my crate fast

## What a 9-year-old might feel

- **Excited, and a bit overwhelmed.** It's a lot of buttons. The color stickers and the "one Mission" focus help.
- **Shy about Spotlight.** That's fine. It's volunteers only, and watching first is completely okay.
- **Frustrated if a mix crashes.** That's when they'll first hear the room shout "Data!" It's meant to make mistakes feel normal, even funny.

## What to ask at dinner

**This week's home question:** *"What's your DJ name, and how did you make it?"*

Also try:
- "What job did you have first: DJ, Co-pilot, Hype or Logger?"
- "Did you go mild, medium or spicy?"
- "What's your 'Next time I'll…' sentence?"
