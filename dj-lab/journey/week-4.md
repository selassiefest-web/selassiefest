## Session 4 · Orange · Mix on the One

**Mission:** "My second song starts at the right moment."

By week 4, your child knows the controller, has built a clean crate (session 2), and can count beats, bars and phrases (session 3). Now they put it together: wait for beat 1 of a new phrase, then bring the next song in.

*The Lab is proposed and not running yet. "Week 4" assumes one session a week.*

## What came before

| Session | Title | What they learned |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Meet the Machine | Play two songs and fade between them |
| 2 | Build Your Crate | Build a crate of clean radio edits, read BPM on screen, find a song in under 20 seconds |
| 3 | Beats, Bars and Phrases | 4 beats make a bar, 8 bars make a phrase. Read the waveform: intro, verse, hook, drop, outro. |

## What your child does this week

- **Watch Me Think:** the coach starts song B on beat 1 of a new phrase, does a quick cut with the crossfader, then a drop swap. Listen for: *"Not yet… not yet… new phrase, beat 1, go."*
- **Deck Time 2 levels:**
  - **Mild:** start song B on beat 1.
  - **Medium:** cut with the crossfader on a phrase change.
  - **Spicy:** do a drop swap, cutting from one drop straight into another.
- **Move Break:** a count-the-phrase walk. Kids change direction every 8 bars.
- **Wordplay Lab:** Answer Songs. Kids pick a song whose title answers the last one, so the crowd hears a conversation.

## Passport skills this week

- Count phrases out loud
- Start song B on the one
- Cut on a phrase change

Your child has a chance at **12 stamps** by the end of this session (3 per session). Six stamps complete a color badge, so this is when many kids earn their **orange badge**, after red in session 2.

## What a 9-year-old might feel

- **More confident.** The routine is familiar now: same blocks, same station jobs.
- **Impatient.** Waiting for the phrase change is hard. "Not yet… not yet…" is the whole lesson.
- **Proud of a level jump.** Some kids try Medium or Spicy for the first time around now. Some stay at Mild, and that's fine. Kids choose, and they can switch any time.

## What to ask at dinner

**This week's home question:** *"Which two songs did you make talk to each other?"*

Also try:
- "Count a phrase for me. Where's the one?"
- "Did you guess the Mission difficulty right?"
- "What was the best pun in the room today?"
