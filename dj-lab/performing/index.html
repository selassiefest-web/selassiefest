Performing at the Rainbow DJ Lab

How public performance works for Rainbow DJ Lab kids. It is always opt-in, always supervised, and always the child's choice.

The short version

Performing is never required. A child can come to every class, earn every Passport stamp and finish the course without ever playing in front of anyone outside the class.

When a child does want to perform, these rules apply every time:

  • It is opt-in. A parent or guardian must sign the performance consent form first. Without that form on file, your child does not perform. That holds even if your child asks to.
  • The child chooses too. Your consent opens the door, and your child still decides on the day. They can say no at any point, even on the stage steps. See Opting out.
  • Spotlight is volunteers only. In class, coaches never call on a child who didn't sign up, and the class app won't put a child in the Spotlight without performance consent on file.
  • A Lab adult is always with them. At any event, a Lab adult you've been told about is assigned to your child. Your child is never alone with one adult out of sight. See Event-day supervision.
  • Ears are protected. Headphones are capped, earplugs are free, and children stay out of the zone in front of the speakers. See Sound and crowd exposure.

What is real today and what is proposed

We want you to know exactly where things stand.

Item Status
Rainbow DJ Lab classes Proposed. The Chicago Park District hasn't approved or permitted them yet, the Rainbow Beach Park Advisory Council vote is pending, and no class dates are set.
Spotlight (in class) Part of the written curriculum. It runs in every session once classes begin.
Rainbow Showcase (session 14, families attend) Part of the written curriculum. It is the last session of the full course.
Rainbow Wednesdays (free summer DJ series) Proposed. Not yet approved.
Open Decks slot for graduates Guaranteed when Rainbow Wednesdays runs. See Graduation guarantee.
Youth Showcase open call, Theme Clash semifinals and Finale Proposed stage shows. Not yet approved.
Music licensing (public performance rights for Spotlight, the Showcase and any public performance) Not yet in place. It's a launch requirement: the Lab needs public performance rights (through ASCAP, BMI, SESAC and GMR, or confirmation that Chicago Park District licenses cover it) before kids play for an audience.

The safety rules on these pages are policies Ras Tafari Inc. commits to. Formal board adoption is a launch requirement, and no class runs until every launch requirement is met.
