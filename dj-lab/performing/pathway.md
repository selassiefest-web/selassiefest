## How the pathway works

The pathway is a ladder, not a requirement. Each step is a little bigger than the one before. A child can stay on any step for as long as they like, skip a step, or step off completely. Going further is never needed to finish the course or to earn Passport stamps.

Before your child takes **any** step beyond in-class Spotlight, a parent or guardian must have signed the [performance consent form](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/performance-consent.html). Your child then says yes again, in their own words, before each performance.

## The steps

| Step | Where | Who watches | How long | Status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Spotlight | In class, every session (minutes 70–82) | Classmates and coaches | 60 seconds on the main speaker | Part of the written curriculum |
| 2. Rainbow Showcase | Session 14, the last class | Classmates, coaches and families | 5 minutes with a partner, or a 10-minute set | Part of the written curriculum |
| 3. Open Decks | Rainbow Wednesdays, the free summer DJ series | The public at the park | Set length confirmed before the event | Guaranteed slot **when Rainbow Wednesdays runs**. The series itself is proposed. |
| 4. Youth Showcase open call | A proposed stage show | The public | Confirmed before the event | **Proposed.** Not yet approved. |
| 5. Theme Clash semifinals and Finale | Proposed stage shows | The public | Confirmed before the event | **Proposed.** Not yet approved. |

## Step 1: Spotlight (in class)

- Spotlight happens near the end of each 90-minute session.
- **Volunteers only.** A child raises their hand to play 60 seconds on the main speaker. Coaches never call on a child who didn't sign up.
- Classmates give each performer one **glow** (something that worked) and one **grow** (one idea).
- Children who don't play still take part by listening and giving glows.

## Step 2: Rainbow Showcase (session 14)

- Families are invited to watch the last class.
- Children choose their own challenge level:
  - **Mild:** play 5 minutes with a partner.
  - **Medium:** play your own 10-minute set.
  - **Spicy:** play your set with a Theme Clash and an MC line.
- A child who doesn't want to play can still be part of the day. They can be the Hype or Logger for a friend, or write glow cards for the performers. Performing isn't one of the published graduation criteria, so not playing doesn't affect graduation.
- Every performer gets glow cards.

## Step 3: Open Decks at Rainbow Wednesdays

- Graduating from the course earns a **guaranteed Open Decks slot** at Rainbow Wednesdays. The guarantee applies when Rainbow Wednesdays runs. That series is still **proposed**. See [Graduation guarantee](/dj-lab/performing/graduation-guarantee.html).
- Using the slot is optional. A family can decline it, or save it for later in the season if one is offered.
- All [event-day supervision](/dj-lab/performing/event-day-supervision.html) rules apply.

## Step 4: Youth Showcase open call

- An open call is an invitation to apply. It is not an assignment.
- The Youth Showcase is a **proposed** stage show and is not yet approved. We will not share dates, a venue or a lineup until they are confirmed.

## Step 5: Theme Clash semifinals and Finale

- The Theme Clash is the creative heart of the course: a DJ drops a cartoon or game theme into a hit song. Kids practice it in session 11 (Indigo: Theme Clash Lab).
- The semifinals and Finale are **proposed** stage shows and are not yet approved.
- "Clash" here means a friendly music contest. No child is ever put down, booed or ranked in front of the crowd by Lab staff.

## SelassieFest: audience vote only

Ras Tafari Inc. also produces **SelassieFest**, Chicago's roots reggae and cultural festival. SelassieFest makes its own decisions about its lineup. A Lab child can reach the SelassieFest stage **only by event audience vote**, under the [conflict of interest policy](/dj-lab/governance/conflict-of-interest.html):

- **Optional.** A child is on the ballot only with a parent or guardian's written consent, given **before the vote is announced**.
- **Counted by someone with no interest in the result,** such as Chicago Park District staff or an outside party named in advance. Lab trainers, Ras Tafari Inc. staff and Q-Ality Sound have no role in counting and no influence over the outcome.
- **One vote per person,** with the voting method announced before voting opens.
- **Ballots show first name and last initial only.** No photos, ages, schools or contact details.
- **SelassieFest gets almost nothing from the Lab:** only a winner's first name and last initial, the performance details and the guardian's consent. Never the roster, contact details or attendance. See the [data-sharing agreement](/dj-lab/governance/data-sharing-agreement-selassiefest.html).

## Rules that apply at every step

- **Consent first:** a signed [performance consent form](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/performance-consent.html) for anything beyond class Spotlight.
- **The child's yes, every time:** asked privately, so no one says yes because of peer pressure.
- **No one picks favorites:** trainers train only. They never select, recommend or rank children for any performance or opportunity.
- **Stop any time:** see [Opting out](/dj-lab/performing/opt-out.html).
- **A named Lab adult nearby:** see [Event-day supervision](/dj-lab/performing/event-day-supervision.html).
- **Clean lyrics only:** radio edits, checked when crates are built.
- **A safety plan for hard moments:** see [Stage freeze protocol](/dj-lab/performing/stage-freeze-protocol.html).
- **Photos and video are separate:** they are covered by the [media consent form](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/media-consent.html). Saying yes to performing is not saying yes to being filmed or photographed by the Lab.
