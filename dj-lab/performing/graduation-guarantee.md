## The guarantee

Every child who graduates from the Rainbow DJ Lab is **guaranteed an Open Decks slot at Rainbow Wednesdays**, the free summer DJ series.

## The condition

**Rainbow Wednesdays is proposed.** It isn't approved yet. The guarantee applies **when Rainbow Wednesdays runs**. If the series doesn't run, there is no slot to give, and we'll tell families plainly.

The Rainbow DJ Lab itself is also proposed. The Chicago Park District hasn't approved or permitted it yet, the Rainbow Beach Park Advisory Council vote is pending, and no class dates are set.

## What the guarantee means

- **A guaranteed spot.** A graduate doesn't have to compete or audition for an Open Decks slot.
- **Still optional.** The slot is an offer, not an obligation. Your child (and you) can decline it with no penalty. See [Opting out](/dj-lab/performing/opt-out.html).
- **Consent first.** A signed [performance consent form](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/performance-consent.html) is needed before your child plays.
- **The same safety rules.** [Event-day supervision](/dj-lab/performing/event-day-supervision.html), [sound protection](/dj-lab/performing/sound-crowd-exposure.html), the [stage freeze protocol](/dj-lab/performing/stage-freeze-protocol.html), and [family transportation](/dj-lab/performing/transportation.html) all apply.

## What it does not mean

- It doesn't guarantee a date, time, set length or stage. Those are confirmed only once Rainbow Wednesdays is scheduled.
- It doesn't guarantee a place in later proposed shows. The Youth Showcase is an open call, and the Theme Clash semifinals and Finale are proposed stage shows.

## Who graduates

Graduation comes at the end of the course. The full course has 14 sessions and ends with session 14, the Rainbow Showcase. The exact completion rules for the full course and the 6-workshop spring fast track will be confirmed before the first class. Here's what we can say now:

- There are no grades and no tests.
- Performing at the Rainbow Showcase isn't required to graduate.
