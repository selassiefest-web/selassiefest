## The promise

Any child, or any parent or guardian, can say no to performing at any time:

- **No questions.** You don't have to give a reason.
- **No penalty.** Opting out never affects Passport stamps, badges, graduation, class participation or how coaches treat your child.
- **No pressure.** Staff will not try to talk a child back into performing. Other children won't be allowed to tease or pressure them either.

## When you can opt out

At any of these moments, and any moment in between:

- Before you sign the [performance consent form](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/performance-consent.html), simply by not signing it.
- After signing, by withdrawing consent.
- The day before an event.
- At check-in on event day.
- Backstage, a minute before going on.
- On stage, in the middle of a set. See the [Stage freeze protocol](/dj-lab/performing/stage-freeze-protocol.html) for how the Lab adult helps a child step off smoothly.

## How a child opts out

We teach children that any of these work, and that they all count the same:

- Telling any Lab adult "I don't want to play."
- Shaking their head or giving a thumbs-down to the Lab adult beside them.
- Telling their parent or guardian, who tells us.
- Simply not raising their hand for Spotlight.

No child has to explain why.

## How a parent or guardian opts out

- **For one event:** tell the Lab adult at check-in, or contact Stephen Henry before the event.
- **For all performances:** contact Stephen Henry at 414-909-3279 or stephen@selassiefest.com and say you are withdrawing performance consent. It takes effect as soon as we receive it. We record the withdrawal with your child's consents, which only coaches can see.
- You can give consent again later if you change your mind.

## What happens to a slot you give up

- An **Open Decks** slot at Rainbow Wednesdays (when that series runs) is your child's to use or decline. Declining it does not cancel graduation.
- Nothing your child gives up is announced to the crowd or the class as a "no-show." The MC simply moves to the next act.

## What still stays the same

A child who never performs still:

- takes part in every class block, including giving glows during Spotlight
- earns Passport stamps and color badges
- graduates from the course
