      Messages from the coach
        How the Lab works

        The shape of every session

        • Nine short blocks with a movement break in the middle. Short and predictable is by design: it frees attention for the music.
        • Groups of four share a controller. Jobs (DJ, Co-pilot, Hype, Logger) rotate every 3 minutes 45 seconds, so everyone gets a turn on the decks.
        • Mistakes are called “Data!” When a mix crashes, the DJ names one reason and tries again.
        • No grades and no paper tests. Kids rate themselves and show skills to a mentor for Passport stamps.

        Safety

        • Headphone volume is capped on every laptop. Earplugs are available.
        • Clean lyrics only: radio edits, checked when crates are built.
        • Every adult mentor is background-checked. Two adults are always in the room.

        The gear

        • Hercules DJControl Inpulse 300 MK2 controllers. Lights under the jog wheels show kids which way to adjust, so they learn by eye before ear.

        Where it leads

        • Graduation gives a guaranteed Open Decks slot at Rainbow Wednesdays, then the Youth Showcase open call, then the Theme Clash semifinals and Finale.
