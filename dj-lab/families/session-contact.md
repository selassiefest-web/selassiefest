## If it's an emergency

**Call 911 first.** Then call the Lab.

## How to reach us during class

| | |
|---|---|
| **Lab phone** | Stephen Henry, 414-909-3279 (call or text) |
| **Email** | stephen@selassiefest.com (not for anything urgent during class) |
| **In person** | Come to the check-in table. You're welcome any time under our [open-door policy](/dj-lab/families/open-door-policy.html) |

Once staff are hired, each class will also have a named lead on site. We'll share that person's contact details with registered families before the first class. We won't name anyone until they're hired and screened.

**Calling during class.** Adults are actively supervising, so the phone may not be answered on the first ring. If you don't get an answer, **text** and say if it's urgent. One adult always stays with the group while another answers.

**Changing who picks up.** Please tell us in writing (text or email) **before** pickup, and make sure that adult is on your authorized-pickup list. We can't add a new adult by phone at the door.

## How we reach you

We use the phone number and email you give at registration. Keep them up to date.

| Situation | How we reach you | When |
|---|---|---|
| Your child is hurt, sick, or something has happened | Phone call | Right away, and the same day at the latest. A written incident report follows within 24 hours |
| Your child can't be found | Phone call | At once, while staff search (Code Adam–style plan) |
| Behavior: parent-call step, or any safety violation | Phone call | During class |
| Epinephrine used | 911, then a phone call to you | Immediately |
| Weather cancellation | Phone, text or email | As soon as the decision is made. See [weather and cancellation](/dj-lab/enroll/weather-cancellation.html) |
| Session recap | Email, if you opted in | After class |

If we can't reach you, we call your emergency contacts.

## Response times

These are our standards once classes are running:

- **During a session, urgent:** we answer or call back as soon as an adult can safely step away. Two adults stay with the children at all times, so the call never leaves kids unsupervised.
- **Non-urgent questions:** a reply within 2 business days.
- **Data requests** (to see, correct or delete your information): within 10 business days. See [your data rights](/dj-lab/privacy/parent-data-rights.html).
- **Safety concerns:** see [report a concern](/dj-lab/safety/report-a-concern.html). Concerns about possible abuse are reported straight to the DCFS Hotline, 1-800-25-ABUSE (1-800-252-2873), and staff don't need anyone's permission to make that call.
