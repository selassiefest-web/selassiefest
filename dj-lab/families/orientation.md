## Before day one

Before the first class of each season, we'll hold a **family orientation**. It's a chance for you and your child to see the space, meet the adults, put your hands on a controller and ask anything, before you ever drop your child off.

> **Date: to be announced.** No class dates are set yet, and the Lab is still proposed: the Chicago Park District has not approved or permitted it. Orientation will be scheduled once the [launch requirements](/dj-lab/governance/index.html) are met. Registered families will be told the date by email.

## Who should come

- Your child, and at least one parent or guardian.
- Anyone on your authorized-pickup list is welcome too, so staff can meet them.
- **Little DJs (5–7):** the grown-up who will stay through class should come, since you'll be learning alongside your child.
- Brothers and sisters are welcome.

## What we'll cover

### 1. Meet the adults

You'll meet the adults who will work with your child. Every one of them must finish screening before their first class: the Chicago Park District's background check (run by the Park District under its rules), references, youth-protection training, and Illinois DCFS mandated reporter training. You'll also hear who to call about any adult: the Chicago Park District, at the Rainbow Beach Park office, (312) 745-1479. No staff are hired yet, so we can't name them here.

### 2. Try the gear

- Kids (and grown-ups) try a **Hercules DJControl Inpulse 300 MK2** controller. The lights under the jog wheels show when two songs are lined up.
- Try the headphones and hear what the capped volume sounds like. Pick up free earplugs if your child wants them.
- See how a station works: 4 kids, one controller, rotating jobs (DJ, Co-pilot, Hype and Logger).

### 3. Tour the space

- Where check-in and sign-out happen.
- The restrooms, and how the buddy system works.
- The cool-down space.
- Exits and where the group goes in bad weather.
- Where medicine is kept.

The emergency action plan and the fieldhouse lockdown plan will be finalized after a site walk with park staff. That's a launch requirement.

### 4. Walk through the paperwork

- Check that your forms are complete: authorized-pickup list, emergency contacts, medical and allergy information, medication authorization and FARE plan if needed, and consents.
- Bring photo ID so staff can see it for the first time.
- See the class app at [/dj-lab/live/](/dj-lab/live/): what your private family link shows (only your own child) and what the in-room station board shows. See [data privacy](/dj-lab/privacy/data-privacy-statement.html).
- Forms: [enrollment forms](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/).

### 5. Questions and answers

Ask anything. Nothing is too small. Common questions so far are in the [enrollment FAQ](/dj-lab/enroll/faq.html).

## Can't make it?

Orientation is strongly encouraged but not required. If you can't come, contact Stephen Henry (414-909-3279, stephen@selassiefest.com) and we'll arrange a short walk-through before or after a class, or answer your questions by phone. You're also welcome to watch any session under our [open-door policy](/dj-lab/families/open-door-policy.html).
