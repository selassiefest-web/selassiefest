# Insurance Certificate

**Status: Not yet in place.** No insurance policy for the Lab has been bound. Coverage is required before the first class, and the certificate will be posted on this page.

## Required coverage

Before any class runs, Ras Tafari Inc. must have:

1. **General liability insurance** of **$1,000,000 per occurrence**, naming the **Chicago Park District as additional insured.**
2. **Sexual abuse and molestation coverage.**

### What these mean, in plain words

- **General liability** covers claims that someone was hurt, or property was damaged, because of the program.
- **Additional insured** means the Chicago Park District is also protected under the policy for claims arising from the Lab's activities on park property.
- **Sexual abuse and molestation coverage** is a separate type of coverage. Many general liability policies exclude abuse claims. We require this coverage on purpose, so that a child who is harmed has a path to compensation. Coverage is never a substitute for prevention. Our first job is to stop abuse from happening; see [Safety](/dj-lab/safety/index.html).

## What we will post here

When coverage is bound, we will post:

- the certificate of insurance, showing the insurer, coverage types, limits, the policy period, and the Chicago Park District as additional insured;
- confirmation that sexual abuse and molestation coverage is included;
- the renewal date.

Policy numbers may be redacted from the posted copy. Park officials can request the full certificate from Stephen Henry.

## If coverage lapses

If any required coverage expires or is cancelled, **classes stop** until it is replaced, and this page will say so.
