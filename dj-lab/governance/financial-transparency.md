# Financial Transparency

**Status: Commitment.** The one-page class budget below is a planning budget. A final budget will be published each year before the year starts.

## A one-page class budget is a launch requirement

Here is the **one-page budget for the DJ classes** (item 14 on the [launch checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html)). It is a planning budget: the two resident instructors are paid $250 a session, the gear, which is being sought through grants and donations, is still to be priced, and insurance, music-licensing and sound, stage and lighting amounts are still to be set.

**No Ras Tafari Inc. officer, director or staff member is paid for work on the Lab today.** A paid role is allowed only if directors with no stake in it approve it, at a fair rate based on comparable programs, paid through payroll and reported publicly (see the [conflict of interest policy](/dj-lab/governance/conflict-of-interest.html), section 12). They may be reimbursed for documented, pre-approved expenses. Both instructors are **paid DJ trainers who are not members of Ras Tafari Inc.**

## The Lab is free

- No tuition and no fees to enroll.
- All equipment is provided: controllers, laptops, headphones and earplugs.
- A child's place never depends on whether their family gives money.

## How the Lab is paid for

The Lab will be funded by a mix of:

- **grants** from foundations and public programs;
- **sponsors**, meaning businesses and organizations that support the program;
- **Ras Tafari Inc.'s own funds.**

No funding amounts for the Lab have been set or committed yet. We will not publish figures until they are real.

Sponsors get recognition. They do **not** get any child's or family's information, any say in who enrolls or performs, or the right to photograph or record children. See the [privacy policy](/dj-lab/privacy/index.html) and the [data-sharing agreement](/dj-lab/governance/data-sharing-agreement-selassiefest.html).

## What we will publish each year

Before each year starts, we will post a budget showing:

1. **Expected income** by source type (grants, sponsors, Ras Tafari Inc.), naming each grant maker and sponsor.
2. **Planned spending** by category, such as equipment, training, insurance, trainer pay, sound, stage and lighting, supplies, and family communications.

After each year ends, we will post:

1. **Actual income and spending** against the budget, with an explanation of any big differences.
2. **Safety spending,** including screening, training and insurance, shown on its own line so families can see we paid for it.
3. **Any money moved between the Lab and SelassieFest,** or other Ras Tafari Inc. programs, and why.

These reports will be part of the [community accountability report](/dj-lab/governance/community-accountability-report.html).

## Who oversees the money

- **Paul Kelly, Vice President and Treasurer,** oversees Lab finances.
- **Separation of duties:** the person who selects a vendor doesn't approve its invoice, and neither of them signs the payment.
- **The board reviews Lab payments every quarter.**
- **Gifts:** no one covered by the policy accepts gifts or hospitality worth more than $50 a year from a vendor or sponsor, and any larger offer is reported within 14 days.
- Spending decisions involving a possible conflict follow the [conflict of interest policy](/dj-lab/governance/conflict-of-interest.html). Q-Ality Sound's terms are approved only by directors with no tie to Q-Ality.
- Concerns about misuse of funds can be raised under the [whistleblower policy](/dj-lab/governance/whistleblower.html).

## Your right to inspect our IRS filings

Federal law gives everyone the right to see certain records of a 501(c)(3) public charity. Ras Tafari Inc. will follow these rules:

- **Annual information returns.** Ras Tafari Inc.'s annual returns filed with the IRS (the Form 990 series), with their schedules and attachments, for the three most recent years. Under IRS rules, donor names and addresses are not required to be disclosed by public charities.
- **The exemption application.** Ras Tafari Inc.'s application for tax exemption, its supporting documents, and any letter or document the IRS issued about the application, including the [determination letter](/dj-lab/governance/501c3-letter.html).

**How to ask:**

- **In person:** at 7700 S. Stony Island Ave., Chicago, IL 60649. Under the IRS regulation, copies requested in person are generally provided the same day.
- **In writing:** email stephen@selassiefest.com or mail the address above. Under the regulation, copies are mailed within 30 days of receiving the request.
- **Cost:** we will not charge for copies sent by email. For paper copies, the law allows only a reasonable fee (no more than standard per-page copying charges plus actual postage), and we may waive it.

You can also search for Ras Tafari Inc. in the IRS Tax Exempt Organization Search tool at irs.gov.
