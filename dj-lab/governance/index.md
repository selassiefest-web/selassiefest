# Governance and Launch Readiness

The Rainbow DJ Lab is a free DJ class program proposed by **Ras Tafari Inc.**, an Illinois not-for-profit corporation and 501(c)(3) public charity at 7700 S. Stony Island Ave., Chicago, IL 60649. It is the DJ-class part of **Full Spectrum at Rainbow Beach**, a proposed year-round music and culture program at Rainbow Beach Park.

**The Lab is proposed, not running.** The Chicago Park District has not approved or permitted it. No class dates are set and no staff are hired.

This section is where we show our work. Where a document exists, we say so. Where it does not exist yet, we say that too, and we say what has to happen before it does.

## Our promise

> **No class runs until every item is complete. This page will show the date each item was completed.**

When an item is finished, its Status will change to "Complete" with the date, and the Evidence link will lead to the proof (or, for private records such as background checks, a summary that confirms completion without revealing anyone's personal information).

### How an item gets marked complete

1. The proof exists in writing (a signed agreement, a certificate, meeting minutes, a confirmation that a CPD background check is complete).
2. A second Ras Tafari Inc. officer, not the person who did the work, checks the proof.
3. We update this table with the completion date and link the evidence.
4. Only when all eighteen rows say "Complete" is a first class date confirmed.

If any item later lapses (for example, an insurance policy expires or a screening comes due), classes pause until it is fixed, and this table will show that too.
