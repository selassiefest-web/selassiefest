# Data-Sharing Agreement with SelassieFest

**Status: Proposed internal policy.** It will be formally adopted with the board's policy vote, which is required before the first class.

## Why this exists

Ras Tafari Inc. runs the Rainbow DJ Lab. Ras Tafari Inc. also produces **SelassieFest**, Chicago's roots reggae and cultural festival. Because one organization runs both, families deserve a clear, written rule that information given to the Lab stays with the Lab.

SelassieFest and the Lab are not separate companies, so this is not a contract between two parties. It is an internal policy that binds Ras Tafari Inc., its officers, staff and volunteers. It is written below as the agreement text.

---

## Agreement text

**Between:** the Rainbow DJ Lab program and the SelassieFest program, both operated by Ras Tafari Inc., 7700 S. Stony Island Ave., Chicago, IL 60649.

### 1. Definitions

1. **Lab Data** means any information collected by or for the Rainbow DJ Lab about a child or family, including: names, ages, contact details, emergency contacts, medical and allergy information, authorized-pickup lists, consents, sign-in and sign-out records, class activity, Passport stamps, coach notes, recordings made on listen-back days, and registration records.
2. **SelassieFest** means the SelassieFest festival and any of its mailing lists, ticketing, vendor, sponsor, volunteer, marketing or fundraising activities.
3. **Opt-In** means a separate, specific and voluntary yes, given by a parent or guardian in writing, that is not bundled with Lab registration or any other consent.

### 2. Commitments

1. **No marketing use.** Lab Data is never used to market SelassieFest, sell SelassieFest tickets, recruit SelassieFest volunteers, or ask for donations to SelassieFest.
2. **No sharing.** Lab Data is never copied, exported, transferred or made available to SelassieFest, its staff, volunteers, vendors, sponsors or service providers.
3. **No list merging.** Lab contact lists are never combined with SelassieFest mailing lists, ticket buyer lists or any other list.
4. **Separate systems.** Lab Data is stored only in the Lab's own systems, with access limited to Lab coaches and the officers responsible for the Lab.
5. **No images.** No photo, video or audio of a Lab child is used in SelassieFest promotion. This includes listen-back recordings and the Rainbow Showcase.
6. **Lab participation is never conditioned** on any family's involvement with SelassieFest.

### 3. Cross-promotion only by separate Opt-In

1. A family may choose to hear about SelassieFest only through a separate Opt-In.
2. The Opt-In is never pre-checked, never required, and never a condition of enrollment or any Lab opportunity.
3. A family that opts in gives SelassieFest only the email address they choose to provide for that purpose, in a form the family fills out themselves. No other Lab Data goes with it.
4. A family can withdraw the Opt-In at any time by contacting Stephen Henry. Withdrawal takes effect within 5 business days.
5. Children are never asked to opt in. Only a parent or guardian can.

General announcements that anyone can see, such as a public flyer or website post about SelassieFest, are not use of Lab Data and are not covered by this agreement.

### 4. Shared staff

Some Ras Tafari Inc. officers or volunteers may work on both programs. When they do, they may use Lab Data only for Lab purposes. Every person with access to Lab Data signs an acknowledgment of this agreement before getting access.

### 5. Exceptions

Lab Data may be disclosed only:

- to emergency responders, DCFS or law enforcement when needed to protect a child or as required by law;
- to the Lab's own service providers, only to run the Lab, as listed in the [privacy policy](/dj-lab/privacy/index.html);
- with the parent or guardian's specific written permission for a specific disclosure.

None of these exceptions allow use for SelassieFest.

### 6. Breach

Any use or sharing of Lab Data in violation of this agreement:

1. is treated as a privacy incident; affected families are told without unreasonable delay;
2. is reported to the Ras Tafari Inc. board and handled under the [enforcement and self-accountability commitments](/dj-lab/governance/enforcement-self-accountability.html);
3. is counted, without names, in the [community accountability report](/dj-lab/governance/community-accountability-report.html);
4. leads to deletion of the misused data from SelassieFest systems, confirmed in writing.

### 7. Review

This agreement is reviewed each year as part of the [annual safety review](/dj-lab/governance/annual-safety-review.html). Any change that weakens it requires a board vote and 30 days' public notice on this page before it takes effect. A change can never apply to data collected under an earlier, stronger version without the family's consent.

### 8. Signatures

To be signed by the President on behalf of the Lab program and recorded in the board minutes when adopted. This page will show the adoption date.
