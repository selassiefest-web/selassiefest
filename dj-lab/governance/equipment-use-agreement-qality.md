**Status: Draft for board approval,** as part of the [Q-Ality Sound vendor and equipment agreement](/dj-lab/governance/qality-vendor-agreement.html) (section 5).

## Who owns the gear

The Lab's DJ controllers and related gear were **donated to Ras Tafari Inc.** They're a charitable asset, used only for Ras Tafari Inc. programs unless the board approves otherwise in writing. They're listed on an inventory that's reconciled at least once a year.

## How it connects

During sessions the gear plugs into Q-Ality Sound's system. That's a technical connection only: it doesn't give Q-Ality ownership, control or any right to the gear.

## Storage

Ras Tafari Inc. transports and stores the gear, between sessions and on program days. Q-Ality has no key, code or access to the storage outside program hours.

## What Q-Ality may not do with it

Q-Ality may not use the gear at other events, for other clients, for its own promotion, recording or testing, or off the Lab site.

## Damage, loss and insurance

- Q-Ality reports any damage or loss within 24 hours.
- Q-Ality's insurance covers the gear while it's on Q-Ality's system or in its possession.
- Ras Tafari Inc. insures the gear otherwise. That coverage is a [launch requirement](/dj-lab/governance/index.html) and isn't in place yet.
- If the agreement ends, any gear Q-Ality holds is returned, inventoried and signed for.

## What families should know

- Children use the same well-kept, inventoried gear every session.
- Headphone volume is capped, and the Lab's safety lead controls the main-speaker volume. See [hearing safety](/dj-lab/safety/hearing-safety.html).
