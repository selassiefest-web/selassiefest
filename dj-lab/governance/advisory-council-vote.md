# Rainbow Beach Park Advisory Council Vote

**Status: Pending.** The Rainbow Beach Park Advisory Council has not voted on the Rainbow DJ Lab. A vote to present the program is required before the first class.

## What a park advisory council is

A park advisory council (PAC) is a volunteer group of neighbors and park users who advise on activities and improvements at their park. The Rainbow Beach Park Advisory Council serves Rainbow Beach Park, 3111 E. 77th St., Chicago, IL 60649.

## Why we are asking for a vote

The Lab would bring a new program, with children and amplified music, into a shared neighborhood park. People who use and care for the park should have a voice before it starts. We will not run classes without the council's vote to present the program.

## Our interest, disclosed

Ras Tafari Inc. is a member of the Rainbow Beach Park Advisory Council, and of several other South Side park advisory councils. Because Ras Tafari Inc. is proposing the Lab, it has an interest in this vote. Its representatives will disclose that interest and follow the council's rules for members with an interest in a matter. See the [conflict of interest policy](/dj-lab/governance/conflict-of-interest.html).

## What we will present

- what the Lab is: a free DJ class program, part of Full Spectrum at Rainbow Beach;
- where and when it would use the park (fieldhouse when space is available, lawn in summer, as the park allows);
- the [safety policies](/dj-lab/safety/index.html), including screening, ratios, sign-out and sound levels;
- the [privacy policy](/dj-lab/privacy/index.html);
- this [Launch Readiness Checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html), so members can see that nothing starts until every item is complete.

## When the vote is held

We will post on this page:

- the meeting date;
- the motion as stated;
- the result;
- any conditions or requests from the council, and how we will meet them;
- a link to the council's minutes, if the council publishes them.

If the council asks for changes, we will make them or explain publicly why we cannot.

This is item 2 on the [Launch Readiness Checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html).
