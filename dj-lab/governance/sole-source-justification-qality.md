## Why one vendor, without bidding

Q-Ality Sound provides **sound, stage and lighting only**. It was chosen without competitive bidding because:

1. **Equipment:** it owns a complete sound, stage and lighting system suited to an outdoor youth program at Rainbow Beach Park.
2. **Skills:** it has experience with live sound, stage setup and outdoor production.
3. **Track record:** it has done this work for Ras Tafari Inc. and partner events (dates are added to the memo).
4. **Compatibility:** the Lab's donated DJ controllers connect to its system without adapters or new gear.
5. **Timing and cost:** bidding would delay the program and is unlikely to lower the total cost for the same scope.

## How the choice is kept honest

- **A written memo** records whether Q-Ality is paid, donating, or both; the amount and funding source; how the price was checked (published rates, comparison quotes or prior invoices); and any relationship between Q-Ality and anyone covered by the [conflict of interest policy](/dj-lab/governance/conflict-of-interest.html). "None" is a valid answer and must be written.
- **Only directors with no tie to Q-Ality vote** on it. Recusals are recorded in the minutes.
- **No exclusivity.** Approval doesn't make Q-Ality the permanent vendor for other programs or events.
- **Re-review** every 24 months, whenever annual payments pass the amount the board sets, and whenever a new program part is added. Each re-review either renews the memo or opens the work to competing quotes.

The full terms are in the [Q-Ality Sound vendor and equipment agreement](/dj-lab/governance/qality-vendor-agreement.html), section 2, and the memo form is Appendix B of the [conflict of interest policy](/dj-lab/governance/conflict-of-interest.html).
