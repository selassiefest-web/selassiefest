## Status and basics

### 1. Is this program actually happening?
It's **proposed**. The curriculum is written and runs in our class app. The Chicago Park District has **not** approved or permitted the program yet, the Rainbow Beach Park Advisory Council vote is pending, no class dates are set, and no staff are hired. **No class runs until every item on our [launch checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html) is done.**

### 2. Who runs it?
**Ras Tafari Inc.**, an Illinois not-for-profit corporation and 501(c)(3) public charity at 7700 S. Stony Island Ave., Chicago. Its President, **Stephen Henry**, leads the Lab and is your contact. The other officers are Paul Kelly (Vice President and Treasurer) and Albert Harris (Corporate Secretary). Ras Tafari Inc. also produces SelassieFest and is a member of the Rainbow Beach Park Advisory Council. See [about](/dj-lab/about/index.html).

### 3. How much does it cost?
**Nothing.** There's no tuition, and all equipment is provided.

### 4. When are classes?
**Dates are not set.** They'll be posted after the park approves the program. The full course is 14 weekly sessions of 90 minutes each, and there's also a spring fast track of 6 two-hour workshops. See [schedule](/dj-lab/enroll/schedule.html).

### 5. Where are classes?
They're planned for **Rainbow Beach Park, 3111 E. 77th St.**: indoors at the fieldhouse when space is available, and on the lawn in summer. Exact rooms and entrances will be shared after a site walk with park staff. See [location](/dj-lab/enroll/locations.html).

### 6. My child is 9. Which group are they in?
**Junior DJs (ages 8–11).** Drop-off is allowed once you've signed an authorized-pickup list. Girls about 9–17 may also choose **Empress Decks**, a girls-only track taught by women DJs.

### 7. Does my child need experience or equipment?
No. We provide controllers (Hercules DJControl Inpulse 300 MK2), laptops, headphones and earplugs. The lights under the jog wheels help beginners learn to beatmatch.

## Safety

### 8. Who will be with my child?
At least **two screened adults at all times**, with **no more than 6 children per adult** and no more than 12 children in a class. No adult is ever alone with a child out of sight. Staff haven't been hired yet, so we can't name them. We will introduce them before the first class.

### 9. How are adults screened?
Before their first class, every adult who works with children goes through a fingerprint-based criminal background check through an Illinois State Police–approved vendor, checks of the Illinois Sex Offender Registry and the national registry at NSOPW.gov, references, and youth-protection training. They're re-screened every 2 years. Every adult also completes Illinois DCFS mandated reporter training. **None of this is done yet, because no one is hired yet.** It's on the launch checklist.

### 10. Is there someone trained in first aid?
That's required. **At least one adult per class** will hold current pediatric first aid/CPR/AED certification from the American Red Cross or American Heart Association. This is a launch requirement and isn't done yet.

### 11. Is the program insured?
**Not yet.** Before launch we must have general liability insurance ($1,000,000 per occurrence, naming the Chicago Park District as additional insured) plus sexual abuse and molestation coverage. No class runs without it.

### 12. Who can pick up my child?
**Only adults on your authorized-pickup list**, after a photo ID check, and every release is recorded in our sign-out log. See [arrival and sign-out](/dj-lab/safety/arrival-signout.html) and the [ID check protocol](/dj-lab/safety/id-check-protocol.html).

### 13. Can my 9-year-old walk home alone?
**Not unless you give written permission in advance.** Without it, your child waits with two staff until an authorized adult arrives.

### 14. What about bathroom trips?
Children go in **pairs**, and an adult waits at the restroom entrance. **Staff never enter a stall area with a child.**

### 15. My child has a food allergy. What happens?
We follow your child's **Food Allergy & Anaphylaxis Emergency Care Plan** (the FARE form). Epinephrine is used per the plan, and **911 is called every time** it's used. No food is shared without your permission. Rescue medicine stays within reach of a trained adult, and other medication is kept locked in its original pharmacy container with your signed authorization. See the [medical and allergy plan](/dj-lab/safety/medical-allergy-plan.html) and the [epinephrine protocol](/dj-lab/safety/epinephrine-protocol.html).

### 16. Is this near the water? Will kids swim?
**No swimming and no water activities.** The park is on the lakefront, but the program never takes place in the water.

### 17. What if there's a storm?
**"When thunder roars, go indoors."** We stay inside until 30 minutes after the last thunder. Classes are cancelled if the National Weather Service issues an Extreme Heat Warning. See [weather and cancellations](/dj-lab/enroll/weather-cancellation.html).

### 18. What if my child gets lost or something happens?
We have a Code Adam–style lost-child plan: the whole team is alerted, the exits are covered, staff search, and we call 911 if the child isn't found within 10 minutes. You're called at once. If there's any incident, you're told **the same day by phone**, and a written report follows within 24 hours. See the [lost child plan](/dj-lab/safety/lost-child-plan.html).

### 19. Will loud music hurt my child's hearing?
Headphone volume is **capped on every laptop**, earplugs are free, the main speaker level is checked with a sound meter, and children stay out of the zone right in front of the speakers. We also coach kids on healthy listening habits.

### 20. What music will they hear?
**Clean lyrics only:** radio edits, checked when crates (music collections) are built.

## Learning, behavior and performing

### 21. Are there grades or tests?
No. Kids rate themselves with a traffic light (I got it / Getting there / Not yet) and earn **Passport stamps** for skills: 3 per session, 42 for the full course. When a mix crashes, we call it **"Data!"**: the DJ names one reason and tries again.

### 22. My child is shy. Will they be put on the spot?
No. **Spotlight is volunteers only.** Coaches never call on a child who didn't sign up. Stations rotate four jobs (DJ, Co-pilot, Hype and Logger), so there's always a role that fits. Kids also pick their own challenge level: Mild, Medium or Spicy.

### 23. How is discipline handled?
There's never physical punishment or shaming, and we never withhold food, water or the restroom. The steps are: reminder, redirect, cool-down with an adult in view, parent call, and finally a pause from the program. See [withdrawing or pausing](/dj-lab/enroll/exit-withdrawal.html) and the [code of conduct](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/code-of-conduct.html).

### 24. Will my child perform in public?
Only with your [performance consent](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/performance-consent.html) and your child's own choice. Graduates get a guaranteed Open Decks slot at Rainbow Wednesdays, our free summer DJ series. **That series is also proposed,** so the slot applies when Rainbow Wednesdays runs. See [performing](/dj-lab/performing/index.html).

## Privacy and technology

### 25. Will you post photos of my child?
Only if you opt in with [media consent](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/media-consent.html). The class app stores **no photos or video**.

### 26. What does the class app show, and who can see it?
Anyone with your class's 6-character code sees the class board: each child's **first name and last initial**, their station, check-in time, in-class activity, Passport stamps and coach notes. Your contact details, medical notes, pickup list and consents are **coaches only**. Please share the class code only with your own family. See [digital tracking consent](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/digital-tracking-consent.html) and [privacy](/dj-lab/privacy/index.html).

### 27. Do you sell or share our data?
No. There are no ads, no tracking pixels, no biometrics and no location tracking. You can review, correct or delete your information, or withdraw consent, at any time.

## Practical

### 28. Do you provide transportation?
No. Families provide transportation, and **staff never drive children in their personal vehicles.** See [transportation and parking](/dj-lab/enroll/transportation-parking.html).

### 29. What if we miss a class?
There's no penalty. Just let us know. Your child catches up through Passport skills. See [attendance](/dj-lab/enroll/attendance-absence.html).

### 30. Can we quit?
Yes, at any time, at no cost. See [withdrawing](/dj-lab/enroll/exit-withdrawal.html).

### 31. Are forms available in Spanish?
Parent forms in English **and Spanish** are a launch requirement. They aren't ready yet.

### 32. Can I watch a class?
Little DJs (5–7) always have their grown-up in the room, and families are invited to the Rainbow Showcase (session 14). Whether Junior DJ parents can observe other sessions depends on the space the park provides. Ask Stephen.
