## Missing a class is okay

Kids get sick, families travel, and school gets busy. **There's no penalty for missing a class,** and your child won't lose their spot for an occasional absence.

## How to report an absence

Please let us know **before class starts** if you can:

- **Text or call:** Stephen Henry, 414-909-3279
- **Email:** stephen@selassiefest.com

Include your child's first name and last initial, plus the class date. You don't have to give a reason. If your child is sick, see [illness and keeping kids home](/dj-lab/enroll/communicable-disease.html).

## If your child doesn't arrive and we haven't heard from you

Because a parent or guardian signs every child in, we always know who is in the room. If a Junior DJ who usually comes is missing and we haven't heard from you, we may call or text to check in. That's just a check-in, not a penalty.

## How kids catch up

Our course is built on **Passport stamps**: 3 skills per session, 42 for the full course. A missed session means some stamps aren't earned yet. They aren't lost.

- At the next class, a mentor can show your child the skills they missed during Deck Time.
- The class app shows which Passport skills are still open, so you and your child can see what to work on.
- Kids practice missed skills at their own pace. There are no grades and no tests.

## Many absences in a row

If your child misses several classes in a row, we'll reach out to ask how we can help. We will always talk with you before making any change to your child's enrollment.

## The Showcase

Session 14 (the Rainbow Showcase) is open to every enrolled child, whatever their attendance. Playing is always a choice.
