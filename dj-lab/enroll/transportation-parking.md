## Families provide transportation

The Rainbow DJ Lab **does not provide transportation**. Families bring their children to class and pick them up.

**Lab staff and volunteers never drive children in their personal vehicles,** not even for a short trip and not even if asked. This protects your child and keeps every adult's behavior observable.

## Parking

The Chicago Park District says **metered parking is available in the lot at Rainbow Beach Park, and there's another lot nearby.** Lakefront parking can fill up on summer days, so leave extra time. Please don't park in fire lanes or block the entrance.

## Public transit

We haven't listed specific bus or train routes here. Service changes, and we'd rather you get the current route than an outdated one. Please plan your trip with:

- the **CTA trip planner** at transitchicago.com, or
- the **RTA trip planner** at rtachicago.org

Enter **3111 E. 77th St., Chicago, IL 60649**.

## Walking and biking

If your child walks or bikes with you:

- Use crosswalks and signals. Streets near the lakefront can be busy.
- Wear a helmet when biking, and bring a lock. Indoor bike storage is not available unless the park confirms it.
- Lakefront wind can be cold even in spring and fall, so bring a layer.

## Can my child come or leave on their own?

For **Junior DJs (ages 8–11)**, a parent or guardian signs your child in, and they're released only to an adult on your authorized-pickup list after a photo ID check. **They are never released to walk home alone unless you've given written permission in advance.** You can choose this on the [authorized pickup form](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/authorized-pickup.html).

See [arrival and sign-out](/dj-lab/safety/arrival-signout.html) and the [ID check protocol](/dj-lab/safety/id-check-protocol.html).

## Running late?

Call or text **Stephen Henry at 414-909-3279**. Your child stays with two screened adults until an authorized adult arrives.
