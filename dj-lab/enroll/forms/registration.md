## Purpose

This is the main enrollment record. It tells us who your child is, places them in the right age group, tells us how to support them, and tells us how to reach you. It also holds the **guardian permission box** that makes the enrollment official.

**Fill it out online:** [registration form](/dj-lab/enroll/register.html)
**Printable version:** [registration.pdf](/dj-lab/assets/forms/registration.pdf)

## Every field

| Field | Required? | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Child's first name | Required | Used by coaches and on the class board. |
| Child's last initial | Required | Tells apart kids with the same first name. We don't put full last names on the board. |
| Age | Required | Placement and safety (for example, sign-out rules for ages 8–11). |
| Age group | Required | Little DJs (5–7), **Junior DJs (8–11)**, Teen DJs (12–17), Grown-Up & Elder DJs (18+), or Empress Decks (a girls-only track, about 9–17). |
| Accommodations | Optional | Anything that helps your child do their best: sensory needs, attention, mobility, language, a quieter spot, extra time. You don't need to share a diagnosis. |
| Guardian name | Required | The adult responsible for enrollment. |
| Guardian email | Required | Confirmation, schedule, cancellations, and recaps if you opt in. |
| Guardian phone | Required | Same-day calls about incidents, illness, weather and pickup. Texts about cancellations. |
| Code of conduct agreement | Required | See [code of conduct](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/code-of-conduct.html). |
| Guardian permission box | Required | You confirm you're the child's parent or legal guardian, that the information is accurate, and that you give permission for your child to take part. For children under 13, this is the verifiable parental consent required by the federal Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). |

The online form also includes the [emergency contacts](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/emergency-contacts.html), [medical and allergy](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/medical-allergy.html), [authorized pickup](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/authorized-pickup.html), and the optional [media](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/media-consent.html), [performance](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/performance-consent.html) and [digital tracking](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/digital-tracking-consent.html) consents.

## Who sees it

- **Class board (anyone with the class code):** only your child's first name and last initial.
- **Coaches and Stephen Henry only:** everything else. It's stored privately with our database provider, Supabase, and never sent to the class page.
- We use Resend to send your confirmation email. We never sell or share your information.

## How long it's kept

Registration records are kept **3 years** for safety and insurance purposes, then deleted (policy commitment). You can review, correct or ask about deletion at any time. See [withdrawing](/dj-lab/enroll/exit-withdrawal.html) and [privacy](/dj-lab/privacy/index.html).

> I am the parent or legal guardian of the child named above. The information I've given is accurate, and I'll update it if it changes. I give permission for my child to take part in the Rainbow DJ Lab, run by Ras Tafari Inc. I understand the program is proposed, that no class will run until its launch requirements are met, and that I may withdraw my child or my consent at any time by contacting Stephen Henry.
