## One online form covers everything

All of these forms are combined into the single online **[registration form](/dj-lab/enroll/register.html)**. It takes about 15–20 minutes. Each page below explains one part of it in detail: what it asks, why, who sees it, and how long we keep it.

If you'd rather fill things out on paper, use the printable PDFs. Bring them to Stephen Henry, or scan or photograph them and email them to **stephen@selassiefest.com**. If a PDF link isn't working yet, contact Stephen and he'll get you a copy.

**Spanish versions** of every parent form are a [launch requirement](/dj-lab/governance/index.html). They aren't ready yet.

## Separate forms we may ask for

Depending on your child's needs, Stephen will follow up with:

- a **medication authorization** for any medication your child brings, which must be in its original pharmacy container
- a **Food Allergy & Anaphylaxis Emergency Care Plan** (the FARE form), signed by your child's doctor, for children with serious allergies

See the [medical and allergy plan](/dj-lab/safety/medical-allergy-plan.html) and [medication storage](/dj-lab/safety/medication-storage.html).

## Who sees form information

Guardian contact details, emergency contacts, medical and allergy notes, the pickup list and consents are **stored privately for coaches only** and are never shown on the class page. They're stored with our database provider, Supabase. See [privacy](/dj-lab/privacy/index.html).

## How long we keep it

Registration records (including these forms) are kept for **3 years** for safety and insurance purposes, then deleted. This is a policy commitment. You can ask to review or correct your information at any time.

## Updating a form

Information changes, so tell us any time. Email **stephen@selassiefest.com** or call or text **414-909-3279**. **Changes to the pickup list must be in writing** (email counts) before the class where they apply.
