## Purpose

If your child is hurt, gets sick, or needs to be picked up and **we can't reach you**, we call these adults. In a medical emergency we call **911 first**, then you, then your emergency contacts.

**Fill it out online:** part of the [registration form](/dj-lab/enroll/register.html)
**Printable version:** [emergency-contacts.pdf](/dj-lab/assets/forms/emergency-contacts.pdf)

## Every field

You'll fill this out for **two contacts**, both required:

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Name | So staff know who they're speaking with. |
| Relationship to child | Helps staff and your child (for example, "Aunt," "Neighbor," "Grandfather"). |
| Phone | The number we call. A mobile number that can receive texts is best. |

**Being an emergency contact does not by itself allow pickup.** Only adults on your [authorized pickup list](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/authorized-pickup.html) may take your child, and they must show photo ID. If you want an emergency contact to be able to pick up, list them on both.

Choose adults who are usually reachable during class time, and let them know you've listed them.

## Who sees it

**Coaches and Stephen Henry only.** It's stored privately and never shown on the class page or in the class app's family view.

## How long it's kept

It's kept as part of the registration record for **3 years** for safety and insurance purposes, then deleted (policy commitment).
