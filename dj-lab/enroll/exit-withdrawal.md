## You can withdraw at any time

The program is free, so withdrawing costs nothing, and there are no fees or forms to fill out. You don't need to give a reason.

**To withdraw,** email **stephen@selassiefest.com** or call or text **414-909-3279**. We'll confirm by email.

When you withdraw:

- We take your child off the class roster and the class board.
- You stop getting class emails and recaps.
- If your child is holding any Lab equipment, please return it. Kids don't take gear home during the course.

## Your data when you leave

You can choose what happens to your information:

- **Delete now.** Ask us to delete your child's information, and we'll delete their in-class activity and Passport and confirm when it's done.
- **Keep the Passport.** Ask us to keep your child's Passport stamps in case they come back.
- **Default.** If you don't ask for anything, child activity data is deleted within 90 days after the season ends.

One exception: **registration records** (contacts, consents and safety-related records such as sign-in and sign-out logs and any incident reports) are kept for **3 years for safety and insurance purposes, then deleted.** Ask us and we'll explain what is kept and why. These are policy commitments. See [privacy](/dj-lab/privacy/index.html).

You can also **withdraw a single consent** (media, performance or digital tracking) without leaving the program.

## When the program may pause a child's participation

We want every child to stay. Pausing is rare and always a last step. Our approach to behavior uses **no physical punishment, no shaming, and never withholding food, water or the restroom.**

The ladder:

1. **Reminder:** a friendly word about the rule.
2. **Redirect:** a different job or spot at the station.
3. **Cool-down space:** a calm spot **with an adult in view**.
4. **Parent call:** we talk with you, the same day.
5. **Pause from the program.**

**Safety violations go straight to a parent call.** These include hurting someone, leaving the group, or bringing anything prohibited such as alcohol, tobacco, vaping or cannabis.

A pause may also happen if a parent or guardian repeatedly breaks the [code of conduct](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/code-of-conduct.html), for example by trying to pick up without being on the list or behaving in a threatening way.

## Our fair process for a pause

If we're considering a pause, we commit to:

1. **Tell you first,** by phone, before or right when it happens, and explain why.
2. **Hear your child's side** and yours. Kids get to explain in their own words, with you present if you wish.
3. **Put it in writing.** You'll get a short written summary of what happened and the decision.
4. **Make a plan to come back.** A pause has a set length and a clear path to return, such as a check-in conversation or an adjusted job or seat.
5. **Offer review.** If you disagree, you can ask for the decision to be reviewed by the officers of Ras Tafari Inc.

**Safety reports are separate.** If we have a concern about a child's safety, staff follow mandated reporter law and report directly to the DCFS Hotline (1-800-25-ABUSE) or 911. That isn't a discipline step, and it isn't something we pause or negotiate.

These policies are ones Ras Tafari Inc. commits to. Formal board adoption is a [launch requirement](/dj-lab/governance/index.html).

## Coming back

A child who withdraws is welcome to come back in a later season. Families may need to update the registration forms.
