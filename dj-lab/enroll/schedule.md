## Every Tuesday, 4 to 6 pm

The Lab is a **year-round program, every Tuesday from 4:00 to 6:00 pm** in the Rainbow Beach Park fieldhouse game room. The first class is planned for **January 12, 2027**, with **39 class Tuesdays** in 2027. The calendar skips every federal-holiday week and CPS spring break. The full dated calendar is on the [Rainbow DJ Lab 2027 page](/full-spectrum/dj-lab-2027.html). Classes start only after the Chicago Park District approves the program and the other launch requirements are complete (see the [launch checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html)). We'll email every registered family.

## Join any Tuesday

Children join on any Tuesday a station is free and learn at their own pace. Missing a class costs nothing: make up any Mission at the next class. Stamps never expire.

## The 14 Missions

There are two Missions for each color of the rainbow. Each Tuesday the instructors feature one Mission for the room (they cycle 1 to 14 across the year), and each child also works on their own next skill:

| # | Mission | # | Mission |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Red: Meet the Machine | 8 | Green: Make a Third Song |
| 2 | Red: Build Your Crate | 9 | Blue: Hot Cues and Loops |
| 3 | Orange: Beats, Bars and Phrases | 10 | Blue: Word Flip |
| 4 | Orange: Mix on the One | 11 | Indigo: Theme Clash Lab |
| 5 | Yellow: Tempo with the Lights On | 12 | Indigo: Mic, MC and Live Lab |
| 6 | Yellow: Beatmatch by Ear | 13 | Violet: Build Your Set |
| 7 | Green: The Three Knobs | 14 | Violet: Rainbow Showcase (families attend) |

## What a Tuesday looks like

| Time | What happens |
|---|---|
| 3:30–4:00 | Instructors set up 12 stations |
| 4:00–5:30 | Core DJ Lab, nine blocks (below) |
| 5:30–6:00 | Guest woman DJ: her story, a signature technique, every child tries it |
| 6:00 | Pickup |

### The 90-minute core

| Minutes | Block |
|---|---|
| 0–7 | Drop In |
| 7–12 | Mission Card |
| 12–20 | Watch Me Think |
| 20–35 | Deck Time 1 |
| 35–40 | Move Break |
| 40–55 | Deck Time 2 |
| 55–70 | Wordplay Lab |
| 70–82 | Spotlight (volunteers only) |
| 82–90 | Check Out |

On **listen-back Missions** (6, 10 and 13), each child records 2 minutes of mixing and reviews it with a mentor.

## Arrival and pickup times

- **Arrive 10 minutes early.** A parent or guardian signs each child in, and the first block starts on time.
- **Pick up on time.** Children are released only to adults on your authorized-pickup list, after a photo ID check. See [arrival and sign-out](/dj-lab/safety/arrival-signout.html).
- Running late? Call or text Stephen Henry at **872-255-9333**.

## The family Showcase

The **Rainbow Showcase** is the one families are invited to attend. Showcases are planned for spring, midyear and year-end. A child plays at the next Showcase after earning all 42 Passport stamps, and only if they want to. See [performing](/dj-lab/performing/index.html).
