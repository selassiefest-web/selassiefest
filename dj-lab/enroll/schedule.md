## Dates are not set yet

**No class dates have been set.** Dates will be posted only after the Chicago Park District approves the program and the other launch requirements are complete. See the [launch checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html). When dates are ready, we'll post them on this page and email every registered family.

## Two ways the course runs

### Full course: 14 weekly sessions, 90 minutes each

There are two sessions for each color of the rainbow:

| # | Session | # | Session |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Red: Meet the Machine | 8 | Green: Make a Third Song |
| 2 | Red: Build Your Crate | 9 | Blue: Hot Cues and Loops |
| 3 | Orange: Beats, Bars and Phrases | 10 | Blue: Word Flip |
| 4 | Orange: Mix on the One | 11 | Indigo: Theme Clash Lab |
| 5 | Yellow: Tempo with the Lights On | 12 | Indigo: Mic, MC and Live Lab |
| 6 | Yellow: Beatmatch by Ear | 13 | Violet: Build Your Set |
| 7 | Green: The Three Knobs | 14 | Violet: Rainbow Showcase (families attend) |

### Spring fast track: 6 workshops, 2 hours each

1. Beatmatching and Blending
2. Building a Set
3. Live Looping and Editing
4. Playing with Live Instruments
5. The Theme Clash
6. Dress Rehearsal on the Beach

## What a 90-minute class looks like

| Minutes | Block |
|---|---|
| 0–7 | Drop In |
| 7–12 | Mission Card |
| 12–20 | Watch Me Think |
| 20–35 | Deck Time 1 |
| 35–40 | Move Break |
| 40–55 | Deck Time 2 |
| 55–70 | Wordplay Lab |
| 70–82 | Spotlight (volunteers only) |
| 82–90 | Check Out |

On **listen-back days** (sessions 6, 10 and 13, and fast-track workshop 6), each child records 2 minutes of mixing and reviews it with a mentor.

## Arrival and pickup times

- **Arrive 10 minutes early.** A parent or guardian signs each child in, and the first block starts on time.
- **Pick up on time.** Children are released only to adults on your authorized-pickup list, after a photo ID check. See [arrival and sign-out](/dj-lab/safety/arrival-signout.html).
- Running late? Call or text Stephen Henry at **414-909-3279**.

## The family Showcase

Session 14, the **Rainbow Showcase**, is the one families are invited to attend. Your child plays only if they want to. See [performing](/dj-lab/performing/index.html).
