## Before you start: an honest status note

The Rainbow DJ Lab is a **proposed** program. Ras Tafari Inc. has written the full curriculum, and it already runs in our class app. Several things are still pending:

- The Chicago Park District has **not** yet approved or permitted the program.
- The Rainbow Beach Park Advisory Council vote is still pending.
- **No class dates are set.**
- No staff are hired yet.

**No class runs until every launch requirement is checked off.** You can read the full list on the [launch checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html). It covers Park District approval, insurance, background checks for every adult, mandated reporter training, first aid/CPR certification, an emergency plan built with park staff, forms in English and Spanish, and a board vote adopting our safety policies.

You're welcome to read everything here and ask questions now. You don't need to commit to anything until dates are posted.

## What it costs

**Nothing.** There's no tuition, and all equipment is provided: controllers, laptops, headphones and earplugs. You will never be asked to buy gear.

## Who can join

| Group | Ages | How it works |
|---|---|---|
| Little DJs | 5–7 | A parent or guardian stays and learns alongside the child. |
| **Junior DJs** | **8–11** | **Drop-off is allowed** with a signed authorized-pickup list. A 9-year-old joins this group. |
| Teen DJs | 12–17 | |
| Grown-Up & Elder DJs | 18 and up | |
| Empress Decks | about 9–17 | A girls-only track taught by women DJs. |

No experience is needed. Kids don't need to own any music gear, and they don't need to read music.

## Class size

A class has **no more than 12 children**. Kids work in stations of 4 that share one controller, and a class has at most 3 stations. There's always a ratio of **no more than 6 children per screened adult**, and **at least two screened adults** are present at all times. See [About the Lab](/dj-lab/about/index.html) and [Safety](/dj-lab/safety/index.html).

## How enrollment works, step by step

1. **Read up.** Start with this section, the [FAQ](/dj-lab/enroll/faq.html) and the [safety pages](/dj-lab/safety/index.html).
2. **Wait for dates.** Dates are posted only after the park approves the program. We'll post the [schedule](/dj-lab/enroll/schedule.html) here.
3. **Register online.** Use the [registration form](/dj-lab/enroll/register.html). It takes about 15–20 minutes. The form page explains what each question is for. If you'd rather use paper, see [printable forms](/dj-lab/enroll/forms/index.html).
4. **Get a confirmation email.** It's sent automatically when your registration goes through.
5. **Talk with us if you'd like.** If your child has allergies, medication, or needs any accommodation, Stephen Henry will follow up with you before the first class. Families with medication will need to provide a signed authorization and, for allergies, a Food Allergy & Anaphylaxis Emergency Care Plan. See the [medical and allergy plan](/dj-lab/safety/medical-allergy-plan.html).
6. **Get your class code.** Enrolled families receive a 6-character code for the [class app](/dj-lab/live/). Please share it only with your own family. Anyone with the code can see the whole class's board.
7. **First class.** Arrive 10 minutes early so you can sign your child in. See [arrival and sign-out](/dj-lab/safety/arrival-signout.html).
