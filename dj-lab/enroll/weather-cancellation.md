## How you'll hear about a cancellation

If a class is cancelled or moved, we'll send **both an email and a text message** to the guardian contact on your registration.

- **Our commitment:** when we can see the problem coming (a forecast heat warning, a storm forecast, or a building issue), we'll notify you **at least 2 hours before class starts**.
- **Sudden changes:** sometimes weather turns fast. If we have to end a class early, we'll **call or text each family**. **Children stay with two screened adults, indoors, until an authorized adult picks them up.** We never send children home on their own because of a cancellation.
- **No message means class is on.** If you're unsure, text Stephen Henry at **414-909-3279**.

Please keep your phone number up to date so these messages reach you.

## Lightning

We follow the National Weather Service: **"When thunder roars, go indoors."** The NWS says there's no safe place outside when thunderstorms are in the area.

- At the first thunder or lightning, the whole group goes indoors right away.
- We stay inside until **30 minutes after the last thunder**. NWS guidance for outdoor activities says to wait at least 30 minutes after the last thunder before going back out.
- If the storm continues, class finishes indoors or ends early. You'll be called or texted.

## Heat

Summer classes on the lawn include:

- a **water break in every block**
- **shade** for the stations and the group
- an **indoor fallback** when the heat index is high

**Class is cancelled** if the National Weather Service issues an **Extreme Heat Warning** (dangerously high heat expected or already happening), or if a **Heat Advisory** makes outdoor class unsafe and no indoor space is available.

Please send your child with a **refillable water bottle**, a hat and sunscreen in summer.

## Cold, wind and rain

Fall through spring, classes are planned indoors at the fieldhouse. In summer, if rain or wind makes the lawn unsafe for children or equipment, we move indoors when space is available. If we can't, we cancel.

## Indoor space depends on the park

Indoor fallback space depends on what the Chicago Park District makes available. The program is **not yet approved**, and the exact indoor rooms will be confirmed after our site walk with park staff. See [location](/dj-lab/enroll/locations.html).

## Missed classes because of weather

A weather cancellation is never counted against your child. Skills from a cancelled class are picked up in later sessions through Passport stamps. See [attendance and absences](/dj-lab/enroll/attendance-absence.html).
