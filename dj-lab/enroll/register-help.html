About the Registration Form

What the Rainbow DJ Lab registration form asks, why we need each item, how long it takes, and what happens after you submit.

Where the form is

The online form is at /dj-lab/enroll/register.html. This page explains it so you know what to expect before you open it. If you prefer paper, see the printable forms.

Please note: no class dates are set yet, and the program still needs Chicago Park District approval. Registering early lets us know you're interested. It doesn't put you on the hook for anything. See the enrollment overview.

How long it takes

Plan on 15–20 minutes. Before you start, have these handy:

  • Phone numbers for two emergency contacts
  • Names, relationships and phone numbers for every adult who may pick up your child
  • Your child's allergy and medication details, if any

What you'll be asked, and why

Item Why we need it
Child's first name and last initial This is how your child shows up on the class board in the app. We don't put full names on the board.
Age and age group To place your child in the right group. A 9-year-old goes in Junior DJs (ages 8–11).
Accommodations So we can set up the room, the pacing and the jobs to fit your child (for example, sensory needs, a quieter spot, or extra time).
Medical and allergy information So the adults in the room know what to watch for and what to do. See medical and allergy.
Guardian name, email and phone So we can reach you the same day about anything that happens, and send your confirmation.
Two emergency contacts In case we can't reach you. See emergency contacts.
Authorized pickup adults (name, relationship, phone) Children are released only to adults on this list, after a photo ID check. See authorized pickup.
May your child sign themselves out? The default is no. Children 8–11 are never released to walk home alone unless a parent or guardian gives written permission in advance.
Media consent (optional) Whether we may photograph or record your child for program use. Saying no has no effect on your child's place. See media consent.
Performance consent (optional) Whether your child may perform at public events beyond class. See performance consent.
Digital tracking consent (optional) Whether your child's in-class activity is recorded in the class app, and whether you'd like recap emails. See digital tracking consent.
Code of conduct agreement (required) You and your child agree to the same simple rules. See code of conduct.
Guardian permission box (required) You confirm you're the child's parent or legal guardian and give permission for them to take part. For children under 13, this is also the verifiable parental consent that federal children's privacy law (COPPA) requires.

The form also uses Cloudflare Turnstile, a quick bot check, to keep spam out.

Who sees your answers

Your contact details, emergency contacts, medical and allergy notes, pickup list and consents are stored privately for coaches only and are never sent to the class page. Families with the class code see only each child's first name and last initial, their station, and their in-class activity. Read more on the privacy page.

We use Supabase to store data and Resend to send email. We don't run ads, use tracking pixels, or sell or share your data.

How long we keep it

  • Registration records are kept for 3 years for safety and insurance purposes, then deleted.
  • Child activity data from the app is deleted within 90 days after the season ends, unless you ask us to keep your child's Passport.

These are policy commitments. You may ask to review, correct or delete your information at any time.

What happens after you submit

  1. You'll get a confirmation email right away. If it doesn't come, check your spam folder, then contact Stephen.
  2. If you listed allergies, medication or accommodations, Stephen Henry will contact you to go over the plan and any paperwork, such as a medication authorization or a FARE emergency care plan.
  3. When dates are set and the launch checklist is complete, you'll get the schedule, location details and your class code.
  4. On the first day, arrive 10 minutes early to sign your child in.

Need to change something?

Contact Stephen Henry at 414-909-3279 or stephen@selassiefest.com. Changes to your pickup list should be made in writing (email is fine) before the class where they apply.
