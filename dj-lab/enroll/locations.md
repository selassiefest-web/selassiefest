## Rainbow Beach Park

**Rainbow Beach Park**
3111 E. 77th St., Chicago, IL 60649

This is a Chicago Park District park in the South Region and the 7th Ward. Its official listing includes a fieldhouse with a gymnasium and a clubroom.

**Status:** the Chicago Park District has **not yet approved** the program or given permission to use space at this park. Everything below describes what we're requesting.

## Indoors and outdoors

- **Fall through spring:** indoors at the park's **fieldhouse**, when space is available.
- **Summer:** outdoors on the **park lawn**, as the park approves, with shade and water breaks. If the weather turns, we'll move indoors or cancel. See [weather and cancellations](/dj-lab/enroll/weather-cancellation.html).

## No water activities

Rainbow Beach is on the lakefront, but **children do not swim during the program, and no part of the program takes place in the water.** Children stay with their group and do not go to the shoreline during class.

## What we'll share after the site walk

We will walk the site with park staff before launch. After that, we'll post:

- the exact room or lawn area for each season
- the entrance to use for drop-off and pickup
- a simple map with the sign-in table, restrooms, the indoor shelter spot and the meeting point
- the emergency plan, including the fieldhouse lockdown plan

These are part of the [launch checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html). We won't publish room names or entrances until they're confirmed.
