Performance Consent: Rainbow DJ Lab, Ras Tafari Inc.

I am the parent or legal guardian of the child named on this registration. By checking this box, I give permission for my child to perform as a DJ (and, if my child chooses, on the microphone) at public events connected with the Rainbow DJ Lab, including the Rainbow Showcase, Open Decks at Rainbow Wednesdays, and related youth showcases, when and if those events take place.

I understand that: 1. My child will always choose whether to perform at any event. No one will pressure my child to perform. 2. A screened Lab adult will supervise my child at every performance. The program's two-adult and sign-out rules apply at every event. 3. Only clean lyrics (radio edits) will be played. 4. Performance times and locations will be shared with me in advance. I'll be told whether I need to be present. 5. My child will be introduced by first name only, unless I say otherwise in writing. 6. Members of the public may watch and may take their own photos. Ras Tafari Inc. will photograph or record my child only if I have also given media consent. 7. Performances are unpaid learning opportunities.

I understand this consent is optional and that refusing it will not affect my child's participation in class.

I may withdraw this consent at any time, including on the day of an event, by contacting Stephen Henry (414-909-3279, stephen@selassiefest.com) or telling the lead coach in person.

This consent lasts until I withdraw it or until the end of the season in which it was given, whichever comes first.
