Retention and Deletion

How long Rainbow DJ Lab keeps each kind of information about children and families, and how it is deleted.

The rule we follow

We keep information only as long as we need it for the reason we collected it, then delete it. The federal COPPA Rule requires this for children's information, and we apply it to everyone.

Status: These retention periods are a policy commitment of Ras Tafari Inc.; formal board adoption is a launch requirement. Where a period isn't decided yet, the table says so, and it will be set in our written retention schedule before the first class.

Retention table

Interest-list entries (before launch) Child's first name and last initial, age and age group; guardian name, email and phone; permission to contact. No health, pickup, custody or emergency details, no consents, no free-text notes Deleted if the Lab hasn't started within 12 months, or sooner on request

Recap email records A record of which session recap was sent for which child, and when Deleted together with that child's activity data
Registration records Guardian name, email and phone; emergency contacts; child's age; accommodations, allergies and medical notes; authorized-pickup list and custody notes; consents Kept 3 years for safety and insurance purposes, then deleted
Registration notice emails The notice of each registration or interest-list entry emailed to Stephen Henry Same as registration records: 3 years, then deleted
Sign-out log (and the sign-out email sent to the parent each time) Who picked up, relationship, ID checked, coach, time To be set in the written retention schedule before the first class. In the app as built today, a child's sign-out records are removed when that child's class record is deleted

Keeping your child's Passport

Your child's Passport is a record of what they learned, and many kids want to keep it. Tell us before the 90 days are up and we'll give you a copy and keep the Passport record. Contact Stephen Henry.

Why registration records are kept longer

If a question ever comes up about an injury, a release at pickup, or a safety concern, we need to show what we knew and what we did. Insurance claims can come up after a season ends. After 3 years, we delete them.

How deletion works

  • Database records are deleted from the Lab's database. Removing a child's record also removes their check-ins, stamps, notes, recap records and sign-out records.
  • Emails in the Lab's email account are deleted on the same schedule.
  • Paper forms are shredded.
  • Our service providers may keep backup copies for a limited time under their own policies before those backups expire. See third-party vendors.

Asking for earlier deletion

You can ask us to delete your child's information at any time. We'll delete everything we're not required to keep for safety, insurance or legal reasons, and tell you what we kept and why. See your data rights.
