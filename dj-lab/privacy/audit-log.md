## The honest answer

**The Lab does not yet have a full audit log of who viewed or changed family data.** Building one is a **launch requirement**: no class runs until it's in place. See the [launch checklist](/dj-lab/governance/index.html).

The class app does keep some records today. This page lists exactly what they are, and what they don't cover.

## What is recorded today

| Record | What it holds | What it tells you |
|---|---|---|
| **Coach sign-in link records** | The coach's email, when a sign-in link was requested, when it expires, and whether it was used | Which coaches requested access to the app, and when. Only emails on the Lab's coach list can request a link |
| **Recap emails sent** | For each child and session: that a recap email was sent, and the time | Which families were emailed a recap, and when |
| **Sign-out records** | Who picked the child up, their relationship, whether photo ID was checked, whether it was a written-permission self sign-out, the coach who released the child, and the time | Every release at pickup, and who checked ID |
| **Check-in times** | The time each child was checked in, per session | Attendance |
| **Registration time** | When each registration was submitted | When a family registered |

## What is not recorded yet

- **Which coach viewed** a family's private details (contact information, medical notes, pickup list), and when.
- **Which coach changed** a child's record, and what changed.
- **Exports or copies** of family data.

These gaps are why a full access audit log is a launch requirement.

## Not related: the partner tracker

Ras Tafari Inc. keeps a separate tracker of activity with partner organizations about the wider Full Spectrum program. It's **unrelated** to the Lab's family data, holds no child or family information, and is not an audit log of the class app.

## The full audit log we're building

Before the first class, the audit log will record, for every access to private family data:

- **who** (which signed-in coach)
- **what** (which child's record, and which fields were viewed or changed)
- **when** (date and time)
- **what changed** (the old and new values for edits)

And it will be:

- **protected:** coaches can't edit or delete log entries
- **reviewed:** checked regularly by Stephen Henry, and by another officer for Stephen's own access
- **available to you:** on request, we'll tell you who has accessed your child's private record, within 10 business days. See [your data rights](/dj-lab/privacy/parent-data-rights.html)

We'll update this page when it's built.
