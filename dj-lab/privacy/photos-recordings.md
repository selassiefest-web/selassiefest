## The short version

- **The class app holds no photos and no video.**
- **Listen-back recordings** are heard by your child and their mentor, kept only until the session review, then deleted unless your family asks for a copy.
- **Photos or video for any outside use need your separate, opt-in media consent.**
- **We never put a child's name with an image.**

> **Status:** The Lab is proposed and not running yet. These are policies Ras Tafari Inc. commits to; formal board adoption is a [launch requirement](/dj-lab/governance/index.html).

## In the class app

The app at [/dj-lab/live/](/dj-lab/live/) has no way to take, upload or store photos, video or audio. Kids appear on the in-room station board by first name and last initial only, and a family's private link shows only their own child. See [data privacy](/dj-lab/privacy/data-privacy-statement.html).

## Listen-back recordings

On **listen-back days** (sessions 6, 10 and 13 of the full course, and workshop W6 of the spring fast track), each kid records **2 minutes** of their own mix and reviews it with a mentor. Hearing yourself back is one of the best ways DJs improve.

| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| What's recorded? | The music your child mixes. It's an audio mix recording, not video |
| Who hears it? | Your child and their mentor during the review, in view of other adults like every one-on-one moment in the Lab. You can hear it too |
| Where is it kept? | On Lab equipment for the session only. It's **not** uploaded to the class app or its database |
| How long is it kept? | **Only until the session review**, then deleted |
| Can we get a copy? | **Yes.** Ask before or at the session, and we'll give a copy to you, the parent or guardian, then delete ours |
| Is it ever shared or posted? | **No.** Not without your separate media consent |
| Can my child skip it? | Yes. Recording is never required, the same as Spotlight |

## Media consent: opt-in only

The registration form asks separately whether you consent to photos or video of your child. **The answer is no unless you say yes.**

If you say **yes**:

- Images may be used to share the Lab's work, for example on Ras Tafari Inc.'s website or in reports to the park and partners.
- **We never put a child's name, or any other identifying detail, with an image.**
- You can **withdraw media consent at any time.** We'll stop using images of your child going forward and remove them from anything we control. (Something already printed can't be recalled.)

If you say **no**:

- We won't take or use photos or video of your child.
- If your child is in a group shot by accident, we'll crop them out or not use the image.

Children with and without media consent take part in exactly the same way.

## Other families and visitors

- Please **don't photograph or record other people's children** at any Lab activity, including from the family area.
- At the **Rainbow Showcase** (session 14), families may photograph their own child. Please don't post images of other children without their family's permission.
- Staff and volunteers **never** use personal phones to photograph children.

## Concerns

If you see an image of your child used in a way you didn't agree to, contact Stephen Henry (414-909-3279, stephen@selassiefest.com) and we'll take it down. Safeguarding concerns about images go through [report a concern](/dj-lab/safety/report-a-concern.html).
