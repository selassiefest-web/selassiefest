# Code of Conduct

This code applies to everyone who contributes to the Rainbow DJ Lab: people who edit this site or app, and coaches, mentors and volunteers in the program.

## Children come first

- Follow the Lab's youth protection policies at all times: [Safety](https://selassiefest.com/dj-lab/safety/index.html) and [Staff screening](https://selassiefest.com/dj-lab/safety/staff-screening.html). Two screened adults, always. Every one-on-one interaction must be observable and interruptible.
- Never contact a Lab child privately, online or off, including by text, direct message or social media. Communication goes to parents and guardians.
- Never put a child's personal information in an issue, commit, pull request, chat or screenshot.
- If you suspect a child is being abused or neglected, call the **DCFS Hotline at 1-800-25-ABUSE (1-800-252-2873)**, or **911** if a child is in immediate danger. You do not need anyone's permission.

## How we treat each other

- Be respectful, patient and honest. No harassment, discrimination, threats or demeaning language.
- Tell the truth about the program. Don't claim something is approved, insured or finished when it isn't.
- Disagree about the work, not the person.
- No alcohol, tobacco, vaping or cannabis at any Lab activity.

## Reporting

Report concerns to Stephen Henry, President, at stephen@selassiefest.com or 414-909-3279. Concerns about the President can go to the other officers. See the [whistleblower policy](https://selassiefest.com/dj-lab/governance/whistleblower.html). Good-faith reporters are protected from retaliation.

## Consequences

Breaking this code can lead to removal of contribution access, removal from the program, and reports to authorities where required. See [Enforcement and self-accountability](https://selassiefest.com/dj-lab/governance/enforcement-self-accountability.html).
