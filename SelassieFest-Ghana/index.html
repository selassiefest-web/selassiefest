Sankofa: The Return Home

Ghana's chapter is built around Sankofa -- the Akan principle that you must look back to move forward. In partnership with Jamaica Village Ghana, we honor the diaspora's journey of repatriation, from the Door of Return at Cape Coast to a new generation choosing to come home.
