        PARKING AGREEMENT

        (Enforceable Under Wisconsin Chapters 703 and 704)

        1. LEGAL AUTHORITY
        This Agreement is adopted pursuant to Wis. Stat. §703.10(1), §703.20(1), and §703.30, granting the Association authority to regulate common elements, including parking, and to impose and collect charges, fines, and enforcement costs. This Agreement is also a binding occupancy contract under Wis. Stat. Chapter 704, and applies to all Unit Occupants as a condition of occupancy.
        2. BINDING EFFECT ON UNIT OCCUPANT
        All Unit Occupants agree to comply with this Agreement. Failure to comply constitutes: (a) a violation under Chapter 703, and (b) a material breach of occupancy rights under Chapter 704. Parking is a revocable privilege, not a right.
        3. ASSIGNMENT OF PARKING RIGHTS
        Parking stalls are limited common elements controlled by the Association. Assignment of one (1) stall is conditioned upon the Unit Occupant maintaining a zero ($0.00) balance. If rent or any required payment is due on the 1st day of the month and is not paid in full, the Unit Occupant is deemed delinquent as of 12:01 a.m. on the 2nd day of the month, and all parking privileges are immediately revoked without further notice. Any vehicle remaining on the property after revocation is considered unauthorized and will be towed at the Unit Occupant's expense by the Association's designated towing provider. The Association will suspend parking rights for delinquent accounts consistent with Wis. Stat. §703.165 and §703.20. Parking is restricted to the stall corresponding to the unit number.
        4. RESIDENT USE ONLY
        Parking stalls are for Unit Occupants only and will not be rented or assigned to non-residents. Temporary guest use is permitted only within the assigned stall and must comply with all provisions of this Agreement.
        5. VEHICLE ELIGIBILITY
        Permitted vehicles include automobiles, vans, motorcycles, and light trucks. Prohibited vehicles include, but are not limited to: trailers of any kind; boats or recreational vehicles; vehicles requiring Class C or higher licensing; unregistered or improperly licensed vehicles; inoperable vehicles. Vehicles with expired registration beyond twenty-four (24) hours are considered non-compliant and will be subject to towing.
        6. STORAGE AND ABANDONMENT
        Vehicles not moved for seven (7) consecutive days are considered stored. Storage without written approval is prohibited and will result in towing.
        7. PARKING POSITION AND USE
        All vehicles must be parked fully within assigned stall lines; not touch or cross boundary markings; not obstruct sidewalks, dumpsters, or access lanes. Motorcycles must use protective material under kickstands. Violations will result in enforcement, including towing.
        8. COMMERCIAL VEHICLES
        Commercial vehicles are prohibited except: (a) temporary service vehicles, or (b) vehicles assigned to approved rented stalls. Unauthorized commercial vehicles will be towed.
        9. VEHICLE CONDITION
        All vehicles must be operable and in good condition. Vehicles with flat tires, leaks, or visible damage are considered inoperable and will be towed after seventy-two (72) hours or sooner if deemed necessary.
        10. MAINTENANCE AND CONDUCT
        The following are prohibited and will result in enforcement action: vehicle repairs or maintenance; oil changes; car washing; cooking, sleeping, or loitering in vehicles.
        11. CLEANLINESS
        Unit Occupants must maintain parking stalls free of oil or fluid stains and garbage or debris. Failure to maintain cleanliness will result in fines and/or enforcement.
        12. FIRE LANES AND ROADWAYS
        All roadways are designated fire lanes. Parking, stopping, or standing for more than three (3) minutes is prohibited. Violations will result in immediate towing without notice.
        13. SNOW REMOVAL COMPLIANCE
        During snow events of two (2) inches or more, vehicles must be removed from the parking lot. 7:30 a.m. is defined as 24 hours after the conclusion of the most recent snowfall event, as determined by the Association. Vehicles must not return until snow removal is complete. Noncompliance will result in towing only, at the Unit Occupant's expense.
        14. VEHICLE REGISTRATION REQUIREMENT
        All vehicles must be registered with the Association (Make / Model / Plate # / VIN #). A valid parking permit must be clearly displayed in the front windshield at all times. Failure to register or display a permit constitutes unauthorized parking and will result in towing.
        15. UNAUTHORIZED PARKING
        Parking in another assigned stall without written permission is prohibited. Unauthorized vehicles will be towed immediately at the Unit Occupant's expense.
        16. ENFORCEMENT AND PENALTIES
        The Association will enforce this Agreement through fines and assessments under Wis. Stat. §703.165, suspension of parking privileges, towing, and legal action. All costs, including towing, administrative fees, and attorney's fees, will be charged to the Unit Occupant and assessed to the Unit.
        17. NOTICE AND DUE PROCESS
        Except for immediate enforcement situations (including fire lanes, unauthorized vehicles, delinquency-based revocation, and snow removal), enforcement will follow: written notice of violation (via email and/or Tenant Portal Announcements); opportunity to cure where applicable; assessment of fines. Notice will be deemed delivered when sent electronically, posted, hand-delivered, or sent via certified mail.
        18. TOWING AUTHORIZATION
        Unit Occupant authorizes the Association and its agents to tow vehicles in violation of this Agreement without prior notice where permitted by law. All towing and storage costs will be the responsibility of the Unit Occupant.
        19. LIABILITY
        The Association is not liable for damage, theft, or loss of any vehicle or its contents.
        20. COST RECOVERY
        The Association will recover all enforcement costs, including towing charges, fines, administrative costs, and attorney's fees. Such costs may be assessed to the Unit under Wis. Stat. §703.165.
        21. PRIORITY AND INCORPORATION
        This Agreement supplements all condominium documents. The stricter provision governs in all cases.
