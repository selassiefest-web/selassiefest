RULES OF THE CLASH

Round Structure

  • 3 rounds + final dubplate round
  • Each selector plays 3 tunes per round
  • Rewinds allowed, but excessive rewinds can count against you

Crowd Decides

  • Applause meter + sound meter
  • Judges panel (veteran selectors)
  • Instant crowd reaction decides tie‑breakers

Dubplate Specials

  • Exclusive, unreleased vocals
  • Custom disses allowed (but respectful)
  • No more than 3 dubplates per selector per round
LEGENDARY CLASHES

Stone Love vs. Killamanjaro (1990s)

The rivalry that defined modern clash. Endless dubplates, iconic rewinds, and a split crowd. Still debated today.

World Clash Finals (2004)

First international clash streamed online. Mighty Crown (Japan) defeated Sentinel (UK) in a 4‑hour battle.

SelassieFest 2024

Chicago Rebel Sound upset defending champion Kingston Thunder with a surprise Bob Marley dubplate in the final round.

CLASH ETIQUETTE

Make Noise

Cheer, whistle, wave your hands. The selector feeds off your energy. If you love a tune, demand a rewind!

No Disrespect

Healthy rivalry is encouraged, but booing and throwing objects is not allowed. One love, one clash.

Stay to the End

The final dubplate round often decides the winner. Don't leave early — you might miss history.
