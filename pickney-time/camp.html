A Summer Rooted in Heritage

Traditional Games

Ring games, yard games, and the play traditions of the Caribbean — drawn from our living heritage archive of over one hundred games.

Music & Storytelling

Drumming, song, Anansi stories, and spoken word — culture passed the way it has always been passed: together, out loud.

Nature & Ital Living

Gardening, healthy food traditions, and time outdoors every single day.

Showcase & Field Days

The summer builds to field-day olympics and a heritage showcase where campers present what they've learned to family and community.
