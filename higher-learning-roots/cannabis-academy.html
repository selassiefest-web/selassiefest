Cannabis Academy

Seed-to-shelf education for growers, entrepreneurs, and industry professionals. Accredited workshops, live demonstrations, and career-focused sessions — all in one immersive day.

Cannabis Academy is the industry hub of Higher Learning Roots. Whether you're a home grower, a dispensary manager, or exploring a new career, our faculty brings real-world expertise. Sessions include hands-on labs, business panels, and regulatory deep dives.
Learning Pathways

Four core tracks

Mix and match sessions across cultivation, extraction, compliance, and business. Each attendee can earn a digital badge by completing three sessions.

Cultivation Science
Soil biology, hydroponics, lighting strategies, integrated pest management, and genetics — from clone to harvest.
Beginner to advanced
Extraction & Chemistry
Solventless and solvent-based extraction, distillation, chromatography, and safety protocols.
Intermediate / lab focus
Compliance & Quality

Managers / operators
Business Leadership
