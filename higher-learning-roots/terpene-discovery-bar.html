Welcome to Higher Learning Roots: SelassieFest’s intentional, safe space dedicated entirely to natural plant sciences, botanical education, and deep ancestral research.

Higher Learning Roots

Bridging pure organic science with ancient landrace heritage. Explore our interactive modules designed to educate the community on responsible botanical relationships, structural chemistry, and sustainable seed-to-shelf journeys.

Terpene Discovery Bar

An immersive organoleptic environment. Train your senses to isolate and discover the structural aromatic compounds shaping plant biology.

The Leaf Library

Browse peer-reviewed manuscripts, historical text, and ancestral research materials highlighting indigenous agricultural preservation.

Smart Buds Biology

Step up to real-time high-definition digital microscope feeds. Inspect glandular trichome development and cellular plant defense mechanisms.

Cannabis Academy

Engage with certified master growers, agronomists, and laboratory directors in deep-dive masterclasses covering regenerative living soil loops.

Interactive Terpene Profile Matrix

Myrcene Earthy

The most abundant terpene in the plant world. Notes of clove and dark soil. Known synergy properties for cellular receptor optimization.

Limonene Citrus

Bright, highly uplifting rind extracts. Acts as a natural adaptogen, encouraging stress relief and mental mental clarity during deep reasoning circles.

Caryophyllene Spicy

Distinct notes of cracked black pepper and wood. Uniquely binds directly to regulatory pathways, operating effectively as a protective dietary cannabinoid.

Pinene Pine

Fresh forest atmosphere compounds. Scientifically recognized for promoting bronchodilation and supporting alert memory retention.
