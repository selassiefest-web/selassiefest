Higher Learning Roots

The living classroom of SelassieFest — where plant science, consumption craftsmanship, history, and community converge. From terpene exploration to the sacred chalice, immerse yourself in hands-on education rooted in respect and curiosity.

Saturday, July 25th, 2026 10:00 AM – 9:00 PM Higher Learning Roots Village 21+ for consumption areas
EXPLORE THE VILLAGE

Eight immersive experiences

From rolling workshops to genetics labs, each space is designed to deepen your relationship with the plant — honoring both science and spirit.

Bluntly Speaking
Full-day craft workshop dedicated to the art of rolling: joints, blunts, fronto leaf, chalice ceremony, bong science, and circle etiquette. Roll with intention.
Explore craft
Budding Minds
Youth & family cannabis science education — plant biology, responsible use dialogues, and the foundation of the modern plant movement.
Discover learning
Cannabis Academy
Seed-to-shelf professional track: cultivation science, compliance, business leadership, and hemp horticulture. Certificates available.
Enroll now
Farm to Dispensary
Supply chain transparency: harvest, processing, packaging, ethics, and sustainability. Meet growers and follow the plant’s journey.
Trace the journey
High Q Lounge
Elevated consumption lounge + curated vibes — relax, reason, and network. Premium airflow, music, and elevated social atmosphere.
Step inside
Leaf Library
Curated collection of cannabis literature, historical texts, scientific papers, rare magazines, and interactive knowledge stations.
Browse collection
Smart Buds
Biology lab with microscopes — observe trichomes, study cannabinoid biosynthesis, and understand cannabis genetics up close.
Go to lab
Terpene Discovery Bar
Smell & flavor education — explore isolated terpenes, understand the entourage effect, and train your palate for full-spectrum appreciation.
Discover aromas
One Love, One Curriculum — All workshops, labs, and discovery bars are included with your SelassieFest admission. Some advanced sessions require sign-up at the village entrance. 21+ ID required for consumption areas.
