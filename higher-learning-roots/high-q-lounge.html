Spliff Society is bringing the heat to SelassieFest with a full day of rolling workshops, product education, and community vibes. We're all about premium, tobacco-free, plant-based products — and we're here to share the love.

The Schedule

Our Full Day at SelassieFest

From 10 AM to 9 PM, Spliff Society leads the Higher Learning Roots village through a complete curriculum of smoke craft, culture, and community. All times Central. All sessions are participatory and open to all 21+ attendees.

Morning
10:00 AM – 12:00 PM

60 min
Welcome & Product Showcase — The Spliff Society Way
Briggs Williams & Team · Main Stage

Kick off the day with Briggs Williams, founder of Spliff Society, as he introduces the brand's mission: elevating the canna-experience with premium, tobacco-free, plant-based products[reference:0]. Get an up-close look at our 100% all-natural palm leaf wraps, botanical cones, rolling papers, and storage solutions[reference:1][reference:2]. Learn what sets Spliff Society apart — and why our products are taking the market by storm.

Product Showcase All Welcome Open Format

Rolling 101 — Palm Leaf Wraps & Botanical Cones
Spliff Society Rolling Team · Rolling Room A

Get hands-on with our signature palm leaf wraps and botanical cones[reference:3]. Learn the art of rolling with 100% all-natural, tobacco-free materials. We'll cover everything from selecting the right wrap to packing, sealing, and finishing your perfect spliff. Whether you're a beginner or a seasoned roller, you'll leave with new skills and a deeper appreciation for the craft.

Hands-On Beginner Friendly All Levels Welcome

The Science of Slow Burn — Why Our Products Stand Out
Spliff Society Product Specialist · Leaf Library

Dive deep into the science behind our slow-burning, plant-based products[reference:4]. We'll explore the anatomy of a palm leaf wrap, the benefits of tobacco-free smoking, and how our botanical cones are handmade by professional artisans[reference:5]. Learn why proper storage — with our glass canisters — is essential for keeping your flower fresh and humidified[reference:6].

Spliff Society Advanced Team · Rolling Room A

Take your rolling to the next level. This advanced workshop covers cone rolling, cannagar construction, and creative techniques that will blow your mind. Prerequisite: basic rolling experience. Space is limited — sign up at the door.

Flavor & Aroma Workshop — Train Your Palate
Spliff Society Flavor Specialist · Leaf Library

Discover the nuances of flavor and aroma in our botanical cones[reference:7]. From Rose to Lotus, Blueberry to Mango, Goji Berry to Cocoa — we'll guide you through a sensory journey[reference:8]. Learn how to identify terpenes, understand flavor profiles, and elevate your smoking experience.

Grinder 101 — The Must-Have Companion
Spliff Society Product Specialist · Rolling Room B

Your grinder is the most important tool in your kit[reference:9]. We'll break down the anatomy of a quality grinder, how to choose the right one for your needs, and tips for maintaining it. Learn why a good grind makes all the difference in your smoking experience.

Grinder Tool Talk Hands-On
5:00 PM
60 min
Circle Etiquette — The Culture of Passing
Spliff Society Elders · Open Stage

Nobody teaches this — and everybody needs it. We lead a candid, funny, and deeply informative session on the unwritten laws of the smoke circle: puff-puff-pass and why it exists, how long is too long, never blowing smoke in anyone's face, the correct direction of the pass, who lights, who holds the conversation, and how the chalice circle differs fundamentally in protocol.

Circle Culture Etiquette Community Session Open Format
6:00 PM
60 min
The Roll-Off — Community Rolling Competition
Judged by Spliff Society Instructors · Open Stage

At the end of a full day of learning, we find out who has leveled up. The Roll-Off is our community rolling competition — open to all attendees. Categories: Best Blunt, Best Joint, Best Creative Roll. Judging criteria: structural integrity, evenness of pack, quality of seal, aesthetic beauty, and how well it burns. Prizes courtesy of Spliff Society.

Competition Community Prizes Awarded All Welcome

Chalice Evening Ceremony — Communal Reasoning Circle
Spliff Society Elders · Chalice Ceremony Space

As the sky darkens, we gather for our most sacred session: the evening chalice ceremony. In the Rastafari tradition, this is not recreation — it is reasoning; a communal seeking of truth and guidance through the holy herb. Words are spoken. The chalice passes. Those who do not participate in consumption are equally welcomed to sit, listen, and be present.

Chalice Ceremony Reasoning Circle Sacred Space All Welcome

Full Spliff Society Team · Open Stage

The day ends where all good things end — with community. Certificates of participation for all who attended three or more sessions. The Roll-Off winners are recognized. Instructors share final words. The village doesn't close — it sends you home with new hands and new knowledge.
