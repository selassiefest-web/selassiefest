The recommended internal pathway

The core internal CPD pathway this matrix is built around:

Park Supervisor / Area Manager + LCA
→ Planning & Development + Law + Operations (South Region)
→ Chief Operating Officer / executive review
→ General Superintendent
→ appropriate Board committee route
→ Board action, if legally required

Running alongside that internal pathway, not substituting for it: Alderman Yancy, community stakeholders, and civic allies (Friends of the Parks, South Shore Cultural Center Advisory Council) build the external support lane. The Mayor's Office and the City Council committee are held in reserve for escalation only.

One exclusion worth stating explicitly

The PAC Governance Committee is not treated as a decision-maker for BBPAC's formation. CPD's published materials describe that committee principally in connection with PAC complaints and Code of Conduct disputes — formation questions route through the Park Supervisor, Area Manager, and LCA instead.
