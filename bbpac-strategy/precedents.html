Precedent Library

Every precedent used anywhere in this system, in one place, with a verification status. Every department, decision-maker and partner deck links into this page rather than repeating the text — so a fact only has to be corrected once.

Purpose: answer "has CPD ever done anything like this before?" without overclaiming.
Rule followed throughout: a precedent is labeled by what it actually proves, not by what we wish it proved.

Read this before citing anything below to an outside audience

This library was assembled from a prior research pass, not from certified primary-source production. Two ratings appear on every item: a star rating (how strong the analogy is) and a verification status (how confirmed the underlying fact is). Items marked "Partially confirmed" or "Pending verification" should not be presented to CPD, the Board, or an Alderman as settled fact — they are exactly the items the FOIA/records campaign (see the Evidence & Verification Status page) exists to close out.

How to use this library from another deck

Link directly to an item's anchor, e.g. ../precedents.html#lane-beach from a department deck, or ../precedents.html#paper-trail when referencing the open Park-Number investigation. Do not paste precedent paragraphs into other decks — link instead, so an edit here updates everywhere automatically.
