Presentation Architecture — Bongo Beach PAC Recognition Campaign

What this system is, in one line

One master case, broken into tailored presentations for every audience with a role in recognizing BBPAC — each version showing that audience only what they need, asking only for what they can actually grant. Ask, ultimately: a written CPD administrative determination that 63rd Street Beach is eligible for a distinct park identity / administrative unit enabling dedicated PAC representation, followed by Board action if required. Why this architecture: different people own different pieces of the decision — nobody should receive content irrelevant to what they can approve.

Read before sending anything to anyone outside the organizing committee

This system was built from a prior research and strategy conversation, not from certified legal or historical production. Every page in this system carries a verification status on its factual claims. Before any deck leaves this organizing committee's hands — especially the CPD, Board, or Alderman decks — route it through the Evidence & Verification Status page and, for anything Law-adjacent, an actual attorney. See Sources & Methodology for the full explanation of what is confirmed, what is analysis, and what is still open.

The three questions hiding inside this campaign

Everything in this system is organized around three distinct government questions, because different offices own each one:

  1. Classification — Can the 63rd Street Beach / Beach House complex be recognized as a distinct park or administrative park unit? (Owned by Planning & Development, with Law weighing in on the mechanism.)
  2. Governance — What CPD action is required to accomplish that — a staff determination, Superintendent action, Board resolution, policy amendment, or some combination? (Owned by Law, the General Superintendent, and the Board.)
  3. PAC recognition — Once the site is separately eligible, how is BBPAC formally created and registered? (Owned by LCA, using CPD's ordinary published PAC-formation process.)

Question 3 is the easy part once Questions 1 and 2 are resolved — CPD's ordinary PAC-formation process is well documented and mostly procedural. The entire strategic effort of this system is aimed at Questions 1 and 2, which is why they are called Gate 0 throughout.

How to keep this system internally consistent

Every fact, precedent, name, and figure in this system should trace back to the Global Master Presentation or the Precedent Library — never re-derived independently inside a department or decision-maker deck. If you need to correct a fact, correct it at the source page and every deck that links to it stays correct. If you're implementing this as a live site (see the handoff memo delivered alongside this system), preserve that link-don't-duplicate discipline; it is what keeps 24 pages from quietly contradicting each other six months from now.
