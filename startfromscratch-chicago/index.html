The teaching fits what you already teach

Start From Scratch workshops cover scratching, production and DJing. Our 14 Missions cover the DJing core, plus the live-performance skills students use at showcases.

Our 14 Missions (42 skills)

  • Meet the Machine, Build Your Crate
  • Beats, Bars and Phrases, Mix on the One
  • Tempo, Beatmatch by Ear, The Three Knobs (EQ)
  • Make a Third Song, Hot Cues and Loops
  • Word Flip, Theme Clash Lab, Mic, MC and Live Lab
  • Build Your Set, Rainbow Showcase

Where AlphaTheta would shape it

  • Software: we would teach on Rekordbox, your own software, so every student learns on AlphaTheta end to end
  • Scratching: a guest-DJ topic or workshop block, in the style of your scratching workshops
  • Production: an optional short block, as in your production workshops
  • Your standards: we adopt your framework and wording wherever you prefer

Our class is slower and longer than a short course: students work through the 14 Missions at their own pace, in 2-hour classes, with about 7 hours of hands-on deck time in all.

Questions you may have

What would AlphaTheta have to do?

Say which size of yes you prefer, approve the wording, and send or loan the gear. We run everything else and report back to you.

Does AlphaTheta have to give money?

No cash is requested. We are raising the operating budget (instructors, insurance, showcases, licensing and safety) through grants and donations.

Who is responsible for the children and the room?

We are. Enrollment, background checks, insurance, safety and day-to-day operations are ours and are in our operating budget. Photos and video are shared only with family consent.

Who keeps the equipment?

It stays with the program for future cohorts, in secure storage. Loaned or demonstration equipment is welcome, and you can name the stations.

Can AlphaTheta control the brand?

Yes. You approve how the Start From Scratch name and your marks are used, and review materials before they go public.

Has Start From Scratch run in the US before?

We could not find a US host on the program's pages, but we cannot rule one out. If one exists, we would welcome an introduction to it. If not, Chicago could be the first, subject to your confirmation.

What if the answer is no?

We open on January 12 with the plan we have, and we part as friends. A short "not now" is a welcome reply.

AlphaTheta launches Start From Scratch in Chicago

AlphaTheta is bringing its Start From Scratch program to Chicago, the birthplace of house music, in partnership with local host Ras Tafari Inc. Beginning January 12, 2027, students in grades 6 to 12 (all ages welcome) can learn to DJ for free every Tuesday, year-round, on Chicago's South Side, with a girls-only track taught by women DJs, accommodations for neurodiverse students and public showcases. Start From Scratch has trained thousands of new DJs from underrepresented communities across Europe, the Middle East and Africa. We propose Chicago as a US host city for Start From Scratch, with the opportunity to become its first, subject to AlphaTheta's confirmation.

Items in this draft that need AlphaTheta's confirmation: the Start From Scratch name for Chicago, whether Chicago would be a US first (we found no earlier US host on the program's pages, but cannot rule one out), and any equipment or Accelerator commitments.
