House music was born in Chicago's clubs and warehouses. A free, community-led DJ program on the city's South Side, led by AlphaTheta, would connect Start From Scratch's mission to the place where the culture it teaches began. Source for the figures above: AlphaTheta's Start From Scratch page.

You lead. We host.

The way Start From Scratch already works with local partners abroad: the brand leads and a local host makes it real. We would be the Chicago host and follow your direction.

Already in place

This is not an idea waiting for a sponsor. The class is planned and scheduled, with the plan written down.

  • Opens January 12, 2027. Every Tuesday, 4 to 6 pm, year-round (39 class Tuesdays in 2027)
  • Free to students and families
  • Grades 6 to 12, open to all ages
  • 12 student stations, so every learner has their own gear during instruction
  • 14 Missions, 42 skills, worked at each student's own pace, finishing with a final set
  • Two resident instructors, paid per session, plus a weekly guest woman DJ
  • Empress Decks, a girls-only track taught by women DJs
  • Accommodations for neurodiverse and sensory-sensitive students: volume-limited headphones, free earplugs, a quieter workspace and visual step guides
  • Public showcases where students perform what they make
  • Written plan and budget, ready to share

AlphaTheta launches Start From Scratch in Chicago

AlphaTheta is bringing its Start From Scratch program to Chicago, the birthplace of house music, in partnership with local host Ras Tafari Inc. Beginning January 12, 2027, students in grades 6 to 12 (all ages welcome) can learn to DJ for free every Tuesday, year-round, on Chicago's South Side, with a girls-only track taught by women DJs, accommodations for neurodiverse students and public showcases. Start From Scratch has trained thousands of new DJs from underrepresented communities across Europe, the Middle East and Africa. Chicago will be its first US host city.

Items in this draft that need AlphaTheta's confirmation: the Start From Scratch name for Chicago, "first US host city," and any equipment or Accelerator commitments.
