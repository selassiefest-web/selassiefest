Our Mission, Vision & Values

SelassieFest Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization dedicated to cultural preservation, education, and community empowerment through the celebration of Ethiopian and Rastafari heritage.

“To honor the living legacy of Emperor Haile Selassie I by nurturing Pan‑African unity, preserving Rastafari traditions, and building a just, educated, and spiritually rooted community through festival, education, and service.”

We achieve this through an annual cultural festival, year‑round educational programming, youth mentorship, Ital food initiatives, and support for Ethiopian and Caribbean diaspora communities in Chicago and beyond.

Vision Statement

A world where African and diasporic heritage is celebrated, where Rastafari principles of love, equity, and natural living guide community life, and where every generation knows the name and teachings of His Imperial Majesty Haile Selassie I as a beacon of African redemption.

Core Values

Unity & Community

We build bridges across generations, cultures, and faiths. SelassieFest is a family‑friendly, inclusive space where all are welcome to share in the spirit of “One Love, One Destiny.”

Cultural Education

We teach the true history of Ethiopia, the Rastafari movement, Pan‑Africanism, and Chicago’s unique role in these global struggles. Knowledge is liberation.

Tradition & Sacred Practice

We preserve Nyabinghi drumming, reasoning circles, Ital cooking, and the liturgical calendar of Rastafari — ensuring these living traditions are passed to the next generation.

Health & Ital Living

We promote natural, plant‑based (Ital) nutrition, holistic wellness, and environmental stewardship as acts of reverence for the earth and the body as the temple of Jah.

Youth Empowerment

Through the Youth Village, scholarships, and mentorship programs, we invest in young leaders who will carry the message of African pride and social justice forward.

Pan‑African Solidarity

We stand with African peoples worldwide, supporting reparatory justice, cultural exchange, and economic cooperation within the diaspora.

As a 501(c)(3) nonprofit, every donation, ticket purchase, and volunteer hour helps sustain our work. Join us in building a legacy of love, learning, and liberation.
