Celebrating Legacy, Unity, and African Heritage

SelassieFest is more than a festival — it is a living tribute to the enduring spirit of Emperor Haile Selassie I, the deep roots of Pan-African solidarity in Chicago, and the global resonance of Rastafari culture. Each year, we gather to honor history through music, drumming, education, community, and celebration.

One Love, One Destiny — Rooted in Ethiopian & Jamaican Heritage

The History

Discover Chicago's deep Ethiopianist roots — from the 1919 Abyssinian Mission sent by Ras Tafari to the early expressions of Pan-African identity that laid the foundation for today's festival.

Mission & Values

We are a community-rooted collective dedicated to preserving African and diasporic heritage, uplifting Rastafari traditions, and creating a space for cultural education, healing, and unity.
