Leadership

Stephen Henry
 President

Founder and driving force behind Ras Tafari Inc and SelassieFest. Stephen leads the organization's vision, community partnerships, and mission to celebrate Ethiopian and Jamaican heritage in Chicago.

Bobby Brown
 Vice President

Bobby supports the President in strategic planning, board governance, and festival operations. He chairs special committees and ensures the organization's community commitments are met with integrity and care.

Nataki
 Executive Director

Visionary leader, cultural preservationist, and custodian of Rastafari heritage. Nataki drives the mission of Ras Tafari Inc with decades of dedication to community empowerment, ancestral reverence, and Pan-African solidarity.

Affiliates

Organizations and community partners who share our roots, our values, and our commitment to Chicago's Jamaican and African diaspora community.

 Health & Cultural Organization
Jamaicans4Life
Chicago Chapter of Jamaicans4Life — working internationally and in the Midwest to address multiple health disparities within the Jamaican diaspora community. Donations accepted via Zelle at jamaicans4life@gmail.com.
jamaican4life.org
 Diaspora Nonprofit
MIJAC — Midwest Jamaica Corps
A nonprofit organization based in Chicago, IL dedicated to supporting and empowering the Jamaican diaspora across the Midwest. Empowering the Jamaican Community — one connection at a time.
mijac.org
 Pan-African Project
Jamaica Village Ghana
A Pan-African development initiative building cultural and economic bridges between the Jamaican diaspora and Ghana — connecting the dots of the African diaspora across continents.
Learn more
 Business & Economic
Midwest Caribbean American Chamber of Commerce
Supporting Caribbean American entrepreneurs and business owners throughout the Midwest, promoting economic development and cultural commerce within the diaspora community.
 Cultural Center
Caribbean Cultural Center Chicago
Celebrating the rich traditions of Caribbean music, arts, cuisine, and heritage in Chicago through festivals, workshops, and community programming year-round.
 Community Association
Chicago Jamaican Association
One of Chicago's longest-standing Jamaican diaspora organizations, providing a home for Jamaican Americans through cultural events, social support, and community advocacy.
 Housing & Reentry
2nd Chance Housing Inc
A nonprofit dedicated to providing transitional and supportive housing for individuals re-entering society. 2nd Chance Housing believes in the dignity of every person and the power of a stable home as the foundation for a new chapter.
