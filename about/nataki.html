NATAKI
Executive Director · Ras Tafari Inc.
Custodian of Heritage · SelassieFest Visionary

✧ Portraits of Service ✧

Nataki is a visionary leader, cultural preservationist, and the Executive Director of Ras Tafari Inc., an organization dedicated to upholding the principles of His Imperial Majesty Haile Selassie I, Ethiopian heritage, and the global Rastafari movement. With decades of dedication to community empowerment, ancestral reverence, and pan-African solidarity, she has become a cornerstone of the SelassieFest experience and a guiding force for roots revival in Chicago and beyond.

Under her stewardship, Ras Tafari Inc. has expanded educational initiatives, interfaith dialogues, and cultural festivals that bridge the wisdom of elders with the energy of youth. Nataki’s leadership is defined by Nyahbinghi principles, social justice advocacy, and an unwavering commitment to Ital living, sustainable agriculture, and cannabis as a sacred sacrament.

“Our work is not merely an event — it is a movement of restoration. Through SelassieFest we honor the legacy of H.I.M., celebrate our roots, and plant seeds for the seventh generation. One love, one destiny.”
— Nataki, Executive Director, Ras Tafari Inc.
✦ Pillars of Impact

Ras Tafari Inc. Leadership

Architect of community programs, cultural symposiums, and legal advocacy for Rastafari rights. Instrumental in establishing the Ethiopian Heritage Grounds as a sacred space for reasoning and worship.

Higher Learning Roots

Creator of the Cannabis Education Village & “From Farm to Dispensary” curriculum, bridging ancestral botanical knowledge with modern wellness and restorative justice.

Nyabinghi Groundation

Pan-African Bridge

Forged partnerships with Ethiopian Orthodox leaders, Jamaican sound system pioneers, and African diaspora scholars, making SelassieFest a global intersection of roots & culture.

A Living Legacy: SelassieFest & Beyond

As Executive Director of Ras Tafari Inc., Nataki has transformed SelassieFest from a small community gathering into a renowned one‑day roots reggae and cultural festival. Her holistic approach weaves together Sound System Yard, Ital Food Marketplace, Healing Grove, and the Reasoning Circle — ensuring that every attendee experiences restoration, education, and joyful resistance. Under her guidance, the festival also features the Youth & Family Village and the Ethiopian Heritage Village, making intergenerational learning a cornerstone of the event.

Nataki’s dedication extends beyond festival days: year‑round she leads herb medicine workshops, restorative justice circles, and cultural exchanges that uplift the South Side Chicago community and the global Rastafari family. Her work is a testament to the living spirit of His Imperial Majesty — “Peace, Love, and Unity for all humanity.”
