Subject: Direct export partnership: Jamaican grocery opening in Chicago 

Dear Export Sales Manager,

We are opening Yawd Dry Goods, a Jamaican grocery and distribution business in Chicago, Illinois. We plan to buy directly from Jamaican manufacturers, farmers and agro-processors rather than through U.S. distributors, and to supply our store, home-delivery customers and Chicago's Caribbean restaurants.

We would like to carry your products. Could you send us:

- Your current export catalogue and wholesale price list
- Minimum order quantities and case or pallet sizes
- FOB pricing from Kingston or Montego Bay
- Your experience shipping to the United States, including your FDA facility registration
- Payment terms, production lead times and preferred freight forwarders

If someone else handles international sales, we would be grateful for an introduction.

We look forward to working with you.
