How it works

The short road home.

Most Jamaican goods on Chicago shelves pass through an importer, then a distributor, then a wholesaler before they reach you, and each one adds a markup. We go to the source instead.

  1. 01

    Made in Jamaica

    We buy straight from Jamaican makers, from household names to small family producers, found through the island's own export and manufacturers' networks and visited in person.

  2. 02

    Shipped by sea

    Goods leave Kingston and Montego Bay by container for the Midwest. We import them ourselves, with FDA and customs paperwork handled properly.

  3. 03

    On the shelf in Chicago

    From our warehouse to our aisles, your doorstep, or the walk-in at your restaurant. One trip, with no extra hands in between.

“

Fewer stops between the factory and the shelf means fairer prices, fresher stock, and things you can't find anywhere else in the city.

Questions

Good to know.

When do you open?

We're lining up suppliers and a location now. People on the early-access list will hear the opening date first, along with any soft-opening invitations.

Where will the store be?

In Chicago. We're looking at neighborhoods with strong Caribbean and African communities, and we'll announce the address to the list before anyone else.

Do you deliver outside Chicago?

We're starting with home delivery across the Chicago area. Shipping farther out is on the roadmap. Add your ZIP code when you sign up so we know where the demand is.

Can my restaurant buy wholesale?

Yes. Restaurant, catering and bakery accounts are a core part of what we're building: case and pallet pricing, scheduled delivery, and custom sourcing. Choose "My restaurant or kitchen" on the form and we'll reach out before we open.

Is everything really from Jamaica?

That's the whole idea. We buy Jamaican-made goods from the people who make them, without going through a U.S. distributor. Every supplier is checked for FDA registration and export readiness before we place an order.

I make a product in Jamaica. Can you carry it?

We'd love to talk. Choose "I'm a Jamaican maker" on the form and tell us what you make. Export-ready producers with FDA-registered facilities are the easiest place to start, but reach out even if you're not there yet.
