Before you start

Have your paperwork ready

Goodstack, Google, Microsoft, Canva and TechSoup all ask for the same information. Type the nonprofit's name exactly as it appears on your IRS letter, with the same spelling, punctuation and "Inc." Most delays come from a name that doesn't match. Tick each item once it's on hand.

Make sure the IRS lists you as a 501(c)(3)
  1. Click Search the IRS list below.
  2. Type your EIN (the 9-digit number on your IRS letter) and click Search.
  3. Make sure your nonprofit shows up under Publication 78 and that the name matches your letter.

Goodstack uses this IRS list. If you're not on it yet (common in the first few months after approval), tell Stephen before you apply anywhere. You can still apply by uploading your IRS determination letter.

Part 1

Web address on Cloudflare & a first info@ email

Step 1 buys the web address from Cloudflare for 10 years in one payment. Cloudflare sells domains at cost with no add-on sales, and the same free account controls where the address points, adds the secure padlock, and protects the site from attacks. Over 10 years it costs about $99, compared with about $226 at GoDaddy. Step 2 gets info@thisisusandwhatwedo.org working right away by forwarding it to the nonprofit's Gmail. That address helps prove to Goodstack that you belong to the organization.

Part 2

Prove you're a nonprofit, then get free email

Goodstack verifies nonprofits for Google, Canva and many other companies. You only need to pass it once. After that, both Google and Microsoft give verified nonprofits email at your own domain for free. Step 5 helps you pick one. Never buy Microsoft 365 or email through GoDaddy or another reseller. You'd pay roughly $240 over 3 years for something your nonprofit gets free, and GoDaddy-managed Microsoft accounts don't qualify for Microsoft's nonprofit grant.

Nonprofit verification is always free. Goodstack, Google, Canva and Microsoft never charge to verify you. Ignore any email asking you to pay to "verify" or "activate" your nonprofit. Goodstack's real emails come from verification@mail.goodstack.org.
Part 3

Accepting donations

None of these has a monthly fee. Zeffy and Givebutter ask donors to add an optional tip, so the nonprofit can keep 100% of the gift. The others take a small cut of each gift at their nonprofit rate. Type in a typical gift to compare.
