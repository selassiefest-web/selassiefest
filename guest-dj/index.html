Rainbow DJ Lab · Guest instructor invitation

Teach one Tuesday at Rainbow Beach

Ras Tafari Inc. opens the Rainbow DJ Lab in January 2027: free, year-round DJ classes for South Side kids in the Rainbow Beach Park fieldhouse. Each class week, one Chicago woman DJ comes in as our guest instructor for the last half hour. We would be honored if one of those Tuesdays were yours.

The room and the details

  • Where: Rainbow Beach Park fieldhouse game room, 3111 E. 77th St., Chicago, IL 60649 · Park District page · Map
  • When: any class Tuesday in 2027. Class runs 4:00–6:00 pm; your session is 5:30–6:00. See all 39 dates
  • Who: up to 12 middle- and high-school students, each on their own DJ controller. Two resident DJ instructors, one man and one woman, are in the room the whole time.
  • What we handle: gear set up and tested, a one-page briefing on that day’s lesson and the students’ levels, snacks, and the Park District background-check paperwork every adult working with youth completes.
  • House rules: clean lyrics only, capped headphone volume, and nobody is ever made to perform.
  • Anticipated pay: $200 for your visit. With your permission, we’ll also feature you with a short interview or photo.
