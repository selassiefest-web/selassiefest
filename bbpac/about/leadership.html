Leadership & Governance

Board, Meetings & Strategic Plan

Where we are: the Chicago Park District recognizes one registered Park Advisory Council per park, and the Jackson Park Advisory Council (JPAC) already holds that role for Jackson Park. This council is organizing as a Ras Tafari Inc. community initiative focused specifically on 63rd Street Beach — working in partnership with JPAC, not in competition with it, toward a role and recognition that reflects that partnership.

This initiative is organized by Ras Tafari Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit and the organization behind SelassieFest. Its board leads the effort while a community steering committee takes shape.

Our Strategic Plan

Rather than launch everything at once, our plan follows the same advice a longtime community member gave us when this council started taking shape: start with a few programs, build a regular rhythm, and grow toward one major annual signature event.

Phase 1

Start Small, Start Regular

Pick a handful of programs from our proposed events — likely beginning with a monthly drum circle and a sunrise wellness gathering — and run them on a predictable schedule.

Phase 2

Build the Rhythm

Layer in stewardship days, youth programming, and vendor opportunities as volunteer capacity and community interest grow.

Phase 3

One Signature Annual Event

Work toward a single flagship Bongo Beach event each year — a homecoming that honors fifty-plus years of history and draws the whole city to the beach.

Meetings

Meetings will be open to the public, as with every Chicago Park Advisory Council. We're proposing a monthly cadence, coordinated with JPAC's own meeting schedule — exact date, time, and location to be announced. Leave your email below and we'll notify you as soon as it's set, and post minutes and our strategic plan here once meetings begin.
