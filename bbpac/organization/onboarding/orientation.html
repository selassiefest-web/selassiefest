STEP 4 / 6The connection

Why both move together

CPD generally recognizes one council per park, and Jackson Park already has one. If Track A succeeds, Track B's path opens up — so neither waits on the other.

Track B's own Phase 0 depends partly on how Track A resolves — CPD's guidelines recognize only one registered PAC per park, and Jackson Park already has one (JPAC). If Track A succeeds, Track B's path gets much more straightforward. That's why both tracks keep building in parallel instead of waiting on one to finish first: nobody knows yet which piece resolves first, and neither track's paperwork is wasted if it doesn't.

The team's working motto“Proper Planning and Preparation Prevents Piss Poor Performance” — when we do reach out, we go with 100% confidence, having left no stone unturned on our end.

The Matrix is closer to a decision tree than a flat checklist. Each item is already sorted into one of a few real states: something we can answer ourselves from precedent (self-resolved), something that genuinely needs an outside agency's answer (externally routed), or something where nobody has identified the right contact yet (unknown). The real job is clearing everything answerable on our own side before anything gets escalated outward.
