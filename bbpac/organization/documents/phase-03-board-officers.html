3-01

Officer Position Descriptions

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

Bylaws Article 7 names the four required offices and their core duties in outline; this document expands each into a working position description a candidate can actually read before deciding to run.

President. Presides at all general membership, board, and executive committee meetings; serves as the Council's primary point of contact with the Chicago Park District (Park Supervisor/Area Manager) and as its public representative; signs contracts, checks, and filings the Board has authorized; is an ex officio, non-voting member of every standing committee except the Nominating Committee; ensures the Annual Board Calendar (3-16) is followed. Minimum expectations: attend at least 75% of regular meetings; complete CPD Volunteer Application and background check within the required post-election window (see 1-27); complete new-officer orientation (3-11) within 30 days of taking office.

Vice President. Assumes the President's duties in their absence, incapacity, or resignation; ordinarily chairs the Governance Committee; leads at least one standing initiative assigned by the Board each term so the role carries real substance beyond succession-in-waiting.

Treasurer. Has custody of and accounts for all Council funds; maintains the books required under Bylaws Article 17 and the Financial Policies & Internal Controls Manual (Phase 4); presents a Treasurer's Report at every regular meeting; coordinates the annual budget, bank reconciliations, and any required state/federal/CPD financial filings; is the primary bank-account signatory alongside at least one other authorized signer (never a sole signatory, per the Authorized Signer Register in Phase 4).

Secretary. Gives all required meeting notices; records, drafts, and circulates minutes for approval; maintains the PAC Binder and Corporate Records Book (Phase 5); processes CPD Officer Update filings (3-08) whenever officers change; is the default custodian of this Organization document library's version history.

Each description closes with a standard succession clause: "If this office becomes vacant, duties transfer per Bylaws Article 11 and the Vacancy Appointment / Special Election Packet (3-25) until a successor is elected or appointed."

3-02

Corporate Director Position Description

IL-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee / Counsel

Under Bylaws Article 8, the elected officers (plus any additional elected directors) constitute the statutory Board of Directors of the Illinois not-for-profit corporation once incorporated. This description exists because "PAC officer" and "Illinois corporate director" are not automatically the same role in every reader's mind, even though this Council structures them as the same people.

A director's duties are fiduciary, not merely administrative: the duty of care (exercise the diligence an ordinarily prudent person would in a like position), the duty of loyalty (act in the Council's interest, not a personal or third party's), and the duty of obedience (act within the Articles of Incorporation, Bylaws, and applicable law). Directors approve the annual budget, authorize contracts above the Delegation of Authority Matrix's officer-approval threshold (Phase 2, document 2-36), and are legally accountable for the corporation's compliance filings even when a specific officer executed them.

Directors who are not also PAC officers (if the membership ever elects additional at-large directors) hold the same fiduciary duties without the day-to-day operational duties assigned to officers under 3-01, and are expected to attend board meetings, review financial reports, and disclose conflicts on the same schedule as officers (3-12).

3-09

Officer Background-Check Compliance Log

CPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Volunteer Coordinator

This is the same confidential status log described in Phase 1 (document 1-28), referenced here because it is fundamentally a board-operations record: it is what the President and Governance Committee check before letting an elected officer sign anything, chair a committee, or handle members' personal data. Tracks submission and clearance dates only — never the underlying background-check report contents — and flags any officer whose clearance has lapsed for immediate Governance Committee attention.

3-10

Board Orientation Manual

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

A standing reference manual, not a one-time onboarding email, so a new officer's first 90 days are guided rather than improvised. Planned sections:

  • Who we are and aren't: the Council's advisory-only relationship to CPD (Bylaws Article 3), the Gate 0 status, and the "Do Not Cross" boundaries published on the Organization overview page.
  • Governance basics: Bylaws structure, quorum/voting rules, committee structure, the Delegation of Authority Matrix.
  • Illinois nonprofit duties: fiduciary duty summary, conflict-of-interest process, why the corporation and the PAC's elected offices are legally the same body.
  • Finance basics: how funds move, who can approve what, where the budget and Treasurer's Reports live.
  • Meetings: the annual calendar, notice rules, minutes process, and recording-consent rule.
  • Beach-specific issues: stewardship, water-safety liaison boundaries, and the Jackson Park Advisory Council coordination relationship.
  • Records: where the PAC Binder and Digital Governance Repository live, and who has access to what.

Reviewed and updated by the Governance Committee at least annually, and immediately after any bylaws amendment.

3-12

Annual Officer / Director Conflict Disclosure

IL-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

Completed by every officer/director at the start of each term and immediately upon any material change. Asks, at minimum: business relationships with prospective vendors, contractors, or sponsors of the Council; family relationships with other officers, staff, or vendors; any donor, grantor, or partner relationship that could create a real or perceived conflict; and any position (paid or volunteer) with an organization that could compete with or influence the Council's decisions. Reviewed by the Governance Committee; any disclosed conflict is logged and, where a specific vote or contract is affected, triggers a recusal recorded on 3-13.

3-14

Board / Officer Code Acknowledgment

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

A one-page signed acknowledgment that the officer/director has received, read, and agrees to follow both the current CPD PAC Code of Conduct (1-02) and the Council's Supplemental Member Code of Conduct (Phase 5, 5-25). 3-15

Confidentiality & Data Handling Acknowledgment

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Data Steward

Signed by anyone with access to membership applications, sign-in sheets, background-check status logs, or donor/financial records — not just officers. Commits the signer to: access this data only as needed for their role; never copy it to personal devices or unapproved cloud accounts; never share it outside the Council without authorization; and return or delete access immediately upon leaving their role (cross-referenced to the Digital Account & Credential Handover Record, 3-29).

3-16

Annual Board Calendar

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: President / Secretary

Adopted at the first board meeting of each term, laying out: the quarterly (minimum) regular meeting dates required by Bylaws Article 12; election timing; budget-adoption timing; the annual filing deadlines pulled from the Initial Annual Compliance Calendar (1-34) and the broader compliance dashboard on the Organization overview page; strategic-planning check-ins; and the annual policy/bylaw review cycle (Phase 2, document 2-40). One calendar, one place, rather than each officer tracking their own deadlines independently.

3-17

Board Meeting Attendance Register

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

Fields: Meeting Date · Officer/Director · Present / Absent / Excused · Running Attendance %

Maintained per meeting and rolled up quarterly; feeds the Board Self-Assessment (3-18) and, if the Bylaws set a good-standing attendance threshold, the Removal / Recall Due-Process Procedure (3-27) if a pattern of absence needs to be addressed formally rather than informally.

3-18

Board Self-Assessment

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

An annual, honestly-answered questionnaire covering: engagement (attendance, meeting preparation), compliance (are filings, background checks, and conflict disclosures actually current), strategy (is the Board spending time on the Council's real priorities or just administrative maintenance), finance (does every director actually understand the Treasurer's Reports they're approving), and succession (is there a credible next slate of candidates). Results are discussed at a board retreat or dedicated agenda item, not filed silently — the point is course-correction, not paperwork.

3-19

Board Recruitment & Succession Plan

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance / Nominating Committee

A living map of who the Council needs at the table versus who's actually there — deliberately checked against the beach's real constituencies: longtime drummers and cultural-tradition participants, dune/environmental stewards, accessibility advocates, youth and family program users, nearby residents, and small-business/vendor voices. Reviewed annually before the Nominating Committee begins candidate outreach (3-20), so recruitment is proactive rather than "whoever happens to volunteer."

3-20

Nominating Committee Charter

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

Establishes the Nominating Committee as an independent election administrator, not an extension of the sitting President's preferences. Charter contents: composition (members who are not themselves standing for re-election that cycle, where feasible); scope (candidate outreach per 3-19, eligibility review per 3-22, ballot preparation per 1-14, and administering the vote per 1-12); reporting (a written slate report to the full membership before the election meeting); and a sunset/renewal each election cycle rather than a standing permanent committee.

3-21

Election Manual

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance / Nominating Committee

Consolidates every election-related document elsewhere in this library into one step-by-step procedure a Nominating Committee member can follow without having to hunt across phases: candidate recruitment timeline → Election Notice issued with the required minimum 14-day lead time (1-11) → Nomination Record opened (1-13) → Candidate Eligibility Certification completed (3-22) → Campaign Conduct Rules distributed (3-23) → ballots prepared under the Ballot Custody / Security Procedure (3-24) → election meeting run per the Election Meeting Agenda (1-12), with a confirmed CPD representative present → results tallied, certified, and minuted (1-15, 1-16) → Officer Acceptance and Orientation begin (3-04, 3-11). Updated any time CPD's notice/attendance rules change.

3-22

Candidate Eligibility Certification

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Nominating Committee

Fields: Candidate Name · Office Sought · Good-Standing Membership Confirmed? (Y/N) · Any Bylaws-Specific Eligibility Criteria Met? (Y/N, list) · Acceptance of Nomination Signed? (Y/N) · Certified By / Date

Completed and certified before a candidate's name is placed on the Official Ballot (1-14) — resolving eligibility questions before an election, rather than after results are already announced, is what keeps a contested race from becoming a governance crisis.

3-23

Campaign Conduct Rules

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

Adopted before any contested election. Prohibits: using Council funds, the Council's email list, website, or social accounts for one candidate's campaign; using the confidential membership contact list (distinct from the public roster) for campaigning without opt-in consent; harassment or personal attacks on other candidates; and knowingly false statements about another candidate or about the Council's own affairs. Violations are referred to the Governance Committee, which can, at most, disqualify a candidate under the Candidate Eligibility Certification process (3-22) — it cannot itself declare an election result.

3-24

Ballot Custody / Security Procedure

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Nominating Committee

Covers the physical/digital chain of custody for ballots: printed/generated only by the Nominating Committee; stored securely until the election meeting; distributed only to confirmed eligible voters at the meeting (matched against the sign-in/eligibility list); collected into a sealed container; counted immediately after voting closes by at least two people who are not themselves candidates in that election, with a Ballot Tally Sheet completed on the spot (1-15); ties and runoffs resolved per the Bylaws; and all ballots retained for a fixed period (consistent with the Records Retention Schedule, Phase 5) in case results are challenged, then destroyed per the Document Destruction Log.

3-25

Vacancy Appointment / Special Election Packet

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Governance Committee

A ready-to-use packet for the two paths Bylaws Article 11 allows for a mid-term vacancy: Board appointment to fill the remainder of a term (short-form nomination and Board vote record), or a special election following the same notice/ballot/certification process as a regular election, scaled to the timeline the vacancy allows. Includes a decision worksheet for which path applies to a given vacancy under the adopted Bylaws.

3-26

Resignation Form / Acknowledgment

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

Fields: Name · Office · Effective Date of Resignation · Reason (optional) · Signature · Received By (Secretary, date)

A dated, written resignation is what actually triggers the CPD Officer Update filing (3-08), removal from bank signatory authority, and the credential handover (3-29) — an informal verbal resignation or a quiet stop in attendance should always be converted to this written record before treating the seat as vacant.

3-27

Removal / Recall Due-Process Procedure

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

Removal of an elected officer is serious and should never happen informally. Procedure: written notice of the specific grounds delivered to the officer with reasonable time to respond; an opportunity for the officer to be heard by the Board or membership (whichever the Bylaws assign the removal vote to); a vote meeting the threshold set in Bylaws Article 11; and, if removal is upheld, immediate escalation into the standard transition process (Officer Update Form 3-08, credential handover 3-29, vacancy packet 3-25). Any complaint underlying a removal action is also logged through the Complaint Intake, Response & Escalation Procedure (Phase 5) so there is one coherent record rather than two competing ones.

3-28

Officer Transition Checklist — By Office

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Outgoing / Incoming Officers

One checklist per office, because a Treasurer's handover (bank signatory changes, QuickBooks/ledger access, tax documents) looks nothing like a Secretary's handover (Binder custody, minutes archive, meeting-notice distribution list). Shared items across every office: digital credential transfer (3-29), CPD Officer Update filed (3-08), open action items transferred, outstanding contracts/vendor relationships flagged, and a joint sign-off from both outgoing and incoming officer confirming the handover is complete — not just started.

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

3-30

Key Volunteer / Committee Chair Succession Plan

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

Elected-officer succession is covered above; this document covers everyone else the Council actually depends on — committee chairs, the volunteer coordinator, a long-serving stewardship lead — whose sudden departure could stall real work even though no election is required to replace them. For each key non-elected role: who could step in on short notice, what institutional knowledge would need to transfer, and where that knowledge is documented (ideally, in the relevant Phase 6 Knowledge Transfer / Institutional Memory Manual rather than solely in that person's head).

3-31

Indemnification / D&O Insurance Review Memo

LEG-CBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Treasurer / Governance / Counsel

Reviews, at least annually and before any major fundraising campaign or contract: whether the Articles of Incorporation and/or Bylaws include a standard Illinois not-for-profit indemnification provision protecting directors/officers who act in good faith within their authority; whether directors-and-officers (D&O) liability insurance is warranted given the Council's actual activity level, fundraising, and risk profile (see the Insurance Register in Phase 4); and what, if anything, CPD's own volunteer/PAC insurance framework already covers versus what it does not. Findings and any coverage decision are documented here rather than assumed, since "we probably have some coverage through CPD" is not itself a risk-management plan.
