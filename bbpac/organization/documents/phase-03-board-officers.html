Phase 3 — Board & Officer Documents

Position descriptions, agreements, background-check tracking, orientation, elections administration and transition records for everyone who holds office.

Working document set — not legal advice, not a claim of recognition. Everything in this library is a governance/compliance draft prepared for the proposed 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council. Nothing here should be read as asserting that the Chicago Park District (CPD) has recognized a separate 63rd Street Beach PAC — see Phase 0: Pre-Formation Gate. Confirm current requirements directly with CPD and with qualified counsel before filing, submitting, or formally adopting anything drawn from this page.
This phase is fully catalogued below — title, classification, owner, purpose and status for all 31 documents — but full drafted text for each one hasn't been written yet. Phase 0, Phase 1, and Phase 2 have complete drafted text; this phase is queued for a later pass. Check back, or see the Organization overview for the publishing roadmap.
