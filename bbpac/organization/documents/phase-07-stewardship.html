Beach & Environmental Stewardship

Bylaws Articles 21-22 commit the Council to stewardship of the beach, dunes, and habitat, and to a water-safety liaison role that stops well short of directing CPD staff. Phase 7 is where those commitments become the actual plans, logs, and checklists volunteers use at the beach — and where the "Do Not Cross" boundaries stop being abstract warnings and become concrete procedure.

Working document, not legal advice. This page is part of a governance/compliance planning library for the proposed 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council. Nothing here should be read as confirming that CPD has recognized a separate 63rd Street Beach PAC — that threshold question, Gate 0, is still open. Every document is labeled Proposed for that reason. Nothing here authorizes any Council volunteer to perform natural-area work, issue safety advisories, or act in place of CPD staff without the specific CPD authorization each document describes.

Every document below is a working draft. None has been adopted or filed unless specifically noted.

7-01

63rd Street Beach Site & Asset Inventory

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Stewardship Committee / CPD Liaison

The comprehensive baseline: the beach and designated swim zone, the historic Beach House (restrooms, showers, drinking fountains, event/rental space), the accessible shoreline route and beach wheelchair access point, the CPD-named "Jackson Park - 63rd St. Beach Dunes" natural area (roughly 12 acres, CPD's own facility name for it), the non-motorized boat launch, the designated distance-swim area, adjacent paths and parking, and cultural/public-art features including the longstanding drumming gathering area. Created once the boundary/scope map (Phase 0, G0-02) is CPD-confirmed, since the inventory should describe exactly the site the Council is actually authorized to advise on — not a larger or smaller footprint. Every other stewardship document in this phase refers back to this inventory rather than re-describing the site from scratch.

7-02

CPD Beaches Rules & Safety Reference

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Safety Committee

The rule, not a summary of it. This reference holds the current CPD beaches rules (swimming hours, designated swim areas, flag system, general beach conduct rules) as CPD publishes them — a link plus an archived copy with a "last checked" date, never a Council-paraphrased version that could drift from what CPD actually says. Anyone drafting volunteer or public materials citing beach rules pulls from here, not from memory.
7-03

63rd Street Beach Facility Fact Sheet

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Safety

A one-page, plain-language fact sheet built from verified CPD sources (7-02, 7-05) and the site inventory (7-01): hours, season, accessibility features, CPD contact points, and facility list — the thing handed to a new volunteer or posted for the public that doesn't require reading the full CPD rules reference to get oriented. Reviewed seasonally, since hours and staffing can change year to year.

7-04

Beach Stewardship Plan

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Stewardship Committee

The annual plan tying this whole phase together: how many cleanups (7-15), what habitat objectives (7-08), what accessibility audits (7-31), what education efforts (7-25), and what CPD referrals are expected this season — set each spring, reported against each fall via the Annual Beach & Stewardship Condition Report (7-41), and feeding the Annual Work Plan (Phase 10, 10-02).

7-05

CPD Natural Areas Rules Reference

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Environmental Committee

The controlling rule set for the dunes/natural area — CPD's real current page title is "Natural Areas Rules and Regulations," not a generic "FAQ" page, and should be cited by that exact title — no unauthorized collection, planting, habitat disturbance, or fire — maintained the same way as 7-02: current CPD source, archived with a checked date, never a Council-invented substitute. Every dune/habitat document below (7-06 through 7-13) operates inside these rules, not around them.

7-06

CPD Community Stewardship Program Handbook / Policy Guide

CPD-CProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Environmental / Volunteer Leads

CPD's own program governing authorized volunteer stewardship in natural areas citywide, real and confirmed name "Community Stewardship Program (CSP)." Volunteers advance through the program via an apprenticeship track toward the real "Lead Stewards" tier, which CPD describes as the foundation of the program's success — worth naming as a concrete goal for any Council volunteer who wants to grow into a habitat-work leadership role, rather than a generic "senior volunteer" title we'd have to invent. The Council obtains and follows the current Handbook/Policy Guide before any dune/habitat volunteer work begins — this is the entry point into the authorization chain that 7-07 through 7-11 depend on.

7-07

Natural Area Stewardship Authorization / Site Agreement

CPD-CProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Environmental Committee / CPD Natural Areas

The written CPD authorization naming exactly what work is approved in the dunes, by whom, with what tools, after what training, and within what site boundaries. No habitat work of any kind proceeds without this document in hand for that specific work — it is the single gate the "do not disturb natural areas without authorization" boundary runs through.

7-08

63rd Street Beach Dunes & Habitat Stewardship Plan

CPD-CBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Environmental Committee / CPD Natural Areas

Site-specific, CPD-approved objectives for the native dune/shoreline habitat — the "Jackson Park - 63rd St. Beach Dunes," CPD's exact facility name for this natural area, home to over 200 bird species including black-crowned night herons, least bitterns, piping plovers, and snowy owls: supporting migratory bird value, monitoring erosion and vegetation health, and defining what volunteer work is authorized under 7-07 to advance those objectives. Drafted jointly with CPD Natural Areas staff, not unilaterally by the Council, and revisited each time the Habitat Work Authorization process (7-10) approves new work.

7-12

Migratory Bird / Nesting Protection Protocol

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Environmental Committee

Guidance for volunteers working near or observing the dunes during migratory/nesting season: keep distance, avoid disturbing vegetation that may shelter nests, and refer any observed distress, injury, or unusual activity to CPD Natural Areas or a qualified wildlife partner rather than intervening directly. Drafted with input from CPD and any partnering conservation organization, not invented internally.

Tracks recurring public foot-traffic damage to the dunes for CPD's awareness and possible signage/fencing response — the Council observes and reports; it does not install its own barriers or signage without CPD authorization.

Shoreline / Erosion Observation Log

Maintained on a regular seasonal cadence and immediately after major storms — a documented, dated record is what makes later capital-advocacy conversations (Phase 10, 10-13/10-15) credible rather than anecdotal.

7-15

Beach Cleanup SOP

CPD-CBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Stewardship / Volunteer Leads

Defines the approved cleanup footprint (from 7-01), required volunteer safety gear (gloves at minimum), waste segregation (recyclables, general trash, hazardous items handled per 7-17), and CPD coordination for large group cleanups (may itself trigger an Activity Request under Phase 8). Reviewed and re-briefed before every cleanup, not assumed to be common knowledge.

7-17

Sharps / Biohazard / Hazardous Debris Escalation Protocol

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Risk / Stewardship Leads

Do not handle it. Volunteers who encounter sharps, suspected biohazard material, or anything else outside routine trash do not pick it up, bag it, or move it unless specifically trained and equipped for that exact category. Mark the location, keep others away, and call 311 or the appropriate CPD/emergency channel immediately. This protocol is read aloud (not just handed out) at the start of every cleanup involving volunteers who haven't done one before.
7-18

Water Quality Public-Information Protocol

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Safety / Communications

The Council does not issue water-quality advisories. This is a "Do Not Cross" boundary made procedural: any Council communication about water quality or swim conditions restates CPD's own official testing/advisory information, with source and date clearly attached, and is removed or updated the moment CPD's own posted information changes. The Council never publishes an independent judgment that the water is or isn't safe.
7-19

CPD Water Quality / Swim Advisory Link & Archive Log

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Safety / Data Lead

Fields: Date · Advisory Status (per CPD) · Source Link/Screenshot

Maintained through the beach season for the sole purpose of spotting seasonal trends worth raising with CPD later (Phase 10, 10-25) — not for making any real-time public safety call itself.

7-20

Community Science / Research Approval Procedure

CPD-CProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Environmental Committee

Any proposed sampling, collecting, monitoring, or research project — even community-science-flavored efforts that sound informal — requires CPD/natural-area research authorization before it starts. This procedure is the checkpoint that catches "let's just take some water samples ourselves" before it becomes a real problem.

7-21

Research Permit / Partner Study File

CPD-CProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Environmental / Partnerships

For an approved research project: permit documentation, methods, data-sharing terms, ethics considerations (especially for any human-subjects component), and final findings — created per project, retained as part of the Partnership Landscape (Phase 10, 10-12).

7-22

Water Sampling Project Approval File

CPD-CProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Environmental / Safety / CPD Liaison

Conditional — do not initiate without approval. If independent supplemental water sampling is ever proposed (beyond CPD's own official testing), this file documents CPD permission, a qualified laboratory and methodology, and strict communication rules: results from independent sampling never substitute for, or get presented as equivalent to, CPD's official water-quality advisories under 7-18. As of this draft, no such project exists or is planned.
7-23

Water Safety & Lifeguard Liaison SOP

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Safety Committee / CPD Liaison

Implements Bylaws Article 22. A designated Safety Committee contact checks in with beach/lifeguard staff on a regular cadence (e.g., start of season and periodically through it) to share community safety-education ideas and escalate observed hazards — but never directs lifeguard staffing, positioning, or operational decisions, which remain entirely CPD's authority. This liaison relationship is built with beach staff input, not designed unilaterally and presented to them.

Beach Flag / Advisory Communication Checklist

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Safety

Checklist, before any safety-related post: Source is current official CPD/NWS information · Source and date/time clearly stated · Language matches (not reinterprets) the source · Post is updated or removed promptly when the underlying advisory changes
7-25

Dangerous Currents / Wave Hazard Education Plan

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Safety / Communications

Annual, pre-season public education using CPD and National Weather Service authoritative messaging about dangerous currents and wave hazards — flyers, social posts, a Facility Fact Sheet (7-03) callout. Purely educational: volunteers delivering this content have no rescue role and are trained to say so explicitly if asked.

7-26

Severe Weather Beach Activity Protocol

CPD-CBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Safety / Events

Any Council-run outdoor activity (workday, cleanup, event) pauses or cancels based on current CPD/NWS conditions, not the Council's own weather judgment call. A designated person is responsible for the go/no-go decision and for notifying registered volunteers/attendees, per the Severe Weather / Cancellation Plan template (Phase 8, 8-11).

7-27

Life Ring / Safety Equipment Observation & Escalation Log

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Safety Committee

Fields: Date · Equipment/Location · Condition Observed (missing/damaged) · Reported to CPD? (Y/N, Date)

Volunteers report; they never repair, replace, or relocate public safety equipment themselves, regardless of how minor the fix might seem.

7-28

Swim / Rescue Non-Interference Policy

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Risk / Safety Committee

The core boundary, stated plainly. Council volunteers do not perform lifeguard or water-rescue functions under any circumstance while acting in a Council capacity, unless separately qualified (e.g., a certified lifeguard volunteering through an authorized CPD program, not through the Council) and operating under that program's own authority — never under the Council's. This policy is acknowledged in writing by every volunteer whose role could plausibly put them near the water (Phase 6, 6-24).
7-29

Distance-Swimming Liaison Protocol

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Safety Committee

A feedback and observation channel specific to the designated distance-swim area, which CPD's own 63rd Street Beach page describes as running parallel to shore between the 1st and 3rd buoys: collecting swimmer feedback on signage and conditions and referring it to CPD/Aquatics, and periodically checking that the buoys, markers, and signage are in place and legible — again, observation and referral, not the Council operating or maintaining anything itself.

7-30

Non-Motorized Boat Launch Liaison & Safety Protocol

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Safety / Stewardship

Covers the 63rd Street non-motorized launch: periodic observation of condition and use patterns, basic user education (right-of-way, posted rules), and referral of any safety or maintenance issue to CPD. The Council has no operational control over the launch and does not represent otherwise.

7-31

Accessibility Audit — Beach Walk / Shoreline Access

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Accessibility Committee

A structured walkthrough of the accessible beach walk and shoreline access route at the start, middle, and end of each beach season: surface condition, obstacles, signage clarity, and a plain description of the actual visitor experience for someone using mobility equipment. Findings go to CPD as advocacy input, not as a Council-issued engineering assessment (Phase 10, 10-13 covers that distinction).

7-38

Environmental Sustainability Policy

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Environmental Committee

Council-wide commitments applied across events and operations: minimizing single-use materials, preferring reusable/compostable options at Council-run activities, and factoring habitat protection into every planning decision that touches the dunes or shoreline — a values statement that other phases' event and stewardship documents are expected to actually implement, not just cite.

7-39

Environmental Justice Statement

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Board / Environmental Committee

Names explicitly that equitable beach access, shoreline safety, public health, habitat protection, and genuine community voice are connected, not separate concerns — a Board-adopted statement meant to inform priority-setting across every phase of this library, from accessibility audits (7-31 through 7-33) to capital advocacy (Phase 10).

7-40
