Strategic Planning & Reporting

The annual accountability rhythm that ties every other phase together: work plans, the required CPD/state/federal reporting pack, KPIs and community assessment, capital-priority setting, and the year-end closeout that resets the whole cycle for next year.

Working document, not legal advice. This page is part of a governance/compliance planning library for the proposed 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council. Nothing here should be read as confirming that CPD has recognized a separate 63rd Street Beach PAC — that threshold question, Gate 0, is still open. Every document is labeled Proposed for that reason, and none of the annual reports below have actually been filed yet.

Full document text

Every document below is a working draft. None has been filed or adopted unless specifically noted.

10-01

Three-to-Five-Year Strategic Plan

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Board / Strategy Committee

Long-term outcomes across every phase of this library: beach stewardship and habitat health (Phase 7), water safety and accessibility (Phase 7), cultural vitality and the drumming tradition relationship (Phase 8), governance maturity (Phases 2-3), and the resource development capacity (Phase 4) needed to fund all of it. Built once real evidence exists — Gate 0 resolved, at least one full annual cycle behind the Council — rather than drafted from assumptions alone.

10-02

Annual Work Plan

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Board / Committees

Fields, per objective: Objective · Owning Committee · Timeline · Budget Line (per Phase 4, 4-12) · Success Measure

The one-year slice of the Strategic Plan (10-01) — adopted alongside the annual budget so goals and money are never set separately from each other.

10-03

Annual CPD PAC Report

CPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: President / Secretary / Treasurer

The core annual filing the Minimum Compliance Dashboard requires for continuing recognition — submitted using CPD's current official report format (see Phase 11, 11-93 for the working evidence checklist), assembled from records already maintained elsewhere in this library rather than reconstructed from scratch each year: election records (Phase 1), the PAC Binder (Phase 6, 6-01), and financial reports (Phase 4). Only relevant once Gate 0 confirms this Council has a CPD relationship that requires this filing at all.

10-04

Annual Illinois NFP Compliance Report File

IL-RCPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

Holds the copy of the Illinois NFP Annual Report actually filed under Phase 5 (5-03), plus an internal checklist confirming it was filed on time and that the resulting good-standing evidence was provided to CPD as its own annual-status requirement expects.

10-05

Annual Federal / Charity Filing Pack

FED-RLEG-CProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Treasurer

Bundles copies of whatever federal/charity filings actually applied that year — the IRS Form 990/990-EZ/990-N (Phase 4, 4-54) if the Council holds its own exemption, and the Illinois AG990-IL (Phase 4, 4-42) if charitable-registration thresholds were met — into one annual file, so "did we file everything we owed this year" has a single answer, not five scattered ones.

10-06

Annual Fundraising / Financial Report to CPD

CPD-RCPD-CProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Treasurer

The annual report CPD's current conduct guidance requires whenever the Council has actually handled funds, including the bank statement evidence CPD asks for — assembled directly from the Annual PAC Fundraising Report file (Phase 4, 4-40), not drafted separately.

10-07

Annual State of 63rd Street Beach / Community Report

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Board / Communications

The plain-language public companion to 10-03 through 10-06's compliance filings: what the Council actually did this year, what it observed about the beach's condition, what the community raised as concerns, where money went, and what's planned next. Published per the Public Document Publication Schedule (Phase 9, 9-26).

10-08

KPI Framework

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Strategy Committee

Example indicators, one per goal area: Governance: % of meetings meeting quorum · Membership: net member growth · Volunteerism: total volunteer hours logged · Stewardship: cleanup events held / debris removed · Accessibility: audit findings resolved within target window · Events: CPD Activity Requests approved without incident · Fundraising: % of annual budget target raised · Community response: average time to close an Issue & Escalation Log entry (Phase 6, 6-20)
10-09

Annual Performance Dashboard

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Strategy / Data Lead

A concise scoreboard against the KPI Framework (10-08) — updated quarterly for internal board use, condensed and published annually as part of the State of the Beach Report (10-07). Trends matter more than any single year's number.

10-10

Community Needs Assessment

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Strategy / Membership

Structured evidence, not impressions: results from the Annual Community Survey (Phase 8, 8-32) and Public Listening Sessions (Phase 8, 8-33), direct observations from stewardship workdays and event feedback, relevant CPD data, and partner input — conducted before drafting or refreshing the Strategic Plan (10-01), not after the plan is already written.

10-11

Stakeholder Map

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Strategy / Partnerships

Names who actually has a stake in 63rd Street Beach beyond the Council's own membership: regular beach users, immediate neighbors, the drumming tradition community, schools, environmental and accessibility advocates, nearby businesses, the specific CPD units the Council interacts with, Jackson Park Advisory Council, and relevant elected offices — kept current, not drafted once and forgotten.

10-12

Partnership Landscape / Relationship Map

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Partnerships Committee

Fields, per partner: Partner Name · Purpose · Owner · Agreement Type & Status (per Phase 8, 8-28/8-29) · Outcomes to Date
10-13

Facility / Asset Condition Assessment

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Stewardship / Capital / Accessibility

Rolls up the year's Beach Facility Condition Walkthroughs (Phase 7, 7-34) and Accessibility Audits (Phase 7, 7-31) into one periodic assessment for CPD advocacy purposes — a community observation report, explicitly not a substitute for CPD's own engineering inspection, and never represented as one.

10-14

Capital Improvement Prioritization Policy

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Capital Committee

Scoring criteria: Safety impact · Accessibility impact · Historic preservation value (Beach House, per Phase 7, 7-36) · Environmental impact · Equity of benefit · Public benefit scale · Cost/readiness · Fit with CPD's own capital plans

Transparent criteria, adopted before any specific project is scored against them — so prioritization can't be retrofitted to favor whichever project someone already wanted.

10-15

Capital Improvement Priority List

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Capital Committee / Membership

The actual ranked list produced by applying 10-14's criteria, updated annually and submitted through CPD channels — a recommendation to CPD, not a Council-authorized capital budget, since the Council has no independent authority to build anything on CPD property.

10-16

Capital Project Pipeline Register

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Capital Committee

Fields, per project: Concept · CPD Status · Scope · Cost Estimate · Funding Strategy · Approvals Obtained · Risks · Completion Status

Tracks every capital idea from concept through the Letter of Intent, Pledge, Proof-of-Funds, and Executed Agreement chain in Phase 4 (4-56 through 4-60) to final closeout (4-60).

10-17

Annual Risk Assessment

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Risk Lead / Board

The annual review that refreshes the Enterprise/PAC Risk Register (Phase 5, 5-43) — scoring governance, safety, event, financial, environmental, cyber, and reputational risks by likelihood and impact, and confirming mitigation owners are actually assigned, not just risks named and left unaddressed.

10-18

Annual Policy & Bylaw Review Report

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

Executes the Annual Governance Review Checklist (Phase 2, 2-40) and Policy Sunset Schedule (Phase 6, 6-36) as an actual annual report: which documents in this library were reviewed this year, what changed, what was formally adopted, and what got retired as obsolete — the mechanism that keeps a 510-document library from calcifying into 510 documents nobody ever looks at again.

10-19

Board Self-Assessment Report

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

The completed, written-up version of the Board Self-Assessment tool (Phase 3, 3-18) — engagement, compliance, strategic contribution, financial oversight, and succession readiness, with specific improvement actions assigned to specific people, not just observations left unaddressed.

10-20

Member / Community Satisfaction Survey

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Membership / Strategy

Measures whether members and the broader community actually trust the Council, find meetings worthwhile, feel heard, and agree with its stated priorities — distinct from the Annual Community Survey (Phase 8, 8-32), which asks about the beach itself rather than about the Council's own performance.

10-21

Volunteer Impact Report

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Volunteer Coordinator

Rolled up from Phase 6 volunteer records: Total Volunteer Hours · Projects Supported · Training Completed (6-26) · Retention Rate · Recognition Given (Phase 8, 8-36) · Safety Incidents, if any
10-22

Beach Stewardship Metrics Report

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Stewardship / Environmental

Aggregates the year's Cleanup Waste Logs (Phase 7, 7-16), habitat work logs (7-11), observation logs (7-13, 7-14), and CPD referrals into one annual stewardship metrics report — feeding directly into the Annual Beach & Stewardship Condition Report (Phase 7, 7-41) and the KPI Dashboard (10-09).

10-23

Programs & Events Impact Report

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Programs Committee

Rolls up each event's After-Action Review (Phase 8, 8-44) for the year: total participation, accessibility outcomes, cultural/community impact (with particular care for how programming interacted with the drumming tradition liaison, Phase 8, 8-21), finances, incidents, and lessons carried into next year's Annual Work Plan (10-02).

10-24

Accessibility Review Report

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Accessibility Committee

The annual synthesis across every accessibility touchpoint in this library: meeting accessibility (Bylaws Article 23), digital accessibility (Phase 9, 9-23), beach-walk/shoreline audits (Phase 7, 7-31), and event accessibility plans (Phase 8, 8-13) — one report naming what's working and what's still a gap, rather than each committee tracking its own piece in isolation.

10-25

Environmental / Water-Information Dashboard

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Environmental / Data Lead

Combines the season's CPD Water Quality/Swim Advisory Archive Log (Phase 7, 7-19) with the Council's own habitat/erosion observations (7-13, 7-14) into one dashboard — every CPD-sourced figure clearly labeled as CPD's own data with a source and date, and every Council observation clearly labeled as the Council's own, never blended together in a way that could look like an official water-quality claim.

10-26

Succession & Leadership Development Review

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

Reviewed every year before elections, alongside the Board Recruitment & Succession Plan (Phase 3, 3-19): which offices have a viable pipeline of interested candidates, which don't, what training gaps exist, and what happens if a key officer leaves unexpectedly before their term ends.

10-27

Annual Meeting Report / Presentation

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: President / Officers

The single presentation that pulls the year together for the full membership at the annual meeting: year-in-review highlights, the Treasurer's financial summary, election results if held that cycle, progress against the Annual Work Plan (10-02), and priorities for the year ahead.

10-28

Year-End Records & Filing Closeout Checklist

CPD-RIL-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Treasurer

The one checklist where the whole library converges. This is not a standalone item — it is the point where nearly every phase's annual deadline gets confirmed complete in one place before the year closes: minutes approved and archived (Phase 6), financial reports/reconciliations current (Phase 4), the PAC Binder up to date (Phase 6, 6-01), the Annual CPD PAC Report filed (10-03), Illinois NFP report filed (10-04), federal/charity filings complete if applicable (10-05), any grant closeouts finished (Phase 4, 4-44), and next year's Annual Board Calendar (Phase 3, 3-16) and Operations Calendar (Phase 6, 6-21) already adopted. If any item on this checklist is incomplete, the year isn't actually closed yet, regardless of the calendar date.
10-29

Strategic Plan Progress Report

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Strategy Committee

A quarterly or semiannual check-in against the multi-year Strategic Plan (10-01) — are the annual work plans (10-02) actually adding up to the longer-term goals, or has the Council drifted, and if so, what course correction does the Board need to make now rather than at the next full plan refresh.

10-30

Policy / Committee Sunset Review

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

On a scheduled cycle (see the Policy Sunset Schedule, Phase 6, 6-36), asks a genuinely hard question of every standing committee and policy in this library: does this still need to exist, is it still doing anything, and if not, is retiring it the honest move rather than leaving it on the books unused. A living document that never sunsets anything isn't actually living — it's just accumulating.
