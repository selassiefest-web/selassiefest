Phase 6 — Operational & Administrative Manuals

Day-to-day recordkeeping, meetings, volunteer administration, incident/escalation protocols and institutional memory for the proposed 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council.

Working document set — not legal advice, not a claim of recognition. Everything on this page and across this library is a governance/compliance draft prepared for the proposed 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council. Nothing here should be read as asserting that the Chicago Park District (CPD) has recognized a separate 63rd Street Beach PAC — see Phase 0: Pre-Formation Gate for that open question. Confirm current requirements directly with CPD and with qualified counsel before filing, submitting, or formally adopting anything drawn from this page.
This phase is fully catalogued below, but not yet fully drafted. Every document has a real register entry (title, classification, owner, purpose, status), but full drafted text has not been written for this phase yet. Phases 0–2 (Pre-Formation Gate, Formation & Recognition, Governance & Bylaws) already have complete drafted text. This phase is queued for a later drafting pass — check back, or see the Organization overview for the publishing roadmap.
