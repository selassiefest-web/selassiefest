Operational & Administrative Manuals

Bylaws Articles 12-13 set the meeting framework; the Voluntary Open-Meeting Transparency Standard (Phase 5, 5-16) promises a published calendar, posted notices, approved minutes, and a public-comment period. Phase 6 is where those promises actually become procedures — plus the Council's records infrastructure, volunteer handbook, and emergency/continuity planning.

Working document, not legal advice. This page is part of a governance/compliance planning library for the proposed 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council. Nothing here should be read as confirming that CPD has recognized a separate 63rd Street Beach PAC — that threshold question, Gate 0, is still open. Every document is labeled Proposed for that reason.

Full document text

Every document below is a working draft. None has been adopted or filed unless specifically noted.

6-01

PAC Binder — Physical In-Park Official File

CPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

The in-park compliance file CPD's current PAC Guidelines expect to exist at the recognized meeting location — distinct from, and narrower than, the permanent Corporate Records Book (Phase 5, 5-07). Contains, matching CPD's own required-contents list verbatim ("bylaws, financial statements, meetings agendas, minutes, sign in sheets, and evidence of Not-For-Profit status with the State of Illinois"): the current Bylaws (2-02) and NFP status evidence (5-04), the current CPD Code of Conduct (1-02), the last twelve months of agendas and approved minutes (6-12), recent Treasurer's Reports (4-14), and sign-in sheets/attendance records (6-13). Established the moment CPD recognition is confirmed (Phase 0/1), and self-audited quarterly against the CPD PAC Binder Compliance Checklist (Phase 5, 5-14).

6-02

Digital Governance Repository / Electronic Binder

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Technology Lead

A cloud mirror of the PAC Binder plus everything the in-park binder can't hold: full corporate history, every policy, every event and grant file, and this Organization document library's source material. Role-based access — officers and committee chairs get what their role needs, not blanket access to everything (donor records, background-check details, and active legal-hold material stay restricted regardless of officer rank). This public library at /bbpac/organization/ is deliberately the public-facing front door onto this repository: what's published here is what the Council is comfortable with anyone reading; what's not published (personal data, in-progress drafts, sensitive logs) stays in the private repository even after this page-by-page rollout is complete.

6-03

Master Document Register

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / PMO Lead

The index of all 510 documents in this checklist — which, as it happens, is not an abstraction here: it's literally the data.js file powering the search catalog at /bbpac/organization/documents/, where every document's ID, title, classification, owner, and drafting status already lives as a maintained, machine-readable record. Updating that file each time a document is drafted, adopted, or revised is keeping this register current — there is no separate spreadsheet to reconcile against it.

6-04

Document Control & Versioning Procedure

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Records Officer

Every governing document carries a visible status (Proposed, Adopted, Superseded), a version marker, and a single authoritative-copy location — for public documents, that location is this library; for internal-only records, the Digital Governance Repository (6-02). When a document is superseded, the prior version is archived, not deleted, and the new version's adoption date is recorded per the Policy Adoption / Amendment Procedure (Phase 2, 2-38).

6-05

File Naming & Folder Taxonomy Standard

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Records Officer

A folder structure mirroring this checklist's own 12 phases (Governance, Board, Finance, Legal, Operations, Stewardship, Programming, Communications, Strategy) plus year-based subfolders for meetings, events, and grants, adopted before the Digital Governance Repository accumulates enough files that retrofitting a structure becomes painful. File names follow a consistent pattern: YYYY-MM-DD_DocumentID_ShortTitle_vN.

6-06

Annual Archive Index

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

A year-end snapshot: every final record produced that year, where it lives (physical binder, digital repository, this public library), and which retention category (Phase 5, 5-10) governs it. Completed as part of the Year-End Records & Filing Closeout Checklist (Phase 10, 10-28).

6-07

Records Backup & Recovery Procedure

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Technology Lead

The Digital Governance Repository (6-02) is backed up automatically by its cloud provider, but automatic backup is not the same as a tested restore. Annually, the Technology Lead performs (and logs) an actual test restore of a sample file set — corporate records, current financials, and the current Bylaws at minimum — to confirm backups are real, current, and usable, not just present.

6-08

Meeting Operations Manual

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Parliamentarian

The end-to-end procedure implementing Bylaws Articles 12-13 and the Voluntary Open-Meeting Transparency Standard (Phase 5, 5-16), start to finish: (1) the Annual Meeting Calendar (6-09) is published each January; (2) notice for each meeting goes out per the Notice Checklist (6-10), with proof of posting retained; (3) the agenda is finalized per the Agenda Development Procedure (6-11) and includes a standing Public Comment slot (6-15); (4) the Secretary confirms quorum (Bylaws Article 14) before any vote; (5) any proposed recording follows the Recording Consent Procedure (Phase 5, 5-20); (6) minutes are drafted and circulated for approval per the Minutes Standard (6-12); (7) approved minutes and any resulting action items are posted and logged (6-16, 6-17). Adopted by the Board and reviewed annually alongside the Annual Governance Review Checklist (Phase 2, 2-40).

6-09

Annual Meeting Calendar / Recurring Schedule Notice

CPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

Published every January (or upon CPD recognition, whichever comes first): a recurring monthly meeting schedule — day, time, and location — for the full calendar year, consistent with the "at least quarterly" floor set by Bylaws Article 12 and current CPD guidance. This is the specific document that fulfills the "annual meeting calendar is published in advance" commitment made in the Voluntary Open-Meeting Transparency Standard (Phase 5, 5-16).

6-10

Meeting Notice Checklist & Proof-of-Posting File

CPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

Checklist, completed per meeting: Notice posted at the meeting location at least 14 days before a regular meeting or election · Notice posted on the website/social channels · Notice emailed to the mailing list · For an emergency or special meeting, a minimum 48-hour notice used instead, per current CPD guidance · Screenshot or photo retained as proof-of-posting, dated

The proof-of-posting file is what actually demonstrates the Voluntary Open-Meeting Transparency Standard was followed for a given meeting, not just asserted after the fact. If a meeting is cancelled, notice of the cancellation is posted immediately and the Park Supervisor or Area Manager is informed.

6-11

Agenda Development Procedure

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: President / Secretary

Committee chairs and officers submit agenda items to the Secretary by a set deadline (e.g., five days before the meeting); the President and Secretary finalize the order, grouping routine items (financial report, committee updates) as a consent section and reserving discussion time for substantive business; the Public Comment slot (6-15) and any CPD staff item always appear on the agenda when applicable; the final agenda is posted per the Notice Checklist (6-10) before the meeting.

6-12

Minutes Preparation & Approval Standard

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

Minutes record, at minimum: date/time/location, attendance and quorum confirmation, every motion with its maker, second, and vote count, any recusal (Phase 5, 5-22), officer/committee reports, and action items. Draft minutes circulate to the Board within a set number of days (e.g., ten) for correction, are formally approved at the next meeting, and are then posted publicly per the Public Document Publication Schedule (Phase 9, 9-26) — this posting step is the specific fulfillment of 5-16's "minutes are posted within a set number of days" commitment.

6-13

Sign-In / Attendance Data Handling Procedure

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

Sign-in sheets collect only name and, optionally, an email opt-in for the mailing list — never phone numbers, addresses, or other data not needed to prove attendance for CPD compliance purposes. Original sign-in sheets go in the PAC Binder (6-01); access to the underlying contact list (as opposed to a public attendance count) is limited to the Secretary and Membership Lead.

6-14

Virtual / Hybrid Meeting SOP

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Technology Lead

Implements Bylaws Article 13. When a meeting is hybrid or virtual: the platform and dial-in number are included in the meeting notice (6-10); per current CPD guidance, the agenda and the prior meeting's minutes are made available at least three days before the virtual meeting takes place, and CPD staff are notified that the meeting will be held virtually; remote attendees count toward quorum and may vote using the platform's polling/hand-raise feature, logged in the minutes the same as an in-person vote; a technical failure affecting a material portion of the meeting is noted in the minutes and may trigger a re-vote at the next meeting; and any recording follows the same consent procedure (Phase 5, 5-20) regardless of format.

6-15

Public Comment / Community Input Procedure

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

A standing agenda item at every regular meeting, typically 2-3 minutes per speaker with a total time cap the Chair may extend at their discretion, open to any member of the public (not just voting members). Comments are heard and logged in the minutes; the Board is not required to respond in the moment but should follow up per the Public Contact & Response Service Standard (Phase 9, 9-30) if a comment raises something needing a real answer. This is the document that fulfills 5-16's "public-comment period appears on every regular agenda" commitment.

6-16

Action Item Tracker

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / PMO Lead

Fields: Action · Source Meeting/Date · Owner · Deadline · Status · Completion Evidence

Reviewed at the start of every meeting so open items don't quietly disappear between minutes approvals.

6-17

Decision / Resolution Register

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

Fields: Resolution # · Date · Summary · Vote (Yes-No-Abstain) · Source Minutes Link

A chronological, numbered index of formal Board/membership decisions — useful when a later question is "did we actually vote on that, and when," without re-reading a year of minutes to find out.

6-18

Correspondence Register

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary

Fields: Date · Direction (In/Out) · Correspondent · Subject · Related Document ID · Follow-Up Needed?

Tracks material letters and emails with CPD, elected offices, funders, and partners — not routine day-to-day traffic, but anything that would matter to a future officer trying to understand why a relationship stands where it does.

6-19

CPD Request / Referral Log

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: CPD Liaison

Fields: Issue · Date Referred · CPD Contact/Channel (e.g. 311) · Status · Resolution Date

Every entry is phrased as a referral to CPD, never as the Council performing the maintenance/safety work itself — consistent with the "Do Not Cross" boundary against directing CPD staff or substituting for CPD operations.

6-20

Issue & Escalation Log

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: President / CPD Liaison

Broader than 6-19 — tracks any unresolved community or operational issue (not only ones already referred to CPD), who owns getting it resolved, and where it stands. Reviewed at every regular meeting so nothing sits unaddressed simply because no single document was tracking it.

6-21

Annual Operations Calendar

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Operations Lead

A single combined calendar overlaying the Annual Meeting Calendar (6-09), beach season milestones, stewardship workdays, planned events, election timing (Phase 3), filing deadlines (Phase 5's Master Compliance Matrix, Phase 10's filing calendar), and the budget cycle (Phase 4) — built so no two obligations quietly collide.

6-22

Officer / Committee Handbook

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

A practical, plain-language companion to the Bylaws and Officer Position Descriptions (Phase 3, 3-01) — what a report to the Board actually looks like, how approvals move through the Delegation of Authority Matrix (Phase 2, 2-36), the expected meeting cadence, and what a transition (Phase 3, 3-28) requires in practice. Written for someone who has never served on a nonprofit board before.

6-23

Committee Handbook

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

Companion to the Standing Committee Charter Set (Phase 2, 2-37) — standard operating expectations that apply across every committee regardless of subject: how to request budget, how and when to report to the Board, how to bring in outside volunteers, and the sunset-review expectation that a committee's charter (and its very existence) gets revisited rather than assumed permanent.

6-24

Volunteer Handbook

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Volunteer Coordinator

The single document every volunteer actually reads before a workday or event shift: conduct expectations (Phase 5, 5-25), check-in/out procedure, required waivers, the specific tasks volunteers are and are not authorized to do (see the "Do Not Cross" boundaries — no directing CPD staff, no lifeguard/rescue functions, no unauthorized habitat work), and which of CPD's real, category-specific volunteer paths applies to a given role: the Long Term Adult Volunteer Application (officers and anyone handling finances or working with youth, per Phase 1, 1-27 through 1-29), the Long Term Minor Volunteer Application (volunteers 16 and younger), or the shorter one-day Volunteer Waiver and Release (single-day event/workday help only) — there is no single generic "CPD Volunteer Application" that covers every role. Also covers how to report an incident (6-30) and how volunteer service is recognized (Phase 8, 8-36).

6-25

Volunteer Orientation / Training Curriculum

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Volunteer Coordinator

Short (20-30 minute) modules delivered before a volunteer's first shift: the CPD/PAC independence boundary, basic beach safety awareness, natural-area rules if the role touches the dunes, event-day roles, code of conduct, and incident escalation. Delivered in person, by video, or as a short written packet depending on scale — the content matters more than the format.

6-26

Volunteer Training / Certification Log

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Volunteer Coordinator

Fields: Volunteer Name · Orientation Completed (Date) · CPD Application/Background Status · Authorized Role(s) · Notes

Maintained confidentially — this log confirms someone is cleared for a role; it does not store the actual background-check report contents (Phase 1, 1-28 covers that distinction).

6-27

Emergency Operations Plan

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Risk / Emergency Committee

Defines what Council volunteers actually do during severe weather, an injury, a missing person, or a major event disruption — and, just as importantly, what they don't: this plan is explicitly subordinate to CPD staff and 911/public-safety authority at every point. Volunteer roles are limited to communication support (directing people to official channels, helping account for a group), never to substituting for lifeguards, medical responders, or CPD staff. Reviewed before every major event (Phase 8).

6-28

Crisis Management Plan

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: President / Risk Lead

Governance-level response to a serious crisis that isn't a physical-safety emergency: a financial irregularity, a leadership scandal, a data breach (Phase 5, 5-38), or a reputational incident. Defines who convenes the Board on short notice, who is authorized to speak publicly during the crisis (Phase 9, 9-16), and the decision point for bringing in Counsel or notifying CPD.

6-29

Business Continuity / Disaster Recovery Plan

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Treasurer / Technology Lead

If the Council loses access to its primary records, bank, or leadership (illness, sudden resignation, a cyber incident) all at once, this plan says who has emergency access to what: the Digital Governance Repository (6-02, via the Digital Access / Credential Management Policy, Phase 5, 5-37), the bank account signatory chain (Phase 4, 4-08), and a minimum viable set of contacts to keep meetings, filings, and communications running until normal leadership capacity is restored. Tested annually via a tabletop walkthrough, not just written and shelved.

6-30

Incident Reporting & Escalation SOP

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Risk Lead

Any volunteer who observes a safety hazard, injury, or incident: documents what was observed (using the Incident Report Form, Phase 11, 11-55), calls the appropriate channel (911 for an emergency, CPD/311 for a facility issue, a lifeguard for a water-safety concern) immediately, and never attempts the fix, rescue, or repair themselves unless specifically trained and authorized for that exact task. Reported incidents are logged and reviewed for pattern by the Risk Lead.

6-31

Lost Person / Lost Child Escalation Protocol

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Risk / Volunteer Leads

Immediate notification to lifeguards, CPD staff, or 911 (for a missing child, always 911 without delay) is the first and controlling step — Council volunteers may help spread a description, watch an area, or comfort a family member while waiting, but never conduct an independent search that could duplicate or interfere with an official one. This protocol is reviewed with all event-day volunteers before any event with meaningful attendance.

6-32

Lost Property Referral Protocol

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Operations Lead

Found property is turned over to CPD staff or the Beach House front desk, not held informally by a volunteer "just in case someone comes back for it" — a simple rule that avoids both liability and the appearance of the Council operating an unofficial lost-and-found.

6-33

Vendor / Partner File Standard

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Operations / Treasurer

Required per vendor/partner file: Approval record · Signed contract/MOU (6-34) · Certificate of insurance (6-35) · Applicable permits · W-9/tax form · Invoices · Conflict-of-interest screen (Phase 5, 5-22/5-23) · Closeout note
6-34

Contract & MOU Register

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Treasurer

Fields: Counterparty · Type (Contract/MOU/MOA) · Start/End Date · Renewal Trigger · Owner · Insurance Required? (Y/N) · Termination Notice Period

Reviewed quarterly so a contract never auto-renews (or lapses) by accident.

6-35

Insurance Certificate / Permit Register

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Operations / Risk Lead

Fields: Holder (Vendor/Council/Partner) · Coverage Type · Expiration Date · Renewal Reminder Sent? (Y/N)

Central log so no event or partnership proceeds on a certificate of insurance that quietly expired the week before.

6-36

Policy Sunset / Periodic Review Schedule

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance Committee

Assigns every adopted policy a review cycle based on risk: high-risk/fast-changing policies (cybersecurity, AI use, insurance) reviewed annually; moderate-risk policies (most operational SOPs) every 2 years; low-risk/stable policies (code of ethics, non-discrimination) every 3 years. Feeds directly into the Policy Register & Version History (Phase 2, 2-39) and the Annual Policy & Bylaw Review Report (Phase 10, 10-18).

6-37

Knowledge Transfer / Institutional Memory Manual

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Past Leadership

The living answer to "why do we do it this way, and who do we actually call at CPD" — recurring relationships (which Park Supervisor, which CPD department for which issue), the reasoning behind non-obvious past decisions, and open initiatives that outlived the officer who started them. Built gradually over the Council's first year rather than written once and frozen; this is, in a real sense, what the entire Organization document library exists to prevent from living only in one departing officer's head.
