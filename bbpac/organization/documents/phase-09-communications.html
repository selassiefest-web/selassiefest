Phase 9 — Communications & Public Relations

Standards governing how this effort speaks publicly — brand, media, social channels, accessibility, and the disclaimers that keep this an independent, advisory voice rather than a stand-in for the Chicago Park District.

Working document set — not legal advice, not a claim of recognition. Everything in this library is a governance/compliance draft prepared for the proposed 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council. Nothing here asserts that the Chicago Park District (CPD) has recognized a separate 63rd Street Beach PAC — see Phase 0 for that open question. Confirm current requirements with CPD and qualified counsel before filing, submitting, or formally adopting anything drawn from this page.
This phase is fully catalogued below — every document's title, classification, owner, purpose, and status — but full drafted text has not been written yet. Phases 0–2 (Pre-Formation Gate, Formation & Recognition, Governance & Bylaws) already have complete drafted text. This phase is queued for a later pass; check back, or see the Organization overview for the publishing roadmap.

Speaking publicly, without speaking for CPD

This phase governs brand standards, social media, the website itself, media relations, crisis communications, accessibility of public materials, and — most importantly — the standard disclaimer language that keeps every public statement clearly identified as an independent, advisory voice rather than an extension of the Chicago Park District.
