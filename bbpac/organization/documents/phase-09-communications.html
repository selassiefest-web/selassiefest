Communications & Public Relations

Bylaws Article 26 governs digital communications and electronic records. Phase 9 turns that into brand standards, social media and website governance, media relations, crisis communications, accessibility, and — most importantly — the standard disclaimer language that keeps every public statement clearly independent of the Chicago Park District.

Working document, not legal advice. This page is part of a governance/compliance planning library for the proposed 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council. Nothing here should be read as confirming that CPD has recognized a separate 63rd Street Beach PAC — that threshold question, Gate 0, is still open. Every document is labeled Proposed for that reason, and no public-facing material should imply otherwise.

9-01

Communications Strategy

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications Committee

The umbrella document the rest of this phase implements: who the Council is actually trying to reach (beach users, immediate neighbors, longtime drummers and tradition-bearers, accessibility advocates, prospective members and donors, CPD, and elected offices), what core messages it repeats consistently (independent advisory body, not CPD; supports and does not control the beach's existing culture; transparent by choice), which channels carry which messages, who's responsible for each channel, and the accessibility/language commitments that apply across all of it. Crisis protocols are set out separately in 9-18 rather than buried in this strategy document.

9-02

Annual Communications Plan / Editorial Calendar

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications Committee

Annual calendar entries: Meeting notices (per Phase 6, 6-09/6-10) · Beach-season safety/facts content · Stewardship workday announcements · Event promotion (Phase 8) · Annual reports (Phase 10) · Membership/fundraising campaigns
9-03

Brand Standards Guide

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications Committee

Not finalized until the Official Name & Naming Consistency Memo (Phase 0, G0-09) and Gate 0 itself are resolved — a brand built around a name or status that later changes is wasted work. Once ready, this guide covers the name/wordmark, logo usage rules (9-04), color palette, typography, photography style (respectful, not stock-generic — real 63rd Street Beach imagery), tone of voice (plain-language, community-facing, never bureaucratic), and the same accessibility contrast/legibility standards required in 9-23.

9-04

Logo Usage Policy

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Governance

Only the Communications Committee (or its designee) issues the Council's logo files; partners and sponsors seeking to co-brand something (an event flyer, a sponsor's own materials referencing the Council) submit a request and receive approved, unaltered artwork rather than recreating the mark themselves. No third party may modify the mark, combine it with their own logo without approval, or use it in a way that implies CPD affiliation beyond what's actually true.

9-05

CPD Name / Logo / Affiliation Usage Standard

CPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / CPD Liaison

Get this exactly right. The Chicago Park District's name, seal, and logo are never used in Council materials in a way that could be read as CPD's own communication, CPD's endorsement, or evidence that CPD has recognized this Council — because, as of this draft, it hasn't (Gate 0, Phase 0). Any reference to "Chicago Park District" or "CPD" in Council materials uses plain text, not CPD's marks, states the relationship accurately (e.g., "a proposed community initiative regarding a CPD park facility," never "a Chicago Park District program"), and any exception requires CPD's own written permission and approved wording, verified with CPD directly before use.
9-06

Independent-PAC Public Disclaimer

CPD-RBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance / Communications

The actual standard language, used on the website footer, printed materials, sponsorship/partnership agreements, and anywhere else the Council's relationship to CPD needs stating plainly:

"The 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council (operating publicly as 'Bongo Beach PAC') is a volunteer, community-led initiative of Ras Tafari Inc., a 501(c)(3) nonprofit. It is independent of the Chicago Park District, does not speak for or bind the Chicago Park District in any capacity, and has not yet been confirmed by the Chicago Park District as a separately recognized Park Advisory Council. All content on this site reflects the Council's own proposed plans and drafts, not official Chicago Park District policy or communications."

This exact language (or the current-recognition-status-updated version of it, once Gate 0 resolves) belongs on every page of this document library and every other public-facing Council page — it is the single most important sentence this whole Phase produces.

9-07

Social Media Policy

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications Committee

Accounts are institutional property (never a single volunteer's personal login, tracked in 9-08), posting authority is limited to designated Communications Committee members and the President, corrections to factual errors are issued promptly and visibly rather than quietly edited away, the Political Activity / Candidate Neutrality Policy (Phase 5, 5-31) applies to every post, personal information about members/volunteers/minors is never posted without consent, and posts are archived per 9-27 rather than lost when a platform changes its retention rules.

9-08

Social Media Account Register

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Technology

Fields, per platform: Platform · Account Handle · Institutional Owner (never a personal name alone) · Admin List · Recovery Email/Phone · MFA Enabled? (Y/N)
9-09

Social Media Moderation Guidelines

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Governance

Spam, threats, and harassment are removed on sight; substantive disagreement and criticism of the Council are not, and are never removed simply for being unflattering. Moderation decisions apply the same standard regardless of the commenter's viewpoint — a rule stated explicitly here precisely so it can be pointed to if anyone alleges otherwise.

9-10

Website Governance Policy

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Technology

This is not a hypothetical future website — it's this document library and the rest of the bbpac.org site already live at selassiefest.com/bbpac/. This policy names who owns editorial authority over it (Secretary for governance content, Communications for public-facing pages, joint sign-off for anything touching CPD's name per 9-05), sets the accessibility standard it must meet (9-23), states the privacy commitments it must honor (9-25), defines the archive/backup relationship to the Digital Governance Repository (Phase 6, 6-02), and names who's responsible for its security (Technology Lead, per the Cybersecurity Policy, Phase 5, 5-35).

Meeting Notice Communications Checklist

CPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

9-13

Email / Newsletter Policy

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications

Subscribers opt in affirmatively (no adding people from a sign-in sheet without separate consent), every email carries a working unsubscribe link honored within a reasonable window, only designated senders send on the Council's behalf, sponsor content (if any) is clearly labeled as such, and sent campaigns are retained per the Records Retention Schedule (Phase 5, 5-10).

9-15

Press Release / Media Advisory Template

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications

Standard release format (headline, dateline, body, boilerplate "About" paragraph incorporating the Independent-PAC Public Disclaimer language from 9-06, approved media contact) — every release states the Council's independent status in the same boilerplate paragraph every time, not left to whoever's drafting that day to remember.

9-16

Media Relations / Spokesperson Policy

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Board / Communications

Names who is authorized to speak to media on the Council's behalf (ordinarily the President or a designated Communications Chair — never any volunteer acting alone), requires factual claims to be checked before going out, and draws a hard line the spokesperson does not cross: describing the Council's own plans and positions is fine; characterizing what CPD itself intends, decided, or will do is not — that belongs to CPD's own spokespeople.

9-18

Crisis Communications Plan

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Risk

Activated for safety incidents, severe weather, financial or governance crises, or serious reputational events — names who has authority to approve a crisis statement, sets a target time-to-first-response, and requires every safety-adjacent statement to defer explicitly to CPD/first-responder information rather than the Council improvising its own account of an unfolding incident. Works alongside the Emergency Operations Plan (Phase 6, 6-27) and Crisis Management Plan (Phase 6, 6-28) — this document is specifically the public-communications piece of that broader response.

9-19

Emergency / Safety Post Approval Protocol

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Safety

Implements the Water Quality Public-Information Protocol and Beach Flag/Advisory Communication Checklist (Phase 7, 7-18/7-24): any post about a beach hazard or advisory reproduces CPD's own current information with a visible source and date, is approved by the Safety Committee before posting, and is updated or removed promptly once CPD's own information changes — a stale advisory left up after conditions change is arguably worse than no post at all.

9-20

Misinformation / Correction Protocol

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications

When the Council itself gets a fact wrong, it corrects the record visibly and promptly rather than quietly editing the post — a labeled correction, not a silent edit. When a rumor or question circulates about actual CPD operations (not Council business), the Council's role is to point people to the authoritative CPD source, not to guess or speculate on CPD's behalf.

9-21

Photography & Media Policy

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Governance

Covers Council-created imagery generally (releases per Phase 8, 8-25 for identifiable subjects, especially minors), and sets a specific expectation for anything touching established cultural practices: images of the drumming tradition or other community-rooted programming are used only with the same respectful-representation standard set in the Cultural Programming Policy (Phase 8, 8-20) and the consultation record (8-22) — never repurposed for fundraising or promotion without the consent that policy already requires.

9-22

Meeting Recording Communications Standard

CPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Communications

The communications-side companion to the Meeting Recording Consent Procedure (Phase 5, 5-20/5-21): any proposed recording is announced in the meeting notice/agenda in advance, not sprung on attendees at the meeting itself, and if a recording is ultimately posted publicly, the posting itself confirms consent was obtained and no objecting attendee's portion is included.

9-23

Digital Accessibility Standard

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Accessibility

Baseline practices: Real heading structure (not bolded text pretending to be a heading) · Alt text on every meaningful image · Captions on video · Sufficient color contrast · Readable, non-scanned PDFs where PDFs are used · Plain-language writing over jargon
9-24

Language Access / Translation Workflow

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Accessibility

Identifies which materials get translation priority (meeting notices, safety information, and the Independent-PAC Public Disclaimer itself are the obvious first candidates) based on the languages actually spoken by the surrounding community, and sets a quality-review step so a translation isn't published without someone competent in that language checking it first.

9-25

Privacy Notice

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Data Steward / Governance

The public-facing plain-language version of the Data Privacy Policy (Phase 5, 5-34): what the Council collects (membership applications, newsletter sign-ups, donation records, event registrations, website analytics if any), why, how long it's kept (per the Records Retention Schedule, Phase 5, 5-10), who can access it, and how to ask a question about your own data. Published before any of that collection actually goes live online.

9-26

Public Document Publication Schedule

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Records / Communications

Names, concretely, what gets proactively posted and how fast: agendas (before each meeting), approved minutes (within the timeline set by the Voluntary Open-Meeting Transparency Standard, Phase 5, 5-16), financial summaries (per meeting, per 4-14), annual reports (Phase 10), and the Bylaws/policies themselves — which, as of this document library, are already the default rather than the exception.

9-27

Communications Archive / Social Media Export Procedure

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Records

At least annually, exports/archives key public communications (major posts, newsletters, press coverage) into the Digital Governance Repository (Phase 6, 6-02) — because relying on a third-party platform to preserve institutional history indefinitely is not a records policy.

9-28

Copyright / Intellectual Property Policy

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Governance / Communications

Photos, designs, maps, recordings, and commissioned works created for the Council are owned by the Council (or licensed for its use, for volunteer-contributed work) unless a specific agreement says otherwise; commissioned vendors/designers sign over rights or grant an explicit license as part of their agreement (cross-reference Phase 8, 8-18/8-29); and nothing in this policy overrides the separate cultural-ownership commitments made in the Cultural Programming Policy (Phase 8, 8-20) or the Drumming Tradition Liaison (8-21) — the Council does not claim IP ownership over a community cultural tradition it merely documents or supports.

9-29

Sponsor / Partner Recognition Style Sheet

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Communications / Finance

Standard, consistent placement rules for sponsor/partner recognition (logo size and placement tiers by sponsorship level, required disclaimers) consistent with the Sponsorship Policy and Sponsorship Recognition Plan (Phase 4, 4-34; Phase 8, 8-38) — so recognition looks the same regardless of who's designing that week's material, and never implies CPD endorsement of a sponsor.

9-30

Public Contact & Response Service Standard

BPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Secretary / Communications

Sets a real target response time for public emails/messages (a common standard is 3-5 business days for a substantive reply, with an immediate acknowledgment sooner), routes inquiries to the right owner (a media question to 9-16's spokesperson, a records request to the Public Information procedure in Phase 5, 5-18, a complaint to the Complaint Intake Procedure in Phase 5, 5-46), protects the requester's own contact information from unnecessary exposure, and escalates anything unresolved past the target window rather than letting it quietly lapse.
