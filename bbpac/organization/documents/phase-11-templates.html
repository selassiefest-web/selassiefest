Phase 11 — Templates, Forms & Appendices

The master template library: 100 reusable forms, letters, checklists, logs and registers that every other phase in this library points back to. Blank, reusable, and the backbone of the whole document set.

Working document set — not legal advice, not a claim of recognition. Everything in this library is a governance/compliance draft prepared for the proposed 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council. Nothing here asserts that the Chicago Park District (CPD) has recognized a separate 63rd Street Beach PAC — see Phase 0 for that open question. Confirm current requirements with CPD and qualified counsel before filing, submitting, or formally adopting anything drawn from this page.
All 100 templates are catalogued below (title, classification, owner, purpose, status), but the actual fillable template layouts have not been built yet — that's a later pass. Phases 0–2 already have complete drafted text. See the Organization overview for the publishing roadmap.
