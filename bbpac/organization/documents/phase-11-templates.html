Templates, Forms & Appendices

The master template library: 100 reusable forms, letters, checklists, logs and registers that every other phase in this library points back to by number. Each one below carries its actual field list or short template body, not just a description — the policy context for each lives in the phase that first referenced it.

Working document set — not legal advice, not a claim of recognition. Everything in this library is a governance/compliance draft prepared for the proposed 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council. Nothing here asserts that the Chicago Park District (CPD) has recognized a separate 63rd Street Beach PAC — see Phase 0 for that open question. Confirm current requirements with CPD and qualified counsel, and use CPD's own current official forms where one exists (11-03, 11-57, 11-73), before filing, submitting, or formally adopting anything drawn from this page.
