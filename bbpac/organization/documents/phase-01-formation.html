Every document below is a working draft. None has been adopted by a board, filed with any agency, or confirmed by CPD unless specifically noted.

1-01

Current CPD Advisory Council Guidelines

CPD-RPending — Obtain Current Version

Owner: Secretary / CPD Liaison

This is not a document the PAC drafts — it is CPD's own controlling guidance, and the single most important reference in this entire library. Every other document on this site should trace back to whatever the current version of this guidance actually says, not the other way around.

What we do with it once obtained:

  • Download the current PDF/webpage directly from chicagoparkdistrict.com/advisory-councils and save a dated copy in the formation file and the physical PAC Binder.
  • Log the version/date in the Source Verification & Authority Log (Phase 11) so anyone reviewing this library later knows exactly which edition every downstream document was built against.
  • Flag any conflict between this page's drafts and a newer CPD edition immediately in the Recognition Conditions / CPD Revision Log (1-33).

Re-verify before every filing. CPD has revised this guidance before and can again; do not rely on a cached or remembered version when submitting anything to CPD.

1-02

Current CPD PAC Code of Conduct

CPD-RPending — Obtain Current Version

Owner: Secretary / Governance Committee

The Code of Conduct is the mandatory behavioral and independence standard CPD applies to every PAC member — it is what makes officer background checks, volunteer applications, and the "advisory only, cannot bind CPD" boundary non-negotiable rather than merely good practice. Obtain the current version directly from CPD; do not draft a substitute. Note that CPD's current guidance names two separate Codes that apply: "PACs and PAC members shall comply with the Facility Users Code of Conduct and the Park Advisory Council Code of Conduct" — this document covers the PAC-specific one; the Facility Users Code of Conduct is a distinct, separately-obtained document.

Distribution plan once obtained: every founding member and every elected officer signs an acknowledgment (see 3-14, Board / Officer Code Acknowledgment) before taking office or acting on the PAC's behalf. The Code is re-distributed and re-acknowledged annually, and immediately whenever CPD issues a revision.

This Code sits above every PAC-authored supplemental conduct policy (5-25) — where the two overlap, CPD's Code controls.

1-03

Letter of Intent to Form a PAC

CPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Founding Committee Chair

Do not send this letter until Gate 0 is resolved. Submitting a formal Letter of Intent before CPD has confirmed whether a separate 63rd Street Beach PAC is even possible risks presuming an answer CPD hasn't given, given the existing Jackson Park Advisory Council. The draft below is prepared now so it is ready the moment CPD's jurisdiction determination (see Phase 0) comes back favorably — or is adapted into a sub-council/committee request if it does not.

[Founding Committee Letterhead / Contact Information] [DATE] [Park Supervisor / Area Manager Name] Chicago Park District [Address] Re: Letter of Intent — Formation of a 63rd Street Beach Park Advisory Council Dear [Park Supervisor / Area Manager Name], On behalf of a group of neighbors, longtime beachgoers, and community stakeholders, I am writing to formally express our intent to form a Park Advisory Council dedicated to 63rd Street Beach, in partnership with the Chicago Park District and consistent with current PAC Guidelines. We understand that 63rd Street Beach is located within Jackson (Andrew) Park, which is served by the existing Jackson Park Advisory Council, and we are requesting CPD's guidance and written determination on whether a separately recognized 63rd Street Beach PAC is appropriate, or whether our community's interest in this specific site is better organized as a committee/sub-council of the Jackson Park Advisory Council or through another CPD-recognized structure. Pending that determination, we propose to hold an initial community meeting at [Location] on [Proposed Date/Time] — consistent with current CPD guidance recommending this meeting take place no sooner than 14 days and no later than 60 days from the date this letter is filed — to introduce this effort, explain the PAC formation process, and invite broad community participation. We would welcome the opportunity to discuss this letter with you or your designee at your earliest convenience. Respectfully, [Name] Founding Committee Chair [Contact Information]

Note the letter itself asks CPD for the Gate 0 determination rather than assuming it — this keeps the request accurate regardless of which way CPD rules.

CPD-RPending — Obtain Current CPD Form

Owner: Membership Lead

This is CPD's own official form, not a PAC-authored substitute — use whatever version CPD currently publishes. Each interested member completes one before being counted toward quorum, voting eligibility, or office eligibility.

Handling: collected applications are stored securely (physical binder plus access-controlled digital scan), tracked against the Founding Membership Eligibility Review Sheet (1-10), and never published publicly. 1-20

New PAC Registration Form

CPD-RPending — Obtain Current CPD Form; Submit Only After Gate 0

Owner: President / Secretary

CPD's own official registration form, completed only after officers are elected and bylaws adopted — and, per Gate 0, only once CPD's written jurisdiction determination is in hand or the approved alternative structure (sub-council, independent nonprofit) is clear. Submitting this prematurely is exactly the "assume recognition" mistake the Executive Findings warn against.

Attachments typically bundled with submission: Adopted Bylaws (1-18/2-02), Election Minutes (1-16), Partnership Pledge (1-21), evidence of Illinois not-for-profit status (1-22/1-23), and the Formation Submission Checklist (1-31) confirming everything is complete.

Partnership Pledge

CPD-RPending — Obtain Current Version; Submit Only After Gate 0

Owner: President / Secretary

CPD's Advisory Council webpage identifies the Partnership Pledge as part of the standard submission package alongside registration. Obtain the current version directly from CPD rather than drafting a substitute — this is a CPD-authored commitment document, and using anything else risks the submission being rejected as incomplete or nonconforming.

1-22

Illinois Not-For-Profit Articles of Incorporation (NFP 102.10 or successor)

CPD-RIL-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: President / Governance Lead / Counsel

CPD requires PACs to register and maintain Illinois not-for-profit status, and this is the founding filing that creates the corporation. Prepared only after the name is confirmed available (see Phase 0, G0-09) and after Gate 0 clarity, since the corporate name and purpose clause should reflect whatever structure CPD ultimately approves (separate PAC vs. "Friends of 63rd Street Beach" vs. other).

Key contents to draft with counsel: corporate name, registered agent/office, purpose clause (advisory/community-support purpose consistent with CPD's PAC framework, not a purpose that implies governmental authority), incorporator information, and dissolution clause (assets to another qualified nonprofit or governmental entity, standard for Illinois NFP/501(c)(3)-track corporations).

1-26

EIN Confirmation (IRS CP 575 or equivalent)

LEG-CBPProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Treasurer

Applied for online at IRS.gov immediately after Illinois incorporation is confirmed — the EIN is a prerequisite for opening a bank account, filing any federal exemption application, and completing vendor/grant W-9 requests. 1-27

CPD Volunteer Application — All Elected Officers

CPD-RPending — Obtain Current CPD Form

Owner: Each Officer / Volunteer Coordinator

Current CPD Code of Conduct requires every elected officer to complete CPD's Volunteer Application and pass a CPD-approved background check within the prescribed post-election window. This is not optional or PAC-discretionary — track submission and clearance for every officer using the tracking record below (1-28) starting the day of the election. Note CPD's current volunteer program is not one generic form: it is split into a Long Term Adult Volunteer Application, a Long Term Minor Volunteer Application, and a separate Group One Day Service Projects Volunteer Application. Officers specifically require the Adult (long-term) track — current CPD Compliance guidance states "All PAC officers and any PAC members handling finances or working with youth must submit an Adult Volunteer Application."

1-28

CPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Confidential log, access restricted to the Volunteer Coordinator and Secretary. The underlying background-check report itself is never retained by the PAC — only the pass/fail clearance status, consistent with data-minimization practice (5-34).

1-29

Volunteer Application / Background Check — Finance & Youth Roles

CPD-RProposed — Pending Board Adoption & CPD Determination

Owner: Volunteer Coordinator

Completed and cleared before the role begins, not retroactively; tracked on the same confidential status log as officer clearances.
