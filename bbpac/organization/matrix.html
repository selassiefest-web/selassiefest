The Mastermind Register

Master Due-Diligence Matrix

Every place a decision, record change, system change, approval, transfer, notification, or new policy could be required to split Bongo Beach off as its own Chicago Park District park — so a YES from CPD goes as smoothly as possible, because every department's needle in the haystack was already found.

This is a due-diligence sweep across every system, department, and record that touches Bongo Beach — governance, GIS, City of Chicago systems, CPD's internal administration, assets, finance, gift acceptance, sponsorship, contracts, permits, maintenance, beach operations, emergency response, insurance, signage, digital identity, records/FOIA, technology, volunteers, ADA, capital planning, environmental review, communications, stakeholders, the policy library, and the small things that get missed. 744 items across 27 domains, plus a 25-phase transition roadmap.

This page is read-only by design — here's how to actually work on an item. Every item has real fields waiting to be filled in as real research happens: Current state (where it lives today, inside Jackson Park's own systems), Proposed state (where it should live once Bongo Beach is its own park), and a checklist of concrete action items that close the gap between them. Join a section to get real write access, or head straight to My Section if you're already assigned — every change is reviewed by a team leader before it counts as done, and recorded permanently either way.
