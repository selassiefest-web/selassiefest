What to actually do on this page

Below is a list of items in your section. For each item, you're answering three questions in the boxes underneath it:

  1. Current state — What's true about this today, while 63rd Street Beach is still part of Jackson Park? (If you don't know yet, that's fine — leave it blank rather than guessing.)
  2. Proposed state — What should happen instead once Bongo Beach is its own separate park?
  3. Progress status — Where this stands: not started, in progress, complete, or reviewed — no change needed.

Fill in whatever you actually know — you don't have to answer everything at once, and you don't have to finish every item today. Click Save under an item when you're done with it; that item's history is updated permanently and immediately, and it's visible to everyone on the Version Log.
