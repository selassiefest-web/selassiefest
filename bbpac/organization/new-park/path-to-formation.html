Track A · Path to Formation

The Gate 0 Recognition Dossier

What it actually takes to ask the Chicago Park District, in writing, to recognize 63rd Street Beach as its own distinct park — seventeen items, tracked honestly as started, partial, or not yet begun.

Two tracks running through the same dossier

Everything below feeds a single goal: a written CPD determination that 63rd Street Beach is eligible for distinct park status (see the three possible outcomes). Some items build the evidentiary case itself; others build the community coalition behind it; a few overlap directly with Track B's own PAC-formation paperwork — those are flagged so nothing gets drafted twice. A separate kind of overlap runs through NP-01, NP-02, and NP-16 below: Track B's own Gate 0 (G0-01, G0-02, G0-03) asks CPD a related jurisdiction question through a different office. Each of those three documents carries a coordination note explaining how to keep both tracks' outbound letters from landing on CPD's desk unlinked. The current status of every item below, and every change ever made to it, is also live and public at the Version Log — this page is the narrative; that one is the record.

Historical and Cultural Significance Report

DOSSIERPartially drafted

NP-07

Representation Gap Analysis

DOSSIERCompleted

A document-by-document review of all 38 publicly posted JPAC meeting minutes (November 2022 – July 2026) against the scale and needs of 63rd Street Beach specifically. JPAC does engage substantively with the beach — a September 2025 resolution on the drum circle's parking dispute, a written reply from CPD's General Superintendent, and annual Earth Day cleanups — but that engagement clusters entirely in a 16-month window; the prior 22 months of minutes reviewed contain no substantive mention of the beach. The honest verdict is structural underrepresentation, not neglect. Full findings, the complete document tally, and the counter-evidence are on the working staff site.

Owner: Unassigned — no Founding Committee has formed yet; this was completed directly  ·  Status: Completed 2026-08-08 — see the full Representation Gap Analysis

NP-16

Formal Request for Written CPD Determination

DOSSIERQuestions drafted, not sent

The single most consequential ask in this whole dossier: get CPD to answer, in writing, a specific set of questions rather than leave the classification question ambiguous forever.

The six questions

  1. Does CPD consider 63rd Street Beach eligible for independent PAC representation?
  2. If not, is the reason solely its current inclusion in Jackson Park No. 19?
  3. What officer/body has authority to designate 63rd Street Beach as a distinct park or administrative park unit?
  4. What procedure is required to obtain such a designation?
  5. Can the General Superintendent, Legislative & Community Affairs, the PAC Governance Committee, or the Board approve a facility-specific PAC?
  6. If current policy prohibits it, what process is required to seek an amendment of that policy?

A written response converts an amorphous bureaucratic barrier into a specific decision that can be addressed, appealed politically, or presented to the Board.

Substantial overlap with Track B's G0-01. G0-01 asks CPD, in writing, essentially the same "can there be independent representation" question, just scoped to PAC jurisdiction rather than park classification. Sending both as fully separate, unreferenced letters risks CPD receiving what looks like two uncoordinated requests from the same committee. Either fold NP-16's six questions into a single joint transmittal alongside NP-01 and G0-01, or make sure each letter explicitly names and attaches the other before it goes out.
Owner: Unassigned — needs a Founding Committee volunteer + an LCA contact at CPD  ·  Next: Formal transmittal to CPD, alongside NP-01 and coordinated with Track B's G0-01

CPD Park-Classification Determination Record

DOSSIERTemplate ready — awaiting CPD response

What follows is the template it will be recorded in, mirroring Track B's CPD-Approved Representation Structure Memorandum (G0-04), so that whatever CPD decides about park classification is captured permanently, in writing, in one place — and so the two tracks' outcomes can be read side by side rather than filed separately.

Template

  • CPD department/contact and title providing the determination: [pending — expected Planning & Development and/or Law]
  • Outcome selected: [Outcome A: distinct park designation / Outcome B: facility-specific PAC without full park separation / Outcome C: policy amendment required / Declined — pending]
  • New park number / administrative unit ID assigned, if any: [pending]
  • Approved geographic/facility boundary: [references the final, reconciled version of NP-02 / G0-02]
  • Conditions attached, if any: [pending]
  • Effective date: [pending]
  • Relationship to G0-04's outcome: [record here whether this determination resolves, supersedes, or runs independently of G0-01's PAC-jurisdiction answer — an Outcome A here would likely make G0-01's sub-council/adopted-site options moot, per the Formation hub's "why both, why now" logic]

Once completed, this record controls how the rest of Track A refers to 63rd Street Beach's classification status, and it should be cross-filed with G0-04 in the Corporate Records Book / Minute Book (Phase 5) and the PAC Binder (Phase 6) so a reader sees both tracks' outcomes together, not just one.

Records & FOIA
FOIA

The Park-Number Paper Trail Requests

FOIANot yet filed

Before submitting the final recognition petition, a targeted public-records campaign should request:

  • The underlying CPD park/property master database — active and inactive records, with status, creation/retirement dates, predecessor/successor park numbers, and Board-action references, plus its audit trail
  • The Seneca Park #1242 assignment instrument
  • The 1999 Board/administrative record combining Rutherford, Sayre, and Rutherford Sayre — and which former park numbers were retired
  • Maggie Daley Park's 2014–15 administrative boundary/status records
  • The exact GIS/property record showing the Park #19 boundary and facility sub-parcels at 63rd Street Beach
  • The official Jackson Park No. 19 property/boundary map and historical maps showing 63rd Street Beach
  • Records on the administrative classification of 63rd Street Beach, and whether the Beach House/Beach ever had a separate park/property number
  • Organizational charts, capital expenditure history, and maintenance/work-order history for the beach
  • Any previous request to create a separate PAC or advisory body for 63rd Street Beach, and any internal CPD interpretation of the "one PAC per park" rule

This is how the campaign discovers whether the current classification is a historical inevitability — or simply an administrative convention that can be changed.
