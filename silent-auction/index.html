✅ Tax-deductible donation • All auction proceeds go directly to Ras Tafari Inc.'s year-round community programming — Ital Marketplace, Healing Grove, Higher Learning Roots, Heritage Village, and the SelassieFest Main Stage. Every bid builds the community.

How the Auction Works

1️⃣ Click "Bid Now" on an item to open its secure Zeffy bidding page → 2️⃣ Place your bid (or increase it any time before the auction closes) → 3️⃣ Highest bid at close wins. Bidding closes 8:00 PM, July 24, 2027 — the winner is announced live from the SelassieFest Main Stage!

💡 No chance involved — this is a straightforward auction, not a raffle. The highest bidder simply wins the item, the same as any other auction.
