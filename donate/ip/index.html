Give the rights, licenses and experiences that fuel a cultural organization: copyrights, royalties, software, event tickets and travel.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

How are these gifts valued?

Donors, with their tax advisor, determine the value. We send a written acknowledgment but do not set the value.

Can I donate software licenses?

Yes. Tell us the product and the terms, and we will confirm whether we can use it.

Do you accept royalty streams?

We review each one individually, including who pays and for how long.

Is my gift tax-deductible?
