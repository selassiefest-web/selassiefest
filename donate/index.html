Support SelassieFest

Ras Tafari Inc. is a cultural curator and community creator dedicated to preserving, celebrating, and advancing Caribbean and African Diaspora culture through education, arts, music, and community engagement. Your donation powers that work — one love, one festival at a time.

 Where Your Donation Goes

Festival Production

Sound systems, stages, and the roots reggae artists who bring SelassieFest to life.

Education & Heritage

Workshops, the Reasoning Circle, and preserving Rastafari and Caribbean history.

Youth Programs

Family-friendly activities, drumming circles, and the Youth Village experience.

Community Outreach

Supporting volunteers, vendors, and the broader Caribbean diaspora community in Chicago.

Tax receipts

Ras Tafari Inc will provide a written acknowledgment for any donation of $250 or more, as required by IRS regulations. For smaller gifts, a receipt is available upon request — just email selassiefest@gmail.com with your donation details.
