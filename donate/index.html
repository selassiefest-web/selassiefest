 Where Your Donation Goes

Festival Production

Sound systems, stages, and the roots reggae artists who bring SelassieFest to life.

Education & Heritage

Workshops, the Reasoning Circle, and preserving Rastafari and Caribbean history.

Youth Programs

Family-friendly activities, drumming circles, and the Youth Village experience.

Community Outreach

Supporting volunteers, vendors, and the broader Caribbean diaspora community in Chicago.
