Launching 2027

The SelassieFest Youth Scholarship

We are building a scholarship for young leaders in our community — and we're building it in the open. The first awards launch in 2027. You can help fund them today.

Help Fund It

Why

Youth empowerment has always been at the center of our mission — through the Youth Village, through mentorship, through Pickney Time Summer Camp. The scholarship is the next step: direct investment in young people who carry the message of African pride, cultural knowledge, and social justice forward into their education and their work.

What We're Building

Education Awards

Direct support toward tuition, books, and training for young community members.

Community-Funded

Built from donations designated to the scholarship fund — every gift tracked and reported.

Announced 2027

Eligibility, award amounts, and the application open with the 2027 season.

Ras Tafari Inc is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit — gifts to the scholarship fund are tax-deductible. When you donate, choose "Youth Scholarship" as your designation.
