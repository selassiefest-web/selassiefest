Heritage Partner

The Ethiopian World Federation

International Council · Harlem, NY · Founded 1937

The Ethiopian World Federation, Incorporated is a proud Heritage Partner of SelassieFest. Founded in Harlem in 1937 under the advisement of Emperor Haile Selassie I, the EWF has stood for over eight decades as a charitable organization dedicated to unity, solidarity, liberty, freedom and self‑determination for Black people worldwide — the same roots and spirit that ground SelassieFest's celebration of Rastafari culture and community.

Come learn about the EWF's history and mission directly from Scheril Murray Powell, Esq., International President of the Ethiopian World Federation — connect with her below.
