PharmaCann Distributes Access. That's What This Festival Is About.

There is a version of cannabis justice that lives on paper — in compliance reports, equity plans, and press releases. And then there is what actually happens when communities get real access to cannabis, real education about its history, real support for the people most harmed by prohibition.

PharmaCann has the network to make the second version real. Verilife dispensaries exist in the neighborhoods where SelassieFest's community lives, shops, and organizes. This is not a partnership we invented — this is a partnership that already exists in the streets and storefronts of Chicago.

What SelassieFest offers is the cultural event that puts PharmaCann's community commitment on a stage where it belongs — visible, documented, celebrated, and woven into the founding story of something Chicago will carry forward for years.
