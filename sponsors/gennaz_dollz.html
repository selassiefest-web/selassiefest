🌸 THE ESSENCE OF GENNAZ & DOLLZ

Where Beauty Meets Healing

Rooted in Chicago's vibrant South Side, Gennaz & Dollz is more than a beauty brand — it's a movement of transformation, healing, and cultural expression. Emerging from a powerful blend of post-operative wellness, body artistry, and community empowerment, the brand has become a trusted name in recovery and self-care.

With a mission to uplift confidence and holistic well-being, Gennaz & Dollz bridges the gap between modern aesthetics and ancestral rituals. “Where Recovery Meets Results” is not just a tagline — it’s a philosophy of care, combining skilled post-surgical massage, non-invasive body contouring, and a heart-centered approach to beauty.[reference:0][reference:1]

Healing is the first step toward true elevation — we nurture the body to restore the spirit.

Culture & Community First

Beyond the treatment room, Gennaz & Dollz pulses with reggae, dancehall, and diaspora energy. The brand is deeply connected to Chicago’s Caribbean music scene, collaborating with selectors and sound system events that honor roots culture and collective joy. This fusion of beauty, sound, and unity naturally aligns with SelassieFest's vision of One Love.

The brand also promotes women's leadership, self-sufficiency, and authentic expression — values amplified by the personalities behind Gennaz & Dollz, including wellness advocates and creative entrepreneurs. Their social presence emphasizes glamour, resilience, and the celebration of Black excellence.[reference:2][reference:3]

Through educational masterclasses, pop-up beauty experiences, and holistic health workshops, Gennaz & Dollz continuously builds bridges between artistry and healing, making wellness accessible and beautiful.

✨ CHICAGO ROOTS • GLOBAL VISION

The Nikki Sunshine Spirit

Nikki Sunshine — holistic coach, yoga teacher, podcaster, and healing advocate — represents the soulful energy that Gennaz & Dollz brings to the community. Her work in sacred anger, feminine leadership and inner transformation resonates with the brand’s commitment to spiritual and emotional healing.[reference:4] Alongside beauty treatments, Gennaz & Dollz encourages radical self-acceptance and personal growth, creating a sanctuary for everyone ready to shine brighter.

Feel, Pray, Love — The Healing Path

Nikki Sunshine’s podcast “Feel Pray Love” (formerly Relationship Badass Podcast) explores letting go of resistance and embracing authentic life. That same vulnerability and strength is mirrored in Gennaz & Dollz’s client-first approach: empowering individuals to reclaim their bodies, confidence, and voices.[reference:5] From post-op massage to wellness lounges, the brand creates a space where recovery meets ritual, and beauty is a form of prayer.
