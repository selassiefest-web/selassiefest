✦ Partnership Philosophy
GTI Doesn't Sponsor SelassieFest.
GTI Helps Build What SelassieFest Becomes.

There's a version of this conversation where we send you a PDF with logos and tiers. That's not this. Green Thumb Industries has already done the hard work of building cannabis culture credibility with conscious Chicago communities — through Good Green's justice-first identity, through the RISE neighborhood presence, through restorative justice investments that most cannabis companies only talk about.

SelassieFest is not asking GTI to associate with a cause. We're inviting GTI into a space that their work helped make possible — even before this festival existed. This is what it looks like when community investment meets community expression. One day. July 25th, 2026. Chicago. And GTI's name is on the door — not as a sponsor, but as a founding architect.
