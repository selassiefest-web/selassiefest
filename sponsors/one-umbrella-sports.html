Sports & Culture Partner

One Umbrella

Kingston, Jamaica · Sports & Entertainment

One Umbrella Sports & Entertainment is a proud sponsor of SelassieFest, bringing the same energy that transforms Reggae Boyz matches and Jamaica Premier League fixtures into high-energy live experiences. Their vision — "transforming lives, one sport at a time" — merges sports, music, and community engagement to amplify Jamaican excellence, the same spirit SelassieFest brings to the stage.

Beyond live events, the team runs One Umbrella TV, a digital home for Jamaican sports highlights and culture, and partners with organizations including the Jamaica Football Federation, Cavalier Football Club, and the Beenie Man Foundation.
