Partner Spotlight

Talvin White

CEO, Canarm Dispensary
“I've been arrested for cannabis multiple times, racially profiled — anything that could go bad with cannabis as a youth happened to me. When they made it legal, I knew I had to be part of the solution.”

Talvin White is a social equity license holder in Illinois, founder of Canarm Dispensary, and a passionate advocate for cannabis justice. His journey from being a victim of the War on Drugs to becoming a cannabis entrepreneur embodies the spirit of resilience and reclamation that SelassieFest stands for.

In a recent interview on The Fine Print, Talvin spoke about the challenges of navigating Illinois' social equity program, the importance of branding, and his vision for a more inclusive cannabis industry. His partnership with SelassieFest reflects our shared commitment to empowering communities that have been disproportionately harmed by prohibition.

Talvin is currently fundraising to open dispensaries in Elburn and North Chicago. He has decided not to pursue the special use permit in Harvard, though a cultivation operation there remains a possibility for the future.
