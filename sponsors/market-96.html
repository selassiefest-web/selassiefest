Neighborhood Dispensary Partner

Market 96

Est. 2023 · Oswego, Illinois

Market 96 was built on a simple idea: cannabis shopping should feel like visiting your neighborhood market. Founded by Blounts & Moore — a team of nine accomplished women partnering with like-minded, socially conscious entrepreneurs — the name honors 1996, the year of the first state-level cannabis legalization in the U.S.

Their Oswego, IL location welcomes every guest to feel comfortable, valued, and at home in their cannabis journey — the same spirit Market 96 brings to SelassieFest's marketplace.
