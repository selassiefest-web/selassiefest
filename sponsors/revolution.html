✦ Partnership Philosophy
Revolution Doesn't Just Sponsor SelassieFest.
Revolution Completes Its Own Name Here.

Most cannabis brands avoid the political weight of a word like "revolution." Revolution Cannabis picked it anyway. That's either marketing — or it's a promise. SelassieFest is where we find out.

The Ital Marketplace, the Higher Learning Roots stage, the Heritage Village — every corner of this festival exists because communities decided to stop waiting for permission and start building what they needed. That is the same spirit that gave Revolution Cannabis its name.

We're not offering Revolution a logo placement. We're offering them a stage where their name means something — where the word "revolution" is not a branding exercise but a living, documented, cultural declaration, shared with thousands of people who have been waiting their whole lives for this day.
