✦ The Mission Alignment
ChiCann Grows the Movement.
SelassieFest Is the Movement.

ChiCann has built its reputation by doing the unsexy, essential work: connecting communities to the cannabis industry through education, workforce development, equity advocacy, and sustainable practice. SelassieFest is that work made visible — a day-long public declaration that plant medicine, cultural heritage, and community healing belong together.

The attendees who arrive at SelassieFest at 6 AM for the Nyabinghi Groundation and stay until the final dub echo at 10 PM are not casual participants. They are the people ChiCann was built for. They distrust corporate cannabis. They research. They organize. They vote with their dollars and their loyalty. Reaching them through SelassieFest is not brand exposure — it is community trust, built in person, over 16 hours.

Why This Partnership Is Different
ChiCann already holds the mission. What SelassieFest adds is the amplification — a live, documented, community-witnessed expression of everything ChiCann stands for.

ChiCann's Presence Across Every Phase
The partnership doesn't begin on July 25th. It begins the moment the agreement is signed — and it doesn't end when the music stops.
Phase 01 — Agreement Through June
Community Announcement & Pre-Festival Seeding
ChiCann featured in SelassieFest's "Founding Partners" announcement across all social channels. Co-branded content distributed through SelassieFest's 5,000+ email subscriber base. ChiCann cross-promotes SelassieFest through its own community networks, newsletter, and partner organizations — activating both audiences simultaneously.
Phase 02 — July Run-Up
Education Content Series & Countdown Activation
ChiCann featured in the "Higher Learning Roots Preview" and "Reasoning Circle Spotlight" social content series. Joint education content — video shorts, workshop previews, equity conversation teasers — goes out across both organizations' channels. ChiCann's brand appears in all paid and organic SelassieFest promotional materials leading up to July 25th.
Phase 03 — July 25, 2026
Festival Day — Full Community Presence
ChiCann's dual anchor activates across Higher Learning Roots and the Reasoning Circle Pavilion. 16 hours of community engagement, education programming, equity conversations, workforce networking, and live advocacy. Documentary film crew captures ChiCann's team and programming throughout the day — real content, not branded footage.
Phase 04 — Post-Event
The Permanent Record & Community Growth
ChiCann featured in the official SelassieFest 2026 recap film — documentary content that travels through cultural media channels far beyond Chicago. Permanent Founding Partner placement on selassiefest.com. New community members recruited at the festival remain connected through ChiCann's programs.
