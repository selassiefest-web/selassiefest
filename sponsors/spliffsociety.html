Canafarm

Craft Cannabis. Community Roots.
Retail: North Chicago & Elburn, Illinois
✦ Meet the Founder

Canafarm is led by Talvin White, a lifelong Illinois cannabis advocate who grew up in North Chicago after moving from Gary, Indiana as a child. Talvin earned his Associate's degree in Art from the College of Lake County, where his mother served as regional coordinator for over three decades.

He comes from a long line of farmers and gardeners, and still keeps a full home garden across three family properties — lettuce, cucumbers, tomatoes, onions, garlic, sage, nettle, yarrow, pears, cherry trees, blueberries, sweet potatoes, and cannabis. He has been a registered medical cannabis patient in Illinois since 2016.

In 2023, Talvin won a social equity dispensary license in Illinois' final licensing lottery round — filing the application himself, without an attorney, for a $250 entry fee. Canafarm now holds retail permits in North Chicago and Elburn, Illinois, and is fundraising to open both locations.

"I'm about natural products with no sprayed-on terpenes, no pesticides, actual old-school strains with real terpene profiles — and being able to sell ounces in the dispensary instead of making people buy eighths all the time."

✦ Community & Education

Canafarm is working with the College of DuPage's agricultural and culinary programs to build out cannabis curriculum, with the goal of getting other colleges to accept transfer credit toward those courses. Talvin is also laying the groundwork for a cannabis industry incubator, aimed at bringing in support and funding for the next generation of cannabis professionals.
