SelassieFest & Spliff Society — united by a shared reverence for the craft of consumption, the rituals of community, and the deep cultural roots of the plant we celebrate. This partnership is built on mutual respect, creative freedom, and a commitment to education.
The Bond

What This Partnership Means

More than a collaboration — a shared commitment to elevating the culture, educating the community, and honouring every tradition that brought us here.

Education & Craft

Spliff Society brings world-class rolling expertise, historical knowledge, and hands-on pedagogy to SelassieFest's Higher Learning Roots village. Together we teach the next generation of practitioners.

Community & Belonging

We share a belief that the smoke circle is a sacred space — one of listening, sharing, and mutual respect. This partnership deepens the sense of belonging for everyone who steps into our village.

Heritage & Roots

From the Rastafari chalice to the global diaspora of smoking traditions, we honour the ancestors who passed down these practices. Spliff Society amplifies that lineage with integrity and heart.

Trust & Creative Freedom

We invite Spliff Society to design their partnership package exactly as they envision — their voice, their style, their imprint on the festival. This is your space to create.

Spliff Society

What They Bring to the Circle

Spliff Society isn't just a collective of expert rollers — they are cultural bearers, educators, and community builders. Here's what they contribute to SelassieFest.

Master Craft

From the classic blunt to the sacred chalice, from the perfect cone to the intricate cannagar — Spliff Society's mastery is unparalleled. Their workshops are pilgrimages for anyone serious about the craft.

Living History

Every session with Spliff Society is a journey through time — tracing the global roots of smoking culture, from Ethiopian clay pipes to Jamaican reasoning circles, from South Asian chillums to the modern American glass movement.

Pedagogy with Patience

Spliff Society teaches with generosity. Their instructors meet every student where they are — from the absolute beginner to the seasoned roller looking to refine their technique. No question is too small, no skill too basic.

Ceremony & Reverence

The chalice ceremony, the reasoning circle, the communal fire — Spliff Society understands that smoking is never just about consumption. It's about connection, reflection, and presence. They hold that space with dignity.
