✦ The Craft Alignment
nuEra Built Illinois Cannabis.
SelassieFest Is Illinois Cannabis Culture.

nuEra was one of the original medical licensees in Illinois — it has been in the room since before the room was legal. That kind of legacy carries weight, and it carries it differently than a publicly traded MSO ever could. It says: we were here before the money, and we'll be here after. SelassieFest's audience knows the difference between a brand that entered cannabis culture and a brand that grew up in it.

nuEra's RisEau cannabis beverages — born from a Chicago family partnership with Two Brothers Brewing — belong on the Ital Marketplace floor. nuEra's Alchemy brand — high-potency, purpose-driven, intentionally named — resonates with an audience who treats plant medicine as sacred. nuEra is not a cannabis company that does community. It is a community company that does cannabis. SelassieFest is where that distinction is finally seen at scale.
