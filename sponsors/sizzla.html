✦ SIZZLA YOUTH FOUNDATION — A LEGACY OF LOVE

Grammy-nominated artiste Sizzla Kalonji founded the Sizzla Youth Foundation to give back to his people — primarily in August Town, Kingston, and its environs. For over 30 years, the Foundation has partnered with New York and British Link Up to host annual back-to-school treats, providing school supplies, clothing, shoes, and scholarships to thousands of children. The Foundation also spearheads peace-building initiatives, including football tournaments and restorative justice programs that have helped keep August Town murder-free.

In 2025, the Foundation was honored with the Humanitarian Award at the Caribbean Music Awards for its steadfast dedication to youth empowerment, education, and community upliftment.
