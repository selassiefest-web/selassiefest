Cat's Cradle

A loop of colorful string is woven around a player's fingers.

Photo Wanted Be the first to send a real photo of Cat's Cradle being played.
Category
Creative Crafts
Materials
A 3-foot loop of yarn or twine string.

How It's Played

A loop of colorful string is woven around a player's fingers. Through a series of intricate loops, twists, and expansions, they create complex geometric patterns (like "Eiffel Tower" or "Fish Net") before transferring the design intact to a partner's fingers.

Cultural Roots

A globally shared game that, in Jamaica, encouraged quiet focus, digital dexterity, and collaborative geometry modeling.

Did You Play

Wherever you grew up — Kingston, Montego Bay, Brooklyn, Toronto, London, Miami — if you remember playing this, we want to hear from you. Send us your story, your photos, or an old video. Every submission helps preserve this game for the next generation.
