KUMAR FYAH

Grammy-Nominated Roots Reggae Vocalist

Jamaican-born roots reggae artist Kumar Fyah brings conscious storytelling, cultural pride, and classic reggae foundations to every performance. Grammy-nominated and recognized by the Recording Academy as one of the Top 10 Artists Shaping Contemporary Reggae, Kumar has collaborated with icons like Chronixx and Protoje, and has shared the stage with reggae royalty worldwide. His music blends conscious reggae with elements of ska, soca, and subtle R&B, creating a sound that is both deeply familiar and undeniably fresh. Known for his powerful live presence and intricate lyricism, Kumar addresses themes of social justice, spiritual awakening, and unity. “Let the music speak,” he says — and at Chicago Ignite, his voice will ignite the afternoon with roots, rhythm, and fire.

Selected highlights: Grammy nomination, Recording Academy Top 10 list, collaborations with Chronixx & Protoje, standout single "5000 Acres", and a reputation as a leader in reggae's revival movement.

ROHAN DA GREAT

Dancehall & Hip-Hop Fusion Artist

Brooklyn-born and now Carolina-based, Rohan da Great is a rising force in dancehall and reggae fusion. The son of legendary dancehall artist Curry Don, Rohan was raised on the sounds of Beenie Man, Pinchers, and Bob Marley, and has since developed his own signature blend of dancehall, R&B, and hip-hop. His single "Fallin'" went #1 on the iTunes Reggae Chart and charted in over 70 countries on Apple Music. He's shared stages with legends like Beres Hammond, Wayne Wonder, Masicka, and Baby Cham, as well as hip-hop heavyweights DaBaby and Dave East. With a DIY spirit and an ever-growing catalog of hits, Rohan's live set is high-energy, unapologetically authentic, and built for the dancehall. Don't miss his headlining performance at Chicago Ignite.

Key tracks: "Fallin'" (#1 iTunes Reggae), "Wicked" (#9 debut), "SummerTime", "HIT", and the EP SixLove.

TEAM US ENTERTAINMENT

Team US Entertainment is the engine behind Chicago Ignite — a Chicago-based promotions and event production company dedicated to amplifying Caribbean music, culture, and community. With deep roots in the city's South Side and a passion for authentic reggae, dancehall, and Afrobeats, Team US Entertainment curates experiences that bring together legendary artists, rising stars, and the vibrant local scene. Known for their hands-on approach, they work closely with artists like DJ Ringo, Money Movements, and Vampire Soundz, and have built a reputation for creating safe, high-energy spaces where the diaspora can connect, dance, and celebrate. This event is powered by their vision, along with the sonic mastery of Drifter Vibes Machine and the unstoppable energy of Vibes Machine Sound.

DJ RINGO — Chicago's Dancehall Selector

A cornerstone of the city's Caribbean party circuit, DJ Ringo is a mainstay at Reggae Sundays, the #1 weekly Caribbean party in Chicago held at BASSLINE. Known for his seamless blends of dancehall, reggae, soca, and Afrobeats, Ringo brings an infectious energy that keeps the dance floor packed. He'll be igniting the afternoon set at Chicago Ignite, spinning exclusive dubplates and keeping the vibrations high.

Chicago's Reggae & Dancehall Pulse

Weekly Caribbean Power

REGGAE SUNDAYS at BASSLINE (2239 S Michigan Ave) is Chicago's premier weekly Caribbean nightlife experience, featuring DJ Ringo and a rotating cast of special guests. It’s the heartbeat of the city's dancehall scene, bringing together reggae, dancehall, soca, and Afrobeats every week.

Cultural Recognition

Chicago's reggae legacy was formally recognized by Cook County, celebrating the life and accomplishments of Asrat Aemro Sellassie and the global impact of reggae in the Greater Chicagoland area. The city is also home to Dancing the Revolution, an acclaimed MCA exhibition exploring dancehall and reggaetón as forms of resistance and joy.

Legendary Local Acts

From the 30-year legacy of Rocket & Gizzae (co-founders of the iconic Wild Hare Reggae Club) to the eclectic fusion of Kumar Fyah and the rising dancehall star Rohan da Great, Chicago’s reggae scene is rich with history and future-forward energy.
