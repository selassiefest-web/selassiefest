🍽️ JAMAICAN MENU & PRICES

Fish Dinner

Choose your fish: Snapper, Parrotfish, Doctor Fish, Butterfish, Jackfish, Kingfish, Mackerel, or Grouper. Prepared escovitch, fried, or steamed. Served with rice & peas, festival, fried plantain, and coleslaw.

$22
Pre-order recommended

Ackee & Saltfish

Jamaica’s national dish – silky ackee sautéed with salted cod, onions, tomatoes, and spices. Served with boiled dumplings, fried plantain, and steamed cabbage.

$18
Breakfast or dinner favourite

Fish Tea (Soup)

Traditional Jamaican fish soup – a rich, peppery broth made with whole small fish (doctor fish, parrotfish, etc.), pumpkin, yam, chocho, and Scotch bonnet. Served with a side of festival.

$10
Comfort food classic
