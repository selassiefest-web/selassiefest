❓ Frequently Asked Questions

Is registration really required?
Yes, to help us plan capacity and materials. However, walk‑ins are absolutely welcome – you can register on the day at the welcome desk.
What prizes are awarded?
Winners receive special badges for their achievements, and overall event champions are awarded standard trophies. Every child gets a participation certificate.
Will there be food vendors?
Yes! Traditional Jamaican food and refreshments will be available for purchase on‑site.
What age group is this for?
All ages are welcome! Games and workshops are designed for children (5–15) but adults will love the nostalgia, music, and storytelling.
Is the event indoor or outdoor?
Mostly outdoor on our beautiful grounds. We have covered areas for workshops and storytelling. Bring a chair if you like!
