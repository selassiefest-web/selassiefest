Curry Fest Chicago

Saturday, October 10, 2026 · 10:00 AM – 10:00 PM
Presented by TRC Events
Date & Time
Saturday, October 10, 2026 — 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM
Venue
McCormick Place, 2301 S Lake Shore Drive, Chicago, IL 60616
Expected Attendance
10,000+ guests from around the world

Curry Fest Chicago is a global curry festival — 100+ curry traditions from every corner of the world, gathered under one roof at McCormick Place to beat the cold season. This isn't a single-cuisine food festival: it's a world tour of how different cultures build a curry, from the Caribbean to South Asia to East and Southeast Asia to Africa, brought together for one epic day.

One World. One Community. Endless Flavor. Our goal is simple: welcome curry lovers from every continent to Chicago, and send them home with a passport full of new favorite dishes.

World of Curry Pavilions

Curry Fest Chicago is organized as a true world tour — fourteen pavilions across all seven continents, each built around the ingredients, techniques, and history of that part of the world. Six continents host real vendors; the seventh, Antarctica, gets an honorary pavilion because even the most isolated place on Earth has a curry tradition worth telling.

🧊 Antarctica Honorary Pavilion

Curry at the End of the Earth

No vendors — just tradition

No countries, no booths — just decades of "Curry Night" keeping morale up through the polar winter at research stations from Japan's Showa Station to the US's McMurdo and South Pole stations. This pavilion honors the real Whole Onion Curry (丸ごと玉ねぎカレー) made famous by The Chef of South Polar — a reminder that curry has reached every continent on Earth, including the one with no permanent population at all.

India: The Curry Mecca

Every curry tradition at this festival — Caribbean, East Asian, African — traces a thread back to India. It's not one cuisine but a subcontinent of them: four distinct macro-regions, each with its own spice logic, its own festivals, and its own claim to what a "curry" really is. South Asian Avenue is built to honor that scale, not flatten it.

Flavor Profiles at a Glance

Kashmiri
Aromatic

Rich, deeply spiced, yogurt-based dishes with fennel and ginger — best known through Rogan Josh.

Punjabi
Rich & Creamy

The crowd-pleasing, buttery comfort food of North India — Butter Chicken and Dal Makhani.

Goan
Tangy & Fiery

Coastal, vibrant, Portuguese-influenced heat — Vindaloo and coconut-based fish stews.

Chettinad
Bold & Peppery

Layered South Indian heat built on fresh-ground black pepper, fennel, and curry leaves.

Kerala
Coastal & Coconut

Lighter and fragrant — seafood or vegetables in coconut milk, mustard seed, and tamarind.

Featured Dishes

A few of the dishes headlining this year's pavilions: Thai Green Curry, Japanese Katsu Curry, Jamaican Goat Curry, Malay Rendang, and South African Bunny Chow — alongside the full India regional lineup above.

Programming

The marketplace pairs the food with live cooking demonstrations from celebrity chefs representing each pavilion, a global spirits and rum-tasting lounge, and a main stage positioned away from the food stalls to keep the acoustics balanced throughout the pavilion.

From Kingston to Kolkata, Bangkok to Lagos — Curry Fest Chicago is built to welcome guests from every continent. With 100+ global curry connections under one roof, we're aiming to bring 10,000 attendees from around the world to McCormick Place for one epic day of food and community.
