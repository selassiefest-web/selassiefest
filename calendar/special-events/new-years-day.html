The Reggae & Champagne NYE Formal

An Upscale Caribbean New Year's Eve Celebration
Thursday, December 31, 2026 · Ringing in 2027
Date & Time
Thursday, December 31, 2026 — Evening into the Midnight Countdown
Venue
To Be Announced — Chicago
Dress Code
Island Black-Tie Required

The Reggae & Champagne NYE Formal is Chicago's premier upscale Caribbean New Year's Eve gala — an evening that trades the beach-party clichés for old-world island grandeur. This is a night where high-society elegance meets the warmth and rhythm of the islands: crystal chandeliers and candlelight, tropical florals threaded through the room, and a countdown to midnight unlike anything else in the city.

The Setting

Picture a ballroom dressed in deep emerald, royal navy, and warm gold, crisp white linens under flickering candlelight, and lush greenery — monstera leaves and orchids — woven between crystal and gold-rimmed glassware. It's the kind of room built for a grand entrance.

Premium Bar

Aged Caribbean rums served neat, alongside champagne cocktails infused with sorrel, passionfruit, and ginger.

Gourmet Caribbean Fusion

A plated dinner elevating island flavors — think coffee-rubbed tenderloin, butter-poached lobster, and a saffron-coconut reduction.

The Midnight Countdown

A champagne toast, a countdown, and a dance floor that stays packed well into the first hours of 2027.

The Musical Experience: Indika, Fully Orchestrated

This is the moment the night is built around. Indika takes the stage not as a standalone reggae band, but as the center of a full orchestral reimagining — reggae and rocksteady classics rearranged for strings, horns, and woodwinds, performed live alongside the band for the entire set. It's not a jazz opener handing off to a reggae act; it's one continuous, genre-crossing performance built specifically for this stage, this city, and this night.

Strings

Two violins, viola, and cello — bringing a soaring, emotional register reggae rarely gets to explore live.

Horns

Trumpet and trombone — rooted in ska and rocksteady tradition, so the horns feel authentic to the genre, not bolted on.

Woodwinds

Flute and clarinet coloring the melodic lines between verses — the details that make an arrangement feel composed, not improvised.

Musical Direction

A dedicated arranger reworks the charts so the orchestra and the band breathe as one unit — the difference between a gimmick and a genuine reimagining.

Expect Indika's own catalog and reggae/rocksteady staples completely reimagined — full-bodied, cinematic, and built for a room this elegant. This is a one-night arrangement you won't hear anywhere else in Chicago.

Dress Code

Island Black-Tie Required. Tuxedos and elegant evening gowns — vibrant tropical flair welcome.

Ring Out 2026 in Style

Whether you're closing out a big year or starting fresh, this is the room to do it in. One night, one countdown, one unmistakably Caribbean way to welcome the new year.
