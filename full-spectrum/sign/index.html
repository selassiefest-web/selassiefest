To the Chicago Park District and the Office of Mayor Brandon Johnson:

We, the undersigned residents of Chicago's South Side, support Full Spectrum at Rainbow Beach, a free, year-round music and culture program for young people and families proposed by Ras Tafari Inc., a South Side 501(c)(3).

We ask the Chicago Park District to approve it, beginning with the Rainbow DJ Lab: a free DJ class every Tuesday from 4 to 6 pm in the Rainbow Beach fieldhouse, starting January 12, 2027, followed in summer by Rainbow Wednesdays, free family music nights on the beach.

A program like this keeps our park busy all year, gives our young people skills, mentors and a stage close to home, and costs our families nothing.

Signed by the residents below.

Who can sign, and how we check

We follow the same standards Chicago uses for signatures on a candidate's petition:

  1. You sign for yourself only. Each person signs in their own hand. No one can sign for a spouse, a parent or a neighbor.
  2. You live on the South Side. Your full street address is matched against public address records and placed in its Chicago community area.
  3. One signature per person. A second signature by the same person, at the same address or from the same email, is not accepted.
  4. You confirm by email. We send a link; an unconfirmed signature is never counted.
  5. Every signature is reviewed. Our team checks each one, line by line. Where possible we check voter registration at your address with the Chicago Board of Election Commissioners. Signatures that are not genuine, outside the area or repeated are struck, and the reason is recorded.
