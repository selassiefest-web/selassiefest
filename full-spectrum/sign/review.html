How to rule on a signature (the same tests used for petition signatures):
  • Genuine and own person: a real signature, not printed initials, and not one hand signing for several people (compare signatures at the same address).
  • Residence: the matched address and community area are on the card. Off the South Side cannot reach this queue.
  • Voter registration: open the Chicago Board lookup and search the signer's name and address. Found → Accept (registered voter). Not found, or you didn't check → Accept as resident, which is counted separately.
  • Strike anything that fails, with the reason. Every ruling is logged with your name.
