=== SLIDE 01 - Full Spectrum at Rainbow Beach ===
Thank you for taking a few minutes with us.
This is Full Spectrum at Rainbow Beach: one program with five parts.
DJ classes, Rainbow Wednesdays, live stage shows, festivals, and fall and holiday parties.
All of it free, year-round, and built for every age and every background, right here on the South Side lakefront.

=== SLIDE 02 - The big free music is downtown. South Shore is nine miles away. ===
Here's the problem.
Chicago's biggest free music happens downtown, at Grant Park and the lakefront around it.
For a family at Seventy-Seventh Street, that is about nine miles each way.
Fares or parking for everyone, an hour or more of travel, and a late ride home with small children or grandparents.
So many families make that trip once a summer, and many never do.

=== SLIDE 03 - Bring it home, and let neighbors be the performers, not just the audience ===
Our answer is simple: bring it home.
Rainbow Beach already has what those downtown stages have. The lake, open lawn, a summer crowd, and a neighborhood full of musicians.
And here, residents are not only the audience. They are the performers.

=== SLIDE 04 - DJ classes, for every age ===
Part A is DJ classes.
Free, hands-on, and year-round, indoors when the beach is closed.
There are tracks for little kids learning with a parent or grandparent, for teens, for grown-ups and elders, and a live lab for young musicians.
We supply the gear, so cost is never the barrier.
And every class ends with a real set in front of a real crowd.

=== SLIDE 05 - Rainbow Wednesdays: fully planned and ready ===
Part B is Rainbow Wednesdays, and it is already fully planned.
Fourteen free Wednesday evenings on the beach, June through Labor Day week.
Each Wednesday is one color of the rainbow and one sound.
Young DJs and young musicians perform, mentored by experienced Chicago DJs, and every night peaks with the Theme Clash, where a theme everyone knows collides with a song everyone loves.

=== SLIDE 06 - Live stage shows: DJs with local and major recording artists ===
Part C is live stage shows.
Concert nights where DJs perform alongside live musicians, local recording artists, and a major recording artist as headliner.
Every lineup opens with young performers who came up through the classes and Rainbow Wednesdays.
So a teenager who learned to blend in February can open for a national act in August.

=== SLIDE 07 - Festivals, including SelassieFest ===
Part D is festivals.
Full days of music, food, culture and family activities.
Ras Tafari Incorporated already produces one: SelassieFest, Chicago's roots reggae and cultural festival. Its 2027 venue is not yet confirmed, and Rainbow Beach is under consideration.
At Rainbow Beach, that means an end-of-summer festival, either SelassieFest itself or a SelassieFest community day, and heritage days: Juneteenth, International Reggae Day, Belize Day, Jamaica's sixty-fifth Independence, the Marcus Garvey Festival, and Trinidad and Tobago Independence.
And the strongest young performers of the season are offered a set at SelassieFest itself.

=== SLIDE 08 - Fall and holiday parties, October through December ===
Part E keeps the park alive when the beach closes.
From October through December, a season of free family parties.
A Kids Kostume Party. An Icons Party for Prince, Michael Jackson, Bob Marley, and Chicago legends like Curtis Mayfield and Donny Hathaway. A Thanks and Giving potluck that honors our elders. A Holiday Steppers set, because steppin' is a South Side tradition.
Then a Christmas party and holiday market, a Kwanzaa night, and a Noon Year's Eve countdown for the little ones.
And the DJ class students play for their own families.

=== SLIDE 09 - Five parts, one ladder ===
Here is why these parts belong together. They are a ladder.
Learn in the classes. Play at Rainbow Wednesdays. Open for the artists at the stage shows. Headline at a festival.
A neighbor can start the year as a spectator and end it on a festival stage, without ever leaving the park.

=== SLIDE 10 - Diversity is built into the format, not added at the end ===
Diversity of participants and audience is built into the format.
Every age, from young children to elders.
Every level, from first-timers to major recording artists.
Every musical culture. Every genre is a color, and music belongs to whoever loves it.
A child can play her grandmother's music in a way her grandmother has never heard it.

=== SLIDE 11 - Everyone is welcome, and here is how we make it true ===
Everyone is welcome at Full Spectrum, whatever their race, background, religion, gender, ability, age or income.
Saying it is the easy part, so here is how we make it true.
Everything is free. We provide the gear. There is accessible seating and sign language interpretation, sound kept to park limits, materials in English and Spanish, and a clean, family-friendly policy at every event.

=== SLIDE 12 - Most young people who step up are boys. We are changing that. ===
Here is something we have learned.
When we open the decks to young people, most of the ones who step up are boys.
Girls are just as interested and just as talented, but far fewer come forward.
So Full Spectrum makes girls a deliberate focus. We call it Empress Decks.
A girls-only class track taught by women DJs. A girl on every Rainbow Wednesday lineup, and a girl opening every stage show.
And we will count it, every season, so the park can see the gap closing.

=== SLIDE 13 - Approve Full Spectrum once, then schedule each part as it's ready ===
So here is our ask.
Review all five parts now, and recognize Full Spectrum as one program at Rainbow Beach Park.
After that, adding a class, a stage show or a festival day is a scheduling conversation with park staff.
Dates, a site plan, insurance and any permit the event needs, confirmed each time.
Not a new proposal, and not a new approval.

=== SLIDE 14 - No cost to the park to approve. The same standards at every event. ===
Approving the program does not commit the park to any cost.
Each part is funded on its own, and none starts until its funding is in place.
And at every event, the park can count on the same standards: full insurance, background checks, clean lyrics, sound kept to limits, and a report back after every season.

=== SLIDE 15 - Let's bring the full spectrum to Rainbow Beach ===
Full Spectrum is a Ras Tafari Incorporated program.
Our next steps are a review with park leadership and a walk-through of the spaces.
Thank you. We would love to bring the full spectrum to Rainbow Beach.
