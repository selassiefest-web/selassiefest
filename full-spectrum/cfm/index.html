A proposal for Local 10-208 and the Music Performance Trust Fund

Live reggae and young DJs, free on the South Side lakefront.

Rainbow Wednesdays is 14 free summer evenings at Rainbow Beach Park, where a professional live band plays with a vocalist and young DJs mix in with the live instruments. We would like Local 10-208 to bring one or two of those nights to the MPTF, starting with Wednesday, July 21, 2027.

Why this belongs with MPTF

A free summer series in a public park, with music as the only draw.

We read the MPTF’s own pages before writing to you. The community events it supports are “admission-free live music presented in parks and public spaces,” including summer series, and most are started by the local union working with parks programs and community leaders. That is what Rainbow Wednesdays is.

Rule 1. The performance is free and open to the public.
Rule 2. Music is the prime reason people are at that place at that time.

Rainbow Beach is a Chicago Park District park on the South Side lakefront. Our partnership proposal is with the Park District and is under review, so every date here is provisional until the Park District confirms.

The night

Wednesday, July 21, 2027: Roots & Culture.

Roots reggae in the sound-system tradition, three days before SelassieFest. A live band and a lead vocalist share the stage with young DJs who mix in with the live instruments, in the spirit of acid jazz, where a live band and a DJ build the night together.

What the audience gets

A real band with guitar, keys and a lead singer (the full lineup to be confirmed with the band), playing roots, lovers rock and dancehall. Then moments where the DJ and the singer play off each other: a rewind, a face-off, a countdown, a dubplate with the singer’s name on it.

What it does for young people

Young DJs from our free Tuesday class sit in with working professionals and learn what it is to lock in with live players. They are not part of this request. The MPTF funds would pay the band.

The band

Ifficial Reggae Movement.

A Chicago-area reggae band that, in its own words, has been “rocking shows all over the Midwest for decades” while creating new and original music (ifficialreggae.com). Lead singers share the front line. The lineup for July 21 will be confirmed with the band.

What we take on as sponsor

Submit our W-9 and sign the MPTF terms. Pay the leader the full amount on time, so musicians, dues and pension are covered. Send any pension and health-and-welfare payments. Provide photographs. Put the MPTF logo on flyers and announcements.

How the money moves

You apply to the MPTF on our behalf. The MPTF sends the grant check to us after the performance. We pay the leader the full amount.

Timeline, 2027 (provisional)

What has to happen, and by when.

  1. Now: you tell us whether this fits, who the leader should be, and what you need from us.
  2. By Wednesday, June 9: everything in your office, which is six weeks before the performance. You also wrote 45 days, which would be Sunday, June 6, so we are planning to the earlier date until you tell us which governs.
  3. Wednesday, July 21, 6:00 to 8:30 pm: the performance at Rainbow Beach Park, if the Park District has confirmed the season.
  4. After the performance: the MPTF check comes to us and we pay the leader in full.

You wrote that you may be able to co-sponsor one or two events. We would welcome your guidance on a second night and will propose a date once the Park District confirms the season.

Two questions for you

What we would like to learn from you.

  1. Are band members who are not Local 10-208 members acceptable? You wrote that membership is preferred, not required. We want to do this properly.
  2. Which deadline governs, six weeks or 45 days? We will plan to whichever you tell us.
