Outbound journey: day-by-day activity, time zones and what happens to the body

Illustrative Sydney to Vancouver to Chicago routing, leaving Thu Mar 25. The flight times are an example (about 20.5 hours door to door); real times come from the ticket. In late March Sydney is on daylight time (UTC+11), Vancouver on daylight time (UTC-7) and Chicago on daylight time (UTC-5), so Chicago is 16 hours behind Sydney. Crossing that many zones, most people adapt as an 8-hour delay of the body clock, which is usually easier than an advance, and settle at roughly an hour or so a day.

7Entry, legal and insurance

Check before committing money: Australians visiting for business or activities usually travel on the Visa Waiver Program with an ESTA. A visitor may not do paid work in the US. A volunteer guest class with expenses covered is normally treated differently from paid work, but do not pay an honorarium or fee until immigration status is confirmed with an immigration attorney or CBP guidance. Fees and rules change; verify each line on the official sites (esta.cbp.dhs.gov, travel.state.gov).
