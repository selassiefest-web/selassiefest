3. Press release

Australian inventors bring a new DJ instrument to Chicago kids at Rainbow Beach

Free Rainbow DJ Lab class on March 30 puts Sway, a MIDI controller, in young hands

CHICAGO, [date]: Two music inventors will fly from Australia to teach Chicago students how to play hot cues and loops on Sway, their new MIDI controller, at a free Rainbow DJ Lab class at Rainbow Beach Park on Tuesday, March 30, 2027, from 3:30 to 7:00 pm.

Isaac Jack and Jeremy Buckley of Audima Labs designed Sway to let anyone play expressive music with their hands. Ras Tafari Inc., the nonprofit behind SelassieFest, invited them to lead Mission 9 of the DJ Lab, a program that has no cost to families and moves each child through 14 Missions at their own pace.

"[Quote from Stephen Henry about why this matters to the South Side: Stephen to write and approve.]"

"[Quote from Isaac Jack or Jeremy Buckley about teaching kids in Chicago: Audima to write and approve.]"

The Rainbow DJ Lab opens Tuesday, January 12, 2027, and runs every Tuesday from 4 to 6 pm with 12 stations and resident instructors. It grows out of SelassieFest, a free roots reggae and cultural festival that drew about 273 people on July 25, 2026, and honors the name of Rainbow Beach, named for the 42nd Rainbow Division.
