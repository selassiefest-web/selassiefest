Proposed guest lesson · Hot Cues and Loops

One Loop, Two Ways

What changes when your hand becomes part of the mix? We’d love to shape this lesson with you.

  1. Make a loop

    A student grabs a few beats on the DJ controller and plays the loop for the group.

  2. Shape the sound

    You show what happens when you lift or glide a hand above Sway to sweep a filter. Share one design decision that made the gesture work.

  3. Play it both ways

    Students take turns with Sway, play the original and altered loop, and describe what they heard.
