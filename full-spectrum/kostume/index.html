Halloween evening · Sunday, October 31, 2027 · Rainbow Beach Park

Kids Kostume Party

Wear your art. A free Halloween party where South Shore's kids are the artists, in the Rainbow Beach fieldhouse.

A proposed Full Spectrum event from Ras Tafari Inc., a South Side 501(c)(3)

The idea

Creative over scary.

Most Halloween costumes come from a bag. Ours come from a kid's imagination. Every child makes something, wears it with pride, and walks the runway in front of the whole room.

The party is the heart of the night. Everything around it is art.

The party

Every artist gets an entrance.

  • The Art Parade: each child walks the runway to their own entrance song, launched live by DJ Lab students
  • Story Art: costumes that tell a story: a song, a book character, a hero from Black history, a purple turtle
  • Portrait Art: every child leaves with a printed portrait in their costume
  • Dancing to a family-friendly DJ set, with clean lyrics only
Judged by local artists, a teen DJ and a child care teacher

The Art Awards.

Wearablebest costume made by hand
Lightbest glow after dark
Storybest costume that tells a story
Familybest family or group art

Plus Best Little Artist (5 and under) and People's Choice, voted by everyone in the room.

Prizes and giveaways

Every child leaves an artist.

  • Winners: art supply kits, 75th Street store coupons, and their song opening the next Rainbow Wednesday
  • Every child: a glow bracelet, a printed portrait and an Artist certificate
  • Neighborhood prizes: donated by local businesses
Safe and welcoming

Built for the littlest artists.

  • Every child comes with a grown-up
  • Hours approved in advance by the Chicago Park District
  • Indoors for the party, in the Rainbow Beach fieldhouse
  • Creative over scary: no gore, so the youngest feel welcome
Who makes it happen

A whole neighborhood in costume.

  • Local artists judge the awards
  • Child care centers and schools bring their families
  • Rainbow Beach DJs: DJ Lab students play the party
  • Open to the whole neighborhood, with any partner role only if the Chicago Park District approves
