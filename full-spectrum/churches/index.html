Full Spectrum at Rainbow Beach · for South Side churches

Gospel, full spectrum.

From classic gospel to neo-gospel, and every genre it can reach, the music of the church belongs in Full Spectrum. Ras Tafari Inc. is bringing a free youth DJ class and a summer of family music nights to Rainbow Beach Park in 2027, and we would like your church in the story. Not as an audience. As the source.

The idea

The whole gospel, across the whole spectrum.

Full Spectrum is built on one rule: every genre is a color, and every color gets its week. Gospel belongs in all of them. We mean the real range, from the spirituals and hymns your grandparents sang, through classic quartet and choir gospel, to neo-gospel and praise music today, and then gospel lyrics and messages carried into every other sound a young person loves. Children learn the music by mixing it, and a message that was written for Sunday gets to meet a new dance floor.

Why now

Gospel DJ parties are on the rise.

Young believers want a night out that is joyful, sober and safe, and organizers around the world are building it: a real DJ, a real dance floor, and gospel at the center. Faith-centered, alcohol-free day parties and club nights are spreading, and Chicago, the city of Dorsey, Mahalia and house music, has the deepest claim on the idea.

Africa

Sober, faith-centered day parties and Gospel House events are growing in Lagos and Nairobi, and in Ghana DJ Delin’s alcohol-free “The Gathering” (Nov 2025) put a gospel DJ in a nightlife-style room.

London

Praise Party is a monthly gospel club night, on the first Friday of every month.

Detroit

The Gospel Melodies Day Party & Brunch, with live DJs, is scheduled for Sept 19, 2026.

Chicago

Full Spectrum can be the neighborhood’s own version: free, family friendly, church-rooted and open to every gospel sound, with children behind the decks.

Sources: OkayAfrica (“Gospel House: the sober day party movement”), 3Music TV (DJ Delin, Nov 2025), A Step Forward (Praise Party), My Christian Events (Detroit listing). Press coverage is anecdotal; no one has measured the overall growth.

What the church gets

Three things your young people can do with us.

Learn

A free DJ class every Tuesday, 4 to 6 pm, opening Jan 12, 2027. Children join any Tuesday and work through 14 Missions at their own pace. Girls are especially wanted, and church youth groups are one of the first places we are inviting.

Play

Rainbow Wednesdays on the beach all summer: free, alcohol-free, clean lyrics only, family friendly. A youth DJ from your congregation can earn a slot in the Theme Clash.

Be heard

The strongest players open for the guest artists, and the best of them play the end-of-summer festival. Your children, on a real stage, in their own neighborhood.

New for churches

Four ways to bring the gospel into the room.

Your choir, in a kid’s remix

With your permission, a choir or praise team records a few phrases, a hook and a hand-clap. Students learn to loop and layer them over any beat they choose: house, hip-hop, reggae, Afrobeats, R&B. Their first mix carries your congregation’s own voice, and the recording stays under your church’s control.

Gospel at Sunset · Aug 4, 2027

A Rainbow Wednesday devoted to gospel in every style, 7 pm on the lake. Choirs and soloists are invited to sing live over a youth DJ’s set, from a hymn over a slow groove to a praise anthem over a dance beat. This is where a young vocalist and a young DJ meet.

Thanks & Giving, with the elders

Before Thanksgiving, a community potluck and food drive honors the neighborhood’s elders, with a Generations Back-to-Back set: a youth DJ and an elder trading records, classic gospel on one side and neo-gospel on the other. Many of those elders learned their music in your pews.

A Christmas DJ party at your church

An alcohol-free family Christmas party in your own fellowship hall, with a youth DJ spinning the full spectrum of the season: carols and Christmas gospel, soul and R&B classics, reggae and Caribbean Christmas, Afrobeats and house. Children DJ beside the choir, and the congregation brings the food and the joy.

Our promise

A safe, sacred-friendly room.

No alcohol at any program. Clean lyrics and radio edits only. Every instructor is background-checked and works to the Chicago Park District’s standards. Every church decides for itself how, or whether, its name and music are used, and no recording of your choir leaves your hands without your yes. We teach the music of the neighborhood and we do not ask anyone to leave their faith at the door or to share ours.

How to say yes

Pick any one. Each is small.

  1. Share one invitationWe write a short, plain invitation to the free DJ class. You read it, and if you like it, you pass it to your youth leader and families. That is all.
  2. Put up a flyerWhere families will see it: the bulletin board, the narthex or the youth room.
  3. Introduce us to your choir directorFor the choir-in-a-remix idea and Gospel at Sunset. A ten-minute call with the director settles whether it fits.
  4. Host a Christmas DJ partyYour fellowship hall, an afternoon or evening in December, an alcohol-free family party. We bring the DJs and the music; you bring the people.
  5. Sponsor a Child DJSupport one student’s place in the free class, matched privately by the program. Any gift also helps equipment, meals and Rainbow Wednesdays.
