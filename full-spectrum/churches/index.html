Full Spectrum at Rainbow Beach · an invitation to South Side churches

Gospel, full spectrum.

From the spirituals and hymns that carried us, to classic gospel, to neo-gospel and praise today, and gospel’s message in every sound a young person loves. Ras Tafari Inc. is building a free youth DJ class and a summer of family music nights at Rainbow Beach Park for 2027, and your church is the source of the music.

“Praise him with the timbrel and dance … Let every thing that hath breath praise the LORD.”Psalm 150, verses 4 and 6

Every church is welcome here.

Baptist, AME, COGIC, Catholic, Methodist, Presbyterian, Lutheran, Pentecostal, Apostolic, nondenominational, storefront and cathedral alike. Your tradition, your sound, your call.
The idea

The whole gospel, across the whole spectrum.

Full Spectrum is built on one rule: every genre is a color, and every color gets its week. Gospel belongs in all of them. Children learn music by mixing it, so a hymn, a quartet harmony or a praise chorus can meet a new beat and a new generation. Seven windows, one light:

Why now

Gospel DJ parties are on the rise.

Young believers want a night out that is joyful and safe, and organizers around the world are building it: a real DJ, a real dance floor, and gospel at the center. Chicago, the city of Dorsey, Mahalia and house music, has the deepest claim on the idea.

Africa

Faith-centered day parties and Gospel House events are growing in Lagos and Nairobi, and in Ghana DJ Delin’s “The Gathering” (Nov 2025) put a gospel DJ in a nightlife-style room.

London

Praise Party is a monthly gospel club night, on the first Friday of every month.

Detroit

The Gospel Melodies Day Party & Brunch, with live DJs, is scheduled for Sept 19, 2026.

Chicago

Full Spectrum can be the neighborhood’s own version: free, family friendly, church-rooted and open to every gospel sound, with children behind the decks.

Sources: OkayAfrica (“Gospel House: the sober day party movement”), 3Music TV (DJ Delin, Nov 2025), A Step Forward (Praise Party), My Christian Events (Detroit listing). Coverage is anecdotal; no one has measured the overall growth.

What your young people get

Learn it. Play it. Be heard.

Learn

The Rainbow DJ Lab

A free DJ class every Tuesday, 4 to 6 pm, opening Jan 12, 2027. Children join any Tuesday and work through 14 Missions at their own pace. Girls are especially wanted, and church youth groups are one of the first places we are inviting.

Play

Rainbow Wednesdays

Free family music nights on the beach all summer, with clean lyrics only. A youth DJ from your congregation can earn a slot in the Theme Clash.

Be heard

The stage

The strongest players open for the guest artists, and the best of them play the end-of-summer festival. Your children, on a real stage, in their own neighborhood.

Made with the church

Four ways to bring the gospel into the room.

Choir & praise team

Your choir, in a kid’s remix

With your permission, a choir or praise team records a few phrases, a hook and a hand-clap. Students learn to loop and layer them over any beat they choose: house, hip-hop, reggae, Afrobeats, R&B. Their first mix carries your congregation’s own voice, and the recording stays under your church’s control.

Wed, Aug 4, 2027 · 7 pm

Gospel at Sunset

A Rainbow Wednesday devoted to gospel in every style, on the lake. Choirs and soloists are invited to sing live over a youth DJ’s set, from a hymn over a slow groove to a praise anthem over a dance beat. This is where a young vocalist and a young DJ meet.

Week before Thanksgiving

Thanks & Giving, with the elders

A community potluck and food drive honors the neighborhood’s elders, with a Generations Back-to-Back set: a youth DJ and an elder trading records, classic gospel on one side and neo-gospel on the other. Many of those elders learned their music in your pews.

December · at your church

A Christmas DJ party

A family Christmas party in your own fellowship hall, with a youth DJ spinning the full spectrum of the season: carols and Christmas gospel, soul and R&B classics, reggae and Caribbean Christmas, Afrobeats and house. Children DJ beside the choir, and the congregation brings the food and the joy.

Our promise

Your church, honored.

Clean lyrics and radio edits only. Every instructor is background-checked and works to the Chicago Park District’s standards. Every church decides for itself how, or whether, its name and music are used, and no recording of your choir leaves your hands without your yes. We teach the music of the neighborhood, and we ask no one to leave their faith at the door or to share ours.

How to say yes

Pick any one. Each is small.

  1. Share one invitationWe write a short, plain invitation to the free DJ class. You read it, and if you like it, you pass it to your youth leader and families. That is all.
  2. Put up a flyerWhere families will see it: the bulletin board, the narthex or the youth room.
  3. Introduce us to your choir directorFor the choir-in-a-remix idea and Gospel at Sunset. A ten-minute call settles whether it fits.
  4. Host a Christmas DJ partyYour fellowship hall, an afternoon or evening in December. We bring the DJs and the music; you bring the people.
  5. Sponsor a Child DJSupport one student’s place in the free class, matched privately by the program. Any gift also helps equipment, meals and Rainbow Wednesdays.
