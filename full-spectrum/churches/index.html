Full Spectrum at Rainbow Beach · for South Side churches

Gospel House, from the pews.

The choir loft, the Hammond organ and the Sunday-morning call and response are where Chicago music began. Ras Tafari Inc. is bringing a free youth DJ class and a summer of family music nights to Rainbow Beach Park in 2027, and we would like your church in the story. Not as an audience. As the source.

The idea

Teach the line from the church choir to the dance floor.

Thomas A. Dorsey built gospel music on the South Side. Mahalia Jackson, the Staple Singers and Curtis Mayfield carried it into soul. Chicago then built house music out of that soul, and the dance floor still sounds like a choir. Our DJ class teaches that line to children, because a kid who knows where the music came from plays it with respect.

What the church gets

Three things your young people can do with us.

Learn

A free DJ class every Tuesday, 4 to 6 pm, opening Jan 12, 2027. Children join any Tuesday and work through 14 Missions at their own pace. Girls are especially wanted, and church youth groups are one of the first places we are inviting.

Play

Rainbow Wednesdays on the beach all summer: free, alcohol-free, clean lyrics only, family friendly. A youth DJ from your congregation can earn a slot in the Theme Clash.

Be heard

The strongest players open for the guest artists, and the best of them play the end-of-summer festival. Your children, on a real stage, in their own neighborhood.

New for churches

Four ways to bring the gospel into the room.

Your choir, in a kid’s remix

With your permission, a choir or praise team records a few phrases, a hook and a hand-clap. Students learn to loop and layer them over a house beat. Their first mix carries your congregation’s own voice, and the recording stays under your church’s control.

Gospel at Sunset · Aug 4, 2027

A Rainbow Wednesday devoted to gospel and spiritual house, 7 pm on the lake. Choirs and soloists are invited to sing live over a youth DJ’s set. Gospel house has always been led by powerful voices, and this is where a young vocalist and a young DJ meet.

Thanks & Giving, with the elders

Before Thanksgiving, a community potluck and food drive honors the neighborhood’s elders, with a Generations Back-to-Back set: a youth DJ and an elder trading records. Many of those elders learned their music in your pews.

A winter practice room

Our summer classes are at the lake. In the cold months children need an indoor, supervised place to keep practicing. A fellowship hall for one afternoon a week would keep young people at the decks through the winter, and we will gladly pay a community rate.

Our promise

A safe, sacred-friendly room.

No alcohol at any program. Clean lyrics and radio edits only. Every instructor is background-checked and works to the Chicago Park District’s standards. Every church decides for itself how, or whether, its name and music are used, and no recording of your choir leaves your hands without your yes. We teach the music of the neighborhood and we do not ask anyone to leave their faith at the door or to share ours.

How to say yes

Pick any one. Each is small.

  1. Share one invitationWe write a short, plain invitation to the free DJ class. You read it, and if you like it, you pass it to your youth leader and families. That is all.
  2. Put up a flyerWhere families will see it: the bulletin board, the narthex or the youth room.
  3. Introduce us to your choir directorFor the choir-in-a-remix idea and Gospel at Sunset. A ten-minute call with the director settles whether it fits.
  4. Offer a winter roomA few hours a week, supervised. A donated room is wonderful, and a community rate is welcome.
  5. Sponsor a Child DJSupport one student’s place in the free class, matched privately by the program. Any gift also helps equipment, meals and Rainbow Wednesdays.
