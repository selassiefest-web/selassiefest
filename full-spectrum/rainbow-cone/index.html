What we are building

Full Spectrum: two ways to begin.

  • Rainbow DJ Lab: free, every Tuesday, 4 to 6 pm, opening January 12, 2027. Twelve stations, 14 Missions and 42 Passport skills, so every child learns at their own pace
  • Rainbow Wednesdays: 14 free summer nights at Rainbow Beach, June 2 to September 1, 2027. Youth sets with DJ mentors, neighborhood artists, and families gathering for free

Rainbow Wednesdays dates and space are planned and depend on the Chicago Park District. The Park District has offered the Rainbow Beach fieldhouse, and our partnership proposal is under review.
