RAINBOW CONE DECK: NARRATION (one block per slide; record each as rc-01.mp3 to rc-12.mp3 in /full-spectrum/rainbow-cone/audio/)
Read warmly and neighbor to neighbor, not like an ad. Numbers are written as spoken.

SLIDE 1 · Cover
Five scoops. One beach.
This is a neighbor's invitation, from Ras Tafari Inc., a South Side nonprofit, to put Rainbow Cone at Rainbow Wednesdays, at Rainbow Beach, in South Shore.

SLIDE 2 · The name fits
Rainbow Cone. Rainbow Beach. Rainbow Wednesdays.
One word, three places. Your South Side institution has been scooping for a hundred years, and our summer series, Rainbow Wednesdays, happens at Rainbow Beach.
This is an invitation from a new neighbor. It was never meant as a request for a favor.

SLIDE 3 · What we are building
We are building Full Spectrum, two ways for young people to begin.
First, the Rainbow DJ Lab. It is free, every Tuesday, from four to six p.m., opening January twelfth, twenty twenty-seven. Twelve stations, fourteen Missions and forty-two Passport skills, so every child learns at their own pace.
Second, Rainbow Wednesdays: fourteen free summer nights at Rainbow Beach, from June second to September first, with youth sets, neighborhood artists, and families gathering for free.
The dates and space depend on the Chicago Park District, which has offered the fieldhouse. Our partnership proposal is under review.

SLIDE 4 · The real numbers
First, a correction. Our first note said fifty a week, which sounded like every week. Here are the real numbers.
Thirty-nine Tuesdays, about twenty scoops each, is seven hundred eighty. Fourteen Wednesday nights, about thirty scoops each, is four hundred twenty.
Together, about twelve hundred scoops, across fifty-three dates in twenty twenty-seven. These are planning numbers for a first year.

SLIDE 5 · Part one: for the program
Part one is for the people in the room.
The twelve students at their own stations. The two resident instructors. A new woman DJ as a guest every week. The teachers who visit, and the administrators who support us.
About twenty scoops each Tuesday, and about thirty on each Wednesday night, as a donation or a deep discount.

SLIDE 6 · Part two: the stand
Part two is where we buy from you.
We would like to buy Rainbow Cone, and set up a stand on the beach for the families who come to Rainbow Wednesdays. We are planning for about one hundred fifty people a night.
So please send us your lowest per-serving price for events, and your rules for a nonprofit that resells. We will set our price for guests from that.
The picture is only an illustration.

SLIDE 7 · Pick a scoop
You choose what fits you.
Option A: donate or discount scoops for the people in the room. Option B: we buy from you and run the stand. Option C: a Rainbow Cone truck stop on a Wednesday, or a benefit night with a percentage coming back.
Tap an option, and it sets the subject line of your reply on the last slide. We are glad to start smaller, with one pilot night.

SLIDE 8 · We have done this before
We have run a public event before.
Ras Tafari Inc. produced SelassieFest twenty twenty-six, a free, one-day roots reggae and cultural festival in Chicago, on July twenty-fifth. About two hundred seventy-three people came, on just forty-seven days of planning.
We are a new nonprofit, a five-oh-one-c-three since June twenty twenty-six, so we plan Rainbow Wednesdays conservatively: about one hundred fifty people a night. We cannot promise attendance, only plan carefully.

SLIDE 9 · Who is already behind it
Here is who is already behind this, confirmed so far.
The Chicago Park District has offered the Rainbow Beach fieldhouse, and our proposal is under review. Shure approved twelve pairs of headphones and two microphones for the students. Algoriddim gave thirteen one-year licenses of djay Pro DJ software. And we have letters of support from Mile Square Health Center South Shore, of UI Health, and from the Avalon Regal Theater.
Our other corporate and foundation requests are still pending, and none of those is confirmed yet.

SLIDE 10 · What you would see
Here is what you would see in return.
A thank-you on our website and in our family emails, with your logo only once you approve it. A clear report: how many scoops were served, on which nights, and what they meant for the program. And a seat at the beach, because your team is welcome at a Tuesday class or a Wednesday night.
We put no sponsor messages on the stage, and we will never use your name or logo without your written okay.

SLIDE 11 · Your four questions
Your four questions, answered in one place.
Weeks per year: up to thirty-nine Tuesdays, plus fourteen summer Wednesdays, fifty-three dates. A pilot night first is fine.
Budget per serving: none for part one, which is a donation or a deep discount. For part two, tell us your event price, and we set ours from it.
Other supporters: the Park District offer, Shure, Algoriddim, UI Health and the Avalon Regal. The rest are pending.
And attendance: about one hundred fifty a night planned, and about two hundred seventy-three came to SelassieFest.

Let's put a scoop on the beach.
Tell us which option fits. A short reply is plenty, and we are glad to talk by phone if that is quicker.
