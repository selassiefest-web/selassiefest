GROWN-UP FACTS

What's actually happening

  • Modi & Grapie Live (proposed): a live story-and-DJ show, created with Tamera Fair and Tonia Evans, as the centerpiece of a family night at Rainbow Beach.
  • Rainbow DJ Lab: a free DJ class every Tuesday, 4 to 6 pm, starting January 12, 2027. Built for grades 6 to 12, open to all ages. Students work through 14 Missions at their own pace, each at their own station.
  • Rainbow Wednesdays: free, alcohol-free family music nights on the beach, June through September 2027, 6:00 to 8:30 pm, with Open Decks for young DJs and a Youth Showcase.
  • Family parties: the Kids Kostume Party in late October, and Noon Year's Eve on December 31, a family countdown to noon with kid DJs.
  • Who runs it: Ras Tafari Inc., a South Side 501(c)(3). Every part is free to families.
