provided music lessons to hundreds of children in the west side of Chicago for over eight years Tamera Fair, on George "GEO" Cooper, in "George (GEO) Cooper: Shadow Warrior," Enigma In Black, July 7, 2017 What that profile describes: the Ella Sheppard School of Music, founded in 2009, giving free lessons to children ages 2 to 14 in space you provided at a Premier center on the West Side.

Imaginative learning, out loud

What does brave sound like?

Your second book is The Purple Turtle Be Brave. In the show, children imagine a feeling, choose a sound for it, try it, listen back and change their minds.

Listening

Hearing the room, the cue and each other.

Sequencing

What comes first, next and last in a scene.

Expressive language

Telling a story into a real microphone.

Feelings into sound

Picking the sound that matches brave, silly or surprised.

Teamwork

A show only works when everyone hits their cue.

Belonging

Seeing themselves in the story, and hearing themselves in it.

These are proposed learning aims. You set the goals for your children.

"I made the rain too dramatic. It sounded like the whole ocean came to snack time. We revised."The narrator, sound designer
