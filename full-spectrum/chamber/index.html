From the Lake to 71st Street

Culture on the lakefront. Commerce on the corridor. How Full Spectrum at Rainbow Beach can help carry South Shore's own plan forward.

From Stephen Henry, President, Ras Tafari Inc., a South Side 501(c)(3)

Same street, same mission

We are already neighbors.

  • Ras Tafari Inc.: 1401 E. 71st St.
  • South Shore Chamber and CDC: 1750 E. 71st St.
  • Full Spectrum: Rainbow Beach Park, 3111 E. 77th St.

You have spent more than a decade bringing business and life back to South Shore's corridors. We want to bring free music and youth training to its lakefront, and send the energy straight back to 71st Street.

Why we came to you

Block by block, for more than a decade.

  • Since 2014: Executive Director of the South Shore Chamber of Commerce and its Community Development Corporation
  • The plans that move South Shore: CorridorLive!, the We the People Community Investment Vehicle, SSA #42 services on 71st and Stony Island, and the Quality-of-Life Plan the CDC convenes
  • Empty storefronts brought back: working with the City to match entrepreneurs to vacant spaces on 71st Street
  • A Chicago Defender Woman of Excellence and a South Shore resident for 25 years

"Look at this beautiful backdrop."

Tonya Trice on South Shore's lakefront, CBS Chicago, Feb. 2024
One idea

The lake draws the crowd. The corridor keeps it.

  • The lakefront: free family music nights and classes bring neighbors and visitors to Rainbow Beach
  • The corridor: vendors, shop-local maps and restaurant orders turn that crowd into business on 71st Street
  • The young people: skills, mentors and a paid stage, close to home
Full Spectrum, year one (2027)

Twelve months of free music in South Shore.

468free DJ Lab seats: every Tuesday from Jan. 12, 4–6 pm, 12 decks
14Rainbow Wednesdays on the beach, June 2 – Sept. 1
300neighbors a night: our goal, about 4,200 visits a summer
$0for families, every part

Plus Remix the Record in February, and free family parties from Halloween to Noon Year's Eve. A first-year program, pending Chicago Park District approval.

South Shore Quality-of-Life Plan (2022)

It is already in your plan.

  • Stewardship: make public spaces like "Rainbow Park/Beach" more accessible and better used → a busy, free, alcohol-free park all year
  • Arts, Culture & Entertainment: South Shore as an arts and culture district, with arts as an economic engine → music by neighbors, for neighbors
  • Public Safety: engage youth in designing and running violence prevention programs → teens on the decks, mentored by a licensed counselor
  • Education: more out-of-school-time activities → a free class every Tuesday, 4 to 6 pm
  • Economic Development: revitalize 71st, 75th and 79th streets → Corridor Nights and local vendors
Built to spend locally

Money that stays on the corridor.

  • About $11,700 a year in DJ Lab snack orders from neighborhood restaurants: $300 every Tuesday
  • 130+ paid sessions for working DJs and instructors
  • Local vendors at Rainbow Wednesdays and the December Christmas Party & Grand Market
  • Sponsor tiers sized for small businesses, with Chamber members first in line
Proposed together

Corridor Nights: 71st Street on the beach.

One Rainbow Wednesday a month spotlights 71st Street: vendor tables for member businesses, shout-outs from the stage, and a shop-local map in every family's hand, pointing them up the street.

A natural fit for SSA #42's corridor marketing, shaped with your team.

Already in motion

Ready for a partner.

  • Space offered: the Chicago Park District offered the Rainbow Beach fieldhouse, Tuesdays 4–6 pm
  • Ready to file: our Park District partnership proposal is board-approved; 80 neighborhood organizations asked for letters
  • Built: the 14-Mission curriculum and class app; Rainbow Wednesdays planned with a $34,220 budget
  • Safe: $1M insurance in force, background checks, clean lyrics, no alcohol, no campaign or sponsor messaging on stage
