Rainbow DJ Lab · Visibility menu for Roland · 2027

Where Roland could be seen, all year

The learning comes first: students making real music on instruments they can put their hands on. The visibility follows from that, in the room every Tuesday, in front of families at showcases and community events, and online. This menu is meant to be chosen from, not taken whole.

Back to the deck

How we handle Roland's name

  • Roland approves first. No logo, product name or artist name is used until Roland has approved the exact use.
  • Park rules apply. Signs and banners on Chicago Park District property go through the Park District, and events in the park need its approval.
  • Students are protected. No student's face, voice or name appears in anything without written family consent. Nothing is sold to students, and instructors teach the instruments rather than promote them.
  • Equipment stays with the Lab. Donated gear belongs to the program, is logged in and out every Tuesday, and serves future students.

The menu

In the room

  • The instruments themselves, used by every student, every week
  • A Beat Track banner in the fieldhouse during class
  • Roland named on the Mission cards and the Beat Track rubric
  • Co-signed Beat Track certificates at graduation

At events

  • A try-it beat table where families make their first pattern
  • Roland on the LED panels of our DJ booths
  • A thank-you from the stage at showcases and on the beach
  • A youth beat stage or tent at SelassieFest

Online and in print

  • Roland on the Lab's website, partner listings and the SelassieFest sponsor page
  • Roland on flyers and posters for showcases and summer nights
  • Short videos of student-made beats on our social channels, tagging Roland
  • A family newsletter note when the Beat Track opens

Story and press

  • A 404 Day on the South Side story, filmed for Roland
  • A case study for Roland's education pages, in the spirit of the Berklee story
  • A joint announcement when the Beat Track launches, if Roland wants one

Educators and artists

  • Two resident instructors trained by Roland, teaching on Roland instruments
  • Guest sessions from Roland artists in the weekly woman guest DJ slot
  • Guest DJs briefed on the Beat Track, so they can build on it

For Roland's teams

  • A classroom field report: what beginners found easy and hard
  • Before-and-after recordings and student work, with consent
  • A short report after each phase, with photos and links to every post

What Roland would send us

  • Approved logo files and any wording Roland prefers
  • Banner artwork, or approval of ours
  • The social accounts Roland wants tagged
  • Try-it units for public events, if they differ from the classroom gear
  • An artist or product specialist for 404 Day or a showcase, if available
  • A contact for approvals, so nothing waits

Next step

Pick what fits on the scoping call. We would start small, prove it in Phase 1 (January 12 to March 30), and grow it only if it is working for both of us.
