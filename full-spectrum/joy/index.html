Two ways to begin

A place to discover what they can do.

  • Rainbow DJ Lab: free, every Tuesday, 4 to 6 pm, opening January 12, 2027. 14 Missions and 42 Passport skills, a dedicated station for each child, and time to learn at their own pace
  • Rainbow Wednesdays: planned free summer nights on the beach. Youth sets with DJ mentors, Chicago house-music heritage, paid neighborhood artists, and families gathering for free

Rainbow Wednesdays is planned for summer 2027. Dates and space are not yet confirmed by the Chicago Park District.

Where support can go

What your generosity makes possible.

  • Time to learn: patient instruction and encouragement from mentors
  • A station of their own: a dedicated deck, laptop and headphones for every child
  • A first audience: sound, guest DJs and neighborhood arts at the park
  • A welcoming experience: the planning and practical support behind each gathering
An invitation

Scott, this beginning can be yours.

The program is designed and the park is the place. What it needs now is a source: the stations, the mentors' time and the first audience.

You could be the one who makes all of it real, and then share a night at Rainbow Beach with friends, watching young talent take its first steps because of you.

What comes back to you

The one people call when they need a DJ.

  • The first call: when a friend asks, "Do you know a good DJ?", you know the talent, you know their development, and you can open the door
  • A living legacy: the songs you grew up with, reinterpreted by young DJs who know where they came from
  • Front-row seats: rehearsals, heritage listening sessions and first public sets, with clear reports on skills, performances and spending
  • First bookings: your introductions can become the first paid opportunities for performers who are ready, with program safeguards

The deepest return is continuity: a song from your youth comes through a young person's 2027 set, and you helped make that exchange a relationship.

A gift does not create ownership, royalties or a share of students' future earnings. Any other arrangement would be separate and explicit.

Your choice

Everyone finds their own connection.

  • For some, it is the music.
  • For others, it is a child finding confidence.
  • For others, it is South Shore coming together.

Support can follow the part of the story that means the most to you.

Staying connected

The joy continues after the gift.

You stay part of the story, not just the first chapter.

What we plan to share with you
  • Milestones: the first Passport stamps, first full sets and first showcase performances
  • Invitations: rehearsals, heritage listening sessions and public showcases, with seats for you and your friends
  • Your song, their set: nominate a track that matters to you and hear how a student reinterprets it
  • Clear reports: attendance, skills demonstrated, performances completed, student feedback and exactly how support was spent
  • A say in what's next: a well-documented first year shows where to grow, whether more stations, instructor training or more sessions

Stories and photographs will respect family consent and student privacy.
