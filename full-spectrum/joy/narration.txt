Rainbow Beach, South Shore, Chicago.
This is Full Spectrum, from Ras Tafari Inc., a South Side nonprofit. And it begins with a gift: a station, a night, a first set.


Imagine that first moment, behind the decks.
The headphones go on. The next track is ready. A mentor offers a quiet nod.
Imagine the smile when the transition lands, and the child looks up to see their family dancing.
This is an imagined moment, one that shows the planned program. It is not a story about a real child, or a past event.


Full Spectrum gives young people two ways to begin.
First, the Rainbow DJ Lab. It is free, every Tuesday, from four to six p.m., opening January twelfth, twenty twenty-seven. Children work through fourteen Missions and forty-two Passport skills, each at a station of their own, learning at their own pace.
Second, Rainbow Wednesdays. These are planned, free summer nights on the beach, with youth sets alongside DJ mentors, Chicago house music heritage, paid neighborhood artists, and families gathering for free.
Rainbow Wednesdays is planned for summer twenty twenty-seven, and the dates and space are not yet confirmed by the Chicago Park District.


On Rainbow Wednesdays, the whole neighborhood gets to cheer.
A young person at the decks. A proud family in the crowd. South Shore, celebrating its own.
Rainbow Wednesdays brings the learning into a shared public experience, right here at Rainbow Beach.


Here is what our first year plans to hold.
Four hundred sixty-eight free class seats, from twelve stations across thirty-nine class Tuesdays. And fourteen summer beach nights, from June second to September first, twenty twenty-seven.
Our first-year plans create room for learning, and for family celebration.
Class seats are session places, not distinct students. Both numbers are planning targets, not results, and the summer dates depend on Chicago Park District approval.


So what does your generosity make possible?
Time to learn: patient instruction, and encouragement from mentors.
A station of their own: a dedicated deck, laptop, and headphones for every child.
A first audience: sound, guest DJs, and neighborhood arts at the park.
And a welcoming experience: the planning and practical support behind every gathering.

And here is what comes back to you. You become the one people call when they need a DJ.
When a friend asks, "Do you know a good DJ?", you know the talent, you know their development, and you can open the door.
It is a living legacy: the songs you grew up with, reinterpreted by young DJs who know where they came from.
You get front-row seats, to rehearsals, heritage listening sessions, and first public sets, with clear reports on skills, performances, and spending.
And your introductions can become the first paid opportunities for performers who are ready, with program safeguards in place.
The deepest return is continuity. A song from your youth comes through a young person's twenty twenty-seven set, and you helped make that exchange a relationship.
A gift does not create ownership, royalties, or a share of students' future earnings. Any other arrangement would be separate, and explicit.


This is what you and we are building: the Full Spectrum Founding Circle.
It is a private circle of supporters, each giving at the level they choose, with a shared goal of one hundred fifty thousand dollars in deposited donations to Ras Tafari Inc.
It works in three steps. Give: every person chooses their own gift, with no required levels. Introduce: each supporter invites two or three people they trust, never as a condition of giving. And gather: small conversations and heritage listening sessions, where friends hear the plan and meet the team.
Your role is Founding Connector. You open the first doors, and Ras Tafari handles follow-up, processing, acknowledgments, and reporting.


And we have built one place for you and your friends. Easy to give. Easy to share.
A direct giving page, with card, PayPal, Venmo, or check, and a call for larger gifts and equipment.
A ready-to-send introduction, a note you personalize and forward in one click.
All the tax paperwork: our IRS determination letter, our EIN, and a written acknowledgment of every gift, so friends can deduct with confidence.
And honest progress: cash deposited toward the one hundred fifty thousand dollars, with pledges and in-kind support reported separately.
It is all on the Founding Circle page of our website.
