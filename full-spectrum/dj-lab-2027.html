Rainbow DJ Lab 2027

A year-round DJ program every Tuesday from 4:00 to 6:00 pm in the Rainbow Beach Park fieldhouse game room. Every child gets their own deck, two resident DJ instructors teach every class, and a woman DJ visits as guest instructor each week. Children join any time, work at their own pace through the 42-skill Passport, and never lose progress for a missed class. The calendar skips every federal-holiday week and CPS spring break.

The time slot

Tuesdays, 4:00 to 6:00 pm, in the fieldhouse

The Chicago Park District offered Rainbow Beach Park’s main fieldhouse game room / clubroom on Tuesday or Thursday from 4 to 6 pm (Oct. 20, 2026). We chose Tuesday. Two hours holds the full 90-minute core class, all nine blocks, and a 30-minute guest session.

The 90-minute core: nine blocks

Drop In · Mission Card · Watch Me Think · Deck Time 1 · Move Break · Deck Time 2 · Wordplay Lab · Spotlight · Check Out. The same nine blocks as the curriculum, now with every child on their own controller.

4× the time on the decks

With four children to a controller, each child got about 7.5 minutes hands-on per class. With one deck each, every child gets the full 30 minutes of Deck Time: about 7 hours across 14 Missions instead of 1.75.

Year-round, rolling enrollment

Learn at your own pace. Complete your rainbow.

How it works

  • Join any Tuesday a station is free. New students start with orientation, hearing safety and the Red Mission.
  • Featured Mission + personal Mission. The instructors demo one Mission for the room; each child works on their own next skill.
  • Miss a class? No problem. Make up any Mission at the next available class. Stamps never expire.
  • Graduate on mastery, not a date. When all 42 skills are stamped and a final set is ready, a child plays the next Rainbow Showcase.
  • Attendance and skills are tracked separately in the class app: showing up doesn’t stamp a skill, and missing a week doesn’t erase one.

Two leads and a weekly guest

The woman guest DJ series

Two resident DJ instructors, one man and one woman, teach every class and track every child’s Passport; each takes six students for coaching and stamps. A different woman DJ takes the last 30 minutes of each class week. Her session builds on that day’s featured Mission, so it adds to the curriculum without replacing it, and it is never required for graduation.

What every guest delivers

  • A memorable personal story
  • One clearly defined DJ skill
  • A chance for every child to show what they learned

Each guest gets a briefing beforehand: the featured Mission, the room’s skill range, child-safety rules and the clean-lyrics policy. With permission, a short interview or photo promotes her and the Lab. The roster rotates through technical mixing, Chicago house, reggae and dancehall, R&B and soul, turntablism, mashups, production, stagecraft and DJ business.

Staffing and budget

What a year of Tuesdays costs

Ras Tafari Inc. rates: resident DJ instructors $50 an hour, guest DJs $200 an hour, and $20 a person in snacks for 15 people (12 students, two residents and the guest), bought from neighborhood restaurants so every class also supports a local business. The two residents are paid for three hours: the two-hour class plus setup, pack-up and Passport records. Equal pay for equal duties.

Method

How Ras Tafari Inc. evaluated the best DJ Lab schedule

  1. Compared every weekday for middle-school, high-school and mixed groups: Mondays lose the most weeks to federal holidays, Fridays compete with games and family plans, and Wednesday came out as the steadiest midweek day.
  2. Matched it to the room we can get. The Park District offered the fieldhouse on Tuesday or Thursday, 4–6 pm, and Wednesday evenings belong to Rainbow Wednesdays in summer. Tuesday keeps the midweek rhythm.
  3. Checked the slot against each group. Middle school fits best; high-school athletes are covered by rolling make-ups.
  4. Fit the class into two hours without cutting the 90-minute core: the guest session became 30 minutes.
  5. Survey families and measure attendance, late arrivals, missed practices, pickup and Passport progress; review after four to six weeks.

Calendar layers

Applied in order: (1) remove every federal-holiday week; (2) remove Chicago Park District facility closures once the 2027 room schedule is confirmed; (3) remove CPS breaks. CPS spring break 2027 is March 22–26 on the district’s approved 2026–27 calendar.
