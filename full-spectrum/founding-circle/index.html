Build the foundation for Full Spectrum.

A private circle of supporters, assembled through trusted relationships, giving at the level each person chooses. The shared goal: $150,000 in deposited donations to Ras Tafari Inc., so Full Spectrum begins on solid ground at Rainbow Beach.

Full Spectrum is a first-year program and depends on Chicago Park District approval. Every donation goes directly to Ras Tafari Inc., a 501(c)(3) public charity.

Give an item

You throwing that out? Give it to a young DJ.

Before you replace it, clear it out or put it in storage, let us see whether it can help a young DJ take the stage.

The upgrade you already replaced, the spare you never use, the equipment your business is retiring: that television could let twelve students watch an instructor's hands, the laptop your office retired could become a young person's own DJ station, and the records you have not played in twenty years could introduce a child to a song their great-grandmother still knows by heart.

Things

Access, time and introductions

Some of the most valuable gifts have no object attached.

Your offer goes straight to Ras Tafari Inc. We reply within a few days to confirm what we can use and how to get it to us.

What we can accept

  1. Items that are clean, complete and in working order, and that fit DJ learning, performance or the beach events. Computers and audio gear go through compatibility, condition and safety checks before they are used, and electronics are wiped securely. We may decline items we cannot use, store or safely give to young people, and we will tell you promptly.
  2. Tax records: we give you a written acknowledgment that describes the item, its condition and the date. It does not state a value, because the IRS leaves valuing the item to the donor. Non-cash gifts over $500 total need Form 8283 with your return (see the tax documents below). We are happy to sign it.
  3. In-kind gifts are tracked as equipment and operating support, separately from cash. They reduce what Full Spectrum must buy; the $150,000 bank-account goal stays a separate measure.

What you receive

  1. Written acknowledgment of every gift, showing the amount, the date and the organization's EIN. For gifts of $250 or more, it also states that no goods or services were received in exchange, which is what the IRS asks donors to keep.
  2. Check, card and PayPal records serve as your proof for smaller gifts, alongside our acknowledgment.
  3. Non-cash gifts: we acknowledge the item, not its value. For donated equipment, the donor determines the value; Form 8283 applies to non-cash gifts over $500.
  4. Year-end summary of all your gifts, sent in January.

IRS forms, if your gift needs one

Cash gifts need no IRS form from the donor: our acknowledgment and your bank, card or check record are the paperwork. You claim the deduction on Schedule A (Form 1040) if you itemize. Forms come in for non-cash gifts such as DJ equipment:

Form 8283, Noncash Charitable Contributions (IRS PDF)Required with your return when non-cash gifts total more than $500. Over $5,000 for one item or group of items, it also needs a qualified appraisal and our signature in Part V. Send us the form and an officer of Ras Tafari Inc. (Stephen Henry, Paul Kelly or Albert Harris) will sign it.
IRS Publication 526, Charitable Contributions (PDF)The IRS guide to what is deductible, how to value gifts and which records to keep.
IRS Publication 1771, Charitable Contributions: Substantiation and Disclosure (PDF)The written-acknowledgment rules, including the $250 threshold.

We link to the IRS originals so you always get the current version. Ras Tafari does not need any form from donors for cash gifts.
