Full Spectrum

For South Shore, a free music classroom on the lakefront. For our young people, paid work and a real stage. For Chicago, proof of what investing in kids sounds like.

A program of Ras Tafari Inc., a South Side 501(c)(3)

The problem

The biggest free music in Chicago is nine miles away.

From 77th Street to Grant Park: transit or parking for everyone, an hour or more of travel, a late ride home with small children and elders. Many South Shore families make that trip once a summer. Many never do.

What if the music came home?

Rainbow Beach, 1960

This beach has been claimed for everyone before.

In 1960, the NAACP Youth Council waded in at Rainbow Beach so every Chicagoan could share it. Velma Murphy was struck by a thrown brick. They did not back down.

67 years later, the next generation comes back. With turntables.

Two resident DJ instructors in every class, and a different woman DJ as guest instructor each week. Join any Tuesday, learn at your own pace, graduate on mastery. Free to families.

Play · Rainbow Wednesdays · June 2 – Sept. 1, 2027

14 summer nights. One color each week.

  • 6:00 to 8:30 pm on the beach, free and alcohol-free, a different sound each week: house, footwork, gospel, reggae, Afrobeats, hip-hop
  • Open Decks for first-timers and a Youth Showcase for the students who have been learning since January
  • Generations Back-to-Back closes every night: a young DJ and a veteran trade songs as the sun goes down
Twelve months, five parts

It does not stop when the beach closes.

  • Remix the Record (February): neighbors research real Black history, check every fact against two sources, and write songs the DJs mix into one journey
  • Live stage shows: young openers share the bill with local artists and headliners
  • Festival days: heritage days of the Caribbean and African diaspora, and SelassieFest (July 24, 2027)
  • Fall and holiday parties: from the Kids Kostume Party to Noon Year's Eve

$0 to attend. Every part.

The mayor has proposed adding 7,000 summer youth jobs in 2027, for roughly 40,000 in all.

Chicago Sun-Times, Oct. 20, 2026

Full Spectrum is ready to be some of those jobs: paid teen DJs and paid sound crew on the South Side lakefront, with mentoring led by licensed counselor Paul Kelly, MA, MHS, LPC.

Already in motion

This is not just an idea.

  • Space offered: the Chicago Park District offered the Rainbow Beach fieldhouse, Tuesdays 4–6 pm (Oct. 20, 2026)
  • Ready to file: our Park District partnership proposal is board-approved; 80 neighborhood organizations have been asked for letters of support
  • Insured: $1,000,000 general liability policy in force
  • Built and planned: the 14-Mission curriculum and class app, and 14 Rainbow Wednesdays with a $34,220 budget
  • Safe: background checks for every adult who works with young people; clean lyrics; no alcohol; no campaign or sponsor messaging on stage
  • Producer: Ras Tafari Inc., a South Side 501(c)(3) that produces SelassieFest
