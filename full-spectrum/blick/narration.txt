BLICK x RAINBOW DJ LAB DECK NARRATION

=== SLIDE 01 - Blick, the art partner of Rainbow Wednesdays ===
Thank you for a few minutes with us.
This is Blick and the Rainbow DJ Lab: the art partner of Rainbow Wednesdays.
Fourteen free Wednesday evenings. Children and neighbors making the art that decorates the scene. And every dollar of our art-materials grant spent at Blick.

=== SLIDE 02 - It started with a single pen. It never stopped supporting artists. ===
Why Blick?
Blick began with a single pen in 1911, and it is still family owned.
Blick says it supports artists at every stage, and believes art education benefits us all.
Rainbow Wednesdays is a local art project, made by neighbors. That is who Blick says it wants to work with.

=== SLIDE 03 - A free DJ class for Chicago children, and a summer series for everyone ===
Who are we?
Ras Tafari Inc. is a Chicago nonprofit. We produced SelassieFest last July, and about two hundred seventy-three people came.
Now we are opening the Rainbow DJ Lab, a free class for children every Tuesday from January twelfth, and Rainbow Wednesdays, fourteen free family evenings on the lawn.

=== SLIDE 04 - The art is made by the community, then it lights up the lawn ===
Here is the scene.
Teens from the DJ Lab invite families and elders to choose each night's color and theme.
Neighbors dye the scrims and paint lanterns. Ribbons, a booth wrap and lamp post color finish the ring.
Red, gold and blue, the three bands of the Rainbow Division, the name Rainbow Beach carries.

=== SLIDE 05 - Every dollar of our $250 art grant is spent at Blick ===
Here is our commitment.
Jane Goodall's Roots and Shoots awarded us a two hundred fifty dollar project grant to make the art for a Rainbow Wednesday.
It can only buy art materials. And we are spending every dollar of it at Blick.
A Blick gift certificate would let each artist buy what that week's design needs, across all fourteen weeks.

=== SLIDE 06 - The crowd Blick would meet: families, teens, elders, artists ===
Who will be there?
Our goal is three hundred people a night, about four thousand two hundred visits over fourteen nights.
Families, teens, elders and artists, on free alcohol free Wednesday evenings from June second to September first.
It is planned for Rainbow Beach Park in South Shore, subject to Chicago Park District approval.

=== SLIDE 07 - Six ways Blick shows up, all summer ===
Here is the offer.
Six ways Blick shows up, all summer.
Logo recognition. Print. Flyering. Digital. A social media shout out. And discount coupons handed out on the lawn.
Each one is built so the children and neighbors see Blick every single week.

=== SLIDE 08 - Blick's logo where everyone looks ===
Number one: logo recognition.
Blick's logo goes on the banner at the DJ booth and on the art wall, every night.
It is on our program page, in this pitch deck, and on the Blick tent where coupons are handed out.
Blick's marketing team approves the logo files, size and placement first.

=== SLIDE 09 - Blick in print, from the first poster to the last ===
Number two: print.
Blick is credited as the art partner on the printed posters and programs for the series.
There is also a printed thank you card for Blick, signed by the artists and children.
Everything goes to Blick for approval before anything is printed.

=== SLIDE 10 - Flyers in the neighborhood, with Blick on them ===
Number three: flyering.
Every Rainbow Wednesdays flyer says Art partner, Blick, with the Blick logo.
They go out at the DJ Lab, at neighborhood businesses, and at every Wednesday.
And each flyer points families to the Blick coupon at the Blick tent.

=== SLIDE 11 - Blick on every page families visit ===
Number four: digital.
Blick's name and logo appear on our Rainbow Wednesdays page and in this narrated deck.
Every email and web update about the series carries a Blick credit.
And a page of photos shows the art being made, each one crediting Blick materials.

=== SLIDE 12 - A shout-out Blick can share ===
Number five: the social media shout out.
A launch thank you post, then a Blick credit in each night's post.
We tag Blick's own channels, Instagram at blickartmaterials and TikTok at blick art, so Blick can reshare the story.
Photos and video show children and neighbors making the art, always with permission from families.

=== SLIDE 13 - The Blick tent: volunteers hand out Blick coupons on the lawn ===
Number six: discount coupons on site.
A Blick tent on the lawn, staffed by Ras Tafari volunteers.
Families and artists take home a Blick discount coupon, from Blick's own offer.
Children can try the same materials at a make and take table. Blick supplies the coupons and approves the tent design.

=== SLIDE 14 - What a Wednesday looks like with Blick in it ===
A Wednesday with Blick in it.
At four, gates open, and the Blick tent is staffed with coupons out.
At five, neighbors dye, paint and decorate with Blick materials.
At sundown the lanterns light, and the DJ booth wears the Blick banner.
Every night, a thank you to Blick from the microphone. And afterward, photos, counts and links go to Blick.

=== SLIDE 15 - Paint it red, gold and blue, with Blick ===
So here is our ask.
A Blick gift certificate toward the art materials for the fourteen weekly artworks.
In return, all six recognitions you just saw, approved by Blick's marketing team first.
We will report back with photos, attendance counts and links after every night.
Thank you. We would love to paint it red, gold and blue with Blick.
