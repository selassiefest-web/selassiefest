Still open across all of it

  • One call to CDPH covers three questions. Whether an uncertified cane crusher is acceptable, whether ETL-Sanitation satisfies them on the burner, and what a temporary food establishment serving fresh juice needs from us. Worth doing before any of this is ordered.
  • Commercial insurance quote. A vehicle titled to the organization needs a commercial policy, and the number changes which van we can afford.
  • Title everything to Ras Tafari Inc., not to an individual. Illinois transfers a title only into the name on the bill of sale, and it cannot be corrected later without a second transfer.
  • Freight is the recurring trap. The van, the cane juicer and the burner all showed a several-hundred-dollar spread between dealers on identical equipment. Price the delivered total every time.

A note on these pages

These are internal working papers. They are set noindex so they stay out of search results, but that is not access control — anyone with the link can read them. They name sellers and state what we think equipment is really worth, so keep the links inside the board.

Prices and availability change quickly, particularly on private vehicle listings. Every figure here is a starting point for a phone call rather than a quote. Permit, titling and fire code notes are for planning the conversations with CDPH, the fire department, the Secretary of State and our insurer — not substitutes for them.
