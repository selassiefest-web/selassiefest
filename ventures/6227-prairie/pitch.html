Owner-occupancy means the property is never abandoned. Albert's skin is in the game — he sleeps there.

Against — risks to weigh

Repayment depends on execution of high-volume event model and grant success. Not a passive investment.

Dual nonprofit structure creates multiple revenue streams: events, grants, City contracts, and flips. Diversified repayment reduces single-point failure.

~$12,441 tax arrearage must be cured at closing. 2023 taxes are at tax sale risk — require cure as condition of funding.

Washington Park/Woodlawn is an active investment corridor (INVEST South/West, Obama Center proximity). Land appreciating — $136K assessed in 2023, $280K in 2024/25.

Property is vacant land — all use value must be created. No rental income backstop if programming stalls.

2nd Chance Housing's City foreclosure pipeline gives investors deal flow on additional properties — upside beyond this note.

Quitclaim deed transfer raises chain-of-title questions. Require title insurance and confirmation that ALSJ mortgage is released.

Community Land Trust eligibility could reduce carrying costs and provide institutional backing.

365 events/year is ambitious. The 4-year ramp (50% Year 1, full Years 2–4) mitigates this risk.
