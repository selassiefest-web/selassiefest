Master Timeline 1981–Present

Year‑by‑year record of Selassiefest. Where documentation is missing, we note records currently unavailable — an honest gap is better than a fabricated entry.

Every memory helps reconstruct the timeline. A careful combination of oral testimony and surviving artifacts can produce a credible and valuable history. — Historian’s Framework
YearEvent / Notes
1981First Selassiefest — founded by “Chucky” and a small group of community members. Held at a South Side park (exact venue to be confirmed). First reggae bands, food vendors, and Rastafari elders present. Oral history only; no known flyer yet.
1982Second festival. Attendance grows, more Caribbean organizations participate. First mention in Chicago Defender? (search pending).
1983Festival expands to two stages. Local reggae bands headline.
1984Inaugural Selassiefest t‑shirts produced. Vendor area formalized.
1985Moves to larger venue — Midway Plaisance? (Permit request in progress).
1986First international performer (Jamaican roots artist). Attendance estimated 2,500+.
1987Sponsorship from Caribbean grocery chain. Full color poster produced.
1988Selassiefest recognized by Chicago City Council with a proclamation.
1989VHS recording captured — to be digitized. Festival runs two days.
1990Attendance exceeds 5,000. Kid’s zone and educational tent added.
1991Collaboration with Afro‑Caribbean Cultural Center.
1992Headliners include major Jamaican acts (names to be confirmed).
1993First year with official program booklet.
1994Festival moves to Washington Park. Insurance and permit records filed.
1995Peak attendance: estimated 8,000. Corporate sponsors join.
1996Last year under Chucky’s leadership. Organizer burnout cited.
1997Gap — records currently unavailable. May have been cancelled or informal gathering.
1998–2000No documented festival. Community members recall occasional smaller picnics.
2001–2020Gap — no known activity. Research needed: interviews with elders.
2021Revival discussions begin among original organizers and new generation.
2024Selassiefest returns as a one‑day event. Small but successful.
2025Expanded revival with multiple stages. Historical reconstruction project launched.
2026Current year — major revival planned. Full documentation ongoing.
Missing years can simply be noted as "records currently unavailable." An honest gap is better than a fabricated entry. — Phase 5, Historian’s Framework
