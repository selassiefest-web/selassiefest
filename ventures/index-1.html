Board authorization

Before any filing · Ras Tafari Inc. board

Nothing gets filed until the board authorizes it in a minuted meeting. This matters for two separate reasons: Illinois nonprofit directors owe fiduciary duties when committing charitable assets to a business venture, and Chase will ask for a document naming Yawd's members and managers — meeting minutes are one of the four documents they accept for that.

Capitalization is the sensitive part. Funding a for-profit from charitable assets is legitimate, but the board must treat it as an investment decision — business plan reviewed, risk assessed, amount sized so a total loss would not impair the mission. Not "we transferred money to start the restaurant." If any funds are donor-restricted, they cannot be used at all.

Name, trademark, domain & handles

Before filing anything · do the clearance first

Illinois name availability is not trademark clearance — they are unrelated systems, and a state LLC registration gives you close to nothing against a federal mark holder in the same class. Clear the name properly before money goes into signage, menus or build-out.

Decide the brand name and domain together

Removing "THE" from the logo makes the trade name Yawd — which is the legal name minus the entity suffix. frontyawd.com then no longer matches what the sign says, and a customer who sees YAWD will type yawd.com. Before Phase 3, settle whether the brand is Yawd (get the matching domain) or Front Yawd (keep frontyawd.com and put FRONT back in the logo). Filing the assumed name is cheap to do and annoying to undo, and the domain you buy today is the one on every menu and napkin.

Trademark knockout search

Search Class 43 (restaurant and food services) for your chosen name and its obvious near-misses — Yawd, Front Yawd, The Yawd, and importantly Yard and Front Yard, since examiners and courts treat phonetic equivalents as confusingly similar. "Front Yard" in particular is a plain-English phrase with likely prior restaurant use somewhere in the US; the patois spelling helps but does not automatically clear it.

Also do a plain commercial search — Google, Instagram, Yelp, Google Maps — for restaurants using the name or its "Yard" spelling. Common-law rights exist without federal registration and can still force a rename.

Domain and handles — register in Yawd's name, not the festival's

This is where separation usually springs a leak. Register the domain in a separate registrar account with a Yawd email address — not the account or the address that holds selassiefest.com. Shared registrar accounts, shared admin emails and shared DNS records are a visible, permanent link in WHOIS history. Turn on WHOIS privacy from the moment of purchase.

Illinois formation

After board approval and name clearance

The Illinois Secretary of State website blocks automated access, so none of the ILSOS links or fees in this phase could be verified. Start from the Business Services home page and navigate from there; confirm every form number and fee on screen before paying.

3.1 — Confirm name availability

Unverified Illinois business entity search — corroborated by illinois.gov as the correct destination

You have already confirmed Yawd LLC is available. Re-check immediately before filing — availability is a snapshot, not a reservation. If formation is more than a few days out, Illinois offers a name reservation (Form LLC-1.15) that holds it for a limited period.

3.2 — File Articles of Organization (Form LLC-5.5)

Unverified ILSOS Business Services

What you will need on hand:

  • Name: Yawd LLC
  • Principal place of business — the restaurant address once you have a lease, otherwise a business address
  • Registered agent and registered office — see the warning below
  • Purpose — Illinois permits a general-purpose statement; restaurant operation is fine
  • Management structure — manager-managed or member-managed, matching the board resolution
  • Sole member: Ras Tafari Inc., an Illinois not-for-profit corporation
  • Duration — perpetual unless you have reason otherwise

Do not use 765 E. 154th Street or Stephen Henry as Yawd's registered agent. Ras Tafari Inc. already uses both. Same agent at the same address is the single most visible thing that makes two entities look like one, and it is the first fact cited in an alter-ego argument. Use a commercial registered agent service, or a different person at a different address.

3.3 — Assumed name (Form LLC-1.20), if the trade name differs

Illinois LLCs and corporations file assumed names with the Secretary of State, not the county — that catches people out. File this only if the public-facing name differs from the exact legal name. If the sign reads Yawd and the legal name is Yawd LLC, filing is cheap insurance; if it reads Front Yawd, it is mandatory. Illinois renews assumed names on a five-year cycle with a prorated fee, so the amount depends on where you land in the cycle.

3.4 — Operating Agreement

Illinois does not file this — it is an internal document, but Chase accepts it as the supplemental document naming members and managers, and your attorney will want it to match the board resolution. It must name Ras Tafari Inc. as sole member and reflect that the membership interest is voted by board resolution rather than at an officer's discretion.

Federal — EIN and the Form 8832 election

Immediately after the Articles are stamped

This is the phase that cannot be missed

A single-member LLC owned by a 501(c)(3) is a disregarded entity by default. Without the Form 8832 election, the restaurant's income and activity are treated as Ras Tafari Inc.'s own, flow straight onto the Form 990, and the entire firewall you paid to build does not exist. Everything else on this page is administration. This is the structure.

4.1 — Get Yawd's own EIN

Verified Apply for an EIN online
Verified About Form SS-4 · PDF — Rev. 12/2025

Confirmed details from the IRS application page: available Mon–Fri 6:00am–1:00am ET, Sat 6:00am–9:00pm ET, Sun 6:00pm–12:00am ET. The session expires after 15 minutes of inactivity and does not save — have everything in front of you. The EIN is issued immediately on approval.

  • The responsible party is a natural person with an SSN or ITIN — one of your officers, not the corporation
  • Yawd needs its own EIN. Never use 42-3036705 for anything Yawd does.

4.2 — File Form 8832

Verified About Form 8832 · Form PDF

There is no separate instructions document for Form 8832 — the instructions are inside the form PDF itself. The current revision is dated December 2013; that is correct, not an error. Anyone who sends you a link to "the 8832 instructions" as a separate file is sending you a dead link.

Elect classification as an association taxable as a corporation. On timing, straight from the form: an election cannot take effect more than 75 days before the date it is filed, nor more than 12 months after. File it right after formation so it can be effective from day one — do not leave it until tax season.

4.3 — What this means for Ras Tafari Inc.'s own return

Verified Schedule R (Form 990) — Related Organizations
Verified IRS — Unrelated Business Income Tax
Verified About Form 990-T
Verified Publication 598 — UBIT — rev. 03/2021, not updated annually; use the UBIT page above for current thresholds

Confirmed thresholds: a Form 990-T is required at $1,000 or more of gross unrelated business income, and estimated tax payments are required where the liability will be $500 or more.

The ownership will be public. Ras Tafari Inc. must disclose Yawd LLC as a controlled entity on Schedule R, and the Form 990 is a public document that appears on ProPublica and Candid. Keeping the brands separate is achievable; keeping the ownership secret is not, and should not be the goal. Make sure Paul and Albert understand this going in. If asked, the answer is simply that the nonprofit owns it and the profits fund the mission — a good answer, and a far better one than appearing to have concealed it.

§512(b)(13) — the controlled-entity trap. Rent, royalties and interest paid from a controlled subsidiary up to the parent get pulled back and taxed as unrelated business income to Ras Tafari Inc. So if Yawd leases the van or the juicing equipment from the nonprofit, that rent is UBI and goes on a 990-T. Dividends and distributions of after-tax profit are not caught by this rule — that is the clean channel for moving money up.

Illinois tax registration

Before the first sale — allow lead time

Yawd must register with the Illinois Department of Revenue to collect and remit sales tax on restaurant food. This is unrelated to Ras Tafari Inc.'s exemption — a nonprofit's exemption covers its purchases, never its sales, and in any case Yawd is a taxable entity in its own right.

Register via MyTax Illinois (Form REG-1)

Verified IDOR — Business registration
Verified MyTax Illinois
Verified Form REG-1 (PDF) — rev. R-07/26

Register online. Confirmed from the IDOR page: MyTax processes in roughly 1–2 business days; paper takes 4–6 weeks, and IDOR asks you to allow 6–8. No registration fee is stated. Questions: 217-785-3707 · REV.CRD@Illinois.gov

Schedules REG-1-A through REG-1-T cover add-ons — liquor and tobacco among them. If Yawd will serve alcohol, that is a separate state liquor licence and a village liquor licence on top of this registration, and it materially changes the build-out, the insurance and the timeline.

Separation discipline

Permanent · this is what actually holds

The filings create the structure. Conduct is what preserves it. If the two entities are run as one in practice, a court or the IRS will treat them as one regardless of what was filed.

AreaRule
MoneySeparate accounts, separate books, separate EIN. Never commingled — not once, not temporarily, not "we'll sort it out later."
DecisionsBoard resolutions of Ras Tafari Inc. direct the membership interest. Overlapping officers are fine; acting in the wrong capacity is not. Minute which hat you were wearing.
