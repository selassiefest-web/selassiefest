  The Story Arc — Told Through Bob Marley's Own Catalog

  • This is one SelassieFest DJ show, not six (6) separate, competing sets — each chapter traces one era of Bob Marley's discography, in order.
  • The arc: The Wailing Wailers → Catch a Fire & Burnin' → Natty Dread & Rastaman Vibration → Exodus & Kaya → Survival & Uprising → Confrontation & Legend.
  • The direct Selassie link: "War" on Rastaman Vibration (1976) sets Emperor Haile Selassie I's 1963 address to the United Nations to music almost word for word — that's Chapter 3, the heart of the whole show.
  • Chapters connect, not compete — each set should hand off to the next, with no duplicate songs, no clashing energy, no starting over.
  • Energy still builds naturally — later chapters can bring real heat, but inside the show's cultural and educational mission, not a generic dancehall party.
  • Selectors stay flexible — times aren't strict, and every selector should be ready for at least 2, preferably 3 chapters, not just the one they're nominally assigned.
  • The goal: one unforgettable cultural experience — Marley's musical journey and His Imperial Majesty's message, told through several DJs working in unison.
