Eligibility Center

Nearly every high-value opportunity below is gated by Chicago residency — of the organization, or of the individual artist applying. Two unresolved facts decide almost the entire strategy:

!

Ras Tafari Inc's registered address (765 E 154th St, South Holland, IL) is outside Chicago city limits. This is independently verified to disqualify the organization as lead applicant for Night Out in the Parks (CPD's own site: "You must be a Chicago-based organization or individual artist... verified at application, contracting, and payment"), and was already flagged the same way on the DCASE CityArts/NAP grants instrument.

This does not block Ras Tafari Inc from participating as a producing/presenting partner under an eligible lead — that pathway is explicit in NAP's own guidelines and implicit in NOITP's individual-artist option.

!

Jay RebL's own bio lists him as based in the Evanston/Skokie area — also outside Chicago. If that's still accurate, he also cannot be the Chicago-resident lead applicant for NOITP, DCASE's Individual Artists Program, Chicago Band Roster, or Chicago Made Music Showcase, all of which require the individual's own Chicago residency, not just the presenting org's.

This is the single most important open question in the whole war room. Two ways to resolve it: (1) confirm whether Jay RebL has a genuine Chicago address we haven't recorded, or (2) identify a real Chicago-resident artist/organization to serve as lead applicant, with Ras Tafari Inc and Jay RebL as partners — exactly the structure already used on the NAP Idea Submission draft.
