Bartender

📢 Multiple Positions Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Bartender Application"

Position Overview

Bartenders mix and serve alcoholic and non‑alcoholic beverages to festival‑goers, process payments, and maintain a clean, efficient bar. This role reports to the Lead Bartender and works alongside barbacks and other bartenders.

Key Responsibilities

  • Prepare and serve drinks (beer, wine, cocktails, non‑alcoholic) quickly and accurately.
  • Check customer IDs and refuse service to minors or intoxicated persons.
  • Process cash and credit card payments using POS system.
  • Maintain bar cleanliness: wipe counters, wash glassware, empty trash.
  • Communicate with barbacks to restock ice, liquor, garnishes, and cups.
  • Follow all responsible beverage service laws and festival policies.
  • Assist with opening and closing duties (setup, breakdown, inventory counts).

Qualifications

  • 1+ year bartending experience (or strong barback experience with willingness to learn).
  • Valid alcohol service certification (TIPS, ServSafe Alcohol, or equivalent) – can be obtained before event.
  • Basic math and cash handling skills.
  • Ability to work in a fast‑paced, noisy outdoor environment.
  • Friendly and patient customer service.
  • Physical ability to stand for full shifts, lift 30 lbs, and move quickly.

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Lead Bartender
  • Works with: Other Bartenders, Barbacks, ID Checkers

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open (2–5 per bar, multiple bars)
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: Event days only (shifts available)
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid hourly + tips (shared pool)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Drinks per hour, accuracy, customer satisfaction

🍹 Common Drinks You'll Make

  • Beer (bottle or draft) – Red Stripe, local craft
  • Wine – red, white (by the glass)
  • Rum Punch – recipe provided
  • Vodka + ginger beer (Ginger Fire)
  • Non‑alcoholic – coconut water, soda, juice

📋 Sample Shift

  • Morning shift: 10:00 AM – 5:00 PM
  • Evening shift: 4:00 PM – 10:00 PM
  • (Bar closes 30 mins before festival end)

🪪 ID Checking Basics

  • Accept only physical government‑issued IDs (driver's license, passport, military ID).
  • No photos of IDs, no expired IDs.
  • If unsure, ask for a second ID or call Lead Bartender.

⚠️ Responsible Bartending at Selassiefest

  • Never serve anyone under 21.
  • Watch for signs of intoxication: slurred speech, unsteady balance, aggressive behavior.
  • Refuse service politely: "I'm sorry, I can't serve you any more alcohol tonight."
  • Call security if a customer becomes belligerent.
  • Offer water and non‑alcoholic options.

🤝 Working with Barbacks

Barbacks are there to support you. Communicate clearly when you need ice, liquor, cups, or clean glassware. A good bartender‑barback team works smoothly and keeps lines moving.

To apply for Bartender: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Bartender Application" in the subject line. Include your resume or a brief description of your bartending experience. Certification can be obtained before the event if not already held.

Experienced barbacks are encouraged to apply for bartender training.
