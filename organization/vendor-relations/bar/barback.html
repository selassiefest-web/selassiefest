Barback

📢 Multiple Positions Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Barback Application"

Position Overview

Barbacks are the backbone of the bar team, ensuring bartenders have everything they need to serve customers quickly. This role reports to the Lead Bartender and works closely with bartenders to restock supplies, clear empties, and maintain cleanliness.

Key Responsibilities

  • Restock ice, liquor, beer, wine, garnishes, cups, and napkins throughout the shift.
  • Clear empty bottles and glassware from the bar surface.
  • Empty trash and recycling bins regularly.
  • Change kegs (if draft beer is served).
  • Keep the bar area clean and safe (wipe spills, sweep).
  • Assist bartenders with opening and closing duties.
  • Run supplies from storage to the bar as needed.

Qualifications

  • No prior experience required – training provided.
  • Physical ability to lift 50 lbs (kegs, cases of liquor) and stand for long shifts.
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to take direction.
  • Ability to work in a fast‑paced, loud outdoor environment.
  • Basic English communication skills.
  • Must be at least 18 (21+ preferred to handle alcohol, but not required).

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Lead Bartender
  • Works with: Bartenders, Bar Manager, other Barbacks

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open (1–2 per bar)
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: Event days only (shifts available)
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid hourly + tip pool (shared with bartenders)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Restocking speed, bar cleanliness, bartender satisfaction

📋 Sample Tasks (Hourly)

  • Check ice bins – fill if below half.
  • Check liquor bottles – replace empty bottles from storage.
  • Clear empty beer bottles and glasses from bar top.
  • Take trash to dumpster.
  • Run clean glassware from dishwasher to bar.

🛠️ Tools You'll Use

  • Ice scoops and buckets
  • Keg tap and hand truck
  • Box cutter (for breaking down boxes)
  • Heavy‑duty trash bags
  • Rubber gloves

📚 Training & Growth

Barbacks receive a 1‑hour orientation covering bar layout, restocking routes, safety, and communication. This is an excellent entry point into the bar industry. Successful barbacks are often promoted to bartender in subsequent festivals.

🤝 Working with Bartenders

Bartenders rely on barbacks to keep them moving. Anticipate needs – if ice is low, fill it before being asked. Keep an eye on trash cans. A great barback makes the whole bar run smoothly and shares in the tip pool.

To apply for Barback: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Barback Application" in the subject line. No experience required – just a strong work ethic.

Volunteer barback positions also available (meal + shirt + festival access).
