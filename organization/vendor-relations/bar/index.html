Bar Team (Alcohol Service)

The Bar Team operates full‑service bars serving beer, wine, and liquor at Selassiefest. All bar staff are trained in responsible beverage service and age verification. The team reports to the Vendor Relations Manager (Dwight) and works closely with festival security.

📧 All bar inquiries: selassiefest@gmail.com (please specify bar role in subject line)

🍺 Bar Manager

Status: Open for applications

Reports to: Vendor Relations Manager (Dwight)

Key Responsibilities:

  • Manages liquor inventory, ordering, and cost control.
  • Supervises all bar staff (lead bartenders, bartenders, barbacks).
  • Ensures compliance with alcohol laws and responsible service training.
  • Coordinates with security on ID checking protocols.
  • Handles cash drops and reconciliations.

⚠️ Responsible Beverage Service

All bar staff must complete a responsible vendor training (e.g., TIPS or state‑approved course) before the festival. Selassiefest is committed to preventing underage drinking and over‑service. Security will assist ID checkers during peak hours.

  • No alcohol served to anyone under 21.
  • Cut off service to visibly intoxicated persons.
  • Check IDs for all看上去 under 30.
  • Acceptable IDs: Driver's license, passport, military ID (no photos of IDs).

📍 Bar Locations

Selassiefest typically operates 2–3 bar locations depending on festival size. Each bar has a similar staffing structure. The Bar Manager floats between bars while each bar has its own Lead Bartender.

Current Bar Openings: Bar Manager, Lead Bartender, Bartenders (multiple), Barbacks (multiple), ID Checkers (shared with security).

To apply: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with the role title in the subject line (e.g., "Bar Manager Application"). Include relevant experience and any alcohol service certifications.
