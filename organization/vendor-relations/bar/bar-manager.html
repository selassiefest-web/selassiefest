Bar Manager

📢 Position Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Bar Manager Application"

Position Overview

The Bar Manager oversees all alcohol service operations at Selassiefest, including inventory management, staff supervision, legal compliance, and financial reconciliation. This role reports to the Vendor Relations Manager (Dwight) and manages lead bartenders, bartenders, barbacks, and coordinates with ID checkers.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage liquor inventory: ordering, receiving, tracking usage, and minimizing waste.
  • Supervise all bar staff (lead bartenders, bartenders, barbacks).
  • Ensure compliance with state and local alcohol laws, including responsible service training.
  • Coordinate with festival security on ID checking and crowd control.
  • Handle cash drops, reconcile daily sales, and prepare financial reports.
  • Conduct pre‑event training for all bar staff on recipes, pouring standards, and safety.
  • Set up bar layouts, equipment, and signage before the festival.
  • Resolve customer complaints and staff issues during the event.

Qualifications

  • 3+ years bar management experience, preferably in high‑volume events or nightlife.
  • Valid alcohol service certification (TIPS, ServSafe Alcohol, or state‑approved).
  • Strong inventory and cost control skills.
  • Ability to lead a team under pressure.
  • Familiarity with POS systems and cash handling.
  • Physical ability to lift beer kegs, cases of liquor, and stand for long hours.

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Vendor Relations Manager (Dwight)
  • Works with: Lead Bartenders, Security (ID checkers), Dwight (budget)
  • Manages: Lead Bartenders, Bartenders, Barbacks

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: 2 months pre‑festival + event days
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid (seasonal contract)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Pour cost %, inventory variance, compliance, sales per labor hour

📋 Sample Daily Tasks (Event Days)

  • 8:00 AM – Receive deliveries, check invoices
  • 9:00 AM – Pre‑shift meeting with bar staff
  • 10:00 AM – Bar opens; monitor service
  • 2:00 PM – Cash drop, inventory check
  • 6:00 PM – Second cash drop, reorder if needed
  • 9:00 PM – Last call, supervise cleanup
  • 11:00 PM – Reconcile sales, secure liquor

📊 Sample Drink Menu (Reggae Festival)

  • Red Stripe beer, local craft brews
  • Rum punch (house signature)
  • Ginger beer + vodka
  • Wine (red/white)
  • Non‑alcoholic: coconut water, ginger beer, sodas

⚖️ Legal & Safety Responsibilities

  • Ensure all bartenders have valid alcohol service certifications.
  • Post required signage (no alcohol to minors, no service to intoxicated persons).
  • Coordinate with local police or ABC officers if required.
  • Maintain incident logs for any alcohol‑related issues.
  • Ensure proper disposal of empty bottles and packaging.

To apply for Bar Manager: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Bar Manager Application" in the subject line. Attach your resume and a copy of your alcohol service certification.
