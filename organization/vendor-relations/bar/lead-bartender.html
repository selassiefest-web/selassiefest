Lead Bartender

📢 Position Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Lead Bartender Application"

Position Overview

The Lead Bartender supervises daily bar operations, trains staff, and ensures quality and speed of service. This role reports to the Bar Manager and leads a team of bartenders and barbacks during the festival.

Key Responsibilities

  • Train bartenders on drink recipes, pouring standards, and POS system.
  • Set up and break down the bar each day (ice, garnishes, liquor, glassware).
  • Handle complex or VIP drink requests.
  • Assist Bar Manager with inventory counts and ordering.
  • Ensure bartenders are checking IDs and following responsible service laws.
  • Maintain cleanliness and organization of the bar.
  • Step in to bartend during peak hours or call‑offs.
  • Resolve customer complaints and de‑escalate conflicts.

Qualifications

  • 2+ years bartending experience, preferably in high‑volume settings (festivals, clubs, busy bars).
  • Valid alcohol service certification (TIPS, ServSafe Alcohol, or equivalent).
  • Strong knowledge of classic and reggae‑themed cocktails (rum punch, ginger beer drinks, etc.).
  • Leadership skills – able to direct a team calmly during rushes.
  • Basic math skills for cash handling and pour cost awareness.
  • Physical ability to lift 40 lbs and stand for long shifts.

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Bar Manager
  • Works with: Bartenders, Barbacks, ID Checkers
  • Manages: Bartenders and Barbacks (in coordination with Bar Manager)

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: Event days + 1‑2 days pre‑festival setup/training
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid hourly + tips (if applicable)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Drink quality, speed, staff adherence to recipes

🍹 Signature Festival Drinks (Lead Bartender's Responsibility)

  • Selassiefest Rum Punch: Dark rum, coconut water, lime, pineapple, nutmeg.
  • Ginger Fire: Vodka, ginger beer, lime, jalapeño.
  • Redemption Red: Red wine + berry liqueur.
  • Non‑alc Ital Cooler: Ginger beer, coconut water, lime, mint.

📋 Sample Pre‑Event Tasks

  • 2 days before: Assist Bar Manager with inventory and bar setup.
  • 1 day before: Lead bartender training session (recipe review, POS practice).
  • Morning of event: Assign bartenders to stations, check supplies.

🍸 Lead Bartender Training Duties

The Lead Bartender conducts a 2‑hour training session before the festival covering:

  • Pour counts and jigger use (ensuring consistent drink strength).
  • ID checking and refusal protocols.
  • Recognizing intoxication and cutoff procedures.
  • POS system basics (Square, Toast, etc.).
  • Cleaning and closing checklists.

🌟 Path to Bar Manager

The Lead Bartender role is a natural stepping stone to Bar Manager in future festivals. Selassiefest promotes internally when possible.

To apply for Lead Bartender: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Lead Bartender Application" in the subject line. Include your resume, any bartending certifications, and a brief note about your experience with high‑volume service.
