Prep Cook

📢 Positions Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Prep Cook Application"

Position Overview

Prep Cooks are the backbone of the kitchen, responsible for chopping vegetables, portioning ingredients, and preparing mise en place for line cooks. This role reports to the Head Cook and works closely with all kitchen stations to ensure smooth service.

Key Responsibilities

  • Wash, peel, chop, and portion vegetables according to prep lists.
  • Prepare sauces, marinades, and dressings following recipes.
  • Label and date all prepared ingredients for proper storage.
  • Maintain a clean and organized prep area.
  • Assist with receiving and storing deliveries.
  • Monitor inventory of prepped items and communicate shortages to Head Cook.
  • Help with kitchen setup and breakdown as needed.

Qualifications

  • Basic knife skills (or willingness to learn).
  • Ability to follow recipes and portion guides.
  • Attention to detail and cleanliness.
  • Food Handler Certification preferred (not required).
  • Reliable and punctual.
  • Physical ability to stand, bend, and lift up to 30 lbs.

Typical Prep Tasks

  • Chopping onions, bell peppers, scallions, garlic, ginger.
  • Peeling and cubing pumpkin, sweet potatoes, yams.
  • Cutting cabbage, carrots, and callaloo.
  • Juicing limes and lemons.
  • Portioning rice and beans.
  • Making jerk marinade and coconut milk base.
  • Preparing fruit cups (mango, pineapple, papaya).

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open (1–2 positions)
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: 2–3 days before festival + event days
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid hourly or volunteer (meal + shirt + ticket)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Prep speed, accuracy, waste reduction

📋 Sample Schedule

  • 2 days before: 8:00 AM – 4:00 PM (heavy prep)
  • 1 day before: 8:00 AM – 2:00 PM (final prep)
  • Event days: 6:00 AM – 12:00 PM (morning prep, then assist service)

🔪 Tools You'll Use

  • Chef's knife and paring knife
  • Cutting boards (color‑coded)
  • Food processor (for sauces)
  • Scale and measuring cups
  • Labels and marker
  • Cambro containers for storage

📚 Training & Growth

Prep Cooks receive on‑the‑job training from the Head Cook. This is an excellent entry point into the Selassiefest kitchen. Successful prep cooks may be invited to return as line cooks in future festivals.

To apply for Prep Cook: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Prep Cook Application" in the subject line. No formal culinary experience required – just a willingness to work hard and learn.

Volunteer prep positions also available for high school students (accompanied by adult).
