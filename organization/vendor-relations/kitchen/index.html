Kitchen Team

The Selassiefest Kitchen prepares and serves authentic ital, vegan, and Caribbean cuisine to festival‑goers, staff, and artists. Under the leadership of Vendor Relations Manager Dwight, the kitchen team ensures high‑quality food, safety, and efficient service.

📧 All kitchen inquiries: selassiefest@gmail.com (please specify kitchen role in subject line)

👨‍🍳 Kitchen Manager

Status: Open for applications

Reports to: Vendor Relations Manager (Dwight)

Key Responsibilities:

  • Oversees all kitchen operations, inventory, and food safety (HACCP).
  • Schedules kitchen staff (cooks, porters, cashiers).
  • Orders food and supplies within budget.
  • Ensures compliance with health department regulations.
  • Coordinates with Dwight on artist catering needs (ital, vegan, allergies).

Seasonal (2 months pre‑festival + event days)

📋 Kitchen Operational Flow

  1. Prep Cooks prepare ingredients (chop, portion, label).
  2. Line Cooks cook dishes per station orders.
  3. Expeditor assembles and checks each plate before service.
  4. Cashier takes payment and hands food to customer.
  5. Dishwasher continuously cleans cookware and utensils.

Artist catering: Kitchen Manager coordinates with Dwight to prepare separate ital meals for green rooms, respecting all dietary restrictions.

To apply for any kitchen position: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with the exact role title (e.g., "Kitchen Manager Application") in the subject line. Include your resume and relevant food handler certifications.
