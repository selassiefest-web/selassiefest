Food Service Cashier

📢 Positions Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Cashier Application"

Position Overview

Food Service Cashiers are the friendly face of Selassiefest's kitchen. They take customer orders, process payments, and hand out prepared food. This role reports to the Kitchen Manager and works closely with the expeditor to ensure accurate and fast service.

Key Responsibilities

  • Greet customers warmly and answer menu questions (e.g., ital, vegan, allergen info).
  • Take orders accurately using a POS system or handwritten ticket.
  • Process cash and credit card payments, making correct change.
  • Communicate orders to the expeditor (or line cooks) clearly.
  • Hand out finished food to customers, ensuring order accuracy.
  • Keep the service counter clean and stocked with utensils, napkins, and condiments.
  • Assist with basic food prep or restocking during slow periods (e.g., filling cups, refilling sauces).
  • Balance cash drawer at start and end of shift.

Qualifications

  • Friendly, patient, and customer‑service oriented.
  • Basic math skills and ability to handle cash accurately.
  • Ability to stand for long hours and work in a fast‑paced outdoor environment.
  • Prior cashier or retail experience preferred but not required.
  • Familiarity with ital/vegan food is a plus.

Typical Tasks

  • "Welcome to Selassiefest Kitchen! What can I get for you?"
  • Ring up order: "One rice & peas, one stew, one ginger beer – that's $18."
  • Take cash or swipe card; give change and receipt.
  • Call out order to expo: "Order up – rice, stew, ginger beer."
  • Receive finished food and hand to customer: "Enjoy your meal!"
  • Wipe down counter, refill napkins, restock cups.

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open (multiple positions)
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: Event days only (shifts available)
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid hourly or volunteer (meal + shirt + ticket)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Transaction speed, cash drawer accuracy, customer feedback

📋 Sample Shift

  • Morning shift: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Evening shift: 2:00 PM – 8:00 PM
  • Training: 30‑minute POS and menu orientation before first shift.

🛠️ Tools & Equipment

  • POS tablet or cash register (Square or similar)
  • Cash drawer and change fund
  • Card reader (contactless/chip)
  • Ticket printer or paper pad
  • Hand sanitizer and gloves

💬 Cashier Customer Service Best Practices

  • Smile and greet every customer – "One love, welcome!"
  • Explain ital food simply: "No salt, no animal products – all natural ingredients."
  • If an item is sold out, suggest alternatives (e.g., "We're out of stew, but the rice & peas is still available.")
  • Keep the line moving – if a customer is indecisive, ask them to step aside.
  • Thank each customer and invite them back.

🤝 Working with the Kitchen

Cashiers are the bridge between customers and the kitchen. Good communication with the expeditor prevents mistakes. If a customer has a complaint or special request, cashiers relay it calmly and clearly.

To apply for Food Service Cashier: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Cashier Application" in the subject line. Include any prior customer service or cash handling experience (not required).

Volunteer cashier positions also available – great for students and first‑time festival staff.
