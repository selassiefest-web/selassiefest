Head Cook / Chef

📢 Position Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Head Cook Application"

Position Overview

The Head Cook leads menu execution and food preparation for Selassiefest's kitchen. This role reports to the Kitchen Manager and supervises line cooks, prep cooks, and kitchen assistants. The Head Cook ensures that all dishes meet quality, taste, and cultural authenticity standards – with a special focus on ital and vegan Rastafari cuisine.

Key Responsibilities

  • Execute recipes and menus developed with the Kitchen Manager.
  • Supervise line cooks and prep cooks during service.
  • Ensure ital cooking principles (no salt, fresh natural ingredients, no animal products) are followed.
  • Maintain food quality, presentation, and consistency.
  • Train junior cooks on proper techniques and safety.
  • Coordinate with the expeditor to ensure timely order flow.
  • Assist the Kitchen Manager with inventory and ordering as needed.
  • Step into any station (grill, fry, cold prep) when short‑handed.

Qualifications

  • 2+ years cooking experience in high‑volume kitchens, ideally festivals or food trucks.
  • Knowledge of ital, vegan, and Caribbean cuisine strongly preferred.
  • Food Handler Certification (or ability to obtain before event).
  • Ability to lead a small team under pressure.
  • Physical stamina to stand for long hours and work in outdoor/heat conditions.
  • Reliable transportation to festival site.

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Kitchen Manager
  • Works with: Line Cooks, Prep Cooks, Expeditor, Dishwasher
  • Manages: Line Cooks and Prep Cooks (in coordination with Kitchen Manager)

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: Event days + 1 week prep
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid (daily rate or seasonal contract)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Food quality scores, waste reduction, ticket times

🍛 Sample Menu Items (Ital/Vegan)

  • Rice & peas (coconut milk base)
  • Steamed cabbage and carrots
  • Plantains (fried or roasted)
  • Ital stew (callaloo, okra, pumpkin)
  • Jerk tofu or jackfruit
  • Festival bread (sweet fried dough)
  • Fresh fruit cups (mango, pineapple)

🍽️ Station Assignments

  • Grill: Jerk tofu, plantains
  • Fry: Festival bread, fritters
  • Cold Prep: Salads, fruit cups, sauces
  • Steam Table: Rice, stews, vegetables

📋 Training & Onboarding (Led by Kitchen Manager)

  • 2 weeks before festival: Menu review, recipe testing, and ital cooking principles workshop.
  • 2 days before: Kitchen setup, equipment orientation, and safety walkthrough.
  • Day before: Dry run with the full kitchen team.
  • During event: Daily pre‑shift huddle to review specials and staff assignments.

🌟 Growth Path

Successful Head Cooks may be considered for Kitchen Manager in future festivals. Selassiefest values promoting from within.

To apply for Head Cook: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Head Cook Application" in the subject line. Attach your resume and a brief note about your experience with ital or vegan cooking.
