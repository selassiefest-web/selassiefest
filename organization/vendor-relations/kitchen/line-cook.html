Line Cook

📢 Multiple Positions Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Line Cook Application"

Position Overview

Line Cooks are responsible for preparing specific dishes according to station assignments (grill, fry, cold prep, or steam table). They report to the Head Cook and work closely with prep cooks and the expeditor to ensure fast, high‑quality service during the festival.

Key Responsibilities

  • Prepare assigned menu items following recipes and plating guides.
  • Maintain cleanliness and organization of station.
  • Communicate with prep cooks to ensure adequate mise en place.
  • Work with expeditor to fire orders in correct sequence.
  • Follow all food safety and sanitation standards.
  • Assist with setup and breakdown of kitchen equipment.
  • Restock station as needed during service.

Qualifications

  • 1+ year line cook experience (or equivalent culinary training).
  • Ability to work quickly and accurately under pressure.
  • Knowledge of basic kitchen equipment (knives, grills, fryers, steam tables).
  • Food Handler Certification preferred (can be obtained before event).
  • Positive attitude and teamwork orientation.
  • Physical ability to stand, lift up to 40 lbs, and work in warm conditions.

Station Assignments (Based on Experience)

  • Grill Station: Jerk tofu, plantains, grilled vegetables.
  • Fry Station: Festival bread, fritters, fried dumplings.
  • Steam Table: Rice & peas, ital stews, steamed vegetables.
  • Cold Prep: Salads, fruit cups, sauces, and garnishes.

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open (2–5 positions)
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: Event days only
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid hourly or volunteer + benefits (meal, shirt, ticket)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Speed, accuracy, waste control

📋 Sample Shifts

  • Morning: 7:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Evening: 2:00 PM – 10:00 PM
  • (Flexible based on festival schedule)

🍽️ What You'll Cook

  • Ital stews (callaloo, pumpkin, okra)
  • Rice & peas (coconut milk)
  • Jerk tofu or jackfruit
  • Fried plantains
  • Festival bread
  • Fresh fruit cups

👨‍🍳 Kitchen Team Culture

Selassiefest's kitchen is a supportive, fast‑paced environment. We value:

  • Respect: For each other, for the food, and for the Rastafari ital tradition.
  • Cleanliness: Every cook maintains a tidy station.
  • Communication: Call out needs, ask for help, and celebrate service together.
  • Growth: Many line cooks return each year and are promoted to prep cook or head cook roles.

To apply for Line Cook: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Line Cook Application" in the subject line. Include a brief resume or description of your cooking experience.

Volunteer line cook positions also available for those with less experience – please indicate "Volunteer Line Cook" in your email.
