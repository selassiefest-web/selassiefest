Expeditor

📢 Position Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Expeditor Application"

Position Overview

The Expeditor (or "Expo") is the communication hub of the kitchen, responsible for coordinating order flow between the line cooks and the cashiers/service window. This role reports to the Kitchen Manager and ensures that every plate leaving the kitchen is accurate, complete, and on time.

Key Responsibilities

  • Receive orders from cashiers (via POS tickets or verbal).
  • Call out orders to line cooks by station (grill, fry, steam, cold).
  • Assemble and garnish plates before sending to window.
  • Check each plate for accuracy, quality, and presentation.
  • Manage ticket timing – prioritize and coordinate multiple orders.
  • Communicate with cashiers about wait times or sold‑out items.
  • Maintain a clean and organized expo station.
  • Assist with restocking and light prep during slow periods.

Qualifications

  • Experience in fast‑paced kitchen (line cook, expo, or food service).
  • Strong communication and multitasking skills.
  • Ability to stay calm under pressure.
  • Basic food safety knowledge.
  • Familiarity with Selassiefest's ital menu (preferred).
  • Physical ability to stand for long hours and lift up to 30 lbs.

Key Skills

  • Ticket management: Organize orders by time and table (if seated).
  • Plating: Know how each dish should look before it goes out.
  • Communication: Call "fire" and "pick up" clearly to cooks.
  • Problem‑solving: Handle missing items or mistakes quickly.

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: Event days only
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid hourly or volunteer + benefits
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Order accuracy, ticket times, waste reduction

📋 Sample Ticket Flow

  1. Cashier rings order: "1 rice & peas, 1 stew, 1 festival bread"
  2. Expo calls: "Order fire – rice, stew, festival"
  3. Line cooks prepare each component.
  4. Cooks call "pick up" when ready.
  5. Expo assembles plate, checks, and calls "order up" to cashier.

🛠️ Tools Used

  • Ticket spike or POS printer
  • Plate warmers or pass
  • Tongs and spoons for garnishing
  • Two‑way radio (to communicate with cashiers)

🤝 Working with the Kitchen Team

The Expeditor is the voice of the kitchen. A great expo:

  • Thanks line cooks after each rush.
  • Calls out "all day" counts to help cooks prioritize.
  • Stays positive and encouraging even during peak hours.

To apply for Expeditor: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Expeditor Application" in the subject line. Include your kitchen or food service experience.
