Dishwasher / Porter

📢 Position Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Dishwasher Application"

Position Overview

The Dishwasher / Porter is essential to kitchen operations, ensuring that all cookware, utensils, and service items are cleaned, sanitized, and returned to stations quickly. This role reports to the Kitchen Manager and also assists with basic food prep, trash removal, and kitchen maintenance.

Key Responsibilities

  • Operate commercial dishwasher (or three‑compartment sink) to clean pots, pans, utensils, and plates.
  • Hand‑wash delicate items as needed.
  • Maintain proper sanitation and chemical levels (dish machine or sinks).
  • Empty trash and recycling bins throughout the kitchen and food court area.
  • Keep dish area and floors clean and dry.
  • Assist with receiving and storing deliveries (stocking shelves, rotating products).
  • Help with basic food prep when dish station is caught up (e.g., washing produce, peeling garlic).
  • Break down cardboard boxes and maintain recycling area.

Qualifications

  • No prior experience required – training provided.
  • Willingness to work hard and stay on your feet.
  • Ability to follow sanitation and safety guidelines.
  • Physical ability to lift up to 50 lbs, bend, and stand for long hours.
  • Positive attitude and teamwork orientation.

Typical Tasks

  • Scrape and spray dishes before washing.
  • Load and unload dishwasher racks.
  • Return clean pots and pans to cooking stations.
  • Take out trash and replace liners.
  • Wipe down counters and sweep floors.
  • Help with unloading food deliveries.

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: Event days only (shifts available)
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid hourly or volunteer (meal + shirt + ticket)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Clean dishes available, kitchen cleanliness, waste management

📋 Sample Shift

  • 10:00 AM – 8:00 PM (with breaks)
  • Morning: Set up dish station, receive deliveries
  • Mid‑day: Continuous dishwashing and trash runs
  • Evening: Final cleanup, break down dish area

🛠️ Tools & Equipment

  • Commercial dishwasher (or three‑sink setup)
  • Detergent and sanitizer chemicals
  • Scrub pads and scrapers
  • Drying racks
  • Heavy‑duty gloves
  • Trash cans and recycling bins
  • Hand truck / dolly (for deliveries)

🌟 Why This Role Matters

The dishwasher keeps the entire kitchen running. Without clean pots, pans, and utensils, cooks cannot prepare food. At Selassiefest, we treat our dishwashers with respect and gratitude – they are the unsung heroes of the festival.

To apply for Dishwasher / Porter: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Dishwasher Application" in the subject line. No experience necessary – just a strong work ethic.

Youth (16+) are welcome to apply for volunteer dishwasher positions.
