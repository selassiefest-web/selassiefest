Kitchen Manager

📢 Position Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Kitchen Manager Application"

Position Overview

The Kitchen Manager oversees all food operations for Selassiefest, including the central kitchen, food booths, and artist catering. This role reports to the Vendor Relations Manager (Dwight) and manages a team of cooks, porters, and cashiers.

Key Responsibilities

  • Manage all kitchen operations, inventory, and food safety (HACCP compliance).
  • Schedule kitchen staff (head cook, line cooks, prep cooks, dishwashers, cashiers).
  • Order food and supplies within budget; track costs and minimize waste.
  • Ensure compliance with local health department regulations and permits.
  • Coordinate with Dwight on artist catering needs (ital, vegan, allergies).
  • Conduct pre‑event training for all kitchen staff on food handling and safety.
  • Oversee setup, service, and teardown of kitchen and food booths.
  • Handle any kitchen emergencies (equipment failure, injuries, supply shortages).

Qualifications

  • 3+ years kitchen management experience, preferably in high‑volume event or festival settings.
  • Food Manager Certification (ServSafe or equivalent).
  • Knowledge of ital, vegan, and Caribbean cuisine strongly preferred.
  • Experience with inventory management and ordering systems.
  • Ability to lead a team under pressure and adapt to changing conditions.
  • Physical ability to stand for long hours and lift up to 50 lbs.

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Vendor Relations Manager (Dwight)
  • Works with: Head Cook, Dwight (for artist catering), Volunteer Coordinator (for kitchen volunteers)
  • Manages: Head Cook, Line Cooks, Prep Cooks, Expeditor, Dishwasher, Food Service Cashiers

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: 2 months pre‑festival + event days
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid (seasonal contract)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Food cost %, health inspection score, customer satisfaction

📋 Sample Daily Tasks (Event Days)

  • 6:00 AM – Arrive, inspect deliveries, assign stations
  • 7:00 AM – Pre‑shift meeting with kitchen staff
  • 8:00 AM – Service begins; monitor quality and speed
  • 12:00 PM – Check inventory; order restocks
  • 3:00 PM – Staff meal break coordination
  • 6:00 PM – Last call; supervise cleanup
  • 8:00 PM – Close kitchen, sign off on logs

🛠️ Tools Used

  • Inventory spreadsheets (Google Sheets)
  • Thermometers and temperature logs
  • Two‑way radio for communication
  • POS system for cashier stations

🤝 Working with Dwight (Vendor Relations Manager)

The Kitchen Manager meets daily with Dwight to:

  • Confirm artist catering needs (ital meals, special diets).
  • Review budget vs. actual spending.
  • Coordinate any cross‑department needs (e.g., ice from beverage stands).
  • Report staffing or equipment issues.

To apply for Kitchen Manager: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Kitchen Manager Application" in the subject line. Attach your resume and a brief cover letter describing your festival or high‑volume cooking experience.
