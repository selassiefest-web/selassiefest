Beverage Server

📢 Multiple Positions Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Beverage Server Application"

Position Overview

Beverage Servers are the friendly faces at Selassiefest's non‑alcoholic drink stands. They pour and serve beverages, process payments, and maintain a clean, inviting station. This role reports to the Beverage Stand Supervisor and works alongside other servers and stock runners.

Key Responsibilities

  • Greet customers warmly and answer questions about drinks (e.g., "What's sorrel?").
  • Pour and serve beverages (water, juice, soda, coffee, cultural drinks).
  • Process cash and credit card payments accurately.
  • Keep the stand clean and organized (wipe counters, empty trash).
  • Monitor inventory at your stand; alert supervisor when supplies are low.
  • Offer reusable cup discounts and promote recycling.
  • Assist with opening and closing duties (setup, restocking, cleaning).

Qualifications

  • No prior experience required – training provided.
  • Friendly, patient, and customer‑service oriented.
  • Basic math skills for cash handling.
  • Ability to stand for long hours and work in an outdoor environment.
  • Must be at least 16 years old (18+ for cash handling recommended).

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Beverage Stand Supervisor
  • Works with: Other Beverage Servers, Stock Runners

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open (2–4 per stand, multiple stands)
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: Event days only (shifts available)
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid hourly or volunteer (meal + shirt + ticket)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Speed, accuracy, customer feedback

🥤 Drinks You'll Serve

  • Bottled water (and refill station assistance)
  • Lemonade (fresh)
  • Orange juice
  • Carrot juice (seasonal)
  • Ginger beer (non‑alcoholic)
  • Sorrel (hibiscus)
  • Coconut water (fresh or packaged)
  • Soda (Coke, Sprite, diet options)
  • Hot coffee, herbal tea

📋 Sample Shift

  • Morning shift: 9:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Evening shift: 2:00 PM – 8:00 PM

💬 Customer Service Best Practices

  • Smile and greet every customer – "Welcome! Thirsty? Try our fresh ginger beer."
  • Explain cultural drinks: "Sorrel is a sweet hibiscus tea – very refreshing."
  • Offer refill discounts: "If you have your own cup, it's $1 off."
  • Keep the line moving; if someone is indecisive, suggest a top seller.
  • Thank each customer: "One love! Enjoy the festival."

🤝 Working with Stock Runners

Stock runners will bring ice, cups, and beverage boxes to your stand. Let them know when you're low on something. Keep your stand organized so they can restock quickly.

To apply for Beverage Server: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Beverage Server Application" in the subject line. No experience needed – just a positive attitude and willingness to learn.

Volunteer positions available for students and first‑time festival staff.
