Beverage Stand Supervisor

📢 Position Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Beverage Stand Supervisor Application"

Position Overview

The Beverage Stand Supervisor oversees all non‑alcoholic beverage operations at Selassiefest, including multiple stands located throughout the festival grounds. This role reports to the Vendor Relations Manager (Dwight) and manages beverage servers and stock runners.

Key Responsibilities

  • Supervise daily operations of 3–5 beverage stands.
  • Train and schedule beverage servers and stock runners.
  • Manage inventory: track beverage levels, cups, ice, and supplies; reorder as needed.
  • Oversee cash handling and daily reconciliation for all stands.
  • Ensure stands are clean, well‑stocked, and comply with health codes.
  • Troubleshoot equipment issues (coolers, dispensers, ice machines).
  • Coordinate with Dwight on supply deliveries and budget.
  • Serve as point of contact for customer complaints or staff issues.

Qualifications

  • 2+ years experience in food/beverage or retail management.
  • Strong inventory and cash handling skills.
  • Ability to lead a team in a fast‑paced outdoor environment.
  • Basic knowledge of health department regulations.
  • Physical ability to lift 40 lbs and stand for long hours.
  • Reliable transportation to festival site.

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Vendor Relations Manager (Dwight)
  • Works with: Stock Runners, Beverage Servers, Dwight
  • Manages: Beverage Servers and Stock Runners

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: 2 weeks pre‑festival (planning) + event days
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid (seasonal contract)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Inventory accuracy, sales per stand, customer satisfaction

📋 Sample Daily Tasks (Event Days)

  • 7:00 AM – Arrive, check all stands, assign staff
  • 8:00 AM – Morning inventory count, place restock orders
  • 9:00 AM – Stands open; monitor service and cleanliness
  • 12:00 PM – Cash pickup, spot checks
  • 3:00 PM – Re‑stock ice and beverages
  • 6:00 PM – Evening cash drop
  • 8:00 PM – Stands close; supervise cleanup and final inventory

🥤 Products You'll Manage

  • Bottled water and refill stations
  • Fresh juices (lemonade, orange, carrot)
  • Cultural drinks (ginger beer, sorrel, coconut water)
  • Sodas and energy drinks
  • Hot coffee and herbal teas
  • Cups, lids, straws, napkins
  • Ice (bagged or from machine)

📚 Supervisor Training Responsibilities

  • Train beverage servers on portion control, cash handling, and customer service.
  • Train stock runners on safe lifting, restocking routes, and ice handling.
  • Conduct health and safety orientation (handwashing, no cross‑contamination).
  • Review sustainability practices (recycling, reusable cup discount).

🤝 Working with Dwight (Vendor Relations Manager)

The Beverage Stand Supervisor meets daily with Dwight to review inventory levels, discuss any supply shortages, and report sales. Dwight handles large orders and vendor contracts; the supervisor manages daily execution.

🌟 Growth Path

This role is ideal for someone interested in event operations. Successful supervisors may be considered for broader vendor management roles in future festivals.

To apply for Beverage Stand Supervisor: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Beverage Stand Supervisor Application" in the subject line. Attach your resume and a brief note about your food/beverage or management experience.
