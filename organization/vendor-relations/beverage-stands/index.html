Beverage Stands (Non‑Alcoholic)

The Beverage Stands team operates separate, alcohol‑free stations serving water, juice, soda, coffee, and cultural drinks like ginger beer, sorrel, and coconut water. These stands are family‑friendly and located throughout the festival grounds. The team reports to the Vendor Relations Manager (Dwight).

📧 All beverage stand inquiries: selassiefest@gmail.com (please specify role in subject line)

🧃 Beverage Stand Supervisor

Status: Open for applications

Reports to: Vendor Relations Manager (Dwight)

Key Responsibilities:

  • Oversees multiple beverage stands during the festival.
  • Manages inventory, restocking, and cash handling across stands.
  • Trains and schedules beverage servers and stock runners.
  • Ensures stands are clean, well‑stocked, and following health codes.
  • Coordinates with Dwight on supply orders (cups, ice, beverages).

🥤 Beverage Stand Menu (Sample)

  • Water: Bottled spring water, dispensers (reusable cup option).
  • Juices: Freshly squeezed lemonade, orange juice, carrot juice.
  • Cultural drinks: Ginger beer (non‑alcoholic), sorrel (hibiscus), coconut water (fresh or packaged).
  • Sodas: Coca‑Cola, Sprite, diet options.
  • Hot drinks: Coffee, herbal tea (cerasee, mint).
  • Energy drinks: One option (e.g., Red Bull).

All beverages are non‑alcoholic. Separate bar areas serve alcohol.

♻️ Sustainability Commitment

Selassiefest encourages reusable cups and water bottle refills. Beverage stands offer a discount for customers who bring their own cups. Stock runners ensure recycling bins are available and used correctly.

📍 Typical Beverage Stand Locations

  • Main entrance (welcome area)
  • Near main stage
  • Heritage Village / Healing Grove
  • Kids' zone / family area
  • Vendor village (near food court)

Current Beverage Stand Openings: Beverage Stand Supervisor, Beverage Servers (multiple), Stock Runners (multiple).

To apply: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with the role title in the subject line (e.g., "Beverage Stand Supervisor Application"). No experience necessary for server/runner roles – training provided.
