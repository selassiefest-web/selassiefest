Stock Runner

📢 Multiple Positions Open – Apply Now

Email: selassiefest@gmail.com with subject "Stock Runner Application"

Position Overview

Stock Runners keep beverage stands supplied with everything they need: ice, cups, beverages, and other supplies. This role reports to the Beverage Stand Supervisor and works closely with beverage servers to ensure no stand runs out during peak hours.

Key Responsibilities

  • Transport ice, beverage boxes, cups, and other supplies from central storage to each beverage stand.
  • Check each stand regularly and ask servers what they need.
  • Change out empty CO₂ tanks for soda dispensers (if applicable).
  • Assist with heavy lifting and loading/unloading deliveries.
  • Maintain cleanliness of storage area and restocking equipment.
  • Help with opening and closing duties (setting up coolers, breaking down).
  • Communicate supply shortages to supervisor immediately.

Qualifications

  • No prior experience required – training provided.
  • Physical ability to lift 50 lbs repeatedly and walk/stand for long hours.
  • Reliable, punctual, and able to take direction.
  • Valid driver's license preferred (for large supply runs, but not required).
  • Must be at least 16 years old (18+ recommended for heavy lifting).

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Beverage Stand Supervisor
  • Works with: Beverage Servers, other Stock Runners

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Open (multiple positions)
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: Event days only (shifts available)
  • 💰 Compensation: Paid hourly or volunteer (meal + shirt + ticket)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Restocking speed, no stand runs out of top items

📦 Typical Items You'll Move

  • Ice (bags or from machine via cart)
  • Cases of bottled water (24‑pack)
  • Juice jugs (gallon sizes)
  • Soda syrup boxes (for fountain dispensers)
  • CO₂ tanks (20–50 lbs)
  • Cups (sleeves of 50–100)
  • Napkins, straws, lids
  • Trash bags and cleaning supplies

📋 Sample Shift

  • Morning shift: 8:00 AM – 3:00 PM
  • Evening shift: 2:00 PM – 9:00 PM

🛡️ Safety & Efficiency Tips

  • Use hand trucks or dollies for heavy items – ask if you need help.
  • Watch for festival attendees when moving through crowds; say "Excuse me, restocking."
  • Keep storage area neat and labeled; broken down boxes go to recycling.
  • Wear gloves when handling ice or chemicals (for cleaning).
  • Hydrate frequently – it's hot work!

🤝 Working with Beverage Servers

Servers will flag you down when they're low. Check in regularly even if not called. A proactive stock runner prevents long lines. You're the hero behind the scenes – servers will appreciate you.

To apply for Stock Runner: Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Stock Runner Application" in the subject line. No experience needed – just a strong work ethic and willingness to stay moving.

Volunteer positions available for youth (16+) and adults.
