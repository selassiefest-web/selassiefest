Talent Booker

🎤 Current Talent Booker: Kirk

Kirk brings decades of experience in the reggae music industry, having booked legendary acts across local, national, and international stages. His deep roots in the Rastafari community and his relationships with artists, agents, and festival promoters make him an invaluable asset to Selassiefest.

Position Overview

The Talent Booker is responsible for identifying, negotiating with, and contracting all performers, speakers, and cultural presenters for Selassiefest. This role reports directly to the Artistic Director and works closely with the Artist Liaison and Stage Manager.

Key Responsibilities

  • Research and discover emerging and established reggae, roots, dub, and Afro‑Caribbean talent.
  • Negotiate contracts, performance fees, travel riders, and technical requirements.
  • Cultivate relationships with booking agents, artist managers, and record labels.
  • Balance the artistic vision with the festival's budget and community values.
  • Collaborate with the Artistic Director to curate a diverse, culturally authentic lineup.
  • Stay informed about visa and international touring requirements for non‑US artists.

Kirk's Background

  • Local: Booked headliners for over 20 reggae showcases in the New York tri‑state area, including Brooklyn, Harlem, and the Bronx.
  • National: Secured talent for major US festivals (e.g., California Roots, Reggae Rise Up) and club tours across 30+ states.
  • International: Arranged North American tours for Jamaican legends (e.g., Burning Spear, Luciano) and emerging artists from the UK, Ethiopia, and Brazil.
  • Fluent in Rastafari culture and the history of reggae as a tool for social justice.

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Artistic Director
  • Works with: Artist Liaison, Stage Manager, Hospitality Coordinator, Marketing Team
  • Manages: Programming Assistant (if applicable)

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Filled (Kirk)
  • 📧 Contact: kirk@selassiefest.com
  • ⏰ Commitment: Year‑round, part‑time (increases 6 months pre‑festival)
  • 🎯 Key Metric: Audience satisfaction & ticket sales attributed to lineup

"Reggae is more than music – it's a message. Every artist I book brings a piece of that message to Selassiefest."
— Kirk

📅 Booking Timeline (Led by Kirk)

  • 9–12 months out: Research, outreach to agents, budget planning.
  • 6–8 months out: Headliner contracts finalized.
  • 4–5 months out: Support acts and local openers booked.
  • 2–3 months out: Confirm all riders, travel, and visas.
  • 1 month out: Final schedule handed to Stage Manager.
  • During event: Available for any artist issues; supports Artist Liaison.

Note: The Talent Booker position is currently held by Kirk. Interested candidates for future consideration may send a resume and booking portfolio to talent@selassiefest.com.
