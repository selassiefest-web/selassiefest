Talent Relations Team

The Talent Relations team is responsible for curating, booking, and supporting all artists, speakers, and cultural presenters at Selassiefest. They ensure a world‑class experience for performers while aligning programming with the festival's mission of community, education, and celebration of the Rastafari and Ethiopian cultural heritage.

🎵 Talent Booker

Reports to: Artistic Director

Key Responsibilities:

  • Researches and identifies potential performers (musicians, DJs, poets, speakers, dancers).
  • Negotiates contracts, fees, and technical riders.
  • Builds and maintains relationships with booking agents and artist managers.
  • Balances budget with artistic vision.

Seasonal or year‑round part‑time

🤝 Artist Liaison

Reports to: Artistic Director

Key Responsibilities:

  • Acts as primary point of contact for booked artists from contract signing to post‑event.
  • Manages artist hospitality: travel, accommodation, meals, green rooms.
  • Communicates schedules, sound check times, and set requirements.
  • Resolves any issues or special requests on‑site.

Seasonal (intensive 2‑3 months pre‑event + event days)

🎛️ Stage Manager

Reports to: Artistic Director

Key Responsibilities:

  • Oversees load‑in, sound checks, and set transitions.
  • Coordinates with technical crew (sound, lighting, video).
  • Ensures sets run on time and cues are executed.
  • Manages stage access and safety.

Event‑only (plus pre‑event planning)

🍽️ Hospitality Coordinator

Reports to: Artistic Director (or Artist Liaison)

Key Responsibilities:

  • Manages green rooms: stocking with water, snacks, toiletries.
  • Coordinates catering for artists and crew.
  • Arranges transportation (shuttles, taxis, car services).
  • Books hotel rooms and manages check‑in/out.

Seasonal (pre‑event + event days)

🏃 Talent Runner (2–4 positions)

Reports to: Artist Liaison or Stage Manager

Key Responsibilities:

  • Escorts artists from green room to stage and vice versa.
  • Runs errands (pick up meals, supplies, etc.).
  • Assists with last‑minute requests (e.g., instrument cables, water).
  • Helps with load‑in/load‑out of gear.

Event‑only (volunteer or paid position)

📋 Programming Assistant (Optional)

Reports to: Talent Booker

Key Responsibilities:

  • Researches emerging artists and local talent.
  • Maintains talent database and contact lists.
  • Assists with scheduling and contract paperwork.
  • Helps with artist communication (email, phone).

Intern or volunteer role

📅 Talent Relations Timeline (Typical Year)

  • 6–9 months before festival: Talent Booker researches and reaches out to headliners.
  • 5–6 months before: Contract negotiations; finalize main stage acts.
  • 3–4 months before: Book supporting acts, speakers, and workshop leaders.
  • 2 months before: Artist Liaison coordinates travel, hospitality, and riders.
  • 1 month before: Final schedules sent; Stage Manager produces run‑of‑show.
  • Event week: Hospitality Coordinator sets up green rooms; Talent Runners trained.
  • Post‑event: Thank‑you notes, feedback surveys, and reconfirmation for next year.

🤝 Collaboration

The Talent Relations team works closely with:

  • Marketing & Communications: To promote artist lineups and interviews.
  • Vendor Relations (Kitchen/Bar): To ensure catering and hospitality meet artist dietary needs.
  • Volunteer Coordination: To recruit stage hands, runners, and green room attendants.
  • Production (Sound/Lighting): To execute technical riders.
