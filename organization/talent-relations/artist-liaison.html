Artist Liaison

🎭 Current Artist Liaison: Kirk

In addition to his role as Talent Booker, Kirk serves as the primary point of contact for all booked artists. His deep relationships with reggae artists and his meticulous attention to hospitality ensure every performer feels welcomed, respected, and supported from contract signing to the final bow.

Position Overview

The Artist Liaison is the bridge between Selassiefest and its talent. This role ensures that all artist needs (travel, accommodation, meals, green rooms, technical requirements) are met before and during the festival. The Artist Liaison reports to the Artistic Director and works closely with the Talent Booker (also Kirk), Stage Manager, and Hospitality Coordinator.

Key Responsibilities

  • Serve as the single point of contact for all booked artists from contract signing through post‑event follow‑up.
  • Coordinate travel (flights, ground transportation), hotel accommodations, and per diems.
  • Manage artist riders (hospitality, backline, technical needs).
  • Oversee green room setup and catering in collaboration with the Hospitality Coordinator.
  • Communicate performance schedules, sound check times, and any changes.
  • Troubleshoot on‑site issues (e.g., lost luggage, medical needs, schedule conflicts).
  • Gather feedback post‑festival to improve future artist experiences.

Kirk's Dual Role Advantage

Because Kirk also books the talent, he has an intimate understanding of each artist's preferences, history, and expectations. This continuity eliminates miscommunication and builds trust. Artists know they are dealing with someone who:

  • Has already negotiated their contract and rider.
  • Understands their cultural and dietary needs (e.g., ital food, Rastafari protocols).
  • Is present at the festival to personally greet them and handle any last‑minute requests.

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Artistic Director
  • Works with: Talent Booker (self), Hospitality Coordinator, Stage Manager, Volunteer Coordinator (for runners)
  • Manages: Talent Runners (event‑day support staff/volunteers)

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Filled (Kirk, concurrent with Talent Booker)
  • 📧 Contact: kirk@selassiefest.com (use for artist inquiries)
  • ⏰ Commitment: Intense 3 months pre‑festival + event days
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Artist satisfaction survey scores, no unresolved rider issues

🎧 Sample Artist Testimonials (Kirk's Work)

  • "Kirk made us feel like family. Every detail was handled." – Luciano
  • "Smooth travel, perfect green room. Blessings." – Queen Ifrica
  • "One of the best‑run artist experiences we've had." – Midnite band manager

🛠️ Tools & Systems Used

  • Artist database (Airtable)
  • Travel booking (Chartered flights, hotel blocks)
  • Rider tracking (Google Docs)
  • On‑site radio communication

📅 During the Festival (Kirk's Daily Routine)

  • Morning: Check green rooms, confirm catering deliveries, review travel manifests for arriving artists.
  • Afternoon: Greet artists at load‑in, escort to green rooms, ensure sound check runs on time.
  • Evening: Monitor performances, handle any artist emergencies, coordinate after‑show hospitality.
  • Late night: Confirm next day's schedule, arrange transportation for departing artists.

🤝 Key Collaborations

  • Hospitality Coordinator: Kirk provides the rider; Hospitality Coordinator executes meals and green room stocking.
  • Stage Manager: Kirk ensures artists are at the stage on time; Stage Manager handles technical cues.
  • Talent Runners: Kirk directs runners to assist with luggage, equipment, and errands.
  • Vendor Relations (Kitchen): Kirk communicates any special dietary needs (ital, vegan, allergies).

Note: The Artist Liaison role is currently filled by Kirk. Given the dual‑role structure, future candidates would need to cover both Talent Booking and Artist Liaison or the organization may split the roles as it grows. Inquiries: artistic@selassiefest.com.
