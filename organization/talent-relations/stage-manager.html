Stage Manager

🎛️ Current Stage Manager: Field Marshall

Field Marshall brings decades of live event experience, having managed stages for major reggae festivals, club tours, and cultural celebrations. Known for keeping performances on schedule and maintaining calm under pressure, Field Marshall ensures every set runs smoothly from sound check to final applause.

Position Overview

The Stage Manager is responsible for all on‑stage operations, including load‑in, sound checks, set transitions, and technical coordination. This role reports to the Artistic Director and works closely with the Talent Booker, Artist Liaison, and the festival's production team (sound, lighting, video).

Key Responsibilities

  • Create and manage the detailed run‑of‑show schedule (cue‑to‑cue).
  • Coordinate load‑in and load‑out for all artists and equipment.
  • Oversee sound checks, ensuring each artist's technical needs are met.
  • Call cues for lighting, video, and sound effects during performances.
  • Manage stage access: only authorized personnel (artists, crew) on stage.
  • Troubleshoot on‑the‑fly issues (e.g., equipment failure, timing overruns).
  • Communicate with the front‑of‑house (FOH) engineer and monitor engineers.
  • Ensure compliance with safety regulations (fire lanes, cable management, emergency exits).

Field Marshall's Background

  • Reggae Festival Experience: Stage managed California Roots, Reggae on the River, Sierra Nevada World Music Festival.
  • Club & Theater Tours: Worked with Steel Pulse, Third World, The Wailers, and many top reggae touring acts.
  • Technical Expertise: Proficient with digital consoles (Yamaha CL/QL, Midas), stage plotting, and wireless systems.
  • Rastafari Cultural Knowledge: Respects artist protocols (e.g., no pork in green rooms, proper handling of the microphone as a "vessel").

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Artistic Director
  • Works with: Talent Booker (Kirk), Artist Liaison (Kirk), Hospitality Coordinator, Production Manager (if hired), Volunteer Coordinator (for stagehands)
  • Manages: Stagehands, backline technicians, and volunteer stage runners

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Filled (Field Marshall)
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com (please mark attention: Stage Manager)
  • ⏰ Commitment: 2 months pre‑festival planning + event days
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: On‑time performance rate, zero safety incidents on stage

🎯 Run‑of‑Show Sample (Single Stage)

  • 10:00–11:00 – Load‑in & line check
  • 11:00–12:00 – Sound check: Opening act
  • 12:00–12:30 – Sound check: Headliner
  • 12:30–13:00 – Doors open / ambient music
  • 13:00–13:45 – Opening act performance
  • 13:45–14:00 – Changeover
  • 14:00–15:30 – Headliner
  • 15:30–16:00 – Load‑out

🛠️ Essential Gear Managed

  • Microphones (wired/wireless)
  • DI boxes for instruments
  • Stage monitors (wedges or IEMs)
  • Backline (drums, amps, keyboards) – as per artist riders
  • Stage plot and input lists

📅 Timeline (Led by Field Marshall)

  • 8 weeks before: Collect all artist stage plots and input lists from Artist Liaison.
  • 6 weeks before: Create master run‑of‑show; share with production team.
  • 4 weeks before: Hire stagehands; coordinate with Volunteer Coordinator.
  • 1 week before: Site visit; mark stage dimensions and power drops.
  • 2 days before: Supervise stage construction and equipment load‑in.
  • Day before: Sound check rehearsals with key artists.
  • Event day: Call the show from backstage; manage transitions and emergencies.
  • Post‑event: Supervise load‑out; return rented equipment.

🤝 Working with Kirk (Talent Booker & Artist Liaison)

Field Marshall coordinates closely with Kirk to ensure artist technical riders are executed. Kirk communicates any last‑minute changes (e.g., an artist adds a keyboard), and Field Marshall adjusts stage plot accordingly. This partnership ensures a seamless artist experience.

Contact: For stage management inquiries or to express interest in future stage crew positions, email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Stage Manager" or "Stage Crew" in the subject line.
