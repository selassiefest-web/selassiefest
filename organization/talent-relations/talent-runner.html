Talent Runner

🏃 Current Talent Runner: Marlon

Marlon is the go‑to support for artists and the Talent Relations team. Known for his calm demeanor, reliability, and deep respect for Rastafari culture, Marlon ensures that every artist's last‑minute needs are met with a smile. Whether fetching a specific tea, escorting an artist to the stage, or helping with luggage, Marlon is always ready.

Position Overview

The Talent Runner provides on‑the‑ground support for artists, the Artist Liaison, and the Stage Manager. This entry‑level role is essential for smooth festival operations. Talent Runners report to the Artist Liaison (Kirk) during event days and may also take direction from the Stage Manager (Field Marshall) and Hospitality Coordinator (Dwight).

Key Responsibilities

  • Escort artists from green rooms to stage and back.
  • Run errands (e.g., pick up meals, water, batteries, instrument cables).
  • Assist with load‑in and load‑out of artist gear.
  • Help stock green rooms with snacks, drinks, and toiletries.
  • Communicate artist needs to the Artist Liaison or Hospitality Coordinator.
  • Provide general hospitality: directions, schedules, and friendly assistance.
  • Be available by radio for any last‑minute requests.

Marlon's Background

  • Community Involvement: Long‑time volunteer at local reggae showcases and community centers in Brooklyn.
  • Reliability: Known for showing up early, staying late, and never complaining.
  • Cultural Sensitivity: Understands Rastafari protocols (e.g., not touching an artist's microphone, respecting dietary restrictions).
  • Physical Readiness: Comfortable lifting gear, walking long distances, and working long hours outdoors.

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Artist Liaison (Kirk) during event days
  • Also takes direction from: Stage Manager (Field Marshall), Hospitality Coordinator (Dwight)
  • Works with: Other Talent Runners, stagehands, volunteer runners

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Filled (Marlon)
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com (for volunteer runner inquiries)
  • ⏰ Commitment: Event days only (plus one training session)
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Response time, artist feedback, reliability

🎒 Typical Tasks (Marlon's Day)

  • 8:00 AM – Check in with Kirk for daily assignments
  • 9:00 AM – Escort artist from hotel to green room
  • 10:30 AM – Fetch ital breakfast from kitchen for green room
  • 12:00 PM – Help load gear for band sound check
  • 2:00 PM – Run to store for herbal tea
  • 4:00 PM – Escort artist to stage; wait for set end
  • 7:00 PM – Assist with load‑out
  • 9:00 PM – Return radios and report to Kirk

🛠️ Gear Marlon Uses

  • Two‑way radio (with earpiece)
  • Festival map and schedule
  • Utility cart or hand truck
  • First aid kit (basic)
  • Water bottles for artists

📋 Talent Runner Training (Led by Kirk & Dwight)

  • Pre‑event orientation: Review artist rider basics, festival layout, and communication protocols.
  • Radio etiquette: How to call for help, report status, and handle emergencies.
  • Hospitality standards: How to interact with artists respectfully, maintain confidentiality.
  • Safety training: Lifting techniques, fire exits, and emergency procedures.
  • Cultural sensitivity: Rastafari protocols, ital food handling, and proper behavior in green rooms.

🌟 From Runner to Leader

Marlon started as a volunteer runner three years ago. His dedication led to a paid position, and he now mentors new runners each festival. Selassiefest believes in growing talent from within – many of our stage managers and hospitality coordinators began as runners.

Interested in becoming a Talent Runner? Email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Talent Runner Application" in the subject line. Volunteers receive meals, festival credentials, and a t‑shirt.
