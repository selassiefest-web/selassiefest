Hospitality Coordinator

🍽️ Current Hospitality Coordinator: Dwight

Dwight brings a passion for hospitality and a deep understanding of Rastafari dietary principles (ital food, vegan, vegetarian). With years of experience catering large events and managing green rooms, Dwight ensures that every artist, speaker, and VIP feels cared for and respected.

Position Overview

The Hospitality Coordinator is responsible for all artist and VIP hospitality, including green room setup, catering, and special accommodations. This role reports to the Artistic Director and works closely with the Artist Liaison (Kirk) and the Kitchen team (under Vendor Relations).

Key Responsibilities

  • Stock and maintain green rooms with water, snacks, toiletries, and comfort items.
  • Coordinate catering for artists, crew, and VIPs based on rider requirements (ital, vegan, allergies, etc.).
  • Manage hotel bookings, check‑in/out, and room assignments.
  • Arrange artist transportation to/from airport, hotel, and festival grounds.
  • Distribute per diems and hospitality packets (wristbands, schedules, maps).
  • Respond to last‑minute requests (e.g., extra towels, specific beverages).
  • Oversee the hospitality volunteer team (runners, green room attendants).

Dwight's Background

  • Catering & Events: Managed hospitality for over 50 music festivals, including California Roots and Reggae Rise Up.
  • Rastafari Ital Cuisine: Extensive knowledge of ital cooking (no salt, no animal products, natural ingredients) and ability to source authentic Caribbean ingredients.
  • Artist Relations: Worked directly with major reggae acts (Damian Marley, Stephen Marley, Chronixx) to fulfill complex riders.
  • Logistics: Skilled in coordinating group travel, hotel blocks, and on‑site transportation.

Reporting & Collaboration

  • Reports to: Artistic Director
  • Works with: Artist Liaison (Kirk), Kitchen Manager (Vendor Relations), Volunteer Coordinator
  • Manages: Green room attendants, hospitality volunteers, and runners

📌 Quick Facts

  • 📅 Status: Filled (Dwight)
  • 📧 Contact: selassiefest@gmail.com (please mark attention: Hospitality)
  • ⏰ Commitment: 2 months pre‑festival + event days
  • 🎯 Key Metrics: Artist satisfaction with hospitality, zero unmet rider items

🥗 Sample Green Room Stock

  • Fresh fruit (mango, banana, citrus)
  • Ital stews and rice & peas
  • Herbal teas (cerasee, mint, ginger)
  • Coconut water, ginger beer, sorrel drink
  • Natural spring water
  • Toiletries (towels, soap, lotion)
  • Comfort items (blankets, pillows)

🚐 Transportation Coordination

  • Airport pickups (JFK, Newark, LaGuardia)
  • Shuttles between hotel and festival grounds
  • VIP cars for headliners

📅 Timeline (Led by Dwight)

  • 8 weeks before: Collect all artist hospitality riders from Artist Liaison (Kirk).
  • 6 weeks before: Book hotel blocks and negotiate group rates.
  • 4 weeks before: Hire green room attendants; coordinate with Volunteer Coordinator.
  • 2 weeks before: Finalize catering menus with Kitchen Manager; confirm special diets.
  • 1 week before: Confirm all travel arrangements; share schedules with artists.
  • 2 days before: Stock green rooms; inspect all spaces.
  • Event days: Manage check‑ins, replenish green rooms, respond to requests.
  • Post‑event: Collect unused supplies; send thank‑you notes to vendors and volunteers.

🤝 Key Collaborations

  • Kirk (Artist Liaison): Dwight receives all artist rider details from Kirk and confirms any special requests.
  • Kitchen Manager: Dwight coordinates ital meals, snacks, and dietary accommodations with the kitchen team.
  • Volunteer Coordinator: Dwight requests green room attendants and hospitality runners.
  • Vendor Relations (Beverage Stands): Dwight ensures green rooms have fresh coconut water, juices, and herbal teas.

Contact: For hospitality inquiries or to volunteer in green room support, email selassiefest@gmail.com with "Hospitality" in the subject line.
