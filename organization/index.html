Ras Tafari Inc. Nonprofit Organization

Ras Tafari Inc. is a registered 501(c)(3) cultural festival dedicated to celebrating the legacy of Emperor Haile Selassie I and promoting community uplift through music, education, and the arts. Our organizational structure ensures effective governance, mission alignment, and operational excellence.

📋 Board of Directors

The governing body provides strategic oversight, fiduciary responsibility, and ensures the festival stays true to its mission.

  • President / Chair
  • Vice President / Vice Chair
  • Treasurer
  • Secretary
  • Directors‑At‑Large (3–9)
