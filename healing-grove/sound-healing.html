✦ VIBRATION AS MEDICINE

In the Rastafari and African diaspora traditions, sound is not just heard – it is felt in the bones, the blood, the spirit. 🎧 The Science of Sound Healing

RESEARCH + ANCESTRAL WISDOM

Frequency & Brain States
Sound vibrations can shift brainwaves from beta (active) to alpha (relaxed) and theta (meditative/deep healing). Studies show reduced cortisol and improved immune response.

Nyabinghi as Medicine
Traditional Nyabinghi drumming uses specific rhythms that synchronize heartbeats and induce trance states – a form of communal sound healing practiced for generations.

Crystal Bowls & Chakra Tuning
Quartz crystal bowls produce pure frequencies that resonate with the body's energy centers (chakras), promoting energetic balance and emotional release.

Educational note: Sound healing is a complementary practice. Our facilitators are trained sound therapists, not medical practitioners.
