Healing Grove — Mental Wellness for Black Men

Rootswork

A space for Black men to set down the weight — speak freely, sit with a brother who understands, and begin the real work of healing. No judgment. No agenda. No performance of strength you don't feel right now.

11:00 AM – 6:00 PM

Free · Donation Welcome
Rootswork is SelassieFest's dedicated mental wellness space for Black men — a shaded, intentional corner of the Healing Grove hosted by Paul Kelly, MA LPC of Finding Balance Counseling & Therapy Services. Walk up when the moment finds you. Stay as long as you need.
Healing is not weakness. It is the most rooted thing a man can do.
How It Works

Three Ways In — Your Pace, Your Terms

Rootswork is built for the way men actually move through a festival. No waiting room. No appointment. No commitment required until you're ready.

🌱
Layer 1 — The Seed
Paul steps to the Main Stage between acts at 12:30 PM. Ten minutes. One truth. A story that lands in the chest — then an invitation to the Grove. No commitment required. Just listen.
🪑
Layer 2 — Dwell Here
Paul is simply present at the Grove Shade Arbor from 11:00 AM–4:00 PM. Two chairs. Some shade. A sign that says "Real Talk. No Judgment." Walk up when the moment finds you. Some conversations are three minutes. Some are thirty.
🔥
Layer 3 — Brothers Circle
A focused, facilitated Reasoning Circle at 4:00 PM for men ready to go deeper. Paul leads. An elder co-facilitates. Capped at 20 brothers. What is spoken here stays here. No phones. No recording.

Come As You Are

What to Expect at Rootswork

Whether you're struggling, curious, skeptical, or just needed a place to sit — all of it is welcome here.

No appointment, no sign-in, no pressure. Walk up when the moment finds you.
Conversations with Paul are confidential. The Brothers Circle is sealed — what's said here stays here.
Two chairs. Some shade. A cup of Ital tea if you want it. Designed to feel like the most natural conversation you've had all year.
No recording. No phones inside the Brothers Circle. No social media posts of this space.
You don't have to be in crisis to deserve this conversation. Curious is enough. Present is enough.
Brothers Circle is capped at 20. Let someone at the resource table know early if you want that seat at 4:00 PM.
Your Host

Meet Paul Kelly

A Black man. A therapist. A brother who studied, practiced, and showed up.

Paul Kelly
MA · LPC · Psychotherapist · Finding Balance Counseling & Therapy Services

Paul is a Masters-level Licensed Professional Counselor specializing in trauma, PTSD, and the full range of challenges that find Black men carrying weight they were never meant to carry alone. He works with individuals, couples, adolescents, and those adjusting to life after incarceration — bringing a multicultural lens and a practice rooted in human connection.

His tools — CBT, DBT, Motivational Interviewing, Mindfulness, Strength-Based therapy — are translated at Rootswork into plain language and lived truth. No jargon. No distance. No clipboard between you and the conversation.

"Even though people are individuals, everyone is connected through a natural energy. This connection enables everyone to work through their problems — without the fear of being alone."
