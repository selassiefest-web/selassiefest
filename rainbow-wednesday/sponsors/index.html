Rainbow Wednesday · an initiative of Ras Tafari Inc.

Help us show that music belongs to everybody.

Free professional DJ education where Deaf, hard-of-hearing and hearing young people learn, create and perform together. We are inviting a small circle of founding partners to build it with us, and to see what the next generation of South Side Chicago does with it.

Talk with usSee the program

Why partners say yes

We are not asking for a logo spot on a DJ party. We are inviting partners to co-author something specific: a community-based inclusive DJ education pilot in a neighborhood park, with Deaf and hard-of-hearing advisors shaping it from day one, and honest results shared afterward.

A specific story

A real program with a named place, real students and measured outcomes, not a one-night sponsorship.

Shaped with the community

Deaf and hard-of-hearing advisors help decide what the experience should be before technology is chosen.

Accountable

Ras Tafari Inc., a 501(c)(3), owns the program. Partners receive a yearly Impact Report. No partner receives children's personal data.

Partnership opportunities

Give what only you can give. Equipment, expertise and funding all count, and we credit each by name.

DJ technology partner

Controllers and equipment for the stations, curriculum alongside our 14 Missions, technical guidance, and a remote co-design session with the original production team. This is its own request: "play the beat."

Accessibility technology partner

A loan, not a purchase: one controller and 12 to 24 wearable sound devices with chargers, instructions and a technician for night one, or a staffed haptics activation for our date. This is its own request: "feel the beat."

Access services partner

ASL interpretation, captioning and screen and lighting setup, so every session is communication-accessible.

Youth pathway partner

A route from learner to developing DJ to teen apprentice to paid youth mentor, with employers, schools and youth-employment programs.

What founding partners receive

  • Credit by name, with permission, on the Rainbow Wednesday page, class materials and at showcases.
  • A seat in the co-design conversation and the technical rehearsal.
  • Photos of students at the stations (with family consent) and the yearly Impact Report.
  • A written case study and a plain-language account of what worked and what we changed.

What partners do not receive

Children's personal information, rights to photograph or record children without family consent, or any say over curriculum or student selection.
