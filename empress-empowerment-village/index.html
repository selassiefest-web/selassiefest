Four Zones
Career Discovery Zone

The Empress Passport

Every participant receives a passport on entry — it turns passive attendance into an active path through all four zones.

Marketplace of PossibilitiesVisit 5 career stations; record one insight from each
Girls Leadership CornerParticipate in one workshop or activity
Women's Nonprofit PavilionConnect with 3 nonprofits; record one resource
Empress StageAttend one panel or keynote session

Completed passports are eligible for a certificate, prize and scholarship drawings, internship listings, and mentorship matching. A "I Will Become…" wall lets every visitor declare — and later return to update — their own career aspiration.
