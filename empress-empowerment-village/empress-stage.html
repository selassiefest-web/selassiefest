Empress Stage

Keynote speeches, panel discussions, and recognition ceremonies — the stage is divided into themed sessions rather than serving as general entertainment.

Programming Schedule
TimeSessionContent
10:00–12:00Women in STEM & HealthcarePanels featuring physicians, engineers, scientists, and technology leaders
12:00–2:00Women in Law, Politics, Business & EntrepreneurshipPanels featuring attorneys, judges, entrepreneurs, and business executives
2:00–4:00Leadership & Civic EngagementPanels on leadership, wealth building, civic participation, and public service
4:00–5:00Awards & RecognitionHonoring women making an impact in the community
5:00–6:00Keynote AddressInspirational closing speaker
Speaker Recruitment
  • CEOs, judges, physicians, engineers, entrepreneurs, elected officials, educators
  • Representation across industries and career levels
  • Speakers commit to a 15–20 minute presentation followed by Q&A

Staffing: stage manager, audio-visual technician, speaker coordinator, emcee.
