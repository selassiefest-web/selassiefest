# Night Out in the Parks 2027: artist packets (due Oct. 24)

Source: `rti/kits/night-out-2027/index.html` (Artists table, budget, and the Oct. 24 step). CPD opens the application Nov. 1 at 12:01 am; due Dec. 8. Albert Harris is the main applicant.

## What each act must give us
1. **Bio**, about 100 words.
2. **Photo** (a live photo is best).
3. **1 or 2 performance links** (video or audio).
4. **Confirmed fee** (budget figures below; change a budget line only if a fee differs).
5. **Summer 2027 availability** (CPD season is June 1 to Dec. 31, 2027; Roots in the Parks runs June to September).
6. **W-9** (needed to be paid and for 1099s).
7. **Background check**: CPD requires one for anyone who has physical interaction with the public.
