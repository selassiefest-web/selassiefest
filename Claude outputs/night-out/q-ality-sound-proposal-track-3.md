## The four review criteria

### 1. Enriching and engaging programming
A sound system night is the way Jamaican music has been shared in the street and the yard for decades. Q-Ality Sound brings that format to the park: a selector reading the crowd, a microphone voice that welcomes people and names the tunes, and a few custom dubplates that carry Q-Ality's own identity. [confirm and add: the styles Q-Ality is known for, a signature segment, and what makes its night different from Prestige Sound's]

### 2. Production viability
Self-contained and run entirely by Q-Ality Sound. Q-Ality provides the sound system, the stage, lighting and power, and sets up, runs and strikes the show. The park supplies the space and a permit fee waiver, and nothing else is asked of it.

- **Levels:** kept within park limits. The sound is aimed away from seating, and earplugs are free for children.
- **Footprint:** [confirm]
- **Power:** Q-Ality brings its own power source, included in the fee.

### 3. Commitment to community connectivity
At each park we start with the park supervisor and the park's partners, and ask what the neighborhood wants, who should open the night, and where flyers will be seen. A local DJ, school group or choir opens, so the stage is shared with the neighborhood. Outreach runs through block clubs, churches, libraries and Caribbean and African diaspora organizations near each park.

**Outreach and marketing plan**
- Six weeks out: meet the park supervisor and partners and set the local opener.
- Four weeks out: CPD's printed postcards and posters (up to 1,000 pieces) at the park, libraries, barbershops, groceries and churches nearby, in English and Spanish.
- Two weeks out: e-blast, social posts tagging the Chicago Park District, and notices to community groups.
- Week of: reminder posts and a street team at the park.

**Accessibility**
- ASL interpretation if funded (optional line below).
- An accessible viewing area on a firm surface, with seating for elders.
- Free earplugs for children, speakers kept away from the seating, and a quieter zone at the back.
- Bilingual English and Spanish flyers, and routes kept clear for wheelchairs and strollers.

### 4. Budget feasibility
Q-Ality Sound's fee is a flat amount per show and covers everything: the selector and microphone, sound system, stage, lights, power and travel. There is no separate equipment, staging or sound rental. The park provides the space and a permit fee waiver. Insurance is carried in the applicant's name.
